\section*{NewGardiner: a small LuaLaTeX how-to}

Write Unicode hieroglyphs inside \verb|\egypt{...}|.  The helpers below are
ordinary TeX commands, so they nest: first make a cluster, then scale, raise,
mirror, rotate, or overlap it.  Their arguments remain normal, searchable
Unicode signs.

\demo{\string\egypt\{\egypt{𓂀𓆣𓇳}\}}{𓂀𓆣𓇳}
\demo{\string\egypt\{\string\egH\{\egypt{𓂀}\}\{\egypt{𓆣}\}\}}{\egH{𓂀}{𓆣}}
\demo{\string\egypt\{\string\egV\{\egypt{𓂀}\}\{\egypt{𓆣}\}\}}{\egV{𓂀}{𓆣}}
\demo{\string\egypt\{\string\egV\{\egypt{𓂀}\}\{\string\egH\{\egypt{𓆣}\}\{\egypt{𓇳}\}\}\}}{\egV{𓂀}{\egH{𓆣}{𓇳}}}
\demo{\string\egypt\{\string\egScale\{0.7\}\{\egypt{𓂀𓆣}\}\}}{\egScale{0.7}{𓂀𓆣}}
\demo{\string\egypt\{\string\egWide\{0.65\}\{\egypt{𓂀𓆣}\}\}}{\egWide{0.65}{𓂀𓆣}}
\demo{\string\egypt\{\string\egTall\{1.35\}\{\egypt{𓂀}\}\}}{\egTall{1.35}{𓂀}}
\demo{\string\egypt\{\egypt{𓂀}\string\egRaise\{0.3em\}\{\string\egScale\{0.55\}\{\egypt{𓇳}\}\}\}}{𓂀\egRaise{0.3em}{\egScale{0.55}{𓇳}}}
\demo{\string\egypt\{\string\egMirror\{\egypt{𓀀𓆣}\}\}}{\egMirror{𓀀𓆣}}
\demo{\string\egypt\{\string\egRotate\{15\}\{\egypt{𓂀}\}\}}{\egRotate{15}{𓂀}}
\demo{\string\egypt\{\string\egOverlap\{\string\egScale\{0.65\}\{\egypt{𓇳}\}\}\{\egypt{𓂀}\}\}}{\egOverlap{\egScale{0.65}{𓇳}}{𓂀}}

\subsection*{Unicode controls and HieroJax}

Unicode also defines joiners, segment markers, insertions, enclosures,
mirroring, rotation selectors, blanks, and damage markers.  For example,
U+13430 is the vertical joiner (\egypt{𓐰}) and U+13431 is the horizontal
joiner (\egypt{𓐱}).  NewGardiner contains glyphs used by implementations of
that standard, but the plain TTF is a glyph source rather than a complete
layout engine: LuaLaTeX does not turn those controls into quadrats by itself.
Use the TeX helpers above for predictable PDF layout.  Use the
\href{https://nederhof.github.io/hierojax/}{HieroJax editor and renderer} when
you need the complete Unicode composition system; its
\href{https://nederhof.github.io/hierojax/syntax.html}{syntax table} is the
compact reference.  Font coverage and releases are documented in the
\href{https://github.com/nederhof/newgardiner}{NewGardiner repository}.
