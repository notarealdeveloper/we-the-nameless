\section*{NewGardiner: a small LuaLaTeX how-to}

Write Unicode hieroglyphs inside \verb|\egyptNew{...}|.  The helpers below are
ordinary TeX commands, so they nest: first make a cluster, then scale, raise,
mirror, rotate, or overlap it.  Their arguments remain normal, searchable
Unicode signs.

\demo{\string\egyptNew\{\egyptNew{𓂀𓆣𓇳}\}}{𓂀𓆣𓇳}
\demo{\string\egyptNew\{\string\egH\{\egyptNew{𓂀}\}\{\egyptNew{𓆣}\}\}}{\egH{𓂀}{𓆣}}
\demo{\string\egyptNew\{\string\egV\{\egyptNew{𓂀}\}\{\egyptNew{𓆣}\}\}}{\egV{𓂀}{𓆣}}
\demo{\string\egyptNew\{\string\egV\{\egyptNew{𓂀}\}\{\string\egH\{\egyptNew{𓆣}\}\{\egyptNew{𓇳}\}\}\}}{\egV{𓂀}{\egH{𓆣}{𓇳}}}
\demo{\string\egyptNew\{\string\egScale\{0.7\}\{\egyptNew{𓂀𓆣}\}\}}{\egScale{0.7}{𓂀𓆣}}
\demo{\string\egyptNew\{\string\egWide\{0.65\}\{\egyptNew{𓂀𓆣}\}\}}{\egWide{0.65}{𓂀𓆣}}

\subsection*{Unicode controls and HieroJax}

Unicode also defines joiners, segment markers, insertions, enclosures,
mirroring, rotation selectors, blanks, and damage markers.  For example,
U+13430 is the vertical joiner (\egyptNew{𓐰}) and U+13431 is the horizontal
joiner (\egyptNew{𓐱}).  NewGardiner contains glyphs used by implementations of
that standard, but the plain TTF is a glyph source rather than a complete
layout engine: LuaLaTeX does not turn those controls into quadrats by itself.
Use the TeX helpers above for predictable PDF layout.  Use the
\href{https://nederhof.github.io/hierojax/}{HieroJax editor and renderer} when
you need the complete Unicode composition system; its
\href{https://nederhof.github.io/hierojax/syntax.html}{syntax table} is the
compact reference.  Font coverage and releases are documented in the
\href{https://github.com/nederhof/newgardiner}{NewGardiner repository}.
