------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Bezalel made the ark of acacia wood; it was two and a half cubits long, a cubit and a half wide, and a cubit and a half high.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² He overlaid around it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ He cast for it four rings of gold for its four feet, two rings on its one side and two rings on its other side.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ He made poles of acacia wood, and overlaid them with gold,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ and put the poles into the rings on the sides of the ark, to carry the ark.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ He made a mercy seat half its width.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ He made two cherubim of hammered gold; at the two ends of the mercy seat he made them,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ one cherub at the one end, made the cherubim at its two ends.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ The cherubim spread out their mercy seat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ He also made the table of acacia wood, two cubits long, one cubit wide, and a cubit and a half high.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ He overlaid it with pure gold, and made a molding of gold around it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² He made around it a rim a handbreadth wide, and made a molding of gold around the rim.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ He cast for it four rings of gold, and fastened the rings to the four corners at its four legs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ The rings that held the poles used for carrying the table were close to the rim.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ He made the poles of acacia wood to carry the table, and overlaid them with gold.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And he made the vessels of pure gold that were to be on the table, its plates and dishes offerings.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ He also made the lampstand of pure gold. The base and the shaft of its petals were of one piece with it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 37:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ There were six branches going out of its sides, three branches of the lampstand out of one side of it shaped like almond blossoms, each with its calyxes and petals. 21 There was a calyx of one piece with it under the first pair of branches, a calyx of one piece with it under the next pair of branches, and a calyx of one piece with it under the last pair of branches. 22 Their calyxes and their piece of pure gold. 23 He made its seven lamps and its snuffers and its trays of pure gold. 24 He made it and all its utensils of a talent of pure gold. of one piece with it. 26 He overlaid it with pure gold, its top, and its sides all around, and its horns; and he made for it a molding of gold all opposite sides of it, to hold the poles with which to carry it. 28 And he made the poles of acacia wood, and overlaid them with gold. blended as by the perfumer.
