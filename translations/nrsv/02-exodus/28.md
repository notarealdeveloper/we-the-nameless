## Exodus 28:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Then bring near to you your brother Aaron, and his sons with him, sons, Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.

## Exodus 28:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² You shall make sacred vestments for the glorious adornment of your brother Aaron.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 28:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 28:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ These are the vestments that they shall make: a breastpiece, an ephod, a robe, a checkered tunic, a turban, and a sash. his sons to serve me as priests,

## Exodus 28:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ they shall use gold, blue, purple, and crimson yarns, and fine linen.

## Exodus 28:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 28:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Exodus 28:21
