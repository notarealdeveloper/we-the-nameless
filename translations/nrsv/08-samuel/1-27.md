------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 27:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ David said in his heart, "I shall now perish one day by the hand of Philistines; then Saul will despair of seeking me any longer within the borders of Israel, and I shall escape out of his hand."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 27:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² So David set out King Achish son of Maoch of Gath.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 27:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ David stayed with Achish at Gath, two wives, Ahinoam of Jezreel, and Abigail of Carmel, Nabal's widow.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 27:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ When Saul was told that David had fled to Gath, he no longer sought for him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 27:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Then David said to Achish, "If I have found favor in your sight, let a place be given me in one of the country towns, so that I may live there; for why should your servant live in the royal city with you?"

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 27:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ So that of Judah to this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 27:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ The length of time that David lived in the country of the Philistines was one year and four months.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 27:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Now David and his men went up and made raids on the Geshurites, the Girzites, and the Amalekites; for these were the landed settlements from Telam on the way to Shur and on to the land of Egypt.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 27:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ David came back to Achish.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 27:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ When Achish asked, "Against whom have you made a raid today?" David would say, "Against the Negeb of Judah," of the Kenites."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 27:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ David left neither man nor woman alive to be 'David has done so and so.'" Such was his practice all the time he lived in the country of the Philistines.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 27:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Achish trusted David, thinking, "He shall always be my servant."
