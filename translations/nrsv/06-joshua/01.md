------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ After the death of Moses the servant of the LORD, the LORD spoke to Joshua son of Nun, Moses' assistant, saying,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² "My servant Moses is the land that I am giving to them, to the Israelites.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Every place that the sole of your foot will tread upon I have given to you, as I promised to Moses.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ From the wilderness and the Lebanon as far as the great river, the river Euphrates, all the land of the Hittites, to the Great Sea in the west shall be your territory.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ No one shall be able to stand against you all the days of your life. As I was with Moses, so I will be with you; I will not fail you or forsake you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Be strong and courageous; for you shall put this people in possession of the land that I swore to their ancestors to give them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Only be strong and very courageous, left, so that you may be successful wherever you go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ This book of the and night, so that you may be careful to act in accordance with all that is written in it. For then you shall make your way prosperous, and then you shall be successful.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ I hereby command you: Be strong and is with you wherever you go."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Then Joshua commanded the officers of the people,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ "Pass through the camp, and command the people: 'Prepare your provisions; possession of the land that the LORD your God gives you to possess.'"

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² To the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh Joshua said,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ "Remember the word that Moses the servant of the place of rest, and will give you this land.'

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Your wives, your little armed before your kindred and shall help them,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ until the LORD gives rest to your kindred as well as to you, and they too take possession of servant of the LORD gave you beyond the Jordan to the east."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ They answered Joshua: "All that you have commanded us we will do, and wherever you send us we will go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ Just as we obeyed Moses you, as he was with Moses!

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Whoever rebels against your orders and disobeys your words, whatever you command, shall be put to death. Only be strong and courageous."
