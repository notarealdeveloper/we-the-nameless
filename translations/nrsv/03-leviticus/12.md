## Leviticus 12:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 12:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Speak to the people of Israel, saying: unclean.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 12:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ On the eighth day the flesh of his foreskin shall be circumcised.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 12:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Her time of blood purification shall be thirty-three days; she shall not touch any holy thing, or come into the sanctuary, until the days of her purification are completed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 12:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ If she bears a female child, blood purification shall be sixty-six days.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 12:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ When the days of her purification are completed, whether for a son or for a daughter, she shall bring to the priest at the entrance of the pigeon or a turtledove for a sin offering.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 12:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ He shall offer it before the female.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 12:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ If she cannot afford a sheep, she shall take two turtledoves shall be clean.
