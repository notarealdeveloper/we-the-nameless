## Leviticus 19:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 19:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Speak to all the congregation of the people of Israel and say to them: You shall be holy, for I the LORD your God am holy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 19:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ You shall each the LORD your God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 19:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Do not turn to idols or make cast images for yourselves: I am the LORD your God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 19:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ When you offer a sacrifice of well-being to the LORD, offer it in such a way that it is acceptable in your behalf.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 19:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ It shall be eaten on the same third day shall be consumed in fire.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 19:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ If it is eaten at all on the third day, it is an abomination; it will not be acceptable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 19:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ All who eat it holy to the LORD; and any such person shall be cut off from the people.
