## Leviticus 11:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying to them:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 11:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Speak to the people of Israel, saying: eat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 11:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Any animal that has divided hoofs and is cleft-footed and chews the cud — such you may eat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 11:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ But among those that chew the cud or have divided hoofs, you shall not eat the following: the camel, for even for you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 11:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ The rock badger, for even though it chews the cud, it does not have divided hoofs; it is unclean for you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 11:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ The hare, for even for you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 11:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ The pig, for even though it has divided hoofs and is cleftfooted, it does not chew the cud; it is unclean for you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 11:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Of their flesh unclean for you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 11:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ These you may eat, of all that are in the waters. Everything in the waters that has fins and scales, whether in the seas or in the streams — such you may eat.
