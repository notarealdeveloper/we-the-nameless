------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² He said to Aaron, "Take a bull calf for a sin offering before the LORD.

## Leviticus 9:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 9:4

## Leviticus 9:6

## Leviticus 9:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------
