------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² When any of you sin and commit a neighbor,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ or have found something lost and lied about it — if you sin thereby —

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ when you have sinned and realize your guilt, and that was committed to you, or the lost thing that you found,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ or principal amount and shall add one-fifth to it. You shall pay it to its owner when you realize your guilt.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And you shall bring to the priest, flock, or its equivalent, for a guilt offering.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ The priest shall make for any of the things that one may do and incur guilt thereby.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Command Aaron and his sons, saying: This is the ritual of the burnt offering. The burnt offering itself while the fire on the altar shall be kept burning.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ The priest shall put on his linen vestments after putting on his linen undergarments next to the burnt offering on the altar, and place them beside the altar.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ carry the ashes out to a clean place outside the camp.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² The fire on the altar shall be kept burning; it shall not go out. Every morning the priest smoke the fat pieces of the offerings of well-being.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ A perpetual fire shall be kept burning on the altar; it shall not go out.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ This is the ritual of the grain offering: The sons of Aaron shall offer it before the LORD, in front of the altar.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ They shall take from it a handful of the choice flour and oil of the grain offering, with all the portion into smoke on the altar as a pleasing odor to the LORD.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ they shall eat it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ It shall not be baked with leaven. I have given it as offering and the guilt offering.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Every male among the descendants them shall become holy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ This is the offering that Aaron and his sons shall offer to the LORD on the day when he is anointed: one-tenth of an ephah of choice flour as a regular offering, half of it in the morning and half in the evening.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ It shall be made with oil on a pieces, and you shall present it as a pleasing odor to the LORD.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And entirely into smoke.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Every grain offering of a priest shall be wholly burned; it shall not be eaten.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ The LORD spoke to Moses, saying:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ Speak to Aaron and his sons, saying: This is the ritual of the sin offering. The sin offering shall be slaughtered; it is most holy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ The priest who offers it as a sin offering shall eat of it; it shall be eaten in a holy place, in the court of the tent of meeting.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ Whatever touches its flesh shall become holy; and when bespattered part in a holy place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ An earthen vessel in which it was boiled shall be broken; but if it is boiled in a bronze vessel, that shall be scoured and rinsed in water.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ Every male among the priests shall eat of it; it is most holy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 6:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ But no sin offering shall be eaten from the holy place; it shall be burned with fire.
