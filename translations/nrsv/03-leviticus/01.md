------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Leviticus 1:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ If the offering is a burnt offering from the herd, you shall offer a male without blemish; you shall bring it to the entrance of the tent of meeting, for acceptance in your behalf before the LORD.

## Leviticus 1:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------
