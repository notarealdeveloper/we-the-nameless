------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Give ear, O heavens, and I will speak; let the earth hear the words of my mouth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² May my teaching drop like the rain, my speech condense like the dew; like showers on new growth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ For I will proclaim the name of the LORD; ascribe greatness to our God!

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ The Rock, his work is perfect, and all his ways are just. just and upright is he;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ yet his degenerate children have dealt falsely with him, a perverse and crooked generation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Do you thus repay the LORD, O foolish and senseless people? who made you and established you?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Remember the days of old, consider the years long past; ask your father, and he will inform you; your elders, and they will tell you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ When the Most High apportioned the nations, according to the number of the gods;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ the LORD's own portion was his people, Jacob his allotted share.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ He sustained him in a desert land, in a howling wilderness waste; guarded him as the apple of his eye.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ As an eagle stirs up its nest, and hovers over its young;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² the LORD alone guided him; no foreign god was with him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ He set him atop the heights of the land, and fed him with produce of the field; with oil from flinty rock;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ curds from the herd, and milk from the flock, with fat of lambs and rams; you drank fine wine from the blood of grapes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Jacob ate his fill; Jeshurun grew fat, and kicked. You grew fat, bloated, and gorged! and scoffed at the Rock of his salvation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ They made him jealous with strange gods, with abhorrent things they provoked him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ They sacrificed to demons, not God, whom your ancestors had not feared.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ You were unmindful of the Rock that bore you; you forgot the God who gave you birth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ The LORD saw it, and was jealous; he spurned his sons and daughters.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ He said: I will hide my face from them, I will see what their end will be; children in whom there is no faithfulness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ They made me jealous with what is no god, provoked me with their idols. provoke them with a foolish nation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² For a fire is kindled by my anger, and burns to the depths of Sheol; and sets on fire the foundations of the mountains.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ I will heap disasters upon them, spend my arrows against them:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ wasting hunger, bitter pestilence. with venom of things crawling in the dust.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ In the street the sword shall bereave, nursing child and old gray head.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ I thought to scatter them and blot out the memory of them from humankind;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ but I feared provocation by the enemy, and say, "Our hand is triumphant; it was not the LORD who did all this."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ They are a nation void of sense; there is no understanding in them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ If they were wise, they would understand this; they would discern what the end would be.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ How could one have routed a thousand, the LORD had given them up?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ Indeed their rock is not like our Rock; our enemies are fools.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² Their vine comes from the vinestock of Sodom, from the vineyards of Gomorrah; their clusters are bitter;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ their wine is the poison of serpents, the cruel venom of asps.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ Is not this laid up in store with me, sealed up in my treasuries?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ Vengeance is mine, and recompense, for the time when their foot shall slip; their doom comes swiftly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ Indeed the LORD will vindicate his people, neither bond nor free remaining.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ Then he will say: Where are their gods,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:38

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁸ who ate the fat of their sacrifices, and drank the wine of their libations? let them be your protection!

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:39

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁹ See now that I, even I, am he; there is no god besides me. I kill and I make alive; I wound and I heal; and no one can deliver from my hand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:40

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁰ For I lift up my hand to heaven,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:41

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴¹ when I whet my flashing sword, and my hand takes hold on judgment; and will repay those who hate me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:42

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴² I will make my arrows drunk with blood, from the long-haired enemy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:43

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴³ Praise, O heavens, his people, worship him, all you gods! and take vengeance on his adversaries; and cleanse the land for his people.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:44

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁴ Moses came and recited all the words of this song in the hearing of the people, he and Joshua son of Nun.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:45

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁵ When Moses had finished reciting all these words to all Israel,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:46

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁶ he said to them: "Take to heart the words of this law.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:47

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁷ This is no trifling matter for you, but rather crossing over the Jordan to possess."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:48

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁸ On that very day the LORD addressed Moses as follows:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:49

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁹ "Ascend giving to the Israelites for a possession;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 32:50

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵⁰ you shall die there on the giving to the Israelites."
