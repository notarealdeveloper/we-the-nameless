------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ You are children of the LORD your God. You must not lacerate yourselves or shave your forelocks for the dead.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² For you are a people holy to the LORD your God; it is you the LORD has chosen out of all the peoples on earth to be his people, his treasured possession.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ You shall not eat any abhorrent thing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ These are the animals you may eat: the ox, the sheep, the goat,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ the deer, the gazelle, the roebuck, the wild goat, the ibex, the antelope, and the mountainsheep.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Any animal that divides the hoof and has the hoof cleft in two, and chews the cud, among the animals, you may eat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Yet of those that chew the cud or have the hoof cleft you shall not eat these: but do not divide the hoof; they are unclean for you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And the pig, carcasses.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Of all that live in water you may eat these: whatever has fins and scales you may eat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And whatever does not have fins and scales you shall not eat; it is unclean for you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ You may eat any clean birds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² But these are the ones that you shall not eat: the eagle, the vulture, the osprey,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ the buzzard, the kite of any kind;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ every raven of any kind;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ the ostrich, the nighthawk, the sea gull, the hawk of any kind;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ the little owl and the great owl, the water hen

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ and the desert owl, the carrion vulture and the cormorant,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ the stork, the heron of any kind; the hoopoe and the bat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And all winged insects are unclean for you; they shall not be eaten.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ You may eat any clean winged creature.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ You shall not eat anything that dies of itself; you may give it to foreigner. For you are a people holy to the LORD your God. You shall not boil a kid in its mother's milk.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² Set apart a tithe of all the yield of your seed that is brought in yearly from the field.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ In the presence of the LORD your God, in the place that he will choose as a dwelling for his name, you shall eat the tithe of your grain, your wine, and your oil, as well as the firstlings of your herd and flock, so that you may learn to fear the LORD your God always.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ But if, when the LORD your God has blessed you, the distance is so great your God will choose to set his name is too far away from you,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ then place that the LORD your God will choose;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ spend the money for you and your household rejoicing together.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ As for the Levites allotment or inheritance with you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ Every third year you shall bring out the full tithe of your produce for that year, and store it within your towns;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 14:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ the Levites, because they their fill so that the LORD your God may bless you in all the work that you undertake.
