------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ This is the blessing with which Moses, the man of God, blessed the Israelites before his death.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² He said: and dawned from Seir upon us; he shone forth from Mount Paran. With him were myriads of holy ones; at his right, a host of his own.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Indeed, O favorite among peoples, all his holy ones were in your charge; accepted direction from you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Moses charged us with the law, as a possession for the assembly of Jacob.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ There arose a king in Jeshurun, the united tribes of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ May Reuben live, and not die out, even though his numbers are few.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And this he said of Judah: and bring him to his people; and be a help against his adversaries.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And of Levi he said: with whom you contended at the waters of Meribah;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ who said of his father and mother, "I regard them not"; and did not acknowledge his children. and kept your covenant.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ They teach Jacob your ordinances, and Israel your law; and whole burnt offerings on your altar.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Bless, O LORD, his substance, and accept the work of his hands; of those that hate him, so that they do not rise again.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Of Benjamin he said: the beloved rests between his shoulders.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And of Joseph he said: and of the deep that lies beneath;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ with the choice fruits of the sun, and the rich yield of the months;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ with the finest produce of the ancient mountains, and the abundance of the everlasting hills;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ with the choice gifts of the earth and its fullness, and the favor of the one who dwells on Sinai. on the brow of the prince among his brothers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ A firstborn bull — majesty is his! His horns are the horns of a wild ox; driving them to the ends of the earth; such the thousands of Manasseh.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And of Zebulun he said: Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out; and Issachar, in your tents.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ They call peoples to the mountain; there they offer the right sacrifices; and the hidden treasures of the sand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And of Gad he said: Blessed be the enlargement of Gad! Gad lives like a lion; he tears at arm and scalp.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ He chose the best for himself, for there a commander's allotment was reserved; and his ordinances for Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And of Dan he said: that leaps forth from Bashan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ And of Naphtali he said: possess the west and the south.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And of Asher he said: Most blessed of sons be Asher; and may he dip his foot in oil.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ Your bars are iron and bronze; and as your days, so is your strength.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ There is none like God, O Jeshurun, majestic through the skies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ He subdues the ancient gods, shatters the forces of old; and said, "Destroy!"

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ So Israel lives in safety, where the heavens drop down dew.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Deuteronomy 33:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ Happy are you, O Israel! Who is like you, and the sword of your triumph! and you shall tread on their backs.
