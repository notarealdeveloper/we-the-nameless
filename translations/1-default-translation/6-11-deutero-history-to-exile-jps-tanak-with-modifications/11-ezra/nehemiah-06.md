------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ When word reached Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arab, and the rest of
our enemies that I had rebuilt the wall and not a breach remained in
it—though at that time I had not yet set up doors in the gateways—

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Sanballat and Geshem sent a message to me, saying, “Come, let us get
together in Kephirim in the Ono valley”; they planned to do me harm.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ I sent them messengers, saying, “I am engaged in a great work and
cannot come down, for the work will stop if I leave it in order to come
down to you.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ They sent me the same message four times, and I gave them the same
answer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Sanballat sent me the same message a fifth time by his servant, who
had an open letter with him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Its text was: “Word has reached the nations, and Geshem too says that
you and the Jews are planning to rebel—for which reason you are building
the wall—and that you are to be their king. Such is the word.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ You have also set up prophets in Jerusalem to proclaim about you,
‘There is a king in Judah!’ Word of these things will surely reach the
king; so come, let us confer together.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ I sent back a message to him, saying, “None of these things you
mention has occurred; they are figments of your imagination”—

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ for they all wished to intimidate us, thinking, “They will desist from
the work, and it will not get done.” Now strengthen my hands!

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Then I visited Shemaiah son of Delaiah son of Mehetabel when he was
housebound, and he said, “Let us meet in the House of God, inside the
sanctuary, And let us shut the doors of the sanctuary, for they are
coming to kill you, By night they are coming to kill you.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ I replied, “Will a man like me take flight? Besides, who such as I
can go into the sanctuary and live? I will not go in.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Then I realized that it was not God who sent him, but that he uttered
that prophecy about me—Tobiah and Sanballat having hired him—

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ because he was a hireling, that I might be intimidated and act thus
and commit a sin, and so provide them a scandal with which to reproach
me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ “O my God, remember against Tobiah and Sanballat these deeds of
theirs, and against Noadiah the prophetess, and against the other
prophets that they wished to intimidate me!”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ The wall was finished on the twenty-fifth of Elul, after fifty-two
days.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ When all our enemies heard it, all the nations round about us were
intimidated, and fell very low in their own estimation; they realized
that this work had been accomplished by the help of our God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ Also in those days, the nobles of Judah kept up a brisk
correspondence with Tobiah, and Tobiah with them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Many in Judah were his confederates, for he was a son-in-law of
Shecaniah son of Arah, and his son Jehohanan had married the daughter of
Meshullam son of Berechiah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 6:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ They would also speak well of him to me, and would divulge my affairs
to him. Tobiah sent letters to intimidate me.
