------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 3:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ When the seventh month arrived—the Israelites being settled in their
towns—the entire people assembled as one man in Jerusalem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 3:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Then Jeshua son of Jozadak and his brother priests, and Zerubbabel son
of Shealtiel and his brothers set to and built the altar of the God of
Israel to offer burnt offerings upon it as is written in the Teaching of
Moses, the man of God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 3:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ They set up the altar on its site because they were in fear of the
peoples of the land, and they offered burnt offerings on it to YHWH,
burnt offerings each morning and evening.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 3:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Then they celebrated the festival of Tabernacles as is written, with
its daily burnt offerings in the proper quantities, on each day as is
prescribed for it,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 3:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ followed by the regular burnt offering and the offerings for the new
moons and for all the sacred fixed times of YHWH, and whatever freewill
offerings were made to YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 3:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ From the first day of the seventh month they began to make burnt
offerings to YHWH, though the foundation of the Temple of YHWH had not
been laid.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 3:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ They paid the hewers and craftsmen with money, and the Sidonians and
Tyrians with food, drink, and oil to bring cedarwood from Lebanon by sea
to Joppa, in accord with the authorization granted them by King Cyrus of
Persia.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 3:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ In the second year after their arrival at the House of God, at
Jerusalem, in the second month, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua
son of Jozadak, and the rest of their brother priests and Levites, and
all who had come from the captivity to Jerusalem, as their first step
appointed Levites from the age of twenty and upward to supervise the
work of the House of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 3:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Jeshua, his sons and brothers, Kadmiel and his sons, the sons of
Judah, together were appointed in charge of those who did the work in
the House of God; also the sons of Henadad, their sons and brother
Levites.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 3:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ When the builders had laid the foundation of the Temple of YHWH,
priests in their vestments with trumpets, and Levites sons of Asaph with
cymbals were stationed to give praise to YHWH, as King David of Israel
had ordained.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 3:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ They sang songs extolling and praising YHWH, “For He is good, His
steadfast love for Israel is eternal.” All the people raised a great
shout extolling YHWH because the foundation of the House of YHWH had
been laid.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 3:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Many of the priests and Levites and the chiefs of the clans, the old
men who had seen the first house, wept loudly at the sight of the
founding of this house. Many others shouted joyously at the top of their
voices.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 3:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ The people could not distinguish the shouts of joy from the people’s
weeping, for the people raised a great shout, the sound of which could
be heard from afar.
