------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ All the tribes of Israel came to David at Hebron and said, “We are
your own flesh and blood.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Long before now, when Saul was king over us, it was you who led Israel
in war; and YHWH said to you: You shall shepherd My people Israel; you
shall be ruler of Israel.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ All the elders of Israel came to the king at Hebron, and King David
made a pact with them in Hebron before YHWH. And they anointed David
king over Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ David was thirty years old when he became king, and he reigned forty
years.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ In Hebron he reigned over Judah seven years and six months, and in
Jerusalem he reigned over all Israel and Judah thirty-three years.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ The king and his men set out for Jerusalem against the Jebusites who
inhabited the region. David was told, “You will never get in here! Even
the blind and the lame will turn you back.” (They meant: David will
never enter here.)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ But David captured the stronghold of Zion; it is now the City of
David.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ On that occasion David said, “Those who attack the Jebusites shall
reach the water channel and [strike down] the lame and the blind, who
are hateful to David.” That is why they say: “No one who is blind or
lame may enter the House.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ David occupied the stronghold and renamed it the City of David; David
also fortified the surrounding area, from the Millo inward.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ David kept growing stronger, for YHWH, the God of Hosts, was with
him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ King Hiram of Tyre sent envoys to David with cedar logs, carpenters,
and stonemasons; and they built a palace for David.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Thus David knew that YHWH had established him as king over Israel and
had exalted his kingship for the sake of His people Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ After he left Hebron, David took more concubines and wives in
Jerusalem, and more sons and daughters were born to David.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ These are the names of the children born to him in Jerusalem:
Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Ibhar, Elishua, Nepheg, and Japhia;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ When the Philistines heard that David had been anointed king over
Israel, the Philistines marched up in search of David; but David heard
of it, and he went down to the fastness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ The Philistines came and spread out over the Valley of Rephaim.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ David inquired of YHWH, “Shall I go up against the Philistines? Will
You deliver them into my hands?” And YHWH answered David, “Go up, and I
will deliver the Philistines into your hands.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ Thereupon David marched to Baal-perazim, and David defeated them
there. And he said, “YHWH has broken through my enemies before me as
waters break through [a dam].” That is why that place was named
Baal-perazim.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ The Philistines abandoned their idols there, and David and his men
carried them off.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² Once again the Philistines marched up and spread out over the Valley
of Rephaim.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ David inquired of YHWH, and He answered, “Do not go up, but circle
around behind them and confront them at the baca trees.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And when you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the baca
trees, then go into action, for YHWH will be going in front of you to
attack the Philistine forces.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 5:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ David did as YHWH had commanded him; and he routed the Philistines
from Geba all the way to Gezer.
