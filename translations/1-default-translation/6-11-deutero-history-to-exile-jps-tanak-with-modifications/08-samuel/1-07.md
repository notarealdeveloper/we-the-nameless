------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ The men of Kiriath-jearim came and took up the Ark of YHWH and brought
it into the house of Abinadab on the hill; and they consecrated his son
Eleazar to have charge of the Ark of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² A long time elapsed from the day that the Ark was housed in
Kiriath-jearim, twenty years in all; and all the House of Israel yearned
after YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And Samuel said to all the House of Israel, “If you mean to return to
YHWH with all your heart, you must remove the alien gods and the
Ashtaroth from your midst and direct your heart to YHWH and serve Him
alone. Then He will deliver you from the hands of the Philistines.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And the Israelites removed the Baalim and Ashtaroth and they served
YHWH alone.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Samuel said, “Assemble all Israel at Mizpah, and I will pray to YHWH
for you.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ They assembled at Mizpah, and they drew water and poured it out before
YHWH; they fasted that day, and there they confessed that they had
sinned against YHWH. And Samuel acted as chieftain of the Israelites at
Mizpah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ When the Philistines heard that the Israelites had assembled at
Mizpah, the lords of the Philistines marched out against Israel. Hearing
of this, the Israelites were terrified of the Philistines

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ and they implored Samuel, “Do not neglect us and do not refrain from
crying out to YHWH our God to save us from the hands of the
Philistines.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Thereupon Samuel took a suckling lamb and sacrificed it as a whole
burnt offering to YHWH; and Samuel cried out to YHWH in behalf of
Israel, and YHWH responded to him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ For as Samuel was presenting the burnt offering and the Philistines
advanced to attack Israel, YHWH thundered mightily against the
Philistines that day. He threw them into confusion, and they were routed
by Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ The men of Israel sallied out of Mizpah and pursued the Philistines,
striking them down to a point below Beth-car.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Samuel took a stone and set it up between Mizpah and Shen, and named
it Eben-ezer: “For up to now,” he said, “YHWH has helped us.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ The Philistines were humbled and did not invade the territory of
Israel again; and the hand of YHWH was set against the Philistines as
long as Samuel lived.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ The towns which the Philistines had taken from Israel, from Ekron to
Gath, were restored to Israel; Israel recovered all her territory from
the Philistines. There was also peace between Israel and the Amorites.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Samuel judged Israel as long as he lived.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Each year he made the rounds of Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah, and acted
as judge over Israel at all those places.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 7:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ Then he would return to Ramah, for his home was there, and there too
he would judge Israel. He built an altar there to YHWH.
