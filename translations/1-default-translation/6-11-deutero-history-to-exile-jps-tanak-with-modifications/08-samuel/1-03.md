------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Young Samuel was in the service of YHWH under Eli. In those days the
word of YHWH was rare; prophecy was not widespread.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² One day, Eli was asleep in his usual place; his eyes had begun to fail
and he could barely see.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ The lamp of God had not yet gone out, and Samuel was sleeping in the
temple of YHWH where the Ark of God was.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ YHWH called out to Samuel, and he answered, “I’m coming.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ He ran to Eli and said, “Here I am; you called me.” But he replied, “I
didn’t call you; go back to sleep.” So he went back and lay down.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Again YHWH called, “Samuel!” Samuel rose and went to Eli and said,
“Here I am; you called me.” But he replied, “I didn’t call, my son; go
back to sleep.”—

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Now Samuel had not yet experienced YHWH; the word of YHWH had not yet
been revealed to him.—

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ YHWH called Samuel again, a third time, and he rose and went to Eli
and said, “Here I am; you called me.” Then Eli understood that YHWH was
calling the boy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And Eli said to Samuel, “Go lie down. If you are called again, say,
‘Speak, LORD, for Your servant is listening.’” And Samuel went to his
place and lay down.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ YHWH came, and stood there, and He called as before: “Samuel!
Samuel!” And Samuel answered, “Speak, for Your servant is listening.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ YHWH said to Samuel: “I am going to do in Israel such a thing that
both ears of anyone who hears about it will tingle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² In that day I will fulfill against Eli all that I spoke concerning
his house, from beginning to end.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And I declare to him that I sentence his house to endless punishment
for the iniquity he knew about—how his sons committed sacrilege at will
—and he did not rebuke them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Assuredly, I swear concerning the house of Eli that the iniquity of
the house of Eli will never be expiated by sacrifice or offering.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Samuel lay there until morning; and then he opened the doors of the
House of YHWH. Samuel was afraid to report the vision to Eli,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ but Eli summoned Samuel and said, “Samuel, my son”; and he answered,
“Here.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And [Eli] asked, “What did He say to you? Keep nothing from me. Thus
and more may God do to you if you keep from me a single word of all that
He said to you!”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Samuel then told him everything, withholding nothing from him. And
[Eli] said, “He is YHWH; He will do what He deems right.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Samuel grew up and YHWH was with him: He did not leave any of
Samuel’s predictions unfulfilled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ All Israel, from Dan to Beer-sheba, knew that Samuel was trustworthy
as a prophet of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 3:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And YHWH continued to appear at Shiloh: YHWH revealed Himself to
Samuel at Shiloh with the word of YHWH;
