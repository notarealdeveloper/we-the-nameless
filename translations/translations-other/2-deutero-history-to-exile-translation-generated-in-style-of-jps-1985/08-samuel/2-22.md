II Samuel 22:1	David addressed the words of this song to the LORD, after the LORD had saved him from the hands of all his enemies and from the hands of Saul.
II Samuel 22:2	He said: O LORD, my crag, my fastness, my deliverer!
II Samuel 22:3	O God, the rock wherein I take shelter: My shield, my mighty champion, my fortress and refuge! My savior, You who rescue me from violence!
II Samuel 22:4	All praise! I called on the LORD, And I was delivered from my enemies.
II Samuel 22:5	For the breakers of Death encompassed me, The torrents of Belial terrified me;
II Samuel 22:6	The snares of Sheol encircled me, The coils of Death engulfed me.
II Samuel 22:7	In my anguish I called on the LORD, Cried out to my God; In His Abode He heard my voice, My cry entered His ears.
II Samuel 22:8	Then the earth rocked and quaked, The foundations of heaven shook— Rocked by His indignation.
II Samuel 22:9	Smoke went up from His nostrils, From His mouth came devouring fire; Live coals blazed forth from Him.
II Samuel 22:10	He bent the sky and came down, Thick cloud beneath His feet.
II Samuel 22:11	He mounted a cherub and flew; He was seen on the wings of the wind.
II Samuel 22:12	He made pavilions of darkness about Him, Dripping clouds, huge thunderheads;
II Samuel 22:13	In the brilliance before Him Blazed fiery coals.
II Samuel 22:14	The LORD thundered forth from heaven, The Most High sent forth His voice;
II Samuel 22:15	He let loose bolts, and scattered them; Lightning, and put them to rout.
II Samuel 22:16	The bed of the sea was exposed, The foundations of the world were laid bare By the mighty roaring of the LORD, At the blast of the breath of His nostrils.
II Samuel 22:17	He reached down from on high, He took me, Drew me out of the mighty waters;
II Samuel 22:18	He rescued me from my enemy so strong, From foes too mighty for me.
II Samuel 22:19	They attacked me on my day of calamity, But the LORD was my stay.
II Samuel 22:20	He brought me out to freedom, He rescued me because He was pleased with me.
II Samuel 22:21	The LORD rewarded me according to my merit, He requited the cleanness of my hands.
II Samuel 22:22	For I have kept the ways of the LORD And have not been guilty before my God;
II Samuel 22:23	I am mindful of all His rules And have not departed from His laws.
II Samuel 22:24	I have been blameless before Him, And have guarded myself against sinning—
II Samuel 22:25	And the LORD has requited my merit, According to my purity in His sight.
II Samuel 22:26	With the loyal You deal loyally; With the blameless hero, blamelessly.
II Samuel 22:27	With the pure You act in purity, And with the perverse You are wily.
II Samuel 22:28	To humble folk You give victory, And You look with scorn on the haughty.
II Samuel 22:29	You, O LORD, are my lamp; The LORD lights up my darkness.
II Samuel 22:30	With You, I can rush a barrier, With my God, I can scale a wall.
II Samuel 22:31	The way of God is perfect, The word of the LORD is pure. He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him.
II Samuel 22:32	Yea, who is a god except the LORD, Who is a rock except God?—
II Samuel 22:33	The God, my mighty stronghold, Who kept my path secure;
II Samuel 22:34	Who made my legs like a deer’s, And set me firm on the heights;
II Samuel 22:35	Who trained my hands for battle, So that my arms can bend a bow of bronze!
II Samuel 22:36	You have granted me the shield of Your protection And Your providence has made me great.
II Samuel 22:37	You have let me stride on freely, And my feet have not slipped.
II Samuel 22:38	I pursued my enemies and wiped them out, I did not turn back till I destroyed them.
II Samuel 22:39	I destroyed them, I struck them down; They rose no more, they lay at my feet.
II Samuel 22:40	You have girt me with strength for battle, Brought low my foes before me,
II Samuel 22:41	Made my enemies turn tail before me, My foes—and I wiped them out.
II Samuel 22:42	They looked, but there was none to deliver; To the LORD, but He answered them not.
II Samuel 22:43	I pounded them like dust of the earth, Stamped, crushed them like dirt of the streets.
II Samuel 22:44	You have rescued me from the strife of peoples, Kept me to be a ruler of nations; Peoples I knew not must serve me.
II Samuel 22:45	Aliens have cringed before me, Paid me homage at the mere report of me.
II Samuel 22:46	Aliens have lost courage And come trembling out of their fastnesses.
II Samuel 22:47	The LORD lives! Blessed is my rock! Exalted be God, the rock Who gives me victory;
II Samuel 22:48	The God who has vindicated me And made peoples subject to me,
II Samuel 22:49	Rescued me from my enemies, Raised me clear of my foes, Saved me from lawless men!
II Samuel 22:50	For this I sing Your praise among the nations And hymn Your name:
II Samuel 22:51	Tower of victory to His king, Who deals graciously with His anointed, With David and his offspring evermore.
