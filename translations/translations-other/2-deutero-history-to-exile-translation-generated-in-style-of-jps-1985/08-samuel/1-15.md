I Samuel 15:1	Samuel said to Saul, “I am the one the LORD sent to anoint you king over His people Israel. Therefore, listen to the LORD’s command!
I Samuel 15:2	“Thus said the LORD of Hosts: I am exacting the penalty for what Amalek did to Israel, for the assault he made upon them on the road, on their way up from Egypt.
I Samuel 15:3	Now go, attack Amalek, and proscribe all that belongs to him. Spare no one, but kill alike men and women, infants and sucklings, oxen and sheep, camels and asses!”
I Samuel 15:4	Saul mustered the troops and enrolled them at Telaim: 200,000 men on foot, and 10,000 men of Judah.
I Samuel 15:5	Then Saul advanced as far as the city of Amalek and lay in wait in the wadi.
I Samuel 15:6	Saul said to the Kenites, “Come, withdraw at once from among the Amalekites, that I may not destroy you along with them; for you showed kindness to all the Israelites when they left Egypt.” So the Kenites withdrew from among the Amalekites.
I Samuel 15:7	Saul destroyed Amalek from Havilah all the way to Shur, which is close to Egypt,
I Samuel 15:8	and he captured King Agag of Amalek alive. He proscribed all the people, putting them to the sword;
I Samuel 15:9	but Saul and the troops spared Agag and the best of the sheep, the oxen, the second-born, the lambs, and all else that was of value. They would not proscribe them; they proscribed only what was cheap and worthless.
I Samuel 15:10	The word of the LORD then came to Samuel:
I Samuel 15:11	“I regret that I made Saul king, for he has turned away from Me and has not carried out My commands.” Samuel was distressed and he entreated the LORD all night long.
I Samuel 15:12	Early in the morning Samuel went to meet Saul. Samuel was told, “Saul went to Carmel, where he erected a monument for himself; then he left and went on down to Gilgal.”
I Samuel 15:13	When Samuel came to Saul, Saul said to him, “Blessed are you of the LORD! I have fulfilled the LORD’s command.”
I Samuel 15:14	“Then what,” demanded Samuel, “is this bleating of sheep in my ears, and the lowing of oxen that I hear?”
I Samuel 15:15	Saul answered, “They were brought from the Amalekites, for the troops spared the choicest of the sheep and oxen for sacrificing to the LORD your God. And we proscribed the rest.”
I Samuel 15:16	Samuel said to Saul, “Stop! Let me tell you what the LORD said to me last night!” “Speak,” he replied.
I Samuel 15:17	And Samuel said, “You may look small to yourself, but you are the head of the tribes of Israel. The LORD anointed you king over Israel,
I Samuel 15:18	and the LORD sent you on a mission, saying, ‘Go and proscribe the sinful Amalekites; make war on them until you have exterminated them.’
I Samuel 15:19	Why did you disobey the LORD and swoop down on the spoil in defiance of the LORD’s will?”
I Samuel 15:20	Saul said to Samuel, “But I did obey the LORD! I performed the mission on which the LORD sent me: I captured King Agag of Amalek, and I proscribed Amalek,
I Samuel 15:21	and the troops took from the spoil some sheep and oxen—the best of what had been proscribed—to sacrifice to the LORD your God at Gilgal.”
I Samuel 15:22	But Samuel said: “Does the LORD delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices As much as in obedience to the LORD’s command? Surely, obedience is better than sacrifice, Compliance than the fat of rams.
I Samuel 15:23	For rebellion is like the sin of divination, Defiance, like the iniquity of teraphim. Because you rejected the LORD’s command, He has rejected you as king.”
I Samuel 15:24	Saul said to Samuel, “I did wrong to transgress the LORD’s command and your instructions; but I was afraid of the troops and I yielded to them.
I Samuel 15:25	Please, forgive my offense and come back with me, and I will bow low to the LORD.”
I Samuel 15:26	But Samuel said to Saul, “I will not go back with you; for you have rejected the LORD’s command, and the LORD has rejected you as king over Israel.”
I Samuel 15:27	As Samuel turned to leave, Saul seized the corner of his robe, and it tore.
I Samuel 15:28	And Samuel said to him, “The LORD has this day torn the kingship over Israel away from you and has given it to another who is worthier than you.
I Samuel 15:29	Moreover, the Glory of Israel does not deceive or change His mind, for He is not human that He should change His mind.”
I Samuel 15:30	But [Saul] pleaded, “I did wrong. Please, honor me in the presence of the elders of my people and in the presence of Israel, and come back with me until I have bowed low to the LORD your God.”
I Samuel 15:31	So Samuel followed Saul back, and Saul bowed low to the LORD.
I Samuel 15:32	Samuel said, “Bring forward to me King Agag of Amalek.” Agag approached him with faltering steps; and Agag said, “Ah, bitter death is at hand!”
I Samuel 15:33	Samuel said: “As your sword has bereaved women, So shall your mother be bereaved among women.” And Samuel cut Agag down before the LORD at Gilgal.
I Samuel 15:34	Samuel then departed for Ramah, and Saul went up to his home at Gibeah of Saul.
I Samuel 15:35	Samuel never saw Saul again to the day of his death. But Samuel grieved over Saul, because the LORD regretted that He had made Saul king over Israel.
