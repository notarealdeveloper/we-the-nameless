Judges 18:1	In those days there was no king in Israel, and in those days the tribe of Dan was seeking a territory in which to settle; for to that day no territory had fallen to their lot among the tribes of Israel.
Judges 18:2	The Danites sent out five of their number, from their clan seat at Zorah and Eshtaol—valiant men—to spy out the land and explore it. “Go,” they told them, “and explore the land.” When they had advanced into the hill country of Ephraim as far as the house of Micah, they stopped there for the night.
Judges 18:3	While in the vicinity of Micah’s house, they recognized the speech of the young Levite, so they went over and asked him, “Who brought you to these parts? What are you doing in this place? What is your business here?”
Judges 18:4	He replied, “Thus and thus Micah did for me—he hired me and I became his priest.”
Judges 18:5	They said to him, “Please, inquire of God; we would like to know if the mission on which we are going will be successful.”
Judges 18:6	“Go in peace,” the priest said to them, “YHWH views with favor the mission you are going on.”
Judges 18:7	The five men went on and came to Laish. They observed the people in it dwelling carefree, after the manner of the Sidonians, a tranquil and unsuspecting people, with no one in the land to molest them and with no hereditary ruler. Moreover, they were distant from the Sidonians and had no dealings with anybody.
Judges 18:8	When [the men] came back to their kinsmen at Zorah and Eshtaol, their kinsmen asked them, “How did you fare?”
Judges 18:9	They replied, “Let us go at once and attack them! For we found that the land was very good, and you are sitting idle! Don’t delay; go and invade the land and take possession of it,
Judges 18:10	for God has delivered it into your hand. When you come, you will come to an unsuspecting people; and the land is spacious and nothing on earth is lacking there.”
Judges 18:11	They departed from there, from the clan seat of the Danites, from Zorah and Eshtaol, six hundred strong, girt with weapons of war.
Judges 18:12	They went up and encamped at Kiriath-jearim in Judah. That is why that place is called “the Camp of Dan” to this day; it lies west of Kiriath-jearim.
Judges 18:13	From there they passed on to the hill country of Ephraim and arrived at the house of Micah.
Judges 18:14	Here the five men who had gone to spy out the Laish region remarked to their kinsmen, “Do you know, there is an ephod in these houses, and teraphim, and a sculptured image and a molten image? Now you know what you have to do.”
Judges 18:15	So they turned off there and entered the home of the young Levite at Micah’s house and greeted him.
Judges 18:16	The six hundred Danite men, girt with their weapons of war, stood at the entrance of the gate,
Judges 18:17	while the five men who had gone to spy out the land went inside and took the sculptured image, the ephod, the teraphim, and the molten image. The priest was standing at the entrance of the gate, and the six hundred men girt with their weapons of war,
Judges 18:18	while those men entered Micah’s house and took the sculptured image, the molten image, the ephod, and the household gods. The priest said to them, “What are you doing?”
Judges 18:19	But they said to him, “Be quiet; put your hand on your mouth! Come with us and be our father and priest. Would you rather be priest to one man’s household or be priest to a tribe and clan in Israel?”
Judges 18:20	The priest was delighted. He took the ephod, the household gods, and the sculptured image, and he joined the people.
Judges 18:21	They set out again, placing the children, the cattle, and their household goods in front.
Judges 18:22	They had already gone some distance from Micah’s house, when the men in the houses near Micah’s mustered and caught up with the Danites.
Judges 18:23	They called out to the Danites, who turned around and said to Micah, “What’s the matter? Why have you mustered?”
Judges 18:24	He said, “You have taken my priest and the gods that I made, and walked off! What do I have left? How can you ask, ‘What’s the matter’?”
Judges 18:25	But the Danites replied, “Don’t do any shouting at us, or some desperate men might attack you, and you and your family would lose your lives.”
Judges 18:26	So Micah, realizing that they were stronger than he, turned back and went home; and the Danites went on their way,
Judges 18:27	taking the things Micah had made and the priest he had acquired. They proceeded to Laish, a people tranquil and unsuspecting, and they put them to the sword and burned down the town.
Judges 18:28	There was none to come to the rescue, for it was distant from Sidon and they had no dealings with anyone; it lay in the valley of Beth-rehob. They rebuilt the town and settled there,
Judges 18:29	and they named the town Dan, after their ancestor Dan who was Israel’s son. Originally, however, the name of the town was Laish.
Judges 18:30	The Danites set up the sculptured image for themselves; and Jonathan son of Gershom son of Manasseh, and his descendants, served as priests to the Danite tribe until the land went into exile.
Judges 18:31	They maintained the sculptured image that Micah had made throughout the time that the House of God stood at Shiloh.
