## 1 Samuel 8:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 8:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And the thing displeased Samuel, when they said, Give us a king to judge us. And Samuel prayed to YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 8:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And YHWH said to Samuel, Hearken to the voice of the people in all that they say to you: for they've not rejected you, but they've rejected me, that I should not reign over them.

---

Footnote 8:7. They have not rejected you, but they have rejected me. The demand for a king is not merely a bad political idea. It is a theological vote of no confidence.
