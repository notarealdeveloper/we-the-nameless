## 2 Samuel 7:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And it was, when the king sat in his house, and YHWH had given him rest round about from all his enemies;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² That the king said to Nathan the prophet, See now, I live in a house of cedar, but the ark of God lives within curtains.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And Nathan said to the king, Go, do all that is in your heart; for YHWH is with you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And it was that night, that the word of YHWH came to Nathan, saying,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Go and tell my servant David, Thus says YHWH, Shall you build me a house for me to live in?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Whereas I've not lived in any house since the time that I brought up the children of Israel from Egypt, even to this day, but have walked in a tent and in a tabernacle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ In all the places in which I've walked with all the children of Israel spoke I a word with any of the tribes of Israel, whom I commanded to feed my people Israel, saying, Why build you not me a house of cedar?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And now shall you say to my servant David, Thus says YHWH of Armies, I took you from the sheepfold, from following the sheep, to be ruler over my people, over Israel:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And I was with you wherever you went, and have cut off all your enemies from your sight, and have made you a great name, like to the name of the great men that are in the earth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Moreover I'll appoint a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, that they may live in a place of their own, and move no more; neither shall the children of wickedness afflict them any more, as before,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And as since the time that I commanded judges to be over my people Israel, and have caused you to rest from all your enemies. Also YHWH tells you that he will make you a house.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And when your days are fulfilled, and you shall sleep with your fathers, I'll set up your seed after you, that shall come out from your body, and I'll establish his kingdom.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ He shall build a house for my name, and I'll establish the throne of his kingdom forever.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ I'll be his father, and he shall be my son. If he commit guilt, I'll discipline him with the rod of men, and with the blows of the children of men:

---

Footnote 7:14. The promise to David is dynastic and disciplinary at the same time: sonship does not cancel the rod.

## 2 Samuel 7:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And your house and your kingdom shall be established forever before you: your throne shall be established forever.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ According to all these words, and according to all this vision, so did Nathan speak to David.

## 2 Samuel 7:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And went king David in, and sat before YHWH, and he said, Who am I, my Lord YHWH? and what is my house, that you have brought me here?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And this was yet a small thing in your sight, my Lord YHWH; but you have spoken also of your servant’s house for a great while to come. And is this the manner of man, my Lord YHWH?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And what can David say more to you? for you, my Lord YHWH, know your servant.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ Because your word’s sake, and according to your own heart, have you done all these great things, to make your servant know them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² Therefore you are great, O YHWH God: for there is none like you, neither is there any God beside you, according to all that we've heard with our ears.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ And what one nation in the earth is like your people, even like Israel, whom God went to redeem for a people to himself, and to make him a name, and to do for you great things and fearsome, for your land, before your people, that you redeemed to you from Egypt, from the nations and their gods?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ Because you have confirmed to yourself your people Israel to be a people to you forever: and you, YHWH, are become their God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And now, O YHWH God, the word that you have spoken concerning your servant, and concerning his house, establish it forever, and do as you have said.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ And let your name be magnified forever, saying, YHWH of Armies is the God over Israel: and let the house of your servant David be established before you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ Because you, O YHWH of Armies, God of Israel, have revealed to your servant, saying, I'll build you a house: so has your servant found in his heart to pray this prayer to you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And now, my Lord YHWH, you are that God, and your words are true, and you have promised this goodness to your servant:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 7:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ So now let it please you to bless the house of your servant, that it may continue forever before you: for you, my Lord YHWH, have spoken it: and with your blessing let the house of your servant be blessed forever.
