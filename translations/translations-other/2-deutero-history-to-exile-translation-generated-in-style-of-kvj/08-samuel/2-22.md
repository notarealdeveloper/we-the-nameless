------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And David spoke to YHWH the words of this song in the day that YHWH had delivered him from the hand of all his enemies, and from the hand of Saul:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And he said, YHWH is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ The God of my rock; in him will I trust: he is my shield, and the horn of my salvation, my high tower, and my refuge, my savior; you save me from violence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ I'll call on YHWH, who is worthy to be praised: so shall I be saved from mine enemies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ When the waves of death compassed me, the floods of ungodly men made me afraid;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ The sorrows of hell compassed me about; the snares of death prevented me;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ In my distress I called on YHWH, and cried to my God: and he did hear my voice from his temple, and my cry did enter into his ears.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And the earth shook and trembled; the foundations of heaven moved and shook, because he was angry.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ There went up a smoke from his nostrils, and fire from his mouth devoured: coals were kindled by it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ He bowed the heavens also, and came down; and darkness was under his feet.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And he rode on a cherub, and did fly: and he was seen on the wings of the wind.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And he made darkness pavilions round about him, dark waters, and thick clouds of the skies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Through the brightness before him were coals of fire kindled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ YHWH thundered from heaven, and the most High uttered his voice.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And he sent out arrows, and scattered them; lightning, and discomfited them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And the channels of the sea appeared, the foundations of the world were discovered, at the rebuking of YHWH, at the blast of the breath of his nostrils.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ He sent from above, he took me; he drew me from many waters;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ He delivered me from my strong enemy, and from them that hated me: for they were too strong for me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ They prevented me in the day of my calamity: but YHWH was my stay.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ He brought me forth also into a large place: he delivered me, because he delighted in me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ YHWH rewarded me according to my righteousness: according to the cleanness of my hands has he recompensed me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² For I've kept the ways of YHWH, and have not wickedly departed from my God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Because all his judgments were before me: and as for his statutes, I didn't depart from them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ I was also upright before him, and have kept myself from mine guilt.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ So YHWH has recompensed me according to my righteousness; according to my cleanness in his eye sight.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ With the merciful you will show yourself merciful, and with the upright man you will show yourself upright.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ With the pure you will show yourself pure; and with the froward you will show yourself unsavoury.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And the afflicted people you will except: but your eyes are on the haughty, that you may bring them down.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ Because you are my lamp, O YHWH: and YHWH will lighten my darkness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ Because by you I've run through a troop: by my God have I leaped over a wall.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ As for God, his way is perfect; the word of YHWH is tried: he is a buckler to all them that trust in him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² Because who is God, except YHWH? and who is a rock, except our God?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ God is my strength and power: and he makes my way perfect.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ He makes my feet like hinds’ feet: and sets me on my high places.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ He teaches my hands to war; so that a bow of steel is broken by mine arms.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ You have also given me the shield of your salvation: and your gentleness has made me great.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ You have enlarged my steps under me; so that my feet didn't slip.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:38

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁸ I've pursued mine enemies, and destroyed them; and turned not again until I had consumed them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:39

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁹ And I've consumed them, and wounded them, that they couldn't arise: yes, they are fallen under my feet.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:40

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁰ Because you have belted me with strength to battle: them that rose up against me have you subdued under me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:41

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴¹ You have also given me the necks of mine enemies, that I might destroy them that hate me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:42

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴² They looked, but there was none to except; even to YHWH, but he answered them not.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:43

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴³ And did I beat them as small as the dust of the earth, I did stamp them as the mire of the street, and did spread them abroad.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:44

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁴ You also have delivered me from the strivings of my people, you have kept me to be head of the heathen: a people that I knew not shall serve me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:45

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁵ Strangers shall submit themselves to me: as soon as they hear, they shall be obedient to me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:46

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁶ Strangers shall fade away, and they shall be afraid from their close places.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:47

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁷ YHWH lives; and blessed be my rock; and exalted be the God of the rock of my salvation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:48

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁸ It is God that avenges me, and that brings down the people under me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:49

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁹ And that brings me forth from mine enemies: you also have lifted me up on high above them that rose up against me: you have delivered me from the violent man.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 22:50

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵⁰ So I'll give thanks to you, O YHWH, among the heathen, and I'll sing praises to your name.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
