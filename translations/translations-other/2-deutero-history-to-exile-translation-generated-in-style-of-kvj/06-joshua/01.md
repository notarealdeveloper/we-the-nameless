------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And it was after the death of Moses, YHWH’s servant, that YHWH spoke to Joshua the son of Nun, Moses’ attendant, saying,

---

Footnote 1:1. Moses' attendant. The Hebrew calls Joshua Moses' m'sharet, the one who serves or attends him. At the end of the book Joshua will be called YHWH's servant. The title is not just promotion. It is the whole plot in two words.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Moses my servant is dead; now therefore arise, go over this Jordan, you, and all this people, to the land that I do give to them, even to the children of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Every place that the sole of your foot shall tread on, I’ve given it to you, as I said to Moses.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ From the wilderness and this Lebanon even to the great river, the river Euphrates, all the land of the Hittites, and to the great sea toward the going down of the sun, shall be your border.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ No man will be able to stand before you all the days of your life: as I was with Moses, so I'll be with you: I won't fail you, nor forsake you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Be strong and courageous: for to this people shall you divide for an inheritance the land, that I swore to their fathers to give them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Only be strong and very courageous, that you may observe to do according to all the law, that Moses my servant commanded you: turn not from it to the right hand or to the left, that you may prosper wherever you go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ This book of the law won't depart out of your mouth; but you shall meditate in it day and night, that you may observe to do according to all that is written in it: for then you shall make your way prosperous, and then you shall have good success.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Have not I commanded you? Be strong and courageous; don’t be afraid, and don’t be shattered: for YHWH your God is with you wherever you go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Pass through the army, and command the people, saying, Prepare you provisions; for within three days you shall pass over this Jordan, to go in to possess the land, that YHWH your God gives you to possess it.

## Joshua 1:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And to the Reubenites, and to the Gadites, and to half the tribe of Manasseh, spoke Joshua, saying,

## Joshua 1:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Remember the word that Moses the servant of YHWH commanded you, saying, YHWH your God has given you rest, and has given you this land.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Your wives, your little ones, and your cattle, shall stay in the land that Moses gave you across the Jordan; but you shall pass before your brothers armed, all the strong men of force, and help them;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Until YHWH have given your brothers rest, as he has given you, and they also have possessed the land that YHWH your God gives them: then you shall return to the land of your possession, and possess it, that Moses YHWH’s servant gave you across the Jordan toward the rising of the sun.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And they answered Joshua, saying, All that you command us we'll do, and wherever you sendest us, we'll go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ According as we listened to Moses in all things, so will we listen to you: only YHWH your God be with you, as he was with Moses.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 1:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Whosoever he be that does rebel against your commandment, and won't listen to your words in all that you command him, he shall be put to death: only be strong and courageous.
