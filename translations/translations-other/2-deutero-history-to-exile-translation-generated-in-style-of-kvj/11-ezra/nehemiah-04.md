------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ But it was, that when Sanballat, and Tobiah, and the Arabians, and the Ammonites, and the Ashdodites, heard that the walls of Jerusalem were made up, and that the breaches began to be stopped, then they were very angry,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And conspired all of them together to come and to fight against Jerusalem, and to hinder it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Nevertheless we made our prayer to our God, and set a watch against them day and night, because of them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And Judah said, The strength of the bearers of burdens is decayed, and there is much rubbish; so that we aren't able to build the wall.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And our foes said, They shall not know, nor see, until we come in the midst among them, and kill them, and cause the work to cease.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And it was, that when the Jews that dwelt by them came, they said to us ten times, From all places whence you shall return to us they will be upon us.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And I set in the lower places behind the wall, and on the higher places, I even set the people after their families with their swords, their spears, and their bows.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And I looked, and rose up, and said to the nobles, and to the rulers, and to the rest of the people, Don't be afraid of them: remember YHWH, that is great and fearsome, and fight for your brothers, your sons, and your daughters, your wives, and your houses.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And it was, when our enemies heard that it was known to us, and God had brought their counsel to nothing, that we returned all of us to the wall, everyone to his work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And it was from that time forth, that half of my servants worked at the work, and the other half of them held both the spears, the shields, and the bows, and the coats of mail; and the rulers were behind all the house of Judah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ They that built on the wall, and they that bare burdens, with those that laded, everyone with one of his hands done in the work, and with the other hand held a weapon.

---

Footnote 4:11. The builders work with one hand and hold a weapon with one hand. It is one of the Bible's plainest pictures of life after exile: masonry with a sword.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² For the builders, everyone had his sword girded by his side, and so built. And he that sounded the trumpet was by me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And I said to the nobles, and to the rulers, and to the rest of the people, The work is great and large, and we're separated upon the wall, one far from another.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ In what place therefore you hear the sound of the trumpet, resort you there to us: our God shall fight for us.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ So we laboured in the work: and half of them held the spears from the rising of the morning until the stars appeared.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Likewise at the same time said I to the people, Let everyone with his servant lodge within Jerusalem, that in the night they may be a guard to us, and work on the day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 4:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ So nor I, nor my brothers, nor my servants, nor the men of the guard that followed me, none of us put off our clothes, except that everyone put them off for washing.
