------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And it was in the month Nisan, in year twenty of Artaxerxes the king: wine was in front of him, and I lifted the wine and gave it to the king. And I had not been bad in front of him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Therefore the king said to me, Why is your face sad, seeing you aren't sick? this is nothing else but sorrow of heart. And I was very badly afraid,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And said to the king, Let the king live forever: why should not my face be sad, when the city, the place of my fathers' graves, lies waste, and the gates of it are consumed with fire?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Then the king said to me, For what do you make request? So I prayed to the God of the skies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And I said to the king, If it please the king, and if your servant have found favour in your sight, that you would send me to Judah, to the city of my fathers' graves, that I may build it.

---

Footnote 2:5. Nehemiah asks to be sent to the city of his fathers' graves. Before it is a building project, it is kinship and bones.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And the king said to me, (the queen also sitting by him,) For how long shall your journey be? and when wilt you return? So it pleased the king to send me; and I set him a time.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Moreover I said to the king, If it please the king, let letters be given me to the governors across the River, that they may convey me over until I come into Judah;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And a letter to Asaph the keeper of the king's forest, that he may give me timber to make beams for the gates of the fortress that belonged to the house, and for the wall of the city, and for the house that I shall enter into. And the king granted me, according to the good hand of my God upon me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And I came to the governors across the River, and gave them the king's letters. Now the king had sent captains of the army and horsemen with me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ When Sanballat the Horonite, and Tobiah the servant, the Ammonite, heard of it, it grieved them exceedingly that there was come a man to seek the welfare of the children of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ So I came to Jerusalem, and was there three days.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And I arose in the night, I and some few men with me; and I had not told any man what my God had put in my heart to do at Jerusalem: nor was there any beast with me, save the beast that I rode upon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And I went out by night by the gate of the valley, even in front of the Dragon Well, and to the Dung Gate, and viewed the walls of Jerusalem, that were broken down, and the gates of it were consumed with fire.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And I went on to the gate of the fountain, and to the king's pool: but there was no place for the beast that was under me to pass.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Then went I up in the night by the brook, and viewed the wall, and turned back, and entered by the gate of the valley, and so returned.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And the rulers did not know where I went, or what I did; and I had not yet told it to the Jews, nor to the priests, nor to the nobles, nor to the rulers, nor to the rest that did the work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And I said to them, You see the distress that we're in, how Jerusalem lies waste, and the gates of it are burned with fire: come, and let us build up the wall of Jerusalem, that we be no more a disgrace.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And I told them of the hand of my God that was good upon me; as also the king's words that he had spoken to me. And they said, Let us rise up and build. So they strengthened their hands for this good work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ But when Sanballat the Horonite, and Tobiah the servant, the Ammonite, and Geshem the Arabian, heard it, they laughed us to sgrain, and despised us, and said, What is this thing that you do? will you rebel against the king?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And I answered them, and said to them, The God of the skies, he will prosper us; therefore we his servants will arise and build: but you have no portion, nor right, nor memorial, in Jerusalem.
