------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And king Solomon was king over all Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And these were the princes that he had: Azariah the son of Zadok the priest,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Elihoreph and Ahiah, the sons of Shisha, scribes: Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud, the recorder.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the army: and Zadok and Abiathar were the priests:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And Azariah the son of Nathan was over the officers: and Zabud the son of Nathan was principal officer, and the king's friend:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And Ahishar was over the household: and Adoniram the son of Abda was over the tribute.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And Solomon had twelve officers over all Israel, which provided food for the king and his household: each man his month in a year made provision.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And these are their names: The son of Hur, in mount Ephraim:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ The son of Dekar, in Makaz, and in Shaalbim, and Bethshemesh, and Elonbethhanan:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ The son of Hesed, in Aruboth: to him pertained Sochoh, and all the land of Hepher:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ The son of Abinadab, in all the region of Dor: which had Taphath the daughter of Solomon to wife:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Baana the son of Ahilud: to him pertained Taanach and Megiddo, and all Bethshean, which is by Zartanah beneath Jezreel, from Bethshean to Abelmeholah, even to the place that is beyond Jokneam:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ The son of Geber, in Ramothgilead: to him pertained the towns of Jair the son of Manasseh, which are in Gilead: to him also pertained the region of Argob, which is in Bashan, sixty great cities with walls and bronze bars:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Ahinadab the son of Iddo had Mahanaim:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Ahimaaz was in Naphtali: he also took Basmath the daughter of Solomon to wife:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Baanah the son of Hushai was in Asher and in Aloth:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ Jehoshaphat the son of Paruah, in Issachar:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Shimei the son of Elah, in Benjamin:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Geber the son of Uri was in the country of Gilead, in the country of Sihon king of the Amorites, and of Og king of Bashan: and he was the only officer that was in the land.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 4:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ Judah and Israel were many, as the sand which is by the sea in multitude, eating and drinking, and making merry.
