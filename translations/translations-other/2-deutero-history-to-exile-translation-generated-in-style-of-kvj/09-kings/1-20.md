## 1 Kings 20:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And Benhadad the king of Syria gathered all his army together: and there were thirty and two kings with him, and horses, and chariots: and he went up and besieged Samaria, and warred against it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 20:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And he sent messengers to Ahab king of Israel into the city, and said to him, Thus says Benhadad,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 20:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Your silver and your gold is my: your wives also and your children, even the goodliest, are mine.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 20:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And the king of Israel answered and said, My lord, king, according to your saying, I am yours, and all that I have.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 20:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And the messengers came again, and said, Thus speaks Benhadad, saying, Although I have sent to you, saying, You shall deliver me your silver, and your gold, and your wives, and your children:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 20:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Yet I'll send my servants to you tomorrow about this time, and they shall search your house, and the houses of your servants: and it shall be, that whatsoever is pleasant in your eyes, they shall put it in their hand, and take it away.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 20:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And the king of Israel called all the elders of the land, and said, Mark, please, and see how this man seeks bad: for he sent to me for my wives, and for my children, and for my silver, and for my gold: and I denied him not.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 20:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And all the elders and all the people said to him, Listen not to him, nor consent.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 20:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Why he said to the messengers of Benhadad, Tell my lord the king, All that you did send for to your servant at the first I'll do: but this thing I may not do. And the messengers departed, and brought him word again.

## 1 Kings 20:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 20:22

## 1 Kings 20:27
