------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ In those days there was no king in Israel: and in those days the tribe of the Danites sought a legacy for themselves to live in: because to that day all their legacy had not fallen to them among the tribes of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And the children of Dan sent of their family five men from their borders, men of force, from Zorah, and from Eshtaol, to spy out the land, and to search it: and they said to them, Go, search the land: who when they came to Mountain of Ephraim, to the house of Micah, they stayed there.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ When they were by the house of Micah, they knew the voice of the young man the Levite: and they turned in there, and said to him, Who brought you here? and what are you doing in this place? and what have you here?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And he said to them, Thus and thus Micah did for me, and hired me, and I’m his priest.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And they said to him, Ask counsel, we please, of God, that we may know whether our way that we go shall be prosperous.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And the priest said to them, Go in peace: your way is before YHWH on which you go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Then the five men departed, and came to Laish, and saw the people that were in it, how they lived careless, after the manner of the Zidonians, quiet and secure: and there was no magistrate in the land, that might put them to shame in anything: and they were far from the Zidonians, and had no business with any man.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And they came to their brothers to Zorah and Eshtaol: and their brothers said to them, What say you?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And they said, Arise, that we may go up against them: for we’ve seen the land, and here, it is very good: and are you still? be not slothful to go, and to enter to take possession of the land.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ When you go, you shall come to a people secure, and to a large land: for God has given it into your hands; a place where there is no lack of anything that is in the earth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And there went from there of the family of the Danites, out of Zorah and out of Eshtaol, six hundred men appointed with weapons of war.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And they went up, and pitched in Kiriath-jearim, in Judah: therefore they called that place Mahaneh-dan to this day: here, it is behind Kiriath-jearim.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And they passed from there to Mountain of Ephraim, and came to the house of Micah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Then answered the five men that spied out the country of Laish, and said to their brothers, Do you know that there is in these houses an ephod, and teraphim, and a carved image, and a cast image? now therefore consider what you have to do.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And they turned thitherward, and came to the house of the young man the Levite, even to the house of Micah, and saluted him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And the six hundred men appointed with their weapons of war, that were of the children of Dan, stood by the entrance of the gate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And the five men that spied out the land went up, and came there and took the carved image, and the ephod, and the teraphim, and the cast image: and the priest stood in the entrance of the gate with the six hundred men who were girded with weapons of war.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And they went into Micah’s house, and brought the carved image, the ephod, and the teraphim, and the cast image. Then said the priest to them, What are you doing?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And they said to him, Be quiet, lay your hand upon your mouth, and go with us, and be to us a father and a priest: is it better for you to be a priest to the house of one man, or that you be a priest to a tribe and a family in Israel?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And the priest’s heart was glad, and he took the ephod, and the teraphim, and the carved image, and came inside the people.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ So they turned and departed, and put the little ones and the cattle and the carriage before them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And when they were a good way from the house of Micah, the men that were in the houses near to Micah’s house were gathered, and overtook the children of Dan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ And they cried to the children of Dan. And they turned their faces, and said to Micah, What is it to you, that you com with such a company?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And he said, You have taken away my gods that I made, and the priest, and you are gone away: and what have I more? and what is this that you say to me, What ails you?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And the children of Dan said to him, Don’t let your voice be heard among us, lest men bitter of soul fall on you, and you lose your life, and the lives of your household.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ And the children of Dan went their way: and when Micah saw that they were too strong for him, he turned and went back to his house.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ And they took the things that Micah had made, and the priest that he had, and came to Laish, to a people quiet and secure: and they struck them by the mouth of the sword, and burned the city with fire.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And there was no rescuer, because it was far from Sidon, and they had no business with any man: and it was in the valley that lies by Bethrehob. And they built a city, and lived in it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ And they called the name of the city Dan, after the name of Dan their father, who was born to Israel: howbeit the name of the city was Laish at the first.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ And the children of Dan set up the carved image: and Jonathan, the son of Gershom, the son of Manasseh, he and his sons were priests to the tribe of Dan until the day of the captivity of the land.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 18:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ And they set them up Micah’s carved image, that he made, all the time that the house of God was in Shiloh.
