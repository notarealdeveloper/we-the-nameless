------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Then Jerubbaal, who is Gideon, and all the people that were with him, got up early, and pitched beside the well of Harod: so that the army of the Midianites were on the north side of them, by the hill of Moreh, in the valley.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And YHWH said to Gideon, The people who are with you are too many for me to give Midian in their hand, or else Israel will glory over me, saying, My hand rescued me.

---

Footnote 7:2. YHWH says the army is too many, and the book means it. Victory here must not be something Israel can invoice to itself.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And therefore go to, proclaim in the ears of the people, saying, Whosoever is fearful and afraid, let him return and depart early from Mountain of Gilead. And there returned of the people twenty and two thousand: and there remained ten thousand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And YHWH said to Gideon, The people are yet too many; bring them down to the water, and I’ll try them for you there: and it shall be, that of whom I say to you, This shall go with you, the same shall go with you: and of whoever I say to you, This shall not go with you, the same shall not go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ So he brought down the people to the water: and YHWH said to Gideon, each one that lapps of the water with his tongue, as a dog lapps, him shall you set by himself; likewise each one that bows down upon his knees to drink.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And the number of those who lapped, putting their hand to their mouth, was three hundred men: but all the rest of the people bowed down upon their knees to drink water.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And YHWH said to Gideon, By the three hundred men that lapped will I save you, and rescue the Midianites into your hand: and let all the other people go each man to his place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ So the people took provisions in their hand, and their trumpets: and he sent all the rest of Israel each man to his tent, and held on to the three hundred men: and the army of Midian was below him in the valley.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And it was the same night, that YHWH said to him, Arise, get you down to the army: for I’ve gave it into your hand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ But if you fear to go down, go you with Phurah your servant down to the army:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And you shall hear what they say: and afterward shall your hands be strengthened to go down to the army. Then went he down with Phurah his servant to the outside of the armed men that were in the army.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And the Midianites and the Amalekites and all the children of the east lay along in the valley like grasshoppers for multitude: and their camels were without number, as the sand by the sea side for multitude.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And when Gideon was come, here: there was a man that told a dream to his man, and said, Here, I dreamed a dream, and, lo, a cake of barley bread tumbled into the army of Midian, and came to a tent, and struck it that it fell, and overturned it, that the tent lay along.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And his man answered and said, This isn’thing else save the sword of Gideon the son of Joash, a man of Israel: for into his hand has God gave Midian, and all the army.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And it was so, when Gideon heard the telling of the dream, and the interpretation of it, that he worshipped, and returned into the army of Israel, and said, Arise: for YHWH has given into your hand the army of Midian.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And he divided the three hundred men into three companies, and he put a trumpet in each man’s hand, with empty pitchers, and lamps within the pitchers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And he said to them, Look on me, and do likewise: and here, when I come to the outside of the camp, it shall be that, as I do, so shall you do.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ When I blow with a trumpet, I and all that are with me, then blow you the trumpets also on every side of all the camp, and say, The sword of YHWH, and of Gideon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ So Gideon, and the hundred men that were with him, came to the outside of the camp in the beginning of the middle watch: and they had but newly set the watch: and they blew the trumpets, and broke the pitchers that were in their hands.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And the three companies blew the trumpets, and broke the pitchers, and held the lamps in their left hands, and the trumpets in their right hands to blow with it: and they cried, The sword of YHWH, and of Gideon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And they stood each man in his place all around the camp: and all the army ran, and cried, and fled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And the three hundred blew the trumpets, and YHWH set each man’s sword against his man, even throughout all the army: and the army fled to Bethshittah in Zbeforerath, and to the border of Abelmeholah, to Tabbath.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ And the men of Israel gathered themselves together out of Naphtali, and out of Asher, and out of all Manasseh, and pursued the Midianites.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 7:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And Gideon sent messengers throughout all Mountain of Ephraim, saying, come down against the Midianites, and take before them the waters to Bethbarah and Jordan. Then all the men of Ephraim gathered themselves together, and took the waters to Bethbarah and Jordan.

## Judges 7:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------
