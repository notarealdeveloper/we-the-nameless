}
{
	\eP{
		And all of the construction of the Tabernacle of the Tent
		of Meeting was finished. And the children of Israel did
		according to everything that YHWH had commanded Moses.
		They did so.
	}
}
{
%	\footnote{
%		finished, saw, work, did, blessed, made holy. The conclusion of the
%		account of the people’s construction of the Tabernacle mirrors the
%		language of the conclusion of the account of {\God}’s creation of the
%		universe (Gen 1:31–2:3). This further reminds us of the role of the
%		Tabernacle as the channel through which divine and human communicate.
%		And it indicates that, after the first revelation at Sinai, such
%		communication requires action by humans in order to receive it.
%	}
}
