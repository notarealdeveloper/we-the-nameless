## II Sam 9

### 4

**Machir:** sold, bartered, acquired. From the root מכר (*mkr*), "to sell."

**Ammiel:** my people is God / people of God. From עַם (*ʿam*, "people, kinsfolk") + אֵל (*ʾel*, "God").

**Lo-debar:** probably "no pasture" or "without pasture." Usually analyzed as לֹא (*lo*, "not") + דְּבָר (*debar*). The traditional explanation "no word/thing" derives the second element from דבר ("word, matter"), but many scholars instead connect it with a noun meaning "pasture." In either case, the first element is spelled **לֹא** ("not"), not **לוֹ** ("to him").

### 12

**Mica:** who is like? Short form of Micaiah ("Who is like YHWH?"). From מִי (*mi*, "who?") + כ (*ke*, "like").

## II Sam 10

### 1

**Ammon:** "people" or "tribal people." Traditionally connected with עַם (*ʿam*, "people").

**Hanun:** gracious, favored. From חנן (*ḥnn*), "to show favor, be gracious."

### 2

**Nahash:** serpent. From נָחָשׁ (*nāḥāš*), "snake."

### 6

**Beth-rehob:** house of Rehob. From בֵּית (*bet*, "house") + Rehob ("broad place").

**Rehob:** broad place, wide place. From רחב (*rḥb*), "to be broad."

**Zobah:** uncertain. Possibly "station," "garrison," or an old place name of unknown origin.

**Maacah:** oppression? crushing? The etymology is uncertain.

**Tob:** good. From טוֹב (*ṭov*), "good."

### 16

**Hadadezer:** Hadad is help. From the storm-god Hadad + עזר (*ʿzr*, "help").

**Helam:** uncertain.

**Shobach:** uncertain; perhaps "roving" or "overflowing."

## II Sam 11

### 3

**Bathsheba:** daughter of the oath (or daughter of abundance). Usually understood as בַּת (*bat*, "daughter") + שֶׁבַע (*ševaʿ*, "seven, oath").

**Eliam:** my God is kin / God is people. From אֵל (*El*) + עַם (*ʿam*).

**Uriah:** YHWH is my light (or my flame). From אוֹר (*ʾor*, "light") + יה (*Yah*).

### 21

**Jerubbesheth:** let Baal/Bosheth contend. A scribal replacement of the older name **Jerubbaal**, with **bosheth** ("shame") substituted for **Baal**.

**Thebez:** uncertain; perhaps "brightness."

---

### 25

**Jedidiah:** beloved of YHWH. From ידיד (*yedid*, "beloved") + יה (*Yah*).

## II Sam 13

### 3

**Jonadab:** YHWH has been generous. From יה (*Yah*) + נדב (*ndb*, "be generous").

**Shimah:** report, fame. From שמע (*šmʿ*, "hear").

### 23

**Baal-hazor:** lord/master of Hazor.

**Hazor:** enclosure, settlement. From חצר (*ḥṣr*), "court, enclosure."

**Ephraim:** fruitful, doubly fruitful. Traditionally from פרה (*prh*, "be fruitful").

### 37

**Talmai:** furrowed? plowman? Usually connected with תלם (*telem*, "furrow").

**Ammihud:** my kinsman is majesty. From עַם (*ʿam*) + הוֹד (*hod*, "glory, majesty").

**Geshur:** bridge? The etymology is uncertain, often connected with גשר (*gšr*, "bridge").

---

## II Sam 14

### 2

**Tekoa:** pitching (a tent) or trumpet blast. Traditionally linked to תקע (*tqʿ*), "to pitch, drive in, blow."

## II Sam 15

### 12

**Ahithophel:** brother of foolishness? brother of folly? The etymology is uncertain and much debated.

**Gilonite:** inhabitant of Giloh.

**Giloh:** exile? joy? The etymology is uncertain.

### 18

**Gittites:** people from Gath.

**Gath:** winepress.

### 19

**Ittai:** with me. Probably from אִתַּי (*ittay*), "with me."

### 23

**Kidron:** dark, gloomy, murky. Probably from קדר (*qdr*), "be dark."

### 27

**Ahimaaz:** my brother is wrath (or anger). From אָח (*ʾaḥ*, "brother") + מעץ/מעץ? The second element is uncertain; the traditional interpretation is "wrath."

### 32

**Hushai:** perhaps "swift" or "eager." Uncertain.

**Archite:** from the town of Archi.

**Archi:** uncertain.

## II Sam 16

### 5

**Bahurim:** young men, youths. Probably from בחור (*baḥur*, "young man").

**Shimei:** famous, renowned. From שמע (*šmʿ*, "hear"), in the passive sense "one who is heard of."

**Gera:** grain? cud? The etymology is uncertain.

---

## II Sam 17

### 17

**En-rogel:** spring of the fuller (or launderer). From עֵין (*ʿen*, "spring") + רֹגֵל (*rogel*).

### 24

**Mahanaim:** two camps. Dual form of מַחֲנֶה (*maḥaneh*, "camp").

### 25

**Amasa:** burden, load. From עמס (*ʿms*), "to bear a burden."

**Ithra:** abundance, excellence. Probably related to יתר (*ytr*, "abundance, remainder").

**Abigal:** my father is joy. From אָב (*ʾav*, "father") + גיל (*gil*, "joy").

**Gilead:** rocky region? rugged country? The etymology remains uncertain.

### 27

**Shobi:** perhaps captive, returning, or glorious. Uncertain.

**Rabbath-ammon:** great city of Ammon. Rabbah means "great."

**Barzillai:** man of iron. From בַּרְזֶל (*barzel*, "iron").

**Rogelim:** fullers' place. Probably related to רֹגֵל (*rogel*, "fuller").

---

## II Sam 18

### 21

**Cushite:** an inhabitant of Cush (Nubia/Ethiopia).

**Cush:** probably "dark" or simply the ancient ethnonym for Nubia; the etymology is uncertain.
