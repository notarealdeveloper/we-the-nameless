		וּבְשַׂרְכֶם אָנִי׃

}
{
	\eX{

		better for you, either that all the sons of Jerubbaal, that are
		seventy and ten persons, reign over you, or that one reign over
		you? remember also that I’m your bone and your flesh.
	}
}
{
}

}
{
	\eX{
		And his mother’s brothers spoke of him in the ears of all the
