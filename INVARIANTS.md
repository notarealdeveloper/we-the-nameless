
The following invariants should be checked for the `make $book` subset builds, for all values of `$book` with a heading below. (i.e., `make genesis`, `make exodus`, etc.). If a book does not yet have a heading below, it doesn't yet have any invariants that need to be checked prior to publication.

## Genesis

Gen 1:14. Footnote 4 in Gen 1, i.e., the big "So it seems like the moon and girls are related somehow." footnote should be on its own page in lite book mode, unbroken.

Gen 1:14. Footnotes 5-14 in Gen 1, i.e., the ten "Translator's Note:" footnotes, should all be on the same page in lite book mode, unbroken.

Gen 19:38. Name him laughter should be on page 187.

Gen Apocrypha. Final page in Genesis with content, where it says _(Narrator: A knowing silence elapses.)_ should be on page 586, and the final numbered page, right after that, should be 587.
