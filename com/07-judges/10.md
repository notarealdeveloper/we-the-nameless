------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ After Abimelech, Tola son of Puah son of Dodo, a man of Issachar,
arose to deliver Israel. He lived at Shamir in the hill country of
Ephraim.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² He led Israel for twenty-three years; then he died and was buried at
Shamir.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ After him arose Jair the Gileadite, and he led Israel for twenty-two
years.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ He had thirty sons, who rode on thirty burros and owned thirty
boroughs in the region of Gilead; these are called Havvoth-jair to this
day.)



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Then Jair died and was buried at Kamon.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ The Israelites again did what was offensive to YHWH. They served the
Baalim and the Ashtaroth, and the gods of Aram, the gods of Sidon, the
gods of Moab, the gods of the Ammonites, and the gods of the
Philistines; they forsook YHWH and did not serve Him.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And YHWH, incensed with Israel, surrendered them to the Philistines
and to the Ammonites.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ That year they battered and shattered the Israelites—for eighteen
years—all the Israelites beyond the Jordan, in [what had been] the land
of the Amorites in Gilead.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ The Ammonites also crossed the Jordan to make war on Judah, Benjamin,
and the House of Ephraim. Israel was in great distress.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Then the Israelites cried out to YHWH, “We stand guilty before You,
for we have forsaken our God and served the Baalim.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ But YHWH said to the Israelites, “[I have rescued you] from the
Egyptians, from the Amorites, from the Ammonites, and from the
Philistines.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² The Sidonians, Amalek, and Maon also oppressed you; and when you
cried out to Me, I saved you from them.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Yet you have forsaken Me and have served other gods. No, I will not
deliver you again.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Go cry to the gods you have chosen; let them deliver you in your time
of distress!”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ But the Israelites implored YHWH: “We stand guilty. Do to us as You
see fit; only save us this day!”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ They removed the alien gods from among them and served YHWH; and He
could not bear the miseries of Israel.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ The Ammonites mustered and they encamped in Gilead; and the
Israelites massed and they encamped at Mizpah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ The troops—the officers of Gilead—said to one another, “Let the man
who is the first to fight the Ammonites be chieftain over all the
inhabitants of Gilead.”
