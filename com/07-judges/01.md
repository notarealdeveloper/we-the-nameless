------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ After the death of Joshua, the Israelites inquired of YHWH, “Which of
us shall be the first to go up against the Canaanites and attack them?”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² YHWH replied, “Let [the tribe of] Judah go up. I now deliver the land
into their hands.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Judah then said to their brother-tribe Simeon, “Come up with us to our
allotted territory and let us attack the Canaanites, and then we will go
with you to your allotted territory.” So Simeon joined them.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ When Judah advanced, YHWH delivered the Canaanites and the Perizzites
into their hands, and they defeated ten thousand of them at Bezek.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ At Bezek, they encountered Adoni-bezek, engaged him in battle, and
defeated the Canaanites and the Perizzites.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Adoni-bezek fled, but they pursued him and captured him; and they cut
off his thumbs and his big toes.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And Adoni-bezek said, “Seventy kings, with thumbs and big toes cut
off, used to pick up scraps under my table; as I have done, so God has
requited me.” They brought him to Jerusalem and he died there.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ The Judites attacked Jerusalem and captured it; they put it to the
sword and set the city on fire.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ After that the Judites went down to attack the Canaanites who
inhabited the hill country, the Negeb, and the Shephelah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ The Judites marched against the Canaanites who dwelt in Hebron, and
they defeated Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai. (The name of Hebron was
formerly Kiriath-arba.)



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ From there they marched against the inhabitants of Debir (the name of
Debir was formerly Kiriath-sepher).



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And Caleb announced, “I will give my daughter Achsah in marriage to
the man who attacks and captures Kiriath-sepher.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ His younger kinsman, Othniel the Kenizzite, captured it; and Caleb
gave him his daughter Achsah in marriage.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ When she came [to him], she induced him to ask her father for some
property. She dismounted from her donkey, and Caleb asked her, “What is
the matter?”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ She replied, “Give me a present, for you have given me away as
Negeb-land; give me springs of water.” And Caleb gave her Upper and
Lower Gulloth.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ The descendants of the Kenite, the father-in-law of Moses, went up
with the Judites from the City of Palms to the wilderness of Judah; and
they went and settled among the people in the Negeb of Arad.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And Judah with its brother-tribe Simeon went on and defeated the
Canaanites who dwelt in Zephath. They proscribed it, and so the town was
named Hormah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And Judah captured Gaza and its territory, Ashkelon and its
territory, and Ekron and its territory.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ YHWH was with Judah, so that they took possession of the hill
country; but they were not able to dispossess the inhabitants of the
plain, for they had iron chariots.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ They gave Hebron to Caleb, as Moses had promised; and he drove the
three Anakites out of there.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ The Benjaminites did not dispossess the Jebusite inhabitants of
Jerusalem; so the Jebusites have dwelt with the Benjaminites in
Jerusalem to this day.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² The House of Joseph, for their part, advanced against Bethel, and
YHWH was with them.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ While the House of Joseph were scouting at Bethel (the name of the
town was formerly Luz),



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ their patrols saw a man leaving the town. They said to him, “Just
show us how to get into the town, and we will treat you kindly.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ He showed them how to get into the town; they put the town to the
sword, but they let the man and all his relatives go free.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ The man went to the Hittite country. He founded a city and named it
Luz, and that has been its name to this day.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ Manasseh did not dispossess [the inhabitants of] Beth-shean and its
dependencies, or [of] Taanach and its dependencies, or the inhabitants
of Dor and its dependencies, or the inhabitants of Ibleam and its
dependencies, or the inhabitants of Megiddo and its dependencies. The
Canaanites persisted in dwelling in this region.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And when Israel gained the upper hand, they subjected the Canaanites
to forced labor; but they did not dispossess them.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ Nor did Ephraim dispossess the Canaanites who inhabited Gezer; so the
Canaanites dwelt in their midst at Gezer.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ Zebulun did not dispossess the inhabitants of Kitron or the
inhabitants of Nahalol; so the Canaanites dwelt in their midst, but they
were subjected to forced labor.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ Asher did not dispossess the inhabitants of Acco or the inhabitants
of Sidon, Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, and Rehob.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² So the Asherites dwelt in the midst of the Canaanites, the
inhabitants of the land, for they did not dispossess them.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ Naphtali did not dispossess the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh or the
inhabitants of Beth-anath. But they settled in the midst of the
Canaanite inhabitants of the land, and the inhabitants of Beth-shemesh
and Beth-anath had to perform forced labor for them.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ The Amorites pressed the Danites into the hill country; they would
not let them come down to the plain.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ The Amorites also persisted in dwelling in Har-heres, in Aijalon, and
in Shaalbim. But the hand of the House of Joseph bore heavily on them
and they had to perform forced labor.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ The territory of the Amorites extended from the Ascent of
Akrabbim—from Sela—onward.
