------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 4:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ When the entire nation had finished crossing the Jordan, YHWH said to
Joshua,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 4:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² “Select twelve men from among the people, one from each tribe,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 4:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ and instruct them as follows: Pick up twelve stones from the spot
exactly in the middle of the Jordan, where the priests’ feet are
standing; take them along with you and deposit them in the place where
you will spend the night.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 4:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Joshua summoned the twelve men whom he had designated among the
Israelites, one from each tribe;



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 4:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ and Joshua said to them, “Walk up to the Ark of YHWH your God, in the
middle of the Jordan, and each of you lift a stone onto his
shoulder—corresponding to the number of the tribes of Israel.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 4:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ This shall serve as a symbol among you: in time to come, when your
children ask, ‘What is the meaning of these stones for you?’



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 4:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ you shall tell them, ‘The waters of the Jordan were cut off because of
the Ark of YHWH’s Covenant; when it passed through the Jordan, the
waters of the Jordan were cut off.’ And so these stones shall serve the
people of Israel as a memorial for all time.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 4:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ The Israelites did as Joshua ordered. They picked up twelve stones,
corresponding to the number of the tribes of Israel, from the middle of
the Jordan—as YHWH had charged Joshua—and they took them along with them
to their night encampment and deposited them there.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 4:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Joshua also set up twelve stones in the middle of the Jordan, at the
spot where the feet of the priests bearing the Ark of the Covenant had
stood; and they have remained there to this day.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 4:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ The priests who bore the Ark remained standing in the middle of the
Jordan until all the instructions that YHWH had ordered Joshua to convey
to the people had been carried out. And so the people speedily crossed
over, just as Moses had assured Joshua in his charge to him.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 4:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And when all the people finished crossing, the Ark of YHWH and the
priests advanced to the head of the people.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 4:12

across armed in the van of the Israelites, as Moses had charged them.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 4:13

YHWH, to the steppes of Jericho for battle.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 4:14

they revered him all his days as they had revered Moses.
