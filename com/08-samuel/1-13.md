## I Samuel 13:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Saul was… years old when he became king, and he reigned... and two years over Israel.

The text here literally says:

_"Saul was dot dot dot years old when he became king, and he reigned dot dot dot and two years over Israel."_

The numbers are missing in the original Hebrew (Masoretic Text), and the entire verse is missing in the Greek (Septuagint), so let's just agree that the best we can do is dot dot dots and move on.

## I Samuel 13:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ The charge for sharpening was a pim for plowshares, mattocks, three-pronged forks, and axes, and for setting the goads.

The word "pim" is only used once in the bible.

Archeologists figured out what it means after digging up some stuff in Israel that said "pim" on it.

Turns out it's a unit of weight that's a bit heavier than a sheet of printer paper and a bit lighter than a AAA battery, which the internet claims weigh nearly the same.

Here it means "however much silver weighs that amount"

Price seems to have been about two days work.

But don't quote me on that.

Not an expert on pims.
