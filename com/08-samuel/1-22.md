------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ David departed from there and escaped to the cave of Adullam; and when his brothers and all his father’s house heard, they joined him down there.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Everyone who was in straits and everyone who was in debt and everyone who was desperate joined him, and he became their leader; there were about four hundred men with him.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ David went from there to Mizpeh of Moab, and he said to the king of Moab, “Let my father and mother come [and stay] with you, until I know what God will do for me.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ So he led them to the king of Moab, and they stayed with him as long as David remained in the stronghold.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ But the prophet Gad said to David, “Do not stay in the stronghold; go at once to the territory of Judah.” So David left and went to the forest of Hereth.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ When Saul heard that David and the men with him had been located—Saul was then in Gibeah, sitting under the tamarisk tree on the height, spear in hand, with all his courtiers in attendance upon him—



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Saul said to the courtiers standing about him, “Listen, men of Benjamin! Will the son of Jesse give fields and vineyards to every one of you? And will he make all of you captains of thousands or captains of hundreds?



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Is that why all of you have conspired against me? For no one informs me when my own son makes a pact with the son of Jesse; no one is concerned for me and no one informs me when my own son has set my servant in ambush against me, as is now the case.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Doeg the Edomite, who was standing among the courtiers of Saul, spoke up: “I saw the son of Jesse come to Ahimelech son of Ahitub at Nob.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ He inquired of YHWH on his behalf and gave him provisions; he also gave him the sword of Goliath the Philistine.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Thereupon the king sent for the priest Ahimelech son of Ahitub and for all the priests belonging to his father’s house at Nob. They all came to the king,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² and Saul said, “Listen to me, son of Ahitub.” “Yes, my lord,” he replied.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And Saul said to him, “Why have you and the son of Jesse conspired against me? You gave him food and a sword, and inquired of God for him—that he may rise in ambush against me, as is now the case.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Ahimelech replied to the king, “But who is there among all your courtiers as trusted as David, son-in-law of Your Majesty and obedient to your bidding, and esteemed in your household?



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ This is the first time that I inquired of God for him; I have done no wrong. Let not Your Majesty find fault with his servant [or] with any of my father’s house; for your servant knew nothing whatever about all this.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ But the king said, “You shall die, Ahimelech, you and all your father’s house.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And the king commanded the guards standing by, “Turn about and kill the priests of YHWH, for they are in league with David; they knew he was running away and they did not inform me.” But the king’s servants would not raise a hand to strike down the priests of YHWH.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Thereupon the king said to Doeg, “You, Doeg, go and strike down the priests.” And Doeg the Edomite went and struck down the priests himself; that day, he killed eighty-five men who wore the linen ephod.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ He put Nob, the town of the priests, to the sword: men and women, children and infants, oxen, asses, and sheep—[all] to the sword.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 22:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ But one son of Ahimelech son of Ahitub escaped—his name was Abiathar—and he fled to David.
