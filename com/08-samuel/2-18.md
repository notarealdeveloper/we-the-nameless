------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ David mustered the troops who were with him and set over them captains
of thousands and captains of hundreds.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² David sent out the troops, one-third under the command of Joab,
one-third under the command of Joab’s brother Abishai son of Zeruiah,
and one-third under the command of Ittai the Gittite. And David said to
the troops, “I myself will march out with you.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ But the troops replied, “No! For if some of us flee, the rest will not
be concerned about us; even if half of us should die, the others will
not be concerned about us. But you are worth ten thousand of us.
Therefore, it is better for you to support us from the town.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And the king said to them, “I will do whatever you think best.” So the
king stood beside the gate as all the troops marched out by their
hundreds and thousands.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ The king gave orders to Joab, Abishai, and Ittai: “Deal gently with my
boy Absalom, for my sake.” All the troops heard the king give the order
about Absalom to all the officers.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ The troops marched out into the open to confront the Israelites, and
the battle was fought in the forest of Ephraim.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ The Israelite troops were routed by David’s followers, and a great
slaughter took place there that day—twenty thousand men.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ The battle spread out over that whole region, and the forest devoured
more troops that day than the sword.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Absalom encountered some of David’s followers. Absalom was riding on a
mule, and as the mule passed under the tangled branches of a great
terebinth, his hair got caught in the terebinth; he was held between
heaven and earth as the mule under him kept going.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ One of the men saw it and told Joab, “I have just seen Absalom
hanging from a terebinth.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Joab said to the man who told him, “You saw it! Why didn’t you kill
him then and there? I would have owed you ten shekels of silver and a
belt.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² But the man answered Joab, “Even if I had a thousand shekels of
silver in my hands, I would not raise a hand against the king’s son. For
the king charged you and Abishai and Ittai in our hearing, ‘Watch over
my boy Absalom, for my sake.’



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ If I betrayed myself—and nothing is hidden from the king—you would
have stood aloof.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Joab replied, “Then I will not wait for you.” He took three darts in
his hand and drove them into Absalom’s chest. [Absalom] was still alive
in the thick growth of the terebinth,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ when ten of Joab’s young arms-bearers closed in and struck at Absalom
until he died.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Then Joab sounded the horn, and the troops gave up their pursuit of
the Israelites; for Joab held the troops in check.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ They took Absalom and flung him into a large pit in the forest, and
they piled up a very great heap of stones over it. Then all the
Israelites fled to their homes.—



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Now Absalom, in his lifetime, had taken the pillar which is in the
Valley of the King and set it up for himself; for he said, “I have no
son to keep my name alive.” He had named the pillar after himself, and
it has been called Absalom’s Monument to this day.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Ahimaaz son of Zadok said, “Let me run and report to the king that
YHWH has vindicated him against his enemies.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ But Joab said to him, “You shall not be the one to bring tidings
today. You may bring tidings some other day, but you’ll not bring any
today; for the king’s son is dead!”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And Joab said to a Cushite, “Go tell the king what you have seen.”
The Cushite bowed to Joab and ran off.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² But Ahimaaz son of Zadok again said to Joab, “No matter what, let me
run, too, behind the Cushite.” Joab asked, “Why should you run, my boy,
when you have no news worth telling?”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ “I am going to run anyway.” “Then run,” he said. So Ahimaaz ran by
way of the Plain, and he passed the Cushite.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ David was sitting between the two gates. The watchman on the roof of
the gate walked over to the city wall. He looked up and saw a man
running alone.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ The watchman called down and told the king; and the king said, “If he
is alone, he has news to report.” As he was coming nearer,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ the watchman saw another man running; and he called out to the
gatekeeper, “There is another man running alone.” And the king said,
“That one, too, brings news.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ The watchman said, “I can see that the first one runs like Ahimaaz
son of Zadok”; to which the king replied, “He is a good man, and he
comes with good news.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ Ahimaaz called out and said to the king, “All is well!” He bowed low
with his face to the ground and said, “Praised be YHWH your God, who has
delivered up the men who raised their hand against my lord the king.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ The king asked, “Is my boy Absalom safe?” And Ahimaaz answered, “I
saw a large crowd when Your Majesty’s servant Joab was sending your
servant off, but I don’t know what it was about.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ The king said, “Step aside and stand over there”; he stepped aside
and waited.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ Just then the Cushite came up; and the Cushite said, “Let my lord the
king be informed that YHWH has vindicated you today against all who
rebelled against you!”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 18:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² The king asked the Cushite, “Is my boy Absalom safe?” And the Cushite
replied, “May the enemies of my lord the king and all who rose against
you to do you harm fare like that young man!”
