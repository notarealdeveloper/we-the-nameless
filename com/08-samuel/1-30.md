## I Samuel 30:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ By the time David and his men arrived in Ziklag, on the third day, the Amalekites had made a raid into the Negeb and against Ziklag; they had stormed Ziklag and burned it down.

David goes back home to the town that the king gave him back in 27:6.

Then three days later, someone's trying to burn it down.

No scratch that.

The noun (the Amalekites, עמלקי) comes before the verb (invaded, פשׁטו) here.

So the town had already been burned down by the time they got there.

So "on the third day" doesn't mean their third day in Ziklag.

The journey took three days.

Arrive in town.

It's already burning.

Note: David is now
1. No longer at the battle.
2. Busy putting out fires.
