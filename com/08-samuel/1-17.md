## I Samuel 17:54

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵⁴ David took the head of the Philistine and brought it to Jerusalem; and he put his weapons in his own tent.

David takes the head to Jerusalem which is sort of a minor plot hole.% \footnote{Jerusalem isn't conquered until 2 Samuel 5. Judges 1:21 says the Jebusites were still living there with Benjamin. David has no meaningful connection with Jerusalem before he captures it. Rather, David is associated with Bethlehem (his birthplace), Judah and Hebron (where he reigns for 7½ years), the wilderness (Adullam, Ziph, En-gedi), and Ziklag (given to him by Achish) but Jerusalem is essentially absent from David's story until the conquest.}

And he also takes his weapons to his tent.

Ok so...

Assuming the tent is near the battle site.

Battle to Jerusalem: - 5 hours 30 mins. - Holding the head. - Holding the weapons.

In Jerusalem - Presumably he drops the head off. - With the Jebusites. - Who run Jerusalem then. - Which is a weird thing to do. - Because they seem to be Canaanites. - Not Philistines. - And not Israelites. - So they're unlikely to have any interest in this head, positively or negatively. - That said, it's a severed head. - So probably negative. - All else being equal. - Back to the road.

Jerusalem to Tent: - About 5 hours 20 mins. - Holding the weapons. - No longer holding the head. - Assuming he dropped it off with the Jebusites.

Tent to Home: - 4 hours 20 minutes.

Alternatively, to play apologetics: - If his tent was at home in Bethlehem.

Then we shave about 8 hours off the total walk.

In addition: - If we don't require the ordering of the trips to match the order in the text. - If we assume he had someone to help him carry the stuff. - If we assume he didn't walk, but drove some cows in a cart or something. - Or any other such assumptions.

We still have to explain taking a severed head to a city that neither side of this battle was involved in or allied with.

Which is a pretty weird behavior, to say the least.

Not just being silly here, there are entire academic papers on this, but we've had enough fun in this verse.

Moving on.
