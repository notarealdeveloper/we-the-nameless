------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Some time afterward, David attacked the Philistines and subdued them;
and David took Metheg-ammah from the Philistines.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² He also defeated the Moabites. He made them lie down on the ground and
he measured them off with a cord; he measured out two lengths of cord
for those who were to be put to death, and one length for those to be
spared. And the Moabites became tributary vassals of David.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ David defeated Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah, who was then on
his way to restore his monument at the Euphrates River.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ David captured 1,700 horsemen and 20,000 foot soldiers of his force;
and David hamstrung all the chariot horses, except for 100 which he
retained.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And when the Arameans of Damascus came to the aid of King Hadadezer of
Zobah, David struck down 22,000 of the Arameans.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ David stationed garrisons in Aram of Damascus, and the Arameans became
tributary vassals of David. YHWH gave David victory wherever he went.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ David took the gold shields carried by Hadadezer’s retinue and brought
them to Jerusalem;



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ and from Betah and Berothai, towns of Hadadezer, King David took a
vast amount of copper.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ When King Toi of Hamath heard that David had defeated the entire army
of Hadadezer,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Toi sent his son Joram to King David to greet him and to congratulate
him on his military victory over Hadadezer—for Hadadezer had been at war
with Toi. [Joram] brought with him objects of silver, gold, and copper.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ King David dedicated these to YHWH, along with the other silver and
gold that he dedicated, [taken] from all the nations he had conquered:



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² from Edom, Moab, and Ammon; from the Philistines and the Amalekites,
and from the plunder of Hadadezer son of Rehob, king of Zobah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ David gained fame when he returned from defeating Edom in the Valley
of Salt, 18,000 in all.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ He stationed garrisons in Edom—he stationed garrisons in all of Edom
—and all the Edomites became vassals of David. YHWH gave David victory
wherever he went.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ David reigned over all Israel, and David executed true justice among
all his people.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Joab son of Zeruiah was commander of the army; Jehoshaphat son of
Ahilud was recorder;



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests;
Seraiah was scribe;



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 8:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Benaiah son of Jehoiada was commander of the Cherethites and the
Pelethites; and David’s sons were priests.
