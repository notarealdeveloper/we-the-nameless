------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ In the third year of King Hoshea son of Elah of Israel, Hezekiah son of King Ahaz of Judah became king.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned in Jerusalem twenty-nine years; his mother’s name was Abi daughter of Zechariah.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ He did what was pleasing to YHWH, just as his father David had done.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ He abolished the shrines and smashed the pillars and cut down the sacred post. He also broke into pieces the bronze serpent that Moses had made, for until that time the Israelites had been offering sacrifices to it; it was called Nehushtan.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ He trusted only in YHWH the God of Israel; there was none like him among all the kings of Judah after him, nor among those before him.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ He clung to YHWH; he did not turn away from following Him, but kept the commandments that YHWH had given to Moses.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And YHWH was always with him; he was successful wherever he turned. He rebelled against the king of Assyria and would not serve him.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ He overran Philistia as far as Gaza and its border areas, from watchtower to fortified town.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ In the fourth year of King Hezekiah, which was the seventh year of King Hoshea son of Elah of Israel, King Shalmaneser of Assyria marched against Samaria and besieged it,
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ and he captured it at the end of three years. In the sixth year of Hezekiah, which was the ninth year of King Hoshea of Israel, Samaria was captured;
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ and the king of Assyria deported the Israelites to Assyria. He settled them in Halah, along the Habor [and] the River Gozan, and in the towns of Media.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² [This happened] because they did not obey YHWH their God; they transgressed His covenant—all that Moses the servant of YHWH had commanded. They did not obey and they did not fulfill it.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ In the fourteenth year of King Hezekiah, King Sennacherib of Assyria marched against all the fortified towns of Judah and seized them.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ King Hezekiah sent this message to the king of Assyria at Lachish: “I have done wrong; withdraw from me; and I shall bear whatever you impose on me.” So the king of Assyria imposed upon King Hezekiah of Judah a payment of three hundred talents of silver and thirty talents of gold.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Hezekiah gave him all the silver that was on hand in the House of YHWH and in the treasuries of the palace.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ At that time Hezekiah cut down the doors and the doorposts of the Temple of YHWH, which King Hezekiah had overlaid [with gold], and gave them to the king of Assyria.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ But the king of Assyria sent the Tartan, the Rabsaris, and the Rabshakeh from Lachish with a large force to King Hezekiah in Jerusalem. They marched up to Jerusalem; and when they arrived, they took up a position near the conduit of the Upper Pool, by the road of the Fuller’s Field.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ They summoned the king; and Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who was in charge of the palace, Shebna the scribe, and Joah son of Asaph the recorder went out to them.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ The Rabshakeh said to them, “You tell Hezekiah: Thus said the Great King, the King of Assyria: What makes you so confident?
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ You must think that mere talk is counsel and valor for war! Look, on whom are you relying, that you have rebelled against me?
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ You rely, of all things, on Egypt, that splintered reed of a staff, which enters and punctures the palm of anyone who leans on it! That’s what Pharaoh king of Egypt is like to all who rely on him.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And if you tell me that you are relying on YHWH your God, He is the very one whose shrines and altars Hezekiah did away with, telling Judah and Jerusalem, ‘You must worship only at this altar in Jerusalem.’
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Come now, make this wager with my master, the king of Assyria: I’ll give you two thousand horses if you can produce riders to mount them.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ So how could you refuse anything even to the deputy of one of my master’s lesser servants, relying on Egypt for chariots and horsemen?
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And do you think I have marched against this land to destroy it without YHWH? YHWH Himself told me: Go up against that land and destroy it.”
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ Eliakim son of Hilkiah, Shebna, and Joah replied to the Rabshakeh, “Please, speak to your servants in Aramaic, for we understand it; do not speak to us in Judean in the hearing of the people on the wall.”
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ But the Rabshakeh answered them, “Was it to your master and to you that my master sent me to speak those words? It was precisely to the men who are sitting on the wall—who will have to eat their dung and drink their urine with you.”
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And the Rabshakeh stood and called out in a loud voice in Judean: “Hear the words of the Great King, the King of Assyria.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ Thus said the king: Don’t let Hezekiah deceive you, for he will not be able to deliver you from my hands.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ Don’t let Hezekiah make you rely on YHWH, saying: YHWH will surely save us: this city will not fall into the hands of the king of Assyria.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ Don’t listen to Hezekiah. For thus said the king of Assyria: Make your peace with me and come out to me, so that you may all eat from your vines and your fig trees and drink water from your cisterns,
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² until I come and take you away to a land like your own, a land of grain [fields] and vineyards, of bread and wine, of olive oil and honey, so that you may live and not die. Don’t listen to Hezekiah, who misleads you by saying, ‘YHWH will save us.’
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ Did any of the gods of other nations save his land from the king of Assyria?
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ Where were the gods of Hamath and Arpad? Where were the gods of Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah? [And] did they save Samaria from me?
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ Which among all the gods of [those] countries saved their countries from me, that YHWH should save Jerusalem from me?”
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ But the people were silent and did not say a word in reply; for the king’s order was: “Do not answer him.”
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 18:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ And so Eliakim son of Hilkiah, who was in charge of the palace, Shebna the scribe, and Joah son of Asaph the recorder came to Hezekiah with their clothes rent, and they reported to him what the Rabshakeh had said.
