------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ The word of YHWH came to Jehu son of Hanani against Baasha:



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² “Because I lifted you up from the dust and made you a ruler over My people Israel, but you followed the way of Jeroboam and caused My people Israel to sin, vexing Me with their sins—



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ I am going to sweep away Baasha and his house. I will make your house like the House of Jeroboam son of Nebat.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Anyone belonging to Baasha who dies in the town shall be devoured by dogs, and anyone belonging to him who dies in the open country shall be devoured by the birds of the sky.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ The other events of Baasha’s reign and his actions and his exploits are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Israel.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Baasha slept with his fathers and was buried in Tirzah. His son Elah succeeded him as king.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ But the word of YHWH had come through the prophet Jehu son of Hanani against Baasha and against his house, that it would fare like the House of Jeroboam, which he himself had struck down, because of all the evil he did which was displeasing to YHWH, vexing him with his deeds.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ In the twenty-sixth year of King Asa of Judah, Elah son of Baasha became king over Israel, at Tirzah—for two years.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ His officer Zimri, commander of half the chariotry, committed treason against him while he was at Tirzah drinking himself drunk in the house of Arza, who was in charge of the palace at Tirzah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Zimri entered, struck him down, and killed him; he succeeded him as king in the twenty-seventh year of King Asa of Judah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ No sooner had he become king and ascended the throne than he struck down all the House of Baasha; he did not leave a single male of his, nor any kinsman or friend.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Thus Zimri destroyed all the House of Baasha, in accordance with the word that YHWH had spoken through the prophet Jehu—



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ because of the sinful acts which Baasha and his son Elah committed, and which they caused Israel to commit, vexing YHWH, the God of Israel, with their false gods.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ The other events of Elah’s reign and all his actions are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Israel.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ During the twenty-seventh year of King Asa of Judah, Zimri reigned in Tirzah for seven days. At the time, the troops were encamped at Gibbethon of the Philistines.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ When the troops who were encamped there learned that Zimri had committed treason and had struck down the king, that very day, in the camp, all Israel acclaimed the army commander Omri king over Israel.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ Omri and all Israel then withdrew from Gibbethon and laid siege to Tirzah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ When Zimri saw that the town was taken, he went into the citadel of the royal palace and burned down the royal palace over himself. And so he died—



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ because of the sins which he committed and caused Israel to commit, doing what was displeasing to YHWH and following the ways of Jeroboam.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ The other events of Zimri’s reign, and the treason which he committed, are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Israel.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ Then the people of Israel split into two factions: a part of the people followed Tibni son of Ginath to make him king, and the other part followed Omri.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² Those who followed Omri proved stronger than those who followed Tibni son of Ginath; Tibni died and Omri became king.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ In the thirty-first year of King Asa of Judah, Omri became king over Israel—for twelve years. He reigned in Tirzah six years.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ Then he bought the hill of Samaria from Shemer for two talents of silver; he built [a town] on the hill and named the town which he built Samaria, after Shemer, the owner of the hill.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ Omri did what was displeasing to YHWH; he was worse than all who preceded him.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ He followed all the ways of Jeroboam son of Nebat and the sins which he committed and caused Israel to commit, vexing YHWH, the God of Israel, with their futilities.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ The other events of Omri’s reign, [and] his actions, and the exploits he performed, are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Israel.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ Omri slept with his fathers and was buried in Samaria; and his son Ahab succeeded him as king.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ Ahab son of Omri became king over Israel in the thirty-eighth year of King Asa of Judah, and Ahab son of Omri reigned over Israel in Samaria for twenty-two years.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ Ahab son of Omri did what was displeasing to YHWH, more than all who preceded him.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ Not content to follow the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, he took as wife Jezebel daughter of King Ethbaal of the Phoenicians, and he went and served Baal and worshiped him.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² He erected an altar to Baal in the temple of Baal which he built in Samaria.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ Ahab also made a sacred post. Ahab did more to vex YHWH, the God of Israel, than all the kings of Israel who preceded him.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 16:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ During his reign, Hiel the Bethelite fortified Jericho. He laid its foundations at the cost of Abiram his first-born, and set its gates in place at the cost of Segub his youngest, in accordance with the words that YHWH had spoken through Joshua son of Nun.
