------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And in the ninth year of his reign, on the tenth day of the tenth
month, Nebuchadnezzar moved against Jerusalem with his whole army. He
besieged it; and they built towers against it all around.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² The city continued in a state of siege until the eleventh year of King
Zedekiah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ By the ninth day [of the fourth month] the famine had become acute in
the city; there was no food left for the common people.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Then [the wall of] the city was breached. All the soldiers [left the
city] by night through the gate between the double walls, which is near
the king’s garden—the Chaldeans were all around the city; and [the king]
set out for the Arabah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ But the Chaldean troops pursued the king, and they overtook him in the
steppes of Jericho as his entire force left him and scattered.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ They captured the king and brought him before the king of Babylon at
Riblah; and they put him on trial.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ They slaughtered Zedekiah’s sons before his eyes; then Zedekiah’s eyes
were put out. He was chained in bronze fetters and he was brought to
Babylon.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ On the seventh day of the fifth month—that was the nineteenth year of
King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon—Nebuzaradan, the chief of the guards, an
officer of the king of Babylon, came to Jerusalem.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ He burned the House of YHWH, the king’s palace, and all the houses of
Jerusalem; he burned down the house of every notable person.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ The entire Chaldean force that was with the chief of the guard tore
down the walls of Jerusalem on every side.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ The remnant of the people that was left in the city, the defectors
who had gone over to the king of Babylon—and the remnant of the
population—were taken into exile by Nebuzaradan, the chief of the
guards.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² But some of the poorest in the land were left by the chief of the
guards, to be vinedressers and field hands.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ The Chaldeans broke up the bronze columns of the House of YHWH, the
stands, and the bronze tank that was in the House of YHWH; and they
carried the bronze away to Babylon.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ They also took all the pails, scrapers, snuffers, ladles, and all the
other bronze vessels used in the service.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ The chief of the guards took whatever was of gold and whatever was of
silver: firepans and sprinkling bowls.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ The two columns, the one tank, and the stands that Solomon provided
for the House of YHWH—all these objects contained bronze beyond
weighing.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ The one column was eighteen cubits high. It had a bronze capital
above it; the height of the capital was three cubits, and there was a
meshwork [decorated] with pomegranates about the capital, all made of
bronze. And the like was true of the other column with its meshwork.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ The chief of the guards also took Seraiah, the chief priest,
Zephaniah, the deputy priest, and the three guardians of the threshold.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And from the city he took a eunuch who was in command of the
soldiers; five royal privy councillors who were present in the city; the
scribe of the army commander, who was in charge of mustering the people
of the land; and sixty of the common people who were inside the city.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ Nebuzaradan, the chief of the guards, took them and brought them to
the king of Babylon at Riblah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ The king of Babylon had them struck down and put to death at Riblah,
in the region of Hamath. Thus Judah was exiled from its land.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon put Gedaliah son of Ahikam son of
Shaphan in charge of the people whom he left in the land of Judah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ When the officers of the troops and their men heard that the king of
Babylon had put Gedaliah in charge, they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah with
Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan son of Kareah, Seraiah son of
Tanhumeth the Netophathite, and Jaazaniah son of the Maachite, together
with their men.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ Gedaliah reassured them and their men, saying, “Do not be afraid of
the servants of the Chaldeans. Stay in the land and serve the king of
Babylon, and it will go well with you.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ In the seventh month, Ishmael son of Nethaniah son of Elishama, who
was of royal descent, came with ten men, and they struck down Gedaliah
and he died; [they also killed] the Judeans and the Chaldeans who were
present with him at Mizpah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ And all the people, young and old, and the officers of the troops set
out and went to Egypt because they were afraid of the Chaldeans.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ In the thirty-seventh year of the exile of King Jehoiachin of Judah,
on the twenty-seventh day of the twelfth month, King Evil-merodach of
Babylon, in the year he became king, took note of King Jehoiachin of
Judah and released him from prison.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ He spoke kindly to him, and gave him a throne above those of other
kings who were with him in Babylon.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ His prison garments were removed, and [Jehoiachin] received regular
rations by his favor for the rest of his life.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 25:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ A regular allotment of food was given him at the instance of the
king—an allotment for each day—all the days of his life.
