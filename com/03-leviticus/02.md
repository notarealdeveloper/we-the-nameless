## Leviticus 2:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Every grain offering that you will bring forward to YHWH shall not be made with leavening, because all leaven and all honey: you shall not burn any of it to smoke as an offering by fire to YHWH.

A few important details: grain offerings should never contain leavening, and neither leaven nor honey is to be burned on the altar. Both may be presented as first produce, but they're not appropriate for the fragrant burnt portion.

No yeast. Also no honey on altar. Who put honey on altar?
