	Then the final king in the list

	\Def{Ubara-Tutu}{
		Ubara-Tutu was, according to some versions of the \emph{Sumerian King List},
		the last antediluvian king of Sumer, ruling from Shuruppak. He was said to have
		reigned for 18{,}600 years. He was the son of Enmendurana, the legendary king
		often compared with the biblical Enoch because he was taken up to heaven without
		dying. His reign ended when the great flood swept over the land.
		Ubara-Tutu is also mentioned briefly in Tablet XI of the \emph{Epic of Gilgamesh},
		where he is identified as the father of Utnapishtim, whom the god Ea warns to build
		a boat in order to survive the coming flood.
