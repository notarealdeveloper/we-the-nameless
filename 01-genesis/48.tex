\Verse{7}
{

}
{
	\ePR{
		And I: when I was coming from Paddan Aram, Rachel died by
		me in the land of Canaan on the way when there was still a
		span of land to come to Ephrat, and I buried her there on
		the Ephrat road. That’s Bethlehem.”
	}
}
{
	\eR{I buried Rachel at Bethlehem, on the road to Fertility. This is still El Shaddai talking by the way.}%
	\fC{REF: Verse 7 fits uncomfortably in its context, connecting neither to the preceding nor to the following verses. And it merges elements of two sources: It refers to Paddan Aram, which is characteristic of P, but it says “there was still a span of land to come to Ephrath,” and it refers to Rachel’s being buried on the Ephrath road, which comes from E (Gen 35:16–20). It therefore appears to be an addition that the Redactor made to the text. Perhaps its function was to separate the two conflicting passages about Ephraim and Manasseh that precede and follow it. See the next note. Alternatively, this verse might after all be P, because it fits with the next P passage (49:29–33). As a unit these two passages could be saying: “As for me: Rachel died and was buried on the road, but when I die I want to be buried back in my ancestral tomb with the other patriarchs and matriarchs.” It is therefore uncertain whether v. 7 is P or R.}
}
