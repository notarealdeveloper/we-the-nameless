	\aC{
		As recognized by most scholars, MT does not make sense, and is also overly
		long. In the first two words of the colon, \heb{משם רעה}, there is a conflated
		reading. One or two letters have dropped out by haplography, the original
		variants being \emph{miššōmēr} and \emph{mērō'ê}; written out in early
