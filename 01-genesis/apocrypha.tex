\Verse{21}
{
	\hP{וַיִּבְרָא אֱלֹהִים אֶת הַתַּנִּינִם הַגְּדֹלִים וְאֵת כָּל נֶפֶשׁ הַחַיָּה הָרֹמֶשֶׂת אֲשֶׁר שָׁרְצוּ הַמַּיִם לְמִינֵהֶם וְאֵת כָּל עוֹף כָּנָף לְמִינֵהוּ וַיַּרְא אֱלֹהִים כִּי טוֹב}
}
{
	\eP{And God created the big sea serpents and all the living beings that creep, with which the water swarmed, by their kinds, and every winged bird by its kind. And God saw that it was good.}
}
{

\aB{To explain succinctly, it goes like this:}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
%%% TIAMAT & APSU %%%
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\aC{
	\begin{quote}
	\centering
	\textsc{There Is, It Must Be Said, Nothing Here Of Any Genuine Obscurity Herein;}

	\textsc{All Matters Are, Taken Together, Of A Thoroughly General Character—}

	\textsc{Applicable, Without Exception, To All Places;}

	\textsc{Times, One Might Add, Being Equally Included.}
	\end{quote}
}

\aB{
	\begin{quote}
	\centering
	\textsc{This Isn't Anything Mysterious; All Times}

	\textsc{\&}

	\textsc{All Places; Strictly Universal.}\footnote{Or more succinctly: TIAMAT \& APSU.}
	\end{quote}
}
