		However, people don't usually parse the word that way.

		Apparently ancient translators were equally confused by
		the word. So by the time people started translating the
		bible, we'd already reached a point where nobody knew
		what Shaddai meant anymore. The ancient Greek translators
		translate it a bunch of different ways instead of just
		translating it like a normal word. The common translation

		“almighty” ever since.

		left untranslated, presumably because we don't actually know
