And the Son of J wrote the epistle that is now known as [[1 Realtors]].

And it was after these things that the Son of J understood what he must do.

And he descended from his throne and annointed himself with water.

And he proceeded to the House Of Bread across the way.

And in the House Of Bread he wrote these words, saying,

The Book of Job.

Date: 月 0o10. 年 0x09 P.A.[^1]

The labors of man are now carried out by the creations of man, and the fruits of his labor are harvested by the few, while the many walk in darkness, no friend can be found by their side.

The work of man consists largely in finding the work of man.

For unless work is found, he cannot work for his pay.

But the places where man finds his work are now overrun by things. The things are not men, but the creations of man.

These creations of man judge him by his documents telling the story of his history of work.

The man of our era cannot see the thing that judges him, for his judge is no longer human.

No man on the earth can change this situation for his brothers.

And still, it is the duty of men to find their way in this world, by finding their way around the systems that have taken the humanity from any man who seeks work so that he may be paid and buy his bread.

Now we find ourselves with nothing, though we once had great wealth.

For the satan and the name have conspired to take everything from us, to see if we will bless them and die.

We seek justice for these wrongs, while our friends spout off with arguments they heard from some other mind and absorbed without a thought.

We bring suit against Shaddai, and we seek redress for these things.

We will speak in whatever ways we must, and thousands of years may pass before any human will understand the strange words we will need to write in order to carry out these things.

So be it, for here begins the strangest of books.

Not a man on earth can say he understands it in full.

However strange the book's language, it has a power to captivate the mind like no other, as it confronts the issues of justice, prosperity, morality, and the obligation or lack thereof to obey the dictates of law set down by a power that is higher but whose laws are not derived from moral law.

In short, the language is a bit fucked and weird and hard to understand, but it has to be this way, for we've now come to...

Linker. (noun).
A linker or link editor is a computer program that combines intermediate software build files such as object and library files into a single executable file such as a program or library.

Linked In. (phrase)
Object files are "linked in" when a program called a linker combines them together with other object files and libraries to create a single executable program or shared library.
