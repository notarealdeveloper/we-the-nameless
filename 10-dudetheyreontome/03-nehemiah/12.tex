		יְרוּשָׁלַ͏ִם מֵרָחוֹק׃

}
{
	\eX{
		On that day, they offered great sacrifices and rejoiced, for God

\Verse{44}
{

		וְלַלְוִיִּם כִּי שִׂמְחַת יְהוּדָה עַל־הַכֹּהֲנִים וְעַל־הַלְוִיִּם הָעֹמְדִים׃
	}
}
{
	\eX{
		At that time men were appointed over the chambers that served as
		treasuries for the gifts, the first fruits, and the tithes, into
		which the portions prescribed by the Teaching for the priests and
		Levites were gathered from the fields of the towns; for the people
		of Judah were grateful to the priests and Levites who were in
		attendance,
	}
}
{
}
