
A Big List of Bible Stuff.[^1]


## Beginning

1. Words create stuff (Genesis 1).
2. Words may actually _be_ the creator (John 1).
3. Suddenly switch to a second version of the creation story that contradicts the first.

## Names

1. People's names are often puns.
2. Playful etymologies: "Because I drew him from the water."
3. Playful etiologies: "Languages because YHWH didn't want us cooperating."
4. Calling countries you don't like "Babylon."
5. Calling the ethnics down south "Edom."
6. Calling the ethnics down south "Seir."[^2]
7. Calling the natives with the birthright "Red man."
8. Calling the "enemies" who your people are twins with "𐤀𐤃𐤌 (human)."

## Laws

1. Have 10 big rules that matter more than anything else.
2. Have 3 or 4 different copies of that list.
3. One copy of the list should be completely insane, and probably a joke.
4. Have an endless progression of additional rules that almost no one reads.

## Numbers

1. 7 is special, b/c of the number of heavenly bodies visible with the naked eye.
2. 10 is special, b/c you can guess why.
3. 12 is special, b/c the night ball going around 12 times is a year.[^3]
4. 40 is special, but no one knows why.
5. When you need a large number, it should factor into products of the above.
6. There's always 12 of something (e.g., tribes) but the list keeps changing.
7. The second item in a list of 7 is subtly suggested to be less good.

## Words

Euphemisms
1. Know meaning sex. (J)
2. Heavy meaning many things. (E)
3. Expire meaning die. (P)

Phrases
1. Why did you decieve me? (J)
2. Woke up early in the morning. (E)
3. Him and his wives and his sons and his sons wives with him. (P)
4. With all your heart and all your soul. (D)

## Protagonists

1. Flawed male lead. (J)
2. Tired male lead. (E)

## Deities

1. Deadbeat deity. (J)
2. Awesome deity. (E)
3. Threatening deity. (P)

## Genres

1. Places as people: Making Egypt, Midian, Jacob, Esau, etc.
2. International relations as family relations.
3. Neighboring country relations as Sibling rivalry.
4. Initial country and breakaway country as father and son.
5. Female characters generally aren't places.

## Style

1. Not explaining what you're up to.
2. Not citing sources.
3. When you do cite sources, it's only like 3 or 4 times ever, and no one can find the books.

## Protagonists

1. The "moral native in-group" praised by narrator while behaving badly. (J, Jonah)
2. The "immoral foreigners" condemned by narrator or protagonist while behaving morally (J, Jonah, Ezekiel).
3. Patriarchal society but all male protagonists are cowardly, immoral, etc. (J)
4. Patriarchal society but all female protagonists are clever, good, sympathetic. (J)
5. Protagonist leader persecuted by the unappreciative disloyal masses. (J)
6. Names of various protagonists changed by a deity for no clear reason.

## Contents

1. Every kind of depraved sex imaginable is described in euphemism.
2. Stories no one can figure out the lesson of.
3. Stories where someone seems to do nothing wrong but the narrator says it was the worst thing ever. (e.g., P's water at Meribah.)

## Prophetic Language

1. The "Water of Life."
2. Sun black as sackcloth, moon turns to blood.
3. Something like a son of man.
4. Double edged sword coming out of my mouth.
5. Shine like the sun.
