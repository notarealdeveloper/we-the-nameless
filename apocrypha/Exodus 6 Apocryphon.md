    κατά. (preposition).
    Transliteration: kata
    downwards, into, towards, for, for the purpose of, down from, against, according to, in conformity with.

    λαμβάνω. (verb).
    Transliteration: lambanō
    to take with the hand, lay hold of, any person or thing
    to take what is one's own, to take to one's self, to make one's own
    to take up a thing to be carried
    to take upon one's self
    to take in order to carry away; without the notion of violence, i.e., to remove, take away
    to claim, procure, accept for one's self
    to associate with one's self as companion, attendant
    to lay hold of, apprehend; of that which when taken is not let go
    to take to one's self, lay hold upon, take possession of, i.e. to appropriate to one's self
    to reach after, strive to obtain
    to take a thing due, to collect, gather (tribute)
    to receive what is offered; to admit, receive
    to not refuse, or to not reject
    to receive a person
    to regard any one's power, rank, external circumstances, and on that account to do some injustice or neglect something
    to take, to choose, select, to experience
    to receive (what is given)

    κατέλαβεν. (verb).
    Transliteration: katalaben
    Roots: κατά and λαμβάνω.
    Definitions:
    1. to lay hold of so as to make one's own, to take into one's self, to accept as one's own, seize tight hold of.
    2. to take possession of, to obtain, attain to.
    3. to detect, perceive, learn.
    4. to lay hold of with the mind, comprehend, apprehend, understood, understand.
    5. to come upon, overcome, obtain, take, overtake, attain, find, comprehend.
