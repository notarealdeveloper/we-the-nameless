SIEGE IS RUN
============

Learn Different™
----------------

An organization of which the current author/investigator is becoming increasingly afraid, with a semantically overloaded trademark symbol and a pragmatically correct name, which was mired in obfuscation before revealing its existence.

The group itself appears never to directly reveal truths about themselves. The book, an internal compilation of separate writings they describe as their "Takeover Manual," (or "TM") appears to be something known as a diversion triad: On the surface, the manual is about a terrorist organization, one that is really hiding the fact that they're a school, but operating outside the law. The book then attempts to mislead the reader into thinking that it's secretly about depravity and vices by a series of maneuvers suggesting that the author has ulterior motives in luring all these underage people into an extra-legal situation by dropping hints of sympathy with all manner of corruption and vices throughout.

That's where they want us to stop, thinking we've found the secret. However, as I've recently discovered, for anyone who keeps looking, once one stitches all the pieces together (i.e., at "link time," in their internal jargon (which seems to have been appropriated (or requisitioned) together from the terminology of both international terrorism and various academic fields)) it becomes clear that the book is describing a ongoing and previously unrecognized coup.

Here's the idea as best I can gather.

L.D. BUSINESS MODEL
-------------------

There are the schools, and there are the "halfway houses."

Learn Different used the untapped potential of human loneliness and interpersonal isolation as the fuel to undermine entrenched social and political institutions, primarily the so-called educational establishment.

The group's apparent disregard for the rules of spelling and grammar seemed at first rather childish and unbefitting of a proper terrorist organization, and was thus dismissed as the youthful rule flouting of a group of teenagers and college students with a shared disdain for the rules of grammatical and professional style.

However, the group's writings often contain variants of the phrase:

    If there's a rule about what comes next, it cannot convey information.

It appears that all along, the disdain for the rules of syntax and semantics may have been primarily a method of sending coded messages.

    We favor encryption over steganography for purposes of serecy, and steganography over encryption for purposes of propaganda.
    With a properly executed social encoding, each message's intended audience can hear clearly what to other groups falls on deaf ears.

In light of this suspicion, past publications and past personal and public communications going back several years have begun to be examined as part of the investigation into L.D. and its members. The public communications of several suspected members have been found to contain a number of hidden messages which become clearer in light of the discovery of L.D.'s relationship with the Nth Stair.

The final chapter in particular of a mathematics textbook by the group's suspected founder has been found to be full of what appears to be encoded L.D. propaganda. For instance, the book's opening sections contain the following statement:

    Removing the Nth stair from a staircase harms the steps after it,
    whether that staircase is a narrative or a mathematical argument.

Another instance of the term "Nth stair" is found in the book's final section. Its meaning remains unclear, but the section is easily recognizable as having been heavily encoded in the same manner that L.D.'s propaganda and covert communication is known for. One paragraph in particular is especially striking.

    They were the fire of the book... The fire that I deleted... The fire that never existed.
    But without them, we never would have gotten here... They were the Nth Stair...
    One more.

In context, the lines make almost no sense.

However, the group has stated elsewhere its tactic of so-called "noise whitening" in prose, a method whereby
the remaining degrees of freedom in the "signal" of written English sentences are used in service of burying
secret handshakes and encoded messages, and thus saturating the medium until the final signal approximates one
of so-called practical maximum entropy -- the prose signals capable of conveying the largest quantity of
both explicit and implicit information.

Given the group's penchant for burying messages in the form of anagrams, the sections of writing
near the two instances of the term Nth Stair in previous work have begun to be mined for clues.

Work on the above paragraph itself remains inconclusive, though one member of the investigative team
noted that the lines can be rearranged to:

    They're the fire of the fire... The "we" that we deleted... The we that never existed.
    Without them, I never would have gotten here...
    They're the Nth Stair.
    One more book.

However, doing so leaves us with a set of leftover letters "if true fiber," and given the
combinatorially large number of possible permutations of any given stream of text, it is
difficult to know when if ever we have gotten something right.

Other sections however, can be reinterpreted much more clearly in light of recently uncovered events.

One would never see this sort of slogan in a real recovery unit, nor is it a fitting slogan for those who have admitted they have a problem. The quote has a fight to it. A fight not against addiction or an internal weakness or flaw, but against some unnamed other.
