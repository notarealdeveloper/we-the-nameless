    The Shannon-Hartley (or SH) theorem describes the theoretical maximum (or TM)
    on the information rate of data that can be communicated with arbitrarily high
    reliability, using an average received signal power S, through a communication
    medium subject to additive gaussian white noise of power N:

    bits per second = H*log2(1 + S/N)

    where
    N is the average power of the noise.
    S is the average power of the signal.
    H is the bandwidth of the channel, measured in Hertz.
