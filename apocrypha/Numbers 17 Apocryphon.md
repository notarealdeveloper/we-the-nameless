    火山 (noun)
    Chinese for volcano. Pinyin: huǒshān.
    Translates literally as "fire mountain."
    (see also: 화산 commonly romanized as hwasan, or more properly: hoasan)

    朝日 (noun)
    Japanese for sunrise (旭). Romaji: asahi.
    Translates literally as "morning sun."
    (see also: 朝の日 (asanohi), 朝の星 (asanohoshi), and 新普神の火 (shinshinshinnohi))

    Abjad (noun)
    An old way of writing. (Arabic: أبجد.)
    In an abjad, each symbol represents a consonant, and vowel sounds are omitted.
    Well known examples of abjads include the writing systems of early Hebrew, Phoenician, and Aramaic.
