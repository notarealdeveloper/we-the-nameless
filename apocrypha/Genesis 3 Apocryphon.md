When Isaac Newton showed that the laws of the heavens and of earth were one and the same, the trajectory of human history was fundamentally changed.

To express the laws of the earth and skies, he could not simply list them clearly and unambiguously in straightforward prose.

Justice to the laws could only be done by inventing a new language, any attempt to do without it would not do justice to the laws.

In the beginning of Newton's creating the physics of earth and skies, he first had to invent calculus, a new language in which the laws of the heavens and earth could be expressed.

He also had a side-project, one that captured his attention as much if not more than all his world changing physics.

We don't often hear about that side project in school.

His passion project was not, in fact, physics.
