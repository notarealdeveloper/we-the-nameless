Anyways, it was nice looking back at this and realizing how far you've helped me to come since then. I don't feel the same shame I used to feel anymore. Since the beginning I promised myself that however our interaction unfolded I had to take every single step in a way such that I wouldn't be ashamed if it ever went public, by accident or on purpose, because that's the only way I could cautiously step myself through the process of falling very much romantically in love with a "kid," which is obviously something I've always known I could do, hypothetically, mentally, because jesus fucking christ most of my life I was a "kid" too, it's not exactly something that's hard to relate to. For anyone. And my god we forget it hard. It's like we live in this tragic dystopian world where there are two classes: the inferior and the elite, and once we collect enough rings around the great fire in the sky we're magically promoted from low class to high and suddenly we have freedom... but as payment we're made to entirely forget our moment-by-moment experience of what it was actually like for us back before when we were down there.
