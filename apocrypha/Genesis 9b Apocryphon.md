Etymology and Shit
==================

1. Supra.
Latin for "above." An academic and legal citation signal used when a writer desires to refer
a reader to an earlier-cited authority. For example, an author wanting to refer to a source in his or
her third footnote would cite: See supra note 3. Or for text in that note: See supra text accompanying note 3.

2. Loc. cit.
An abbreviation for "Loco citato," a Latin phrase meaning "in the place cited," and appearing
to a sizable proportion of non-native Latin speakers to mean someting akin to "crazy city potato."

3. Op. cit.
An abbreviation for "Opere citato," a Latin phrase meaning "in the work cited".[1]
[Footnote 1: An anagram for Erotica Poet, Riot To Peace, Octopi Eater, Cite Rape Too, Re-ice Potato, and Epic Eta Root.[R1]]
[Recursive Footnote 1: Root. (noun). See Radical.]

4. Ibid.
Latin, short for "ibidem," meaning "in the same place." Used to provide an endnote or footnote citation or reference for a source that was cited in the preceding endnote or footnote. This is similar in meaning to idem (meaning something that has been mentioned previously; the same), abbreviated Id., which is commonly used in legal citation. To find the ibid. source, one must look at the reference preceding it.

SBG(T?): Ha, we've talked about it before when you were here. You may have been writing though. Anyways it was a long time ago. Apparently someone thought it would be funny to slip him some Thorazine. This schizophrenia drug the doctors gave them at this institution awhile back, but they never took it. Doctors give you all kinds of shit when you're homeless and they don't take the time to talk to you. Anyways, it was one of these nights with the antics and I guess at some point someone just slipped it in his drink or something.

Newcomer: What happened?

SBG(T?): Dunno exactly but he almost died. Apparently it didn't interact well with his brain or whatever else he was taking at the time. All I know is before the night was over he was out cold. Soaked his shirt in vomit and coughed-up blood. Seemed to have the dexterity and tidiness compulsion to take it off, but not the inhibitions he usually has about his body. Chest was all covered in scars. He was just laying there but no one could lift him, which granted doesn't make a lot of sense because he's not an especially heavy guy. It's just like the physics of the universe had decided he belonged there. Members all felt horrible. Didn't mean for it to get out of hand like that. Then K put a blanket over him to hide the scars and laid down next to him with her head on his chest. Said she wanted to make sure his heart was still beating. I'd say she probably just wanted to cuddle. Maybe both.

Newcomer: Did you ask her?

SBG(T?): What me? Nah, I never met K. These are just old Stonewall stories. I honestly don't think half these things ever happened. Though then again, we say the same thing about a lot of things that did.

(Narrator: Narrator stares into the back of Stonewall, overcome by a feeling of uncertain understanding.)
