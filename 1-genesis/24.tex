%	\footnote{
%		make something happen. The Hebrew includes the particle n’, conveying
%		that this is a polite request. Even as a request, though, it is
%		remarkable: the servant himself asks for a miraculous sign from God, and
%		he himself names what that sign should be. No human thus far, including
