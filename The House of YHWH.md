You subsequently indicated that your name is Jealous, that your name is Holy, and that your name is not to be taken in vain.

You also provided four alternate identities, none of which could be matched to a government-issued ID:

- L. Shaddai

2. You failed to provide acceptable photo identification.

We cannot accept a golden calf, a golden hemorrhoid, or the rather lewd picture of you and your "Asherah" in the nude from Kuntillet Ajrud as photographic identification.

Please stop submitting these. We have them in your file.

3. We cannot verify your place of birth.

You provided the following:

> Birthplace:
> Midian, Seir, Edom, Horeb, Sinai, Egypt.

That is, in fact, six locations, four of which we were unable to verify as a place. The fifth entry appears to refer to a largely uninhabited peninsula rather than a city, state, or country of birth. The sixth is a valid nation state, but when contacted for additional information you refused to provide a birth year.

- several countries did not yet exist at the time of your claimed residence;
- several countries or territories no longer exist;
- one city changed names;

- one address is simply "a bush";
- two addresses were listed as having been occupied for the period of "day" and "night," neither of which is an acceptable occupancy period;
- one address is a pillow;

- one residence was captured by the Philistines;
- one subsequently destroyed Dagon;
- none of the temples turned up in any available public records;
- the final property appears to be registered to another owner.

Verifying this residential history against available public records required a substantial amount of our underwriting staff's time.

5. You have provided no proof of income.

You submitted no W-2s, tax returns, pay stubs, bank statements, or other documentation of income or employment.

Under "Occupation," you wrote:

> "I AM."

Under "Employer," you wrote:

> "I AM THAT I AM."

Under "Annual Income," you wrote:

> "The silver is mine, and the gold is mine."

This is a claim of ownership, not documentation of income.

To reiterate, eligibility for a conventional 30-year mortgage is based primarily on documented, qualifying income, not simply on the value of assets you claim to own.

This applies also to the claim you submitted:

> The first birth of every womb is mine.

We cannot accept unspecified infants as proof of either assets or income.

When asked to identify the financial institution holding these assets, you replied:

> "The earth is mine, and the fullness thereof."

Our underwriting department has determined that this is not a bank.

6. Your assets cannot be verified.

You claim ownership of:

- all silver;
- all gold;
- the cattle on a thousand hills;
- the heavens;
- the earth;
- and everything therein.

Unfortunately, none of these assets can be verified.

See Item 1.

7. Your employment history cannot be verified.

You describe yourself variously as:

Creator, King, Shepherd, Warrior, Judge, Potter, Husband, Father, Consuming Fire, and "Man of War."

We require a documented history of stable and continuing income.

"Creator of Heaven and Earth" could not be independently verified because all references provided appear to have been written by you or your associates.

8. Your credit history is unavailable.

Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion report no credit file under any of the names provided.

You responded that you existed before they did.

This does not establish a credit history.

9. We cannot verify the source of your proposed down payment.

You indicated that the necessary funds would be obtained "from the Egyptians" and "from the peoples of the land that I am giving you to dispossess before you," including but not limited to the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.

We require documentation establishing the source of all funds used at closing.

None of these constitutes an acceptable source of funds for a conventional mortgage.

We have referred this matter to Compliance.

10. Your stated housing requirements are inconsistent.

You state repeatedly that you require a house.

You also state repeatedly that heaven is your throne, earth is your footstool, and no house made by human hands can contain you.

Please clarify why you are applying for a 30-year fixed-rate mortgage.

11. Your occupancy declaration cannot be reconciled with your application.

For intended occupancy, you checked:

☑ Primary Residence

You then wrote:

> "Do I not fill heaven and earth?"

She claimed to know an individual named "Daname," but she insisted he was not her husband, but merely her "baby daddy." We take this to mean that she disputes the claim that the two of you are legally married.

13. There are unresolved title issues.

You state that the land was promised to Abraham and his descendants forever.

Our title company could find no documentation of this in any public records.

14. Your requested loan term exceeds program guidelines.

Under "Desired Term," you entered:

> "From everlasting to everlasting."

The maximum available term is 30 years.

15. Outstanding levies.

In response to the question of whether there are any outstanding levies, you wrote:

> The sons of Aaron.

We're not sure what this means.

16. Your application contains threatening language.

In response to:

What happens if you fail to make payments?

you wrote:

> "The heavens will be rolled up like a scroll."

That will not be necessary.

Foreclosure is a legal process, and rolling up the heavens does not bear on the process in any way.

For these reasons, we regret to inform you that your mortgage application has been DENIED.

You may reapply when you can provide:

1. one verifiable legal name;

2. one verifiable current address;

3. a complete and verifiable residential history;

4. acceptable government-issued photo identification;

5. documented stable income;

6. verifiable assets and source of funds;

7. a loan term offered under one of our mortgage programs; and

8. reasonable evidence that the property you intend to purchase can, in fact, contain you.

We appreciate your interest in our company.
