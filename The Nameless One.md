GRGiRi. (גַרגִירִי)
- The name of the god of the universe, according to the majority of world religions circa. 0x800 P.A.
- Not to be pronounced.

---

See [[The Logos of Ba'al]] for the background.

The worship of this deity (Gritty) is the prevailing religion in the world at the time of the coming of the Son of J.

He is castigated for speaking of their god as one among many, and for making an image of the deity from the ancient records and distributing it to the people so that they may know their history.
