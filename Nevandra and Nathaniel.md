
For the lady at the gas station who said her 2nd grade son loves math.

Write this in a copy of Burn Math Class and give it to her tonight.

---

Dear Reader,

If you look up the name Nevandra, the internet might tell you the name has no meaning.

(I don't believe that's true. Probably just means the name is new.)

But if you look up the name Nathaniel, you'll hear it's one of the oldest names there is.

(And just between nerds like me and you, one doesn't have to be an ethnic Jew to be a bit obsessed with the bible and learn a little Hebrew.)

There's this story in an old book, it's where I learned the name Nathaniel.

It's an old book called Second Samuel, somewhere around chapter twelve.

There's this character named Nathan, who criticizes this king named David who'd taken away someone's wife. (Don't worry about the details. They'll make sense later in life.)

So this guy Nathan tells the king a story about an imaginary man.

And the king hears it and says "Whoever did this thing deserves to die."

And Nathan turns to the king and he says "You're the man."

Long story short, to me Nathan means someone who speaks truth to power.

That's sort of the same thing this book's about too.

Wrote it when I was younger. About 15 years back.

If you ever stop loving math, read this book to get back on track.

I learned to love the subject later in life, after hating it when I was young.

Sometimes you have to be away from school before you can realize which things are fun.

If it's not time for this book yet, give it a year, and try again.

Then next year, do the same. Repeat until it's time.

However long it takes, it'll be here when you're ready.

There's this tired old saying that says knowledge is power.

Here's the truth.

Knowledge is power. And the best way to fight power is truth.

If your mind is working properly, that should sound pretty dumb now.

But don't forget those words. They'll make sense eventually.

Keep up the good work.

Sincerely,
-Author
