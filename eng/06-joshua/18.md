------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ The whole community of the Israelite people assembled at Shiloh, and
set up the Tent of Meeting there. The land was now under their control;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² but there remained seven tribes of the Israelites which had not yet
received their portions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ So Joshua said to the Israelites, “How long will you be slack about
going and taking possession of the land which YHWH, the God of your
fathers, has assigned to you?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Appoint three men of each tribe; I will send them out to go through
the country and write down a description of it for purposes of
apportionment, and then come back to me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ They shall divide it into seven parts—Judah shall remain by its
territory in the south, and the house of Joseph shall remain by its
territory in the north.—

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ When you have written down the description of the land in seven parts,
bring it here to me. Then I will cast lots for you here before YHWH our
God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ For the Levites have no share among you, since the priesthood of YHWH
is their portion; and Gad and Reuben and the half-tribe of Manasseh have
received the portions which were assigned to them by Moses the servant
of YHWH, on the eastern side of the Jordan.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ The men set out on their journeys. Joshua ordered the men who were
leaving to write down a description of the land—“Go, traverse the
country and write down a description of it. Then return to me, and I
will cast lots for you here at Shiloh before YHWH.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ So the men went and traversed the land; they described it in a
document, town by town, in seven parts, and they returned to Joshua in
the camp at Shiloh.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Joshua cast lots for them at Shiloh before YHWH, and there Joshua
apportioned the land among the Israelites according to their divisions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ The lot of the tribe of the Benjaminites, by their clans, came out
first. The territory which fell to their lot lay between the Judites and
the Josephites.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² The boundary on their northern rim began at the Jordan; the boundary
ascended to the northern flank of Jericho, ascended westward into the
hill country and ran on to the Wilderness of Beth-aven.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ From there the boundary passed on southward to Luz, to the flank of
Luz—that is, Bethel; then the boundary descended to Atroth-addar [and]
to the hill south of Lower Beth-horon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ The boundary now turned and curved onto the western rim; and the
boundary ran southward from the hill on the south side of Beth-horon
till it ended at Kiriath-baal—that is, Kiriath-jearim—a town of the
Judites. That was the western rim.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ The southern rim: From the outskirts of Kiriath-jearim, the boundary
passed westward and ran on to the fountain of the Waters of Nephtoah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Then the boundary descended to the foot of the hill by the Valley of
Ben-hinnom at the northern end of the Valley of Rephaim; then it ran
down the Valley of Hinnom along the southern flank of the Jebusites to
En-rogel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ Curving northward, it ran on to En-shemesh and ran on to Geliloth,
facing the Ascent of Adummim, and descended to the Stone of Bohan son of
Reuben.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ It continued northward to the edge of the Arabah and descended into
the Arabah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ The boundary passed on to the northern flank of Beth-hoglah, and the
boundary ended at the northern tongue of the Dead Sea, at the southern
end of the Jordan. That was the southern boundary.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ On their eastern rim, finally, the Jordan was their boundary. That
was the portion of the Benjaminites, by their clans, according to its
boundaries on all sides.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And the towns of the tribe of the Benjaminites, by its clans, were:
Jericho, Beth-hoglah, Emek-keziz,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² Beth-arabah, Zemaraim, Bethel,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Avvim, Parah, Ophrah,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ Chephar-ammonah, Ophni, and Geba—12 towns, with their villages.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ Also Gibeon, Ramah, Beeroth,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ Mizpeh, Chephirah, Mozah,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ Rekem, Irpeel, Taralah,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 18:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ Zela, Eleph, and Jebus—that is, Jerusalem—Gibeath [and] Kiriath: 14
towns, with their villages. That was the portion of the Benjaminites, by
their clans.
