## 2 Samuel 9:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And there was of the house of Saul a servant whose name was Ziba. And when they had called him to David, the king said to him, Are you Ziba? And he said, Your servant is he.

## 2 Samuel 9:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And the king said, Is there not yet any of the house of Saul, that I may show the kindness of God to him? And Ziba said to the king, Jonathan has yet a son, that is lame on his feet.

## 2 Samuel 9:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And the king said to him, Where is he? And Ziba said to the king, Here: he is in the house of Machir, the son of Ammiel, in Lodebar.

## 2 Samuel 9:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And king David sent, and got him from the house of Machir, the son of Ammiel, from Lodebar.

## 2 Samuel 9:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And when Mephibosheth, the son of Jonathan, the son of Saul, came to David, he fell on his face, and bowed. And David said, Mephibosheth. And he answered, Here: your servant!

## 2 Samuel 9:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And David said to him, Fear not: for I'll surely show you kindness for Jonathan your father’s sake, and will restore you all the land of Saul your father; and you shall eat bread at my table continually.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 9:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And he bowed, and said, What is your servant, that you should look on such a dead dog as I am?

## 2 Samuel 9:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And the king called to Ziba, Saul’s servant, and said to him, I've given to your master’s son all who belonged to Saul and to all his house.
