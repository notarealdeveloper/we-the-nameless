## 2 Samuel 24:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And again the anger of YHWH was kindled against Israel, and he moved David against them to say, Go, number Israel and Judah.

---

Footnote 24:1. The anger of YHWH moves David, but the cost falls on Israel. The theology is not smoothed out; it is left with sharp edges.
