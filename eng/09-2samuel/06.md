## 2 Samuel 6:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And it was so, that when they that bare the ark of YHWH had gone six paces, he sacrificed oxen and fatlings.

## 2 Samuel 6:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And David danced before YHWH with all his might; and David was belted with a linen ephod.

---

Footnote 6:14. David dances with all force before YHWH. The king is not dignified in front of the ark, and the story knows how undignified that looks.
