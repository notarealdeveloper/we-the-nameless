------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ These are the nations that YHWH left so that He might test by them all the Israelites who had not known any of the wars of Canaan,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² so that succeeding generations of Israelites might be made to experience war—but only those who had not known the former wars:


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ the five principalities of the Philistines and all the Canaanites, Sidonians, and Hivites who inhabited the hill country of the Lebanon from Mount Baal-hermon to Lebo-hamath.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ These served as a means of testing Israel, to learn whether they would obey the commandments which YHWH had enjoined upon their fathers through Moses.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ The Israelites settled among the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites;


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ they took their daughters to wife and gave their own daughters to their sons, and they worshiped their gods.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ The Israelites did what was offensive to YHWH; they ignored YHWH their God and worshiped the Baalim and the Asheroth.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ YHWH became incensed at Israel and surrendered them to King Cushan-rishathaim of Aram-naharaim; and the Israelites were subject to Cushan-rishathaim for eight years.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ The Israelites cried out to YHWH, and YHWH raised a champion for the Israelites to deliver them: Othniel the Kenizzite, a younger kinsman of Caleb.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ The spirit of YHWH descended upon him and he became Israel’s chieftain. He went out to war, and YHWH delivered King Cushan-rishathaim of Aram into his hands. He prevailed over Cushan-rishathaim,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ and the land had peace for forty years. When Othniel the Kenizzite died,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² the Israelites again did what was offensive to YHWH. And because they did what was offensive to YHWH, YHWH let King Eglon of Moab prevail over Israel.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ [Eglon] brought the Ammonites and the Amalekites together under his command, and went and defeated Israel and occupied the City of Palms.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ The Israelites were subject to King Eglon of Moab for eighteen years.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Then the Israelites cried out to YHWH, and YHWH raised up a champion for them: the Benjaminite Ehud son of Gera, a left-handed man. It happened that the Israelites sent tribute to King Eglon of Moab through him.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ So Ehud made for himself a two-edged dagger, a gomed in length, which he girded on his right side under his cloak.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ He presented the tribute to King Eglon of Moab. Now Eglon was a very stout man.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ When [Ehud] had finished presenting the tribute, he dismissed the people who had conveyed the tribute.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ But he himself returned from Pesilim, near Gilgal, and said, “Your Majesty, I have a secret message for you.” [Eglon] thereupon commanded, “Silence!” So all those in attendance left his presence;


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ and when Ehud approached him, he was sitting alone in his cool upper chamber. Ehud said, “I have a message for you from God”; whereupon he rose from his seat.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ Reaching with his left hand, Ehud drew the dagger from his right side and drove it into [Eglon’s] belly.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² The fat closed over the blade and the hilt went in after the blade—for he did not pull the dagger out of his belly—and the filth came out.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Stepping out into the vestibule, Ehud shut the doors of the upper chamber on him and locked them.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ After he left, the courtiers returned. When they saw that the doors of the upper chamber were locked, they thought, “He must be relieving himself in the cool chamber.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ They waited a long time; and when he did not open the doors of the chamber, they took the key and opened them—and there their master was lying dead on the floor!


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ But Ehud had made good his escape while they delayed; he had passed Pesilim and escaped to Seirah.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ When he got there, he had the ram’s horn sounded through the hill country of Ephraim, and all the Israelites descended with him from the hill country; and he took the lead.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ “Follow me closely,” he said, “for YHWH has delivered your enemies, the Moabites, into your hands.” They followed him down and seized the fords of the Jordan against the Moabites; they let no one cross.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ On that occasion they slew about 10,000 Moabites; they were all robust and brave men, yet not one of them escaped.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ On that day, Moab submitted to Israel; and the land was tranquil for eighty years.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ After him came Shamgar son of Anath, who slew six hundred Philistines with an oxgoad. He too was a champion of Israel.
