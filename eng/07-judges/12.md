------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ The men of Ephraim mustered and crossed [the Jordan] to Zaphon. They
said to Jephthah, “Why did you march to fight the Ammonites without
calling us to go with you? We’ll burn your house down over you!”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Jephthah answered them, “I and my people were in a bitter conflict
with the Ammonites; and I summoned you, but you did not save me from
them.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ When I saw that you were no saviors, I risked my life and advanced
against the Ammonites; and YHWH delivered them into my hands. Why have
you come here now to fight against me?”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And Jephthah gathered all the men of Gilead and fought the
Ephraimites. The men of Gilead defeated the Ephraimites; for they had
said, “You Gileadites are nothing but fugitives from Ephraim—being in
Manasseh is like being in Ephraim.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ The Gileadites held the fords of the Jordan against the Ephraimites.
And when any fugitive from Ephraim said, “Let me cross,” the men of
Gilead would ask him, “Are you an Ephraimite?”; if he said “No,”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ they would say to him, “Then say shibboleth”; but he would say
“sibboleth,” not being able to pronounce it correctly. Thereupon they
would seize him and slay him by the fords of the Jordan. Forty-two
thousand Ephraimites fell at that time.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Jephthah led Israel six years. Then Jephthah the Gileadite died and he
was buried in one of the towns of Gilead.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ After him, Ibzan of Bethlehem led Israel.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ He had thirty sons, and he married off thirty daughters outside the
clan and brought in thirty girls from outside the clan for his sons. He
led Israel seven years.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Then Ibzan died and was buried in Bethlehem.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ After him, Elon the Zebulunite led Israel; he led Israel for ten
years.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Then Elon the Zebulunite died and was buried in Aijalon, in the
territory of Zebulun.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ After him, Abdon son of Hillel the Pirathonite led Israel.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ He had forty sons and thirty grandsons, who rode on seventy
jackasses. He led Israel for eight years.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Then Abdon son of Hillel the Pirathonite died. He was buried in
Pirathon, in the territory of Ephraim, on the hill of the Amalekites.
