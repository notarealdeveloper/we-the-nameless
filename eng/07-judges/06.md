------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Then the Israelites did what was offensive to YHWH, and YHWH delivered them into the hands of the Midianites for seven years.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² The hand of the Midianites prevailed over Israel; and because of Midian, the Israelites provided themselves with refuges in the caves and strongholds of the mountains.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ After the Israelites had done their sowing, Midian, Amalek, and the Kedemites would come up and raid them;


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ they would attack them, destroy the produce of the land all the way to Gaza, and leave no means of sustenance in Israel, not a sheep or an ox or an ass.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ For they would come up with their livestock and their tents, swarming as thick as locusts; they and their camels were innumerable. Thus they would invade the land and ravage it.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Israel was reduced to utter misery by the Midianites, and the Israelites cried out to YHWH.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ When the Israelites cried to YHWH on account of Midian,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ YHWH sent a prophet to the Israelites who said to them, “Thus said YHWH, the God of Israel: I brought you up out of Egypt and freed you from the house of bondage.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ I rescued you from the Egyptians and from all your oppressors; I drove them out before you, and gave you their land.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And I said to you, ‘I YHWH am your God. You must not worship the gods of the Amorites in whose land you dwell.’ But you did not obey Me.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ An angel of YHWH came and sat under the terebinth at Ophrah, which belonged to Joash the Abiezrite. His son Gideon was then beating out wheat inside a winepress in order to keep it safe from the Midianites.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² The angel of YHWH appeared to him and said to him, “YHWH is with you, valiant warrior!”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Gideon said to him, “Please, my lord, if YHWH is with us, why has all this befallen us? Where are all His wondrous deeds about which our fathers told us, saying, ‘Truly YHWH brought us up from Egypt’? Now YHWH has abandoned us and delivered us into the hands of Midian!”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ YHWH turned to him and said, “Go in this strength of yours and deliver Israel from the Midianites. I herewith make you My messenger.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ He said to Him, “Please, my lord, how can I deliver Israel? Why, my clan is the humblest in Manasseh, and I am the youngest in my father’s household.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ YHWH replied, “I will be with you, and you shall defeat Midian to a man.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And he said to Him, “If I have gained Your favor, give me a sign that it is You who are speaking to me:


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ do not leave this place until I come back to You and bring out my offering and place it before You.” And He answered, “I will stay until you return.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ So Gideon went in and prepared a kid, and [baked] unleavened bread from an ephah of flour. He put the meat in a basket and poured the broth into a pot, and he brought them out to Him under the terebinth. As he presented them,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ the angel of God said to him, “Take the meat and the unleavened bread, put them on yonder rock, and spill out the broth.” He did so.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ The angel of YHWH held out the staff that he carried, and touched the meat and the unleavened bread with its tip. A fire sprang up from the rock and consumed the meat and the unleavened bread. And the angel of YHWH vanished from his sight.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² Then Gideon realized that it was an angel of YHWH; and Gideon said, “Alas, O Lord GOD! For I have seen an angel of YHWH face to face.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ But YHWH said to him, “All is well; have no fear, you shall not die.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ So Gideon built there an altar to YHWH and called it Adonai-shalom. To this day it stands in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ That night YHWH said to him: “Take the young bull belonging to your father and another bull seven years old; pull down the altar of Baal which belongs to your father, and cut down the sacred post which is beside it.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ Then build an altar to YHWH your God, on the level ground on top of this stronghold. Take the other bull and offer it as a burnt offering, using the wood of the sacred post that you have cut down.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ So Gideon took ten of his servants and did as YHWH had told him; but as he was afraid to do it by day, on account of his father’s household and the townspeople, he did it by night.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ Early the next morning, the townspeople found that the altar of Baal had been torn down and the sacred post beside it had been cut down, and that the second bull had been offered on the newly built altar.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ They said to one another, “Who did this thing?” Upon inquiry and investigation, they were told, “Gideon son of Joash did this thing!”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ The townspeople said to Joash, “Bring out your son, for he must die: he has torn down the altar of Baal and cut down the sacred post beside it!”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ But Joash said to all who had risen against him, “Do you have to contend for Baal? Do you have to vindicate him? Whoever fights his battles shall be dead by morning! If he is a god, let him fight his own battles, since it is his altar that has been torn down!”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² That day they named him Jerubbaal, meaning “Let Baal contend with him, since he tore down his altar.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ All Midian, Amalek, and the Kedemites joined forces; they crossed over and encamped in the Valley of Jezreel.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ The spirit of YHWH enveloped Gideon; he sounded the horn, and the Abiezrites rallied behind him.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ And he sent messengers throughout Manasseh, and they too rallied behind him. He then sent messengers through Asher, Zebulun, and Naphtali, and they came up to meet the Manassites.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ And Gideon said to God, “If You really intend to deliver Israel through me as You have said—


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ here I place a fleece of wool on the threshing floor. If dew falls only on the fleece and all the ground remains dry, I shall know that You will deliver Israel through me, as You have said.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:38

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁸ And that is what happened. Early the next day, he squeezed the fleece and wrung out the dew from the fleece, a bowlful of water.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:39

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁹ Then Gideon said to God, “Do not be angry with me if I speak just once more. Let me make just one more test with the fleece: let the fleece alone be dry, while there is dew all over the ground.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:40

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁰ God did so that night: only the fleece was dry, while there was dew all over the ground.
