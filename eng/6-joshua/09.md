## Joshua 9:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And it was, when all the kings that were across the Jordan, in the hills, and in the valleys, and in all the borders of the great sea over against Lebanon, the Hittite, and the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite, heard of it;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² That they gathered themselves together, to fight with Joshua and with Israel, with one accord.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And when the inhabitants of Gibeon heard what Joshua had done to Jericho and to Ai,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ They did work with cunning, and went and made as if they had been ambassadors, and took old sacks on their asses, and wine bottles, old, and rent, and bound up;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And old shoes and clouted on their feet, and old garments on them; and all the bread of their provision was dry and moldy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And they went to Joshua to the camp at Gilgal, and said to him, and to the men of Israel, We be come from a far country: now therefore make you a league with us.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And the men of Israel said to the Hivites, Peradventure you live among us; and how shall we make a league with you?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And they said to Joshua, We are your servants. And Joshua said to them, Who are you? and from from where come you?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And they said to him, From a very far country your servants are come because of the name of YHWH your God: for we have heard the fame of him, and all that he did in Egypt,

## Joshua 9:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And all that he did to the two kings of the Amorites, that were beyond Jordan, to Sihon king of Heshbon, and to Og king of Bashan, that was at Ashtaroth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Therefore our elders and all the inhabitants of our country spoke to us, saying, Take provisions with you for the journey, and go to meet them, and say to them, We are your servants: therefore now make you a league with us.

## Joshua 9:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² This our bread we took hot for our provision out of our houses on the day we came forth to go to you; but now, here, it is dry, and it is moldy:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And these bottles of wine, that we filled, were new; and, here, they be rent: and these our garments and our shoes are become old by reason of the very long journey.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And the men took of their provisions, and asked not counsel at the mouth of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And Joshua made peace with them, and made a league with them, to let them live: and the chieftains of the congregation swore to them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And it was at the end of three days after they had made a league with them, that they heard that they were their neighbours, and that they lived among them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And the children of Israel journeyed, and came to their cities on the third day. Now their cities were Gibeon, and Chephirah, and Beeroth, and Kirjathjearim.

## Joshua 9:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:19

¹⁹ But all the chieftains said to all the congregation, We have sworn to them by YHWH God of Israel: now therefore we may not touch them.

## Joshua 9:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 9:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And the chieftains said to them, Let them live; but let them be cutters of wood and drawers of water to all the congregation; as the chieftains had promised them.

## Joshua 9:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And Joshua called for them, and he spoke to them, saying, Therefore have you deceived us, saying, We are very far from you; when you live among us?

## Joshua 9:23
