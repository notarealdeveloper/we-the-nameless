## Joshua 12:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Now these are the kings of the land, that the children of Israel struck, and possessed their land across the Jordan toward the rising of the sun, from the river Arnon to mount Hermon, and all the plain on the east:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 12:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived in Heshbon, and ruled from Aroer, that is on the bank of the river Arnon, and from the middle of the river, and from half Gilead, even to the river Jabbok, that is the border of the children of Ammon;

## Joshua 12:3

## Joshua 12:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And the border of Og king of Bashan, that was of the remnant of the giants, who lived at Ashtaroth and at Edrei,

## Joshua 12:5

## Joshua 12:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------
