------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 21:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And they spoke to them at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, saying, YHWH commanded by the hand of Moses to give us cities to live in, with the pasture lands of it for our cattle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 21:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------
