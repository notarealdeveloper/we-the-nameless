------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And these are the countries that the children of Israel inherited in the land of Canaan, that Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the heads of the fathers of the tribes of the children of Israel, distributed for inheritance to them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² By lot was their inheritance, as YHWH commanded by the hand of Moses, for the nine tribes, and for the half tribe.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ For Moses had given the inheritance of two tribes and an half tribe across the Jordan: but to the Levites he gave none inheritance among them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ For the children of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim: therefore they gave no part to the Levites in the land, except cities to live in, with their pasture lands for their cattle and for their substance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ As YHWH commanded Moses, so the children of Israel did, and they divided the land.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And the children of Judah came to Joshua in Gilgal: and Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenezite said to him, You knowest the thing that YHWH said to Moses the man of God concerning me and you in Kadeshbarnea.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Forty years old was I when Moses the servant of YHWH sent me from Kadeshbarnea to espy out the land; and I brought him word again as it was in mine heart.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Nevertheless my brothers that went up with me made the heart of the people melt: but I wholly followed YHWH my God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And Moses swore on that day, saying, Surely the land on that your feet have trodden shall be your inheritance, and your children’s for ever, because you have wholly followed YHWH my God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And now, here, YHWH has kept me alive, as he said, these forty and five years, even since YHWH spoke this word to Moses, while the children of Israel wandered in the wilderness: and now, lo, I am this day eighty and five years old.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ As yet I am as strong this day as I was in the day that Moses sent me: as my strength was then, even so is my strength now, for war, both to go out, and to come in.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Now therefore give me this mountain, of that YHWH spoke in that day; for you heardest in that day how the Anakims were there, and that the cities were great and fortified: if so be YHWH will be with me, then I shall be able to drive them out, as YHWH said.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And Joshua blessed him, and gave to Caleb the son of Jephunneh Hebron for an inheritance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Hebron therefore became the inheritance of Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenezite to this day, because that he wholly followed YHWH God of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 14:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And the name of Hebron before was Kirjatharba; that Arba was a great man among the Anakims. And the land had rest from war.
