------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And the word of YHWH came to Jehu the son of Hanani against Baasha, saying,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Because as I exalted you out of the dust, and made you prince over my people Israel: and you have walked in the way of Jeroboam, and have made my people Israel to sin, to provoke me to anger with their sins:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Here: I'll take away the posterity of Baasha, and the posterity of his house: and will make your house like the house of Jeroboam the son of Nebat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Him that dies of Baasha in the city shall the dogs eat: and him that dies of his in the fields shall the birds of the air eat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And the rest of the acts of Baasha, and what he did, and his might, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And Baasha lay down with his fathers, and was buried in Tirzah: and Elah his son reigned in his place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And also by the hand of the prophet Jehu the son of Hanani came the word of YHWH against Baasha, and against his house, even for all the bad that he did in YHWH's eyes, in provoking him to anger with the work of his hands, in being like the house of Jeroboam: and because he killed him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ In the twenty and sixth year of Asa king of Judah began Elah the son of Baasha to reign over Israel in Tirzah, two years.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And his servant Zimri, commander of half his chariots, conspired against him, as he was in Tirzah, drinking himself drunk in the house of Arza steward of his house in Tirzah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And Zimri went in and struck him, and killed him, in the twenty and seventh year of Asa king of Judah, and reigned in his place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And it was when he began to reign, as soon as he sat on his throne, that he slaughtered all the house of Baasha: he left him not one that pisses against a wall, neither of his kinsfolks, nor of his friends.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Thus did Zimri destroy all the house of Baasha, according to the word of YHWH, that he spoke against Baasha by Jehu the prophet.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ For all the sins of Baasha, and the sins of Elah his son, by that they sinned, and by that they made Israel to sin, in provoking YHWH God of Israel to anger with their empty things.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And the rest of the acts of Elah, and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ In the twenty and seventh year of Asa king of Judah did Zimri reign seven days in Tirzah. And the people were encamped against Gibbethon, who belonged to the Philistines.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And the people that were encamped heard say, Zimri has conspired, and has also killed the king: why all Israel made Omri, the commander of the army, king over Israel that day in the camp.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And Omri went up from Gibbethon, and all Israel with him, and they besieged Tirzah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And it was when Zimri saw that the city was taken, that he went into the palace of the king's house, and burnt the king's house over him with fire, and died.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ For his sins that he sinned in doing bad in YHWH's eyes, in walking in the way of Jeroboam, and in his sin that he did, to make Israel to sin.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And the rest of the acts of Zimri, and his treason that he worked, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And were the people of Israel divided into two parts: half of the people followed Tibni the son of Ginath, to make him king: and half followed Omri.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² But the people that followed Omri prevailed against the people that followed Tibni the son of Ginath: so Tibni died, and Omri reigned.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ In the thirty and first year of Asa king of Judah began Omri to reign over Israel, twelve years: six years reigned he in Tirzah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And he bought the hill Samaria of Shemer for two talents of silver, and built on the hill, and called the name of the city that he built, after the name of Shemer, owner of the hill, Samaria.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ But Omri worked bad in the eyes of YHWH, and did worse than all that were before him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ And the rest of the acts of Omri that he did, and his might that he showed, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

## 1 Kings 16:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 16:29

²⁹ And in the thirty and eighth year of Asa king of Judah began Ahab the son of Omri to reign over Israel: and Ahab the son of Omri reigned over Israel in Samaria twenty and two years.

## 1 Kings 16:30
