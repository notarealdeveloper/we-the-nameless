------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 1:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And King David was old, coming in days; and they covered him with clothes, but he was not warm.

---

Footnote 1:1. Coming in days is the Hebrew old-age idiom. The body of David, so hot in Samuel, is now a cold political fact.
