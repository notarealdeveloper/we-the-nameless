## 1 Kings 12:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And all Israel saw that the king had not listened to them. And the people returned the king a word, saying, What portion do we have in David? And no legacy in Jesse’s son! To your tents, Israel! Now see your house, David. And Israel went to its tents.

---

Footnote 12:16. To your tents, Israel echoes the old tribal cry. The united monarchy comes apart in a sentence that sounds older than the monarchy.
