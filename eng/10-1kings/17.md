------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And Elijah the Tishbite, from the settlers of Gilead, said to Ahab, As YHWH, Israel’s God, lives, before whom I stand: there shall not be dew or rain these years except by my word.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And the word of YHWH came to him, saying,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Go hence, and turn you eastward, and hide yourself by the brook Cherith, that is before Jordan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And it shall be, that you shall drink of the brook: and I have commanded the ravens to feed you there.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And he went and did according to the word of YHWH: for he went and dwelt by the brook Cherith, that is before Jordan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And the ravens brought him bread and flesh in the morning, and bread and flesh in the evening: and he drank of the brook.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And it was after a while, that the brook dried up, because there had been no rain in the land.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And the word of YHWH came to him, saying,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Arise, go to Zarephath, which belongs to Zidon, and dwell there: here:, I have commanded a widow woman there to sustain you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And he arose and went to Zarephath. And when he came to the gate of the city, here: the widow woman was there gathering of sticks: and he called to her, and said, Fetch me, please, a little water in a vessel, that I may drink.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And as she was going to fetch it, he called to her, and said, Bring me, please, a morsel of bread in your hand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And she said, As YHWH your God lives, I have not a cake, but an handful of meal in a barrel, and a little oil in a cruse: and, here: I am gathering two sticks, that I may go in and prepare it for me and my son, that we may eat it, and die.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And Elijah said to her, Fear not: go and do as you have said: but make me of it a little cake first, and bring it to me, and after make for you and for your son.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ For thus says YHWH God of Israel, The barrel of meal shall not waste, neither shall the cruse of oil fail, until the day that YHWH sends rain on the earth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And she went and did according to the saying of Elijah: and she, and he, and her house, did eat many days.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And the barrel of meal wasted not, neither did the cruse of oil fail, according to the word of YHWH, that he spoke by Elijah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And it was after these things, that the son of the woman, the mistress of the house, fell sick: and his sickness was so heavy, that there was no breath left in him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And she said to Elijah, What have I to do with you, O you man of God? are you come to me to call my sin to remembrance, and to kill my son?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And he said to her, Give me your son. And he took him out of her bosom, and carried him up into a loft, where he abode, and laid him on his own bed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And he cried to YHWH, and said, YHWH my God, have you also brought bad on the widow with whom I sojourn, by killing her son?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And he stretched himself on the child three times, and cried to YHWH, and said, YHWH my God, please, let this child's life come into him again.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And YHWH heard the voice of Elijah: and the life of the child came into him again, and he revived.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ And Elijah took the child, and brought him down out of the room into the house, and delivered him to his mother: and Elijah said, See, your son lives.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 17:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And the woman said to Elijah, And by this I know that you are a man of God, and that the word of YHWH in your mouth is truth.
