## 1 Kings 22:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And he said, Therefore hear YHWH’s word. I saw YHWH sitting on His throne, and all the army of the skies standing by Him, on His right and on His left.

---

Footnote 22:19. The army of the skies stands around YHWH like a royal court. The scene is not monotheism cleaned for company; it is Israelite theology with the furniture still in the room.
