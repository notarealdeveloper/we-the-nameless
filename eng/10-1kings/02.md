------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And the days of David drew nigh that he should die: and he charged Solomon his son, saying,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² I am going in the way of all the earth. And be strong, and be a man.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And observe YHWH your God’s charge, to walk in His ways, to observe His laws, His commandments, His judgments, and His testimonies, as written in Moses’ law, so that you’ll act wisely in all that you do and everywhere you turn there.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ That YHWH may continue his word that he spoke concerning me, saying, If your children take heed to their way, to walk before me in truth with all their heart and with all their life, there shall not fail you (said he) a man on the throne of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Moreover you know also what Joab the son of Zeruiah did to me, and what he did to the two commanders of the armies of Israel, to Abner the son of Ner, and to Amasa the son of Jether, whom he slaughtered, and shed the blood of war in peace, and put the blood of war on his belt that was about his waist, and in his shoes that were on his feet.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Do therefore according to your wisdom, and let not his gray head go down to the grave in peace.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ But show kindness to the sons of Barzillai the Gileadite, and let them be of those that eat at your table: for so they came to me when I fled because of Absalom your brother.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And here: you have with you Shimei the son of Gera, a Benjamite of Bahurim, which cursed me with a grievous curse in the day when I went to Mahanaim: but he came down to meet me at Jordan, and I swore to him by YHWH, saying, I'll not put you to death with the sword.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And now don’t hold him innocent: for you are a wise man, and know what you ought to do to him: but his gray head bring you down to the grave with blood.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And David lay down with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And the days that David reigned over Israel were forty years: seven years reigned he in Hebron, and thirty and three years reigned he in Jerusalem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And sat Solomon on the throne of David his father: and his kingdom was established greatly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And Adonijah the son of Haggith came to Bathsheba the mother of Solomon. And she said, Comest you peaceably? And he said, Peaceably.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ He said moreover, I have somewhat to say to you. And she said, Say on.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And he said, You know that the kingdom was mine, and that all Israel set their faces on me, that I should reign: but the kingdom is turned about, and has become my brother's: for it was his from YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And now I ask one petition of you, deny me not. And she said to him, Say on.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And he said, Speak, please, to Solomon the king, (for he'll not say you no,) that he give me Abishag the Shunammite to wife.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And Bathsheba said, Well: I'll speak for you to the king.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Bathsheba therefore went to king Solomon, to speak to him for Adonijah. And the king rose up to meet her, and bowed himself to her, and sat down on his throne, and caused a seat to be set for the king's mother: and she sat on his right hand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And she said, I desire one small petition of you: please, say me not no. And the king said to her, Ask on, my mother: for I'll not say you no.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And she said, Let Abishag the Shunammite be given to Adonijah your brother to wife.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And king Solomon answered and said to his mother, And why do you ask Abishag the Shunammite for Adonijah? ask for him the kingdom also: for he is my elder brother: even for him, and for Abiathar the priest, and for Joab the son of Zeruiah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ And king Solomon swore by YHWH, saying, God do so to me, and more also, if Adonijah have not spoken this word against his own life.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And now, as YHWH lives, which has established me, and set me on the throne of David my father, and who has made me a house, as he promised, Adonijah shall be put to death this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And king Solomon sent by the hand of Benaiah the son of Jehoiada: and he fell on him that he died.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ And to Abiathar the priest said the king, Go to Anathoth, to your own fields: for you are worthy of death: but I'll not at this time put you to death, because you carried the ark of YHWH God before David my father, and because you have been afflicted in all in which my father was afflicted.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ And Solomon thrust out Abiathar from being priest to YHWH: that he might fulfil the word of YHWH, that he spoke concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And tidings came to Joab: for Joab had turned after Adonijah, though he turned not after Absalom. And Joab fled to the tabernacle of YHWH, and caught hold on the horns of the altar.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ And it was told king Solomon that Joab was fled to the tabernacle of YHWH: and, here: he is by the altar. And Solomon sent Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, saying, Go, fall on him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ And Benaiah came to the tabernacle of YHWH, and said to him, Thus says the king, Come forth. And he said, Nay: but I'll die here. And Benaiah brought the king word again, saying, Thus said Joab, and thus he answered me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ And the king said to him, Do as he has said, and fall on him, and bury him: that you may take away the innocent blood, which Joab shed, from me, and from the house of my father.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² And YHWH shall return his blood on his own head, who fell on two men more righteous and better than he, and slaughtered them with the sword, my father David not knowing of it, that is, Abner the son of Ner, commander of the army of Israel, and Amasa the son of Jether, commander of the army of Judah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ Their blood shall therefore return on the head of Joab, and on the head of his seed for ever: but on David, and on his seed, and on his house, and on his throne, shall there be peace for ever from YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada went up, and fell on him, and struck him: and he was buried in his own house in the wilderness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ And the king put Benaiah the son of Jehoiada in his room over the army: and Zadok the priest did the king put in place of Abiathar.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ And the king sent and called for Shimei, and said to him, Build you a house in Jerusalem, and dwell there, and go not forth thence anywhere.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ For it shall be, that on the day you go out, and passest over the brook Kidron, you shall know for certain that you shall surely die: your blood shall be on your own head.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:38

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁸ And Shimei said to the king, The saying is good: as my lord the king has said, so will your servant do. And Shimei dwelt in Jerusalem many days.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:39

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁹ And it was at the end of three years, that two of the servants of Shimei ran away to Achish son of Maachah king of Gath. And they told Shimei, saying, Here: your servants be in Gath.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:40

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁰ And Shimei arose, and saddled his donkey, and went to Gath to Achish to seek his servants: and Shimei went, and brought his servants from Gath.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:41

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴¹ And it was told Solomon that Shimei had gone from Jerusalem to Gath, and was come again.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:42

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴² And the king sent and called for Shimei, and said to him, Did I not make you to swear by YHWH, and protested to you, saying, Know for a certain, on the day you go out, and walkest abroad anywhere, that you shall surely die? and you said to me, The word that I have heard is good.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:43

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴³ Why then have you not kept the oath of YHWH, and the commandment that I have charged you with?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:44

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁴ The king said moreover to Shimei, You know all the badness that your heart knows, that you did to David my father: therefore YHWH shall return your badness on your own head:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:45

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁵ And king Solomon shall be blessed, and the throne of David shall be established before YHWH for ever.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 2:46

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁶ And the king commanded Benaiah the son of Jehoiada: which went out, and fell on him, that he died. And the kingdom was established in the hand of Solomon.
