------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And Solomon reigned over all kingdoms from the river to the land of the Philistines, and to the border of Egypt: they brought presents, and served Solomon all the days of his life.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And Solomon's provision for one day was thirty measures of fine flour, and sixty measures of meal,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Ten fat oxen, and twenty oxen out of the pastures, and a hundred sheep, beside deer, and gazelles, and roebucks, and fattened birds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ For he had dominion over all the region on this side the river, from Tiphsah even to Azzah, over all the kings on this side the river: and he had peace on all sides round about him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And Judah and Israel dwelt safely, every man under his vine and under his fig tree, from Dan even to Beersheba, all the days of Solomon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And Solomon had forty thousand stalls of horses for his chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And those officers provided food for king Solomon, and for all that came to king Solomon's table, every man in his month: they lacked nothing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Barley also and straw for the horses and swift camels brought they to the place where the officers were, every man according to his charge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And God gave Solomon wisdom and understanding very much, and breadth of heart, even as the sand that is on the sea shore.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And Solomon's wisdom was greater than the wisdom of all the children of the east country, and all the wisdom of Egypt.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ For he was wiser than all men: than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, and Chalcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol: and his name was in all nations round about.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And he spoke three thousand proverbs: and his songs were a thousand and five.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And he spoke of trees, from the cedar tree that is in Lebanon even to the hyssop that springs out of the wall: he spoke also of animals, and of bird, and of creeping things, and of fish.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And there came of all people to hear the wisdom of Solomon, from all kings of the earth, who had heard of his wisdom.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And Hiram king of Tyre sent his servants to Solomon: for he had heard that they had anointed him king in place of his father: for Hiram was ever a lover of David.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And Solomon sent to Hiram, saying,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ You know how David my father could not build a house to the name of YHWH his God for the wars that were about him on every side, until YHWH put them under the soles of his feet.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ But now YHWH my God has given me rest on every side, so that there is neither adversary nor bad thing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And here: I purpose to build a house to the name of YHWH my God, as YHWH spoke to David my father, saying, Your son, whom I'll set on your throne in your place, he shall build a house to my name.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And now command that they cut me cedar trees out of Lebanon: and my servants shall be with your servants: and to you I will give wage for your servants according to all that you shall appoint: for you know that there is not among us any that knows how to cut wood like to the Sidonians.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And it was when Hiram heard the words of Solomon, that he rejoiced greatly, and said, Blessed be YHWH this day, which has given to David a wise son over this great people.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And Hiram sent to Solomon, saying, I have considered the things that you sent to me for: and I'll do all your desire concerning wood of cedar, and concerning wood of cypress.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ My servants shall bring them down from Lebanon to the sea: and I'll convey them by sea in floats to the place that you shall appoint me, and will cause them to be discharged there, and you shall receive them: and you shall accomplish my desire, in giving food for my household.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And Hiram gave Solomon cedar trees and cypress trees according to all his desire.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And Solomon gave Hiram twenty thousand measures of wheat for food to his household, and twenty measures of pure oil: thus gave Solomon to Hiram year by year.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ And YHWH gave Solomon wisdom, as he promised him: and there was peace between Hiram and Solomon: and they two made a league together.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ And king Solomon raised a levy out of all Israel: and the levy was thirty thousand men.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And he sent them to Lebanon, ten thousand a month by courses: a month they were in Lebanon, and two months at home: and Adoniram was over the levy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ And Solomon had sixty and ten thousand that bore burdens, and eighty thousand cuters in the mountains:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ Beside the chief of Solomon's officers that were over the work, three thousand and three hundred, which ruled over the people that worked in the work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ And the king commanded, and they brought great stones, costly stones, and cuted stones, to lay the foundation of the house.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 5:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² And Solomon's builders and Hiram's builders did cut them, and the stonesquarers: so they prepared wood and stones to build the house.
