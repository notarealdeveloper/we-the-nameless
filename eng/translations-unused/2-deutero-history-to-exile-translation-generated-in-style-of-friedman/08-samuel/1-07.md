I Samuel 7:1	The men of Kiriath-jearim came and took up the Ark of YHWH and brought it into the house of Abinadab on the hill; and they consecrated his son Eleazar to have charge of the Ark of YHWH.
I Samuel 7:2	A long time elapsed from the day that the Ark was housed in Kiriath-jearim, twenty years in all; and all the House of Israel yearned after YHWH.
I Samuel 7:3	And Samuel said to all the House of Israel, “If you mean to return to YHWH with all your heart, you must remove the alien gods and the Ashtaroth from your midst and direct your heart to YHWH and serve Him alone. Then He will deliver you from the hands of the Philistines.”
I Samuel 7:4	And the Israelites removed the Baalim and Ashtaroth and they served YHWH alone.
I Samuel 7:5	Samuel said, “Assemble all Israel at Mizpah, and I will pray to YHWH for you.”
I Samuel 7:6	They assembled at Mizpah, and they drew water and poured it out before YHWH; they fasted that day, and there they confessed that they had sinned against YHWH. And Samuel acted as chieftain of the Israelites at Mizpah.
I Samuel 7:7	When the Philistines heard that the Israelites had assembled at Mizpah, the lords of the Philistines marched out against Israel. Hearing of this, the Israelites were terrified of the Philistines
I Samuel 7:8	and they implored Samuel, “Do not neglect us and do not refrain from crying out to YHWH our God to save us from the hands of the Philistines.”
I Samuel 7:9	Thereupon Samuel took a suckling lamb and sacrificed it as a whole burnt offering to YHWH; and Samuel cried out to YHWH in behalf of Israel, and YHWH responded to him.
I Samuel 7:10	For as Samuel was presenting the burnt offering and the Philistines advanced to attack Israel, YHWH thundered mightily against the Philistines that day. He threw them into confusion, and they were routed by Israel.
I Samuel 7:11	The men of Israel sallied out of Mizpah and pursued the Philistines, striking them down to a point below Beth-car.
I Samuel 7:12	Samuel took a stone and set it up between Mizpah and Shen, and named it Eben-ezer: “For up to now,” he said, “YHWH has helped us.”
I Samuel 7:13	The Philistines were humbled and did not invade the territory of Israel again; and the hand of YHWH was set against the Philistines as long as Samuel lived.
I Samuel 7:14	The towns which the Philistines had taken from Israel, from Ekron to Gath, were restored to Israel; Israel recovered all her territory from the Philistines. There was also peace between Israel and the Amorites.
I Samuel 7:15	Samuel judged Israel as long as he lived.
I Samuel 7:16	Each year he made the rounds of Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah, and acted as judge over Israel at all those places.
I Samuel 7:17	Then he would return to Ramah, for his home was there, and there too he would judge Israel. He built an altar there to YHWH.
