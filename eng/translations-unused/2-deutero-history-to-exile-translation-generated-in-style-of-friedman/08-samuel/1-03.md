I Samuel 3:1	Young Samuel was in the service of YHWH under Eli. In those days the word of YHWH was rare; prophecy was not widespread.
I Samuel 3:2	One day, Eli was asleep in his usual place; his eyes had begun to fail and he could barely see.
I Samuel 3:3	The lamp of God had not yet gone out, and Samuel was sleeping in the temple of YHWH where the Ark of God was.
I Samuel 3:4	YHWH called out to Samuel, and he answered, “I’m coming.”
I Samuel 3:5	He ran to Eli and said, “Here I am; you called me.” But he replied, “I didn’t call you; go back to sleep.” So he went back and lay down.
I Samuel 3:6	Again YHWH called, “Samuel!” Samuel rose and went to Eli and said, “Here I am; you called me.” But he replied, “I didn’t call, my son; go back to sleep.”—
I Samuel 3:7	Now Samuel had not yet experienced YHWH; the word of YHWH had not yet been revealed to him.—
I Samuel 3:8	YHWH called Samuel again, a third time, and he rose and went to Eli and said, “Here I am; you called me.” Then Eli understood that YHWH was calling the boy.
I Samuel 3:9	And Eli said to Samuel, “Go lie down. If you are called again, say, ‘Speak, LORD, for Your servant is listening.’” And Samuel went to his place and lay down.
I Samuel 3:10	YHWH came, and stood there, and He called as before: “Samuel! Samuel!” And Samuel answered, “Speak, for Your servant is listening.”
I Samuel 3:11	YHWH said to Samuel: “I am going to do in Israel such a thing that both ears of anyone who hears about it will tingle.
I Samuel 3:12	In that day I will fulfill against Eli all that I spoke concerning his house, from beginning to end.
I Samuel 3:13	And I declare to him that I sentence his house to endless punishment for the iniquity he knew about—how his sons committed sacrilege at will —and he did not rebuke them.
I Samuel 3:14	Assuredly, I swear concerning the house of Eli that the iniquity of the house of Eli will never be expiated by sacrifice or offering.”
I Samuel 3:15	Samuel lay there until morning; and then he opened the doors of the House of YHWH. Samuel was afraid to report the vision to Eli,
I Samuel 3:16	but Eli summoned Samuel and said, “Samuel, my son”; and he answered, “Here.”
I Samuel 3:17	And [Eli] asked, “What did He say to you? Keep nothing from me. Thus and more may God do to you if you keep from me a single word of all that He said to you!”
I Samuel 3:18	Samuel then told him everything, withholding nothing from him. And [Eli] said, “He is YHWH; He will do what He deems right.”
I Samuel 3:19	Samuel grew up and YHWH was with him: He did not leave any of Samuel’s predictions unfulfilled.
I Samuel 3:20	All Israel, from Dan to Beer-sheba, knew that Samuel was trustworthy as a prophet of YHWH.
I Samuel 3:21	And YHWH continued to appear at Shiloh: YHWH revealed Himself to Samuel at Shiloh with the word of YHWH;
