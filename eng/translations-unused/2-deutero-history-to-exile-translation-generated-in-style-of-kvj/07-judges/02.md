------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And an angel of YHWH came up from Gilgal to Bochim, and said, I brought you up out of Egypt, and I brought you to the land that I swore to your fathers: and I said, I will not break my covenant with you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And you shall make no covenant with the inhabitants of this land; you shall break down their altars: but you have not listened to my voice: why have you done this?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And I also said, I’ll not drive them out from in front of you; but they shall be as thorns in your sides, and their gods shall be a snare to you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And it was when the angel of YHWH spoke these words to all the children of Israel, that the people lifted their voice, and wept.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And they called the name of that place Bochim: and they sacrificed there to YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And when Joshua had let the people go, the children of Israel went each man to his legacy to take possession of the land.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And the people served YHWH all the days of Joshua, and all the days of the elders that lengthened days after Joshua, who had seen all the great work of YHWH, that he did for Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of YHWH, died, a hundred and ten years old.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And they buried him in the border of his legacy in Timnath-heres, in the Mountain of Ephraim, on the north side of the Mount Gaash.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And also all that generation were gathered to their fathers, and another generation got up after them that did not know YHWH, and also the work that He had done for Israel.

---

Footnote 2:10. A generation that did not know YHWH is not ignorance as lack of data. In this book, not knowing becomes not serving, and then serving the wrong gods.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And the children of Israel did what was bad in the eyes of YHWH, and served Baals:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And they forsook YHWH God of their fathers, that brought them out of the land of Egypt, and followed other gods, of the gods of the peoples that were around them, and bowed to them, and angered YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And they forsook YHWH, and served the Baal and the Ashtaroth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And the anger of YHWH was hot against Israel, and he gave them in the hand of plunderers, and they plundered them, and he sold them in the hands of their enemies all around, so that they could not any longer stand before their enemies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Wherever they went out, the hand of YHWH was against them for bad, as YHWH had said, and as YHWH had sworn to them: and they were greatly distressed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Nevertheless YHWH raised up judges, that rescued them from the hand of those that plundered them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And yet they would not listen to their judges, but they whored after other gods, and bowed to them: they turned quickly out of the way that their fathers walked in, obeying YHWH’s commandments; but they did not do so.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And when YHWH raised them up judges, then YHWH was with the judge, and rescued them from the hand of their enemies all the days of the judge: for it YHWH was moved by their groaning because of those pressing them and squeezing them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And it was when the judge was dead, that they returned, and corrupted themselves more than their fathers, in following other gods to serve them, and to bow down to them; they did not drop from their own doings, and not from their stubborn way.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And the anger of YHWH was hot against Israel: and he said, Because this people have transgressed my covenant that I commanded their fathers, and have not listened to my voice;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ I also won’t again drive out any from in front of them of the nations that Joshua left when he died:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² That through them I may test Israel, whether they are keeping the way of YHWH to walk in it, as their fathers kept it, or not.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 2:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Therefore YHWH left those nations, without driving them out hastily: and He did not give them in the hand of Joshua.
