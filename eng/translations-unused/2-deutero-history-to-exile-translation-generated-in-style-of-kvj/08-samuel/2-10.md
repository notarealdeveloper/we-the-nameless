## 2 Samuel 10:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 10:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Why Hanun took David’s servants, and shaved off the one half of their beards, and cut off their garments in the middle, even to their buttocks, and sent them away.

## 2 Samuel 10:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ When they told it to David, he sent to meet them, because the men were greatly ashamed: and the king said, Stay at Jericho until your beards be grown, and then return.

## 2 Samuel 10:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------
