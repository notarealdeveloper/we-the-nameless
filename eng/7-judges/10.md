------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And after Abimelech there arose to defend Israel Tola the son of Puah, the son of Dodo, a man of Issachar: and he lived in Shamir in Mountain of Ephraim.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And he judged Israel twenty and three years, and died, and was buried in Shamir.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And after him arose Jair, a Gileadite, and judged Israel twenty and two years.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And he had thirty sons that rode on thirty ass colts, and they had thirty cities, that are called Havothjair to this day, that are in the land of Gilead.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And Jair died, and was buried in Camon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And the children of Israel did what was bad again in the eyes of YHWH, and served Baals, and Ashtaroth, and the gods of Syria, and the gods of Sidon, and the gods of Moab, and the gods of the children of Ammon, and the gods of the Philistines, and forsook YHWH, and served not him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And the anger of YHWH was hot against Israel, and he sold them in the hands of the Philistines, and in the hands of the children of Ammon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And that year they vexed and oppressed the children of Israel: eighteen years, all the children of Israel that were on the other side Jordan in the land of the Amorite, that is in Gilead.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Moreover the children of Ammon passed over Jordan to fight also against Judah, and against Benjamin, and against the house of Ephraim; so that Israel was sore distressed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And the children of Israel cried to YHWH, saying, We have sinned against you, both because we’ve forsaken our God, and also served Baals.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And YHWH said to the children of Israel, Did not I rescue you from the Egyptians, and from the Amorite, from the children of Ammon, and from the Philistines?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² The Zidonians also, and the Amalekites, and the Maonites, did oppress you: and you cried to me, and I gave you out of their hand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Yet you have forsaken me, and served other gods: therefore I’ll rescue you no more.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Go and cry to the gods that you have chosen; let them rescue you in the time of your tribulation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And the children of Israel said to YHWH, We have sinned: do you to us whatever seems good to you; rescue us only, we please, this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And they put away the foreign gods from among them, and served YHWH: and his soul was grieved for the misery of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ Then the children of Ammon were gathered, and encamped in Gilead. And the children of Israel assembled themselves together, and encamped in Mizpah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 10:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And the people and princes of Gilead said one to another, What man is he that will begin to fight against the children of Ammon? he shall be head over all the inhabitants of Gilead.
