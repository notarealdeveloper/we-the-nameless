## Judges 21:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And the men of Israel had sworn in Mizpah, saying, There shall not any of us give his daughter to Benjamin as a wife.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And the people came to the house of God, and stayed there till until evening before God, and lifted their voices, and wept sore;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And said, YHWH God of Israel, why is this happened in Israel, that there should be today one tribe lacking in Israel?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And it was in the morning, that the people rose early, and built there an altar, and offered burned offerings and peace offerings.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And the children of Israel said, Who is there among all the tribes of Israel that came not up with the congregation to YHWH? For they had made a great oath concerning him that came not up to YHWH to Mizpah, saying, He shall surely be put to death.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And the children of Israel repented them for Benjamin their brother, and said, There is one tribe cut off from Israel this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ How shall we do for wives for them that remain, seeing we’ve sworn by YHWH that we’ll not give them of our daughters to wives?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And they said, What one is there of the tribes of Israel that came not up to Mizpah to YHWH? And here: there came none to the camp from Jabeshgilead to the assembly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ For the people were numbered, and here: there were none of the inhabitants of Jabeshgilead there.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And the congregation sent there twelve thousand men of the sons of force, and commanded them, saying, Go and strike the inhabitants of Jabeshgilead by the mouth of the sword, with the women and little ones.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And this is the thing that you shall do, You shall completely destroy every male, and every woman that has lain by man.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And they found among the inhabitants of Jabeshgilead four hundred young virgins, that had known no man by lying with any male: and they brought them to the camp to Shiloh, that is in the land of Canaan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And the whole congregation sent some to speak to the children of Benjamin that were in the rock Rimmon, and to call in peace to them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And Benjamin came again at that time: and they gave them wives that they had saved alive of the women of Jabeshgilead: and yet so they sufficed them not.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And the people repented them for Benjamin, because that YHWH had made a breach in the tribes of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Then the elders of the congregation said, How shall we do for wives for them that remain, seeing the women are destroyed out of Benjamin?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And they said, There must be a legacy for them that be escaped of Benjamin, that a tribe be not destroyed out of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ But we may not give them wives of our daughters: for the children of Israel have sworn, saying, Cursed be he that gives a wife to Benjamin.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Then they said, Here: there is a feast of YHWH in Shiloh from days todays in a place that is on the north side of Bethel, on the east side of the highway that goes up from Bethel to Shechem, and on the south of Lebonah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ Therefore they commanded the children of Benjamin, saying, Go and lie in wait in the vineyards;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And see, and here, if the daughters of Shiloh come out to dance in dances, then come you out of the vineyards, and catch you each man his wife of the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And it shall be, when their fathers or their brothers come to us to complain, that we’ll say to them, Be favourable to them for our sakes: because we reserved not to each man his wife in the war: for you didn’t give to them at this time, that you should be guilty.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ And the children of Benjamin did so, and took them wives, according to their number, of them that danced, whom they caught: and they went and returned to their legacy, and repaired the cities, and lived in them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And the children of Israel departed from there at that time, each man to his tribe and to his family, and they went out from there each man to his legacy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 21:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ In those days there was no king in Israel. Each man would do what was straight in his eyes.

Footnote 21:25. The book ends with everyone doing what is straight in his eyes. That is not freedom. It is the last line of the autopsy.
