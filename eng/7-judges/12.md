------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And the men of Ephraim gathered themselves together, and went northward, and said to Jephthah, Why did you pass over over to fight against the children of Ammon, and didn’t call us to go with you? We’ll burn your house upon you with fire.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And Jephthah said to them, I and my people were at great strife with the children of Ammon: and when I called you, you gave me not out of their hands.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And when I saw that you gave me not, I put my life in my hands, and passed over against the children of Ammon, and YHWH gave them into my hand: therefore then are you come up to me this day, to fight against me?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Then Jephthah gathered all the men of Gilead, and fought with Ephraim: and the men of Gilead struck Ephraim, because they said, You Gileadites are fugitives of Ephraim among the Ephraimites, and among the Manassites.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And the Gileadites took the crossings of the Jordan before the Ephraimites: and it was so, that when those Ephraimites that were escaped said, Let me go over; that the men of Gilead said to him, Are you an Ephraimite? If he said, No;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And they said to him, Say now Shibboleth: and he said Sibboleth: for he could not set his mouth to speak it right. Then they took him, and killed him at the crossings of the Jordan: and there fell at that time from Ephraim forty-two thousand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And Jephthah judged Israel six years. Then died Jephthah the Gileadite, and was buried in one of the cities of Gilead.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And after him Ibzan of Bethlehem judged Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And he had thirty sons, and thirty daughters, whom he sent abroad, and took in thirty daughters from abroad for his sons. And he judged Israel seven years.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 12:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Then died Ibzan, and was buried at Bethlehem.
