## Judges 4:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And the children of Israel again did bad in the eyes of YHWH, when Ehud was dead.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And YHWH sold them in the hand of Jabin king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor; the commander of whose army was Sisera, who lived in Harosheth-hagoyim.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And the children of Israel cried to YHWH: for he had nine hundred chariots of iron: and twenty years he mightily oppressed the children of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And Deborah, a prophetess, the wife of Lapidoth, she judged Israel at that time.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And she lived under the palm tree of Deborah between Ramah and Bethel in Mountain of Ephraim: and the children of Israel came up to her for judgment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And she sent and called Barak the son of Abinoam out of Kedeshnaphtali, and said to him, Hath not YHWH God of Israel commanded, saying, Go and draw toward Mountain Tabor, and take with you ten thousand men of the children of Naphtali and of the children of Zebulun?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And I’ll draw to you to the river Kishon Sisera, the commander of Jabin’s army, with his chariots and his multitude: and I’ll rescue him into your hand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And Barak said to her, If you’ll go with me, then I’ll go: but if you’ll not go with me, then I’ll not go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And she said, I’ll surely go with you: notwithstanding the journey that you tak shall not be for your honour: for YHWH shall sell Sisera in the hand of a woman. And Deborah arose, and went with Barak to Kedesh.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And Barak called Zebulun and Naphtali to Kedesh: and he went up with ten thousand men at his feet: and Deborah went up with him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And Heber the Kenite, that was of the children of Hobab the father-in-law of Moses, had separated himself from the Kenites, and pitched his tent to the plain of Zaanaim, that is by Kedesh.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And they told Sisera that Barak the son of Abinoam had gone up to Mountain Tabor.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And Sisera gathered all his chariots, even nine hundred chariots of iron, and all the people that were with him, from Harosheth-hagoyim to the river of Kishon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And Deborah said to Barak, Up: for this is the day in that YHWH has given Sisera into your hand: isn’t YHWH gone out before you? So Barak went down from Mountain Tabor, and ten thousand men after him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And YHWH discomfited Sisera, and all his chariots, and all his army, by the mouth of the sword before Barak; so that Sisera lighted down off his chariot, and fled away on his feet.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ But Barak pursued after the chariots and after the army, to Harosheth-hagoyim: and all the army of Sisera fell by the mouth of the sword: and not one was left.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ But Sisera fled away on his feet to the tent of Jael the wife of Heber the Kenite: for there was peace between Jabin the king of Hazor and the house of Heber the Kenite.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And Jael went out to meet Sisera, and said to him, Turn in, my lord, turn in to me; fear not. And when he turned in to her into the tent, she covered him with a rug.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And he said to her, Give me, please, a little water to drink: for I’m thirsty. And she opened a skin of milk, and gave him drink, and covered him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ Again he said to her, Stand in the door of the tent, and it shall be, when any man does come and enquire of you, and say, Is there any man here? that you shall say, No.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 4:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And Jael, Heber’s wife, took a peg of the tent and put the hammer in her hand, and came to him quietly and drove the peg into his temple, and it went down into the ground. And he was fast asleep and worn out, and he died.

---

Footnote 4:21. Jael uses a tent peg and a hammer, household tools in the hand of a woman. Sisera comes in looking for cover and gets the roof of the tent, personally delivered.
