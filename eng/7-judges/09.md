------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And Abimelech the son of Jerubbaal went to Shechem to his mother’s brothers, and communed with them, and with all the family of the house of his mother’s father, saying,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Speak, please, in the ears of all the men of Shechem, Whether is better for you, either that all the sons of Jerubbaal, that are seventy and ten persons, reign over you, or that one reign over you? remember also that I’m your bone and your flesh.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And his mother’s brothers spoke of him in the ears of all the men of Shechem all these words: and their hearts inclined to follow Abimelech: for they said, He is our brother.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And they gave him seventy and ten pieces of silver out of the house of Baalberith, with that Abimelech hired vain and light persons, that followed him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And he went to his father’s house at Ophrah, and killed his brothers the sons of Jerubbaal, seventy men, upon one stone: but Jotham the youngest son of Jerubbaal was left: for he hid himself.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And all the men of Shechem gathered, and all the house of Millo, and went, and made Abimelech king, by the plain of the pillar that was in Shechem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And when they told it to Jotham, he went and stood in the top of Mountain Gerizim, and lifted up his voice, and cried, and said to them, Hearken to me, you men of Shechem, that God may listen to you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ The trees went, going, to anoint a king over them. And they said to the olive tree, Reign over us.

---

Footnote 9:8. Jotham's trees are not ornamental. They are a political theory with bark on it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ But the olive tree said to them, Should I leave my fatness, with that by me they honour God and man, and go to be promoted over the trees?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And the trees said to the fig tree, Come you, and reign over us.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ But the fig tree said to them, Should I forsake my sweetness, and my good fruit, and go to be promoted over the trees?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Then said the trees to the vine, Come you, and reign over us.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And the vine said to them, Should I leave my wine, that cheers God and man, and go to be promoted over the trees?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Then said all the trees to the bramble, Come you, and reign over us.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And the bramble said to the trees, If in truth you anoint me king over you, then come and put your trust in my shadow: and if not, let fire come out of the bramble, and devour the cedars of Lebright away.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And therefore, if you have done truly and sincbeforely, in that you have made Abimelech king, and if you have dealt well with Jerubbaal and his house, and have done to him according to the deserving of his hands;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ (For my father fought for you, and adventured his life far, and gave you from the hand of Midian:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And you are risen up against my father’s house this day, and have killed his sons, seventy and ten persons, upon one stone, and have made Abimelech, the son of his maidservant, king over the men of Shechem, because he is your brother;)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ If you then have dealt truly and sincbeforely with Jerubbaal and with his house this day, then rejoice you in Abimelech, and let him also rejoice in you:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ But if not, let fire come out from Abimelech, and devour the men of Shechem, and the house of Millo: and let fire come out from the men of Shechem, and from the house of Millo, and devour Abimelech.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And Jotham ran away, and fled, and went to Beer, and lived there: for fear of Abimelech his brother.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² When Abimelech had reigned three years over Israel,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Then God sent an bad spirit between Abimelech and the men of Shechem: and the men of Shechem dealt treacherously with Abimelech:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ That the cruelty done to the seventy and ten sons of Jerubbaal might come, and their blood be laid upon Abimelech their brother, that killed them: and upon the men of Shechem, that aided him in the killing of his brothers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And the men of Shechem set those lying in wait for him in the top of the mountains, and they robbed all that came along that way by them: and it was told Abimelech.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ And Gaal the son of Ebed came with his brothers, and went over to Shechem: and the men of Shechem put their confidence in him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ And they went out into the fields, and gathered their vineyards, and trode the grapes, and made merry, and went into the house of their god, and ate and drink, and cursed Abimelech.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And Gaal the son of Ebed said, Who is Abimelech, and who is Shechem, that we should serve him? isn’t he the son of Jerubbaal? and Zebul his officer? serve the men of Hamor the father of Shechem: for why should we serve him?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ And would to God this people were under my hand! then would I remove Abimelech. And he said to Abimelech, Increase your army, and come out.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ And when Zebul the ruler of the city heard the words of Gaal the son of Ebed, his anger was kindled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ And he sent messengers to Abimelech secretly, saying, Here, Gaal the son of Ebed and his brothers be come to Shechem: and here, they fortify the city against you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² And therefore up by night, you and the people that is with you, and lie in wait in the field:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ And it shall be, that in the morning, as soon as the sun is up, you shall rise early, and set upon the city: and here, when he and the people that is with him come out against you, then may you do to them as you shall find occasion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ And Abimelech got up, and all the people that were with him, by night, and they lay in wait against Shechem in four companies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ And Gaal the son of Ebed went out, and stood in the entrance of the gate of the city: and Abimelech got up, and the people that were with him, from lying in wait.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ And when Gaal saw the people, he said to Zebul, Here: there come people down from the top of the mountains. And Zebul said to him, You see the shadow of the mountains as if they were companions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ And Gaal spoke again, and said, See there come people down by the middle of the land, and another company come along by the plain of Meonenim.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:38

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁸ Then said Zebul to him, Where is now your mouth, with that you saidst, Who is Abimelech, that we should serve him? isn’t this the people that you have despised? go out, I pray now, and fight with them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:39

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁹ And Gaal went out before the men of Shechem, and fought with Abimelech.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:40

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁰ And Abimelech chased him, and he fled before him, and many were overthrown and wounded, even to the entrance of the gate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:41

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴¹ And Abimelech lived at Arumah: and Zebul thrust out Gaal and his brothers, that they should not live in Shechem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:42

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴² And it was in the morning, that the people went out into the field: and they told Abimelech.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:43

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴³ And he took the people, and divided them into three companies, and lay in wait in the field, and looked, and here, the people were come forth out of the city: and he got up against them, and struck them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:44

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁴ And Abimelech, and the company that was with him, rushed forward, and stood in the entrance of the gate of the city: and the two other companies ran upon all the people that were in the fields, and killed them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:45

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁵ And Abimelech fought against the city all that day: and he took the city, and killed the people that was in it, and beat down the city, and sowed it with salt.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:46

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁶ And when all the men of the tower of Shechem heard that, they entered into an hold of the house of the god Berith.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:47

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁷ And it was told Abimelech, that all the men of the tower of Shechem were gathered.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:48

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁸ And Abimelech got him up to Mountain Zalmon, he and all the people that were with him: and Abimelech took an axe in his hand, and cut down a bough from the trees, and took it, and laid it on his shoulder, and said to the people that were with him, What you have seen me do, hurry, and do as I’ve done.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:49

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁹ And all the people likewise cut down each man his bough, and followed Abimelech, and put them to the hold, and set the hold on fire upon them; so that all the men of the tower of Shechem died also, about a thousand men and wocompanions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:50

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵⁰ Then went Abimelech to Thebez, and encamped against Thebez, and took it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:51

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵¹ But there was a strong tower within the city, and there fled all the men and women, and all they of the city, and shut it to them, and got them up to the top of the tower.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:52

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵² And Abimelech came to the tower, and fought against it, and went hard to the door of the tower to burn it with fire.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:53

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵³ And a woman cast a piece of a millstone upon Abimelech’s head, and all to broke his skull.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:54

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵⁴ Then he called hastily to the young man his armourbearer, and said to him, Draw your sword, and kill me, that men say not of me, A women killed him. And his young man thrust him through, and he died.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:55

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵⁵ And when the men of Israel saw that Abimelech was dead, they departed each man to his place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:56

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵⁶ Thus God returned the bad thing of Abimelech, that he did to his father, in killing his seventy brothers:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 9:57

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵⁷ And all the bad of the men of Shechem did God render upon their heads: and upon them came the curse of Jotham the son of Jerubbaal.
