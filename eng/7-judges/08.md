------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And the men of Ephraim said to him, What is this thing you have done to us, that you did not call us, when you went to fight with the Midianites? And they pressed him hard.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And he said to them, What have I done now in comparison of you? Is not the gleaning of the grapes of Ephraim better than the vintage of Abiezer?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ God has given into your hands the princes of Midian, Oreb and Zeeb: and what was I able to do in comparison of you? Then their anger was abated toward him, when he had said that.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And Gideon came to Jordan, and passed over, he, and the three hundred men that were with him, faint, yet pursuing them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And he said to the men of Succoth, Give, please, loaves of bread to the people that follow me: for they be faint, and I’m pursuing after Zebah and Zalmunna, kings of Midian.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And the princes of Succoth said, Are the hands of Zebah and Zalmunna now in your hand, that we should give bread to your army?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And Gideon said, Therefore when YHWH has given Zebah and Zalmunna into my hand, then I’ll tear your flesh with the thorns of the wilderness and with briers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And he went up from there to Penuel, and spoke to them likewise: and the men of Penuel answered him as the men of Succoth had answered him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And he spoke also to the men of Penuel, saying, When I come again in peace, I’ll break down this tower.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And Zebah and Zalmunna were in Karkor, and their armies with them, about fifteen thousand men, all that were left of all the armies of the children of the east: for there fell a hundred and twenty thousand men that drew sword.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And Gideon went up by the way of them who lived in tents on the east of Nobah and Jogbehah, and struck the army: for the army was secure.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And when Zebah and Zalmunna fled, he pursued them, and took the two kings of Midian, Zebah and Zalmunna, and discomfited all the army.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And Gideon the son of Joash returned from battle before the sun was up,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And caught a young man of the men of Succoth, and inquired of him: and he described to him the princes of Succoth, and the elders of it, even seventy and seventeen companions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And he came to the men of Succoth, and said, Here Zebah and Zalmunna, with whom you did upbraid me, saying, Are the hands of Zebah and Zalmunna now in your hand, that we should give bread to your men that are weary?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And he took the elders of the city, and thorns of the wilderness and briers, and with them he taught the men of Succoth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And he beat down the tower of Penuel, and killed the men of the city.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Then said he to Zebah and Zalmunna, What manner of men were they whom you killed at Tabor? And they answered, As you are, so were they; each one resembled the children of a king.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And he said, They were my brothers, even the sons of my mother: as YHWH lives, if you had saved them alive, I would not kill you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And he said to Jether his firstborn, Up, and kill them. But the youth drew not his sword: for he feared, because he was yet a youth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ Then Zebah and Zalmunna said, Rise you, and fall upon us: for as the man is, so is his strength. And Gideon arose, and killed Zebah and Zalmunna, and took away the ornaments that were on their camels’ necks.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² Then the men of Israel said to Gideon, Rule you over us, both you, and your son, and your son’s son also: for you have gave us from the hand of Midian.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ And Gideon said to them, I’ll not rule over you, and not shall my son rule over you: YHWH shall rule over you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And Gideon said to them, Let me ask a request of you, that you would give me each man the earrings of his plunder. (For they had golden earrings, because they were Ishmaelites.)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And they answered, We will willingly give them. And they spread a garment, and did cast in it each man the earrings of his plunder.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ And the weight of the golden earrings that he requested was a thousand and seven hundred shekels of gold; beside ornaments, and collars, and purple clothing that was on the kings of Midian, and beside the chains that were about their camels’ necks.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 8:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ And Gideon made it into an ephod and set it in his city, in Ophrah. And all Israel whored after it there, and it became a snare to Gideon and to his house.

---

Footnote 8:27. The ephod becomes a snare after Gideon refuses kingship. Judges often lets the wrong thing happen right after the right sentence.
