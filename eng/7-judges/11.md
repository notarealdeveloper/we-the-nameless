------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And Jephthah the Gileadite was a strong man of force, and he was the son of a whore: and Gilead fathered Jephthah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And Gilead’s wife bore him sons: and his wife’s sons grew up, and they thrust out Jephthah, and said to him, You shall not possess in our father’s house: for you are the son of a foreign woman.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Then Jephthah fled from his brothers, and lived in the land of Tob: and there were gathered empty men to Jephthah, and went out with him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And it was after days, that the children of Ammon made war against Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And it was that when the children of Ammon made war against Israel, the elders of Gilead went to bring Jephthah out of the land of Tob:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And they said to Jephthah, Come, and be our commander, that we may fight with the children of Ammon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And Jephthah said to the elders of Gilead, Did not you hate me, and expel me out of my father’s house? and why are you come to me now when you are in distress?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And the elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, Therefore we turn again to you now, that you may go with us, and fight against the children of Ammon, and be our head over all the inhabitants of Gilead.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And Jephthah said to the elders of Gilead, If you bring me home again to fight against the children of Ammon, and YHWH give them in front of me, shall I be your head?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And the elders of Gilead said to Jephthah, YHWH be witness between us, if we don’t do so according to your words.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Then Jephthah went with the elders of Gilead, and the people made him head and commander over them: and Jephthah uttered all his words before YHWH in Mizpah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And Jephthah sent messengers to the king of the children of Ammon, saying, What is there to me and to you, that you are come against me to fight in my land?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And the king of the children of Ammon answered to the messengers of Jephthah, Because Israel took away my land, when they came up out of Egypt, from Arnon even to Jabbok, and to Jordan: now therefore restore those lands again in peace.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And Jephthah sent messengers again to the king of the children of Ammon:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And said to him, Thus says Jephthah, Israel did not take the land of Moab, and not the land of the children of Ammon:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ But when Israel came up from Egypt, and walked through the wilderness to the Sea of Reeds, and came to Kadesh;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ Then Israel sent messengers to the king of Edom, saying, Let me, please, pass through your land: but the king of Edom would not listen to it. And in like manner they sent to the king of Moab: but he would not consent: and Israel stayed in Kadesh.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Then they went along through the wilderness, and went around the land of Edom, and the land of Moab, and came by the east side of the land of Moab, and pitched on the other side of Arnon, but did not come inside the border of Moab: for Arnon was the border of Moab.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And Israel sent messengers to Sihon king of the Amorite, the king of Heshbon: and Israel said to him, Let us pass, we please, through your land into my place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ But Sihon did not trust Israel to pass through his border: but Sihon gathered all his people together, and pitched in Jahaz, and fought against Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And YHWH God of Israel gave Sihon and all his people in the hand of Israel, and they struck them: so Israel took possession of all the land of the Amorite, the inhabitants of that country.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And they took possession of all the borders of the Amorite, from Arnon even to Jabbok, and from the wilderness even to Jordan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ So now YHWH God of Israel has dispossessed the Amorite from in front of his people Israel, and should you take possession of it?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ Will not you take possession of what Chemosh your god gives you to take possession of? So whoever YHWH our God shall drive out from in front of us, them will we take possession of.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And now are you anything better than Balak the son of Zippor, king of Moab? did he ever strive against Israel, or did he ever fight against them,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ While Israel lived in Heshbon and her towns, and in Aroer and her towns, and in all the cities that be along by the borders of Arnon, three hundred years? why therefore did you not recover them within that time?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ And I have not sinned against you, but you do me wrong to war against me: YHWH the Judge shall judge this day between the children of Israel and the children of Ammon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ But the king of the children of Ammon did not listen to the words of Jephthah that he sent him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ Then spirit of YHWH came upon Jephthah, and he passed over Gilead, and Manasseh, and passed over Mizpah of Gilead, and from Mizpah of Gilead he passed over to the children of Ammon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ And Jephthah vowed a vow to YHWH, and said, If you shall give the children of Ammon into my hands,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ then it shall be: the one who comes out, who comes out from the doors of my house to meet me when I return in peace from the children of Ammon, shall be YHWH’s, and I shall offer him up as a burned offering.

---

Footnote 11:31. Jephthah's vow is worded with terrible openness: whoever comes out. The tragedy is already inside the grammar.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² So Jephthah passed over to the children of Ammon to fight against them: and YHWH gave them into his hands.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ And he struck them from Aroer, as far as your coming to Minnith, even twenty cities, and to the Abel-keramim, with a very great slaughter. Thus the children of Ammon were subdued before the children of Israel.

## Judges 11:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ And Jephthah came to Mizpah to his house, and here, his daughter came out to meet him with tambourines and with dances: and she was his only child; and he had from himself no son or daughter except her.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ And she said to him, My father, if you have opened your mouth to YHWH, do to me according to what has proceeded out of your mouth; because YHWH has taken vengeance for you of your enemies, even of the children of Ammon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:38

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁸ And he said, Go. And he sent her away for two months: and she went with her companions, and wept over her virginity upon the mountains.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 11:39
