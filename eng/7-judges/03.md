## Judges 3:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And these are the nations that YHWH left, to test Israel by them, all who had not known all the wars of Canaan;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Only that the generations of the children of Israel might know, to teach them war, only those who had not known it before;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ five lords of the Philistines, and all the Canaanite, and the Sidonians, and the Hivite who lived in mount Lebright away, from Mount Baal-hermon to the entering in of Hamath.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And they were for testing Israel by them, to know whether they would listen to YHWH’s commandments that he commanded their fathers by the hand of Moses.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And the children of Israel lived among the Canaanite, Hittites, and Amorites, and Perizzites, and Hivites, and Jebusites:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And they took their daughters as wives, and gave their daughters they gave to their sons, and served their gods.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And the children of Israel did what was bad in the eyes of YHWH, and forgot YHWH their God, and served Baals and the Asherahs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Therefore the anger of YHWH was hot against Israel, and he sold them in the hand of Chushan-rishathaim king of Aram-naharaim: and the children of Israel served Chushan-rishathaim eight years.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And when the children of Israel cried to YHWH, YHWH raised up a rescuer to the children of Israel, and he rescued them, even Othniel the son of Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And spirit of YHWH came upon him, and he judged Israel, and went out to war: and YHWH gave Chushan-rishathaim king of Aram-naharaim into his hand: and his hand prevailed against Chushan-rishathaim.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And the land was quiet forty years. And Othniel the son of Kenaz died.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And the children of Israel did what was bad again in the eyes of YHWH: and YHWH strengthened Eglon the king of Moab against Israel, because they had done bad in the eyes of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And he gathered to him the children of Ammon and Amalek, and went and struck Israel, and took possession of the city of palm trees.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ So the children of Israel served Eglon the king of Moab eighteen years.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ But when the children of Israel cried to YHWH, YHWH raised them up a rescuer, Ehud the son of Gera, a Benjamite, a man left-handed: and by him the children of Israel sent a present to Eglon the king of Moab.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ But Ehud made him a dagger that had two edges, of a cubit length: and he girded it under his clothing upon his right thigh.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And he brought the present to Eglon king of Moab: and Eglon was a very fat man.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And when he had finished bringing the present, he sent away the people that bore the present.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ But he himself turned again from the carved images that were by Gilgal, and said, I’ve a secret errand to you, king: who said, Keep silence. And all that stood by him went out from him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And Ehud came to him: and he was sitting in a summer upper room, that he had for himself alone. And Ehud said, I’ve a message from God to you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And Ehud put out his left hand and took the dagger from his right thigh and thrust it into his belly.

---

Footnote 3:21. Ehud's left hand, the locked upper room, the fat closing over the blade: the Hebrew tells the assassination with almost indecent physical calm.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And the handle also went in after the blade: and the fat closed upon the blade, so that he could not draw the dagger out of his belly: and the dirt came out.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Then Ehud went forth through the porch, and shut the doors of the upper room upon him, and locked them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ When he was gone out, his servants came: and when they saw that, here, the doors of the upper room were locked, they said, Surely he is covering his feet in his summer chamber.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 3:26
