------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And the children of Israel did what was bad again in the eyes of YHWH: and YHWH gave them in the hand of the Philistines forty years.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And there was a man of Zorah, of the family of the Danites, whose name was Manoah: and his wife was barren, and had not borne.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And the angel of YHWH appeared to the woman, and said to her, Here now, you are barren, and have borne none: but you shall conceive, and bear a son.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And therefore beware, please, and drink not wine and not strong drink, and eat not any unclean thing:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ For, lo, you shall conceive, and bear a son: and no razor shall come on his head: for the child shall be a Nazirite to God from the womb: and he shall begin to rescue Israel from the hand of the Philistines.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Then the woman came and told her husband, saying, A man of God came to me, and his countenance was like the countenance of an angel of God, very terrible: but I asked him not from where he was, and not told he me his name:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ But he said to me, Here, you shall conceive, and bear a son: and now drink no wine and not strong drink, and not eat any unclean thing: for the child shall be a Nazirite to God from the womb to the day of his death.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Then Manoah intreated YHWH, and said, my Lord, let the man of God that you did send come again to us, and teach us what we shall do to the child that shall be born.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And God listened to the voice of Manoah: and the angel of God came again to the woman as she sat in the field: but Manoah her husband wasn’t with her.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And the woman hurried, and ran, and showed her husband, and said to him, Here: the man appeared to me, who came to me the other day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And Manoah arose, and went after his wife, and came to the man, and said to him, Are you the man that spoke to the woman? And he said, I’m.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And Manoah said, And let your words happened. How shall we order the child, and how shall we do to him?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And the angel of YHWH said to Manoah, Of all that I said to the woman let her beware.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ She may not eat of anything that coms of the vine, and not let her drink wine or strong drink, and not eat any unclean thing: all that I commanded her let her observe.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And Manoah said to the angel of YHWH, please, let us detain you, until we shall have made ready a kid for you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And the angel of YHWH said to Manoah, Yough you detain me, I’ll not eat of your bread: and if you’ll offer a burned offering, you must offer it to YHWH. For Manoah knew not that he was an angel of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And Manoah said to the angel of YHWH, What is your name, that when your sayings happened we may do you honour?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 13:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And the angel of YHWH said to him, Why is this that you ask my name? And it is wonderful.

---

Footnote 13:18. The messenger says his name is wonderful, or beyond knowing. Manoah asks for a label; the scene gives him mystery with fire under it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
