------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 17:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And there was a man of Mountain of Ephraim, whose name was Micah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 17:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And he said to his mother, The eleven hundred shekels of silver that was taken from you, about that you cursed, and spoke of also in my ears, here, the silver is with me; I took it. And his mother said, Blessed be you of YHWH, my son.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 17:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And when he had restored the eleven hundred shekels of silver to his mother, his mother said, I had wholly dedicated the silver to YHWH from my hand for my son, to make a carved image and a cast image: now therefore I’ll restore it to you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 17:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Yet he restored the money to his mother: and his mother took two hundred shekels of silver, and gave them to the founder, who made of it a carved image and a cast image: and they were in the house of Micah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 17:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And the man Micah had a house of gods, and made an ephod, and teraphim, and consecrated one of his sons, who became his priest.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 17:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ In those days there was no king in Israel: each man would do what was straight in his eyes.

---

Footnote 17:6. No king in Israel is not a neutral date-stamp. It is the book's refrain for moral weather.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 17:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And there was a young man out of Bethlehem in Judah of the family of Judah, who was a Levite, and he sojourned there.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 17:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And the man departed out of the city from Bethlehem in Judah to sojourn where he could find a place: and he came to Mountain of Ephraim to the house of Micah, as he journeyed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 17:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And Micah said to him, Whence are you coming? And he said to him, I’m a Levite of Bethlehem in Judah, and I go to sojourn where I may find a place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 17:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And Micah said to him, Live with me, and be to me a father and a priest, and I’ll give you ten shekels of silver for the days, and a array of clothes, and your provisions. So the Levite went in.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 17:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And the Levite was content to live with the man: and the young man was to him as one of his sons.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 17:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And Micah consecrated the Levite: and the young man became his priest, and was in the house of Micah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 17:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And Micah said, Now I know that YHWH will do me good, seeing I’ve a Levite as my priest.
