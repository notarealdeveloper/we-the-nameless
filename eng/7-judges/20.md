## Judges 20:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And the chief of all the people, even of all the tribes of Israel, presented themselves in the assembly of the people of God, four hundred thousand footmen that drew sword.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 20:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And the Levite, the husband of the woman that was killed, answered and said, I came into Gibeah that belongs to Benjamin, I and my concubine, to stay.

## Judges 20:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And the men of Gibeah rose against me, and surrounded the house all around upon me by night, and thought to have killed me: and my concubine have they forced, that she is dead.

## Judges 20:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And I took my concubine, and cut her in pieces, and sent her throughout all the country of the legacy of Israel: for they have committed lewdness and folly in Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 20:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Here: you are all children of Israel; give here your advice and counsel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 20:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And all the people arose as one man, saying, We won’t any of us go to his tent, and not will we any of us turn into his house.

## Judges 20:9
