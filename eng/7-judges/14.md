------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And Samson went down to Timnath, and saw a woman in Timnath of the daughters of the Philistines.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And he came up, and told his father and his mother, and said, I’ve seen a woman in Timnath of the daughters of the Philistines: now therefore get her for me as a wife.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Then his father and his mother said to him, Is there never a woman among the daughters of your brothers, or among all my people, that you go to take a wife of the uncircumcised Philistines? And Samson said to his father, Get her for me: for she pleases me well.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ But his father and his mother knew not that it was of YHWH, that he sought an occasion against the Philistines: for at that time the Philistines had dominion over Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And Samson went down, and his father and his mother, to Timnath, and came to the vineyards of Timnath: and here, a young lion roared against him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And spirit of YHWH came mightily upon him, and he rent him as he would have rent a kid, and he had nothing in his hand: but he told not his father or his mother what he had done.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And he went down, and talked with the woman: and she pleased Samson well.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And after a time he returned to take her, and he turned aside to see the carcase of the lion: and here: there was a swarm of bees and honey in the carcase of the lion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And he took of it in his hands, and went on eating, and came to his father and mother, and he gave them, and they ate: but he told not them that he captured the honey out of the carcase of the lion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ So his father went down to the woman: and Samson made there a feast: for so used the young men to do.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And it was when they saw him, that they brought thirty companions to be with him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And Samson said to them, I’ll now put out a riddle to you: if you can certainly declare it me within the seven days of the feast, and find it out, then I’ll give you thirty sheets and thirty change of garments:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ But if you can’t declare it me, then shall you give me thirty sheets and thirty change of garments. And they said to him, put out your riddle, that we may hear it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And he said to them, From the eater came out food, and from the strong came out sweet. And they could not tell the riddle in three days.

---

Footnote 14:14. Samson's riddle is clever, unfair, and born from a carcass he should not have touched. This is Judges in miniature: wit with flies on it.

## Judges 14:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And it was on the seventh day, that they said to Samson’s wife, Entice your husband, that he may tell us the riddle, lest we burn you and your father’s house with fire: have you called us to dispossess us? Isn’t it so?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And Samson’s wife wept before him, and said, You only hate me, and you don’t love me: you have put out a riddle to the children of my people, and have not told it to me. And he said to her, Here, I’ve not told it to my father and not my mother, and shall I tell it to you?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And she wept before him the seven days, while their feast lasted: and it was on the seventh day, that he told her, because she lay sore upon him: and she told the riddle to the children of her people.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And the men of the city said to him on the seventh day before the sun went down, What is sweeter than honey? And what is stronger than a lion? and he said to them, If you had not plowed with my heifer, you had not found out my riddle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 14:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------
