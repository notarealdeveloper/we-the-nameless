------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And the children of Israel did what was bad in the eyes of YHWH: and YHWH gave them in the hand of Midian seven years.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And the hand of Midian prevailed against Israel: and because of the Midianites the children of Israel made them the dens that are in the mountains, and caves, and strong holds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And so it was, when Israel had sown, that the Midianites came up, and the Amalekites, and the children of the east, even they came up against them;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And they encamped against them, and destroyed the increase of the earth, till you come to Gaza, and left no sustenance for Israel, and not sheep, and not ox, and not ass.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ For they came up with their cattle and their tents, and they came as grasshoppers for multitude: for both they and their camels were without number: and they entered into the land to destroy it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And Israel was greatly impoverished because of the Midianites: and the children of Israel cried to YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And it was when the children of Israel cried to YHWH because of the Midianites,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ That YHWH sent a prophet to the children of Israel, that said to them, Thus says YHWH God of Israel, I brought you up from Egypt, and brought you forth out of the house of slavery;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And I gave you from the hand of the Egyptians, and from the hand of all that oppressed you, and drove them out from in front of you, and gave you their land;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And I said to you, I’m YHWH your God; fear not the gods of the Amorite, in whose land you live: but you have not listened to my voice.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And there came an angel of YHWH, and sat under an oak that was in Ophrah, that pertained to Joash the Abiezrite: and his son Gideon threshed wheat by the winepress, to hide it from the Midianites.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And the angel of YHWH appeared to him and said to him, YHWH is with you, strong man of force.

---

Footnote 6:12. The angel calls Gideon a strong man of force while Gideon is hiding wheat in a winepress. Divine address has a sense of humor, or a long fuse.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And Gideon said to him, Oh my Lord, if YHWH be with us, why then is all this befallen us? and where be all his miracles that our fathers told us of, saying, Did not YHWH bring us up from Egypt? but now YHWH has forsaken us, and gave us in the hands of the Midianites.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And YHWH looked upon him, and said, Go in this your might, and you shall save Israel from the hand of the Midianites: have not I sent you?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And he said to him, Oh my Lord, with that shall I save Israel? here, my family is poor in Manasseh, and I’m the least in my father’s house.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And YHWH said to him, Surely I’ll be with you, and you shall strike the Midianites as one man.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And he said to him, If now I’ve found grace in your sight, then show me a sign that you talk with me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Depart not from here, please, until I come to you, and bring forth my present, and set it before you. And he said, I’ll stay until you come again.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And Gideon went in, and made ready a kid, and unleavened cakes of an ephah of flour: the flesh he put in a basket, and he put the broth in a pot, and brought it out to him under the oak, and presented it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And the angel of God said to him, Take the flesh and the unleavened cakes, and lay them upon this rock, and pour out the broth. And he did so.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ Then the angel of YHWH put out the end of the staff that was in his hand, and touched the flesh and the unleavened cakes: and there got up fire out of the rock, and consumed the flesh and the unleavened cakes. Then the angel of YHWH departed out of his sight.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And when Gideon perceived that he was an angel of YHWH, Gideon said, Ah, YHWH God! for because I’ve seen an angel of YHWH face to face.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ And YHWH said to him, Peace be to you; fear not: you shall not die.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ Then Gideon built an altar there to YHWH, and called it Jehovahshalom: to this day it is yet in Ophrah of the Abiezrites.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And it was the same night, that YHWH said to him, Take your father’s young bullock, even the second bullock of seven years old, and break down the altar of Baal that your father has, and cut down the Asherah that is by it:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ And build an altar to YHWH your God upon the top of this rock, in the ordered place, and take the second bullock, and offer a burned sacrifice with the wood of the Asherah that you shall cut down.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ Then Gideon took ten men of his servants, and did as YHWH had said to him: and so it was, because he feared his father’s household, and the men of the city, that he could not do it by day, that he did it by night.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And when the men of the city arose early in the morning, here, the altar of Baal was cast down, and the Asherah was cut down that was by it, and the second bullock was offered upon the altar that was built.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ And they said one to another, Who has done this thing? And when they inquired and asked, they said, Gideon the son of Joash has done this thing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ Then the men of the city said to Joash, Bring out your son, that he may die: because he has cast down the altar of Baal, and because he has cut down the Asherah that was by it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ And Joash said to all that stood against him, Will you plead for Baal? will you save him? he that will plead for him, let him be put to death whilst it is yet morning: if he be a god, let him plead for himself, because one has cast down his altar.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² Therefore on that day he called him Jerubbaal, saying, Let Baal plead against him, because he has thrown down his altar.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ Then all the Midianites and the Amalekites and the children of the east were gathered, and went over, and pitched in the valley of Jezreel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ But spirit of YHWH came upon Gideon, and he blew a trumpet: and Abiezer was gathered after him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ And he sent messengers throughout all Manasseh; who also was gathered after him: and he sent messengers to Asher, and to Zebulun, and to Naphtali: and they came up to meet them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ And Gideon said to God, If you’ll save Israel by my hand, as you have said,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ Here, I’ll put a fleece of wool in the floor: and if the dew be on the fleece only, and it be dry upon all the earth beside, then shall I know that you’ll save Israel by my hand, as you have said.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:38

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁸ And it was so: for he got up early in the morning, and thrust the fleece together, and wringed the dew out of the fleece, a bowl full of water.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:39

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁹ And Gideon said to God, Let not your anger be hot against me, and I’ll speak but this once: let me prove, please, but this once with the fleece; let it now be dry only upon the fleece, and upon all the ground let there be dew.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 6:40

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁰ And God did so that night: for it was dry upon the fleece only, and there was dew on all the ground.
