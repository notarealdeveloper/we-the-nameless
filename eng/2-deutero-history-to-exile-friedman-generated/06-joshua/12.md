## Joshua 12:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ The following are the local kings whom the Israelites defeated and
whose territories they took possession of: East of the Jordan, from the
Wadi Arnon to Mount Hermon, including the eastern half of the Arabah:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 12:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² King Sihon of the Amorites, who resided in Heshbon and ruled over part
of Gilead—from Aroer on the bank of the Wadi Arnon and the wadi proper
up to the Wadi Jabbok [and] the border of the Ammonites—

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 12:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ and over the eastern Arabah up to the Sea of Chinnereth and, southward
by way of Beth-jeshimoth at the foot of the slopes of Pisgah on the
east, down to the Sea of the Arabah, that is, the Dead Sea.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 12:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Also the territory of King Og of Bashan—one of the last of the
Rephaim—who resided in Ashtaroth and in Edrei

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 12:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ and ruled over Mount Hermon, Salcah, and all of Bashan up to the
border of the Geshurites and the Maacathites, as also over part of
Gilead [down to] the border of King Sihon of Heshbon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 12:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ These were vanquished by Moses, the servant of YHWH, and the
Israelites; and Moses, the servant of YHWH, assigned that territory as a
possession to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of
Manasseh.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 12:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And the following are the local kings whom Joshua and the Israelites
defeated on the west side of the Jordan—from Baal-gad in the Valley of
the Lebanon to Mount Halak, which ascends to Seir—which Joshua assigned
as a possession to the tribal divisions of Israel:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 12:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ in the hill country, in the lowlands, in the Arabah, in the slopes, in
the wilderness, and in the Negeb—[in the land of] the Hittites, the
Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the
Jebusites.
