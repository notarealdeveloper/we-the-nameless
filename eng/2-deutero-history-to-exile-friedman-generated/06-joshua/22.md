Joshua 22:1	Then Joshua summoned the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh,
Joshua 22:2	and said to them, “You have observed all that Moses the servant of YHWH commanded you, and have obeyed me in everything that I commanded you.
Joshua 22:3	You have not forsaken your kinsmen through the long years down to this day, but have faithfully observed the Instruction of YHWH your God.
Joshua 22:4	Now YHWH your God has given your kinsmen rest, as He promised them. Therefore turn and go to your homes, to the land of your holdings beyond the Jordan that Moses the servant of YHWH assigned to you.
Joshua 22:5	But be very careful to fulfill the Instruction and the Teaching that Moses the servant of YHWH enjoined upon you, to love YHWH your God and to walk in all His ways, and to keep His commandments and hold fast to Him, and to serve Him with all your heart and soul.”
Joshua 22:6	Then Joshua blessed them and dismissed them, and they went to their homes.
Joshua 22:7	To the one half-tribe of Manasseh Moses had assigned territory in Bashan, and to the other Joshua assigned [territory] on the west side of the Jordan, with their kinsmen. Furthermore, when Joshua sent them off to their homes, he blessed them
Joshua 22:8	and said to them, “Return to your homes with great wealth—with very much livestock, with silver and gold, with copper and iron, and with a great quantity of clothing. Share the spoil of your enemies with your kinsmen.”
Joshua 22:9	So the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh left the Israelites at Shiloh, in the land of Canaan, and made their way back to the land of Gilead, the land of their own holding, which they had acquired by the command of YHWH through Moses.
Joshua 22:10	When they came to the region of the Jordan in the land of Canaan, the Reubenites and the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manasseh built an altar there by the Jordan, a great conspicuous altar.
Joshua 22:11	A report reached the Israelites: “The Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh have built an altar opposite the land of Canaan, in the region of the Jordan, across from the Israelites.”
Joshua 22:12	When the Israelites heard this, the whole community of the Israelites assembled at Shiloh to make war on them.
Joshua 22:13	But [first] the Israelites sent the priest Phinehas son of Eleazar to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead,
Joshua 22:14	accompanied by ten chieftains, one chieftain from each ancestral house of each of the tribes of Israel; they were every one of them heads of ancestral houses of the contingents of Israel.
Joshua 22:15	When they came to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh in the land of Gilead, they spoke to them as follows:
Joshua 22:16	“Thus said the whole community of YHWH: What is this treachery that you have committed this day against the God of Israel, turning away from YHWH, building yourselves an altar and rebelling this day against YHWH!
Joshua 22:17	Is the sin of Peor, which brought a plague upon the community of YHWH, such a small thing to us? We have not cleansed ourselves from it to this very day;
Joshua 22:18	and now you would turn away from YHWH! If you rebel against YHWH today, tomorrow He will be angry with the whole community of Israel.
Joshua 22:19	If it is because the land of your holding is unclean, cross over into the land of YHWH’s own holding, where the Tabernacle of YHWH abides, and acquire holdings among us. But do not rebel against YHWH, and do not rebel against us by building for yourselves an altar other than the altar of YHWH our God.
Joshua 22:20	When Achan son of Zerah violated the proscription, anger struck the whole community of Israel; he was not the only one who perished for that sin.”
Joshua 22:21	The Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh replied to the heads of the contingents of Israel: They said,
Joshua 22:22	“God, YHWH God! God, YHWH God! He knows, and Israel too shall know! If we acted in rebellion or in treachery against YHWH, do not vindicate us this day!
Joshua 22:23	If we built an altar to turn away from YHWH, if it was to offer burnt offerings or meal offerings upon it, or to present sacrifices of well-being upon it, may YHWH Himself demand [a reckoning].
Joshua 22:24	We did this thing only out of our concern that, in time to come, your children might say to our children, ‘What have you to do with YHWH, the God of Israel?
Joshua 22:25	YHWH has made the Jordan a boundary between you and us, O Reubenites and Gadites; you have no share in YHWH!’ Thus your children might prevent our children from worshiping YHWH.
Joshua 22:26	So we decided to provide [a witness] for ourselves by building an altar—not for burnt offerings or [other] sacrifices,
Joshua 22:27	but as a witness between you and us, and between the generations to come—that we may perform the service of YHWH before Him with our burnt offerings, our sacrifices, and our offerings of well-being; and that your children should not say to our children in time to come, ‘You have no share in YHWH.’
Joshua 22:28	We reasoned: should they speak thus to us and to our children in time to come, we would reply, ‘See the replica of YHWH’s altar, which our fathers made—not for burnt offerings or sacrifices, but as a witness between you and us.’
Joshua 22:29	Far be it from us to rebel against YHWH, or to turn away this day from YHWH and build an altar for burnt offerings, meal offerings, and sacrifices other than the altar of YHWH our God which stands before His Tabernacle.”
Joshua 22:30	When the priest Phinehas and the chieftains of the community—the heads of the contingents of Israel—who were with him heard the explanation given by the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the Manassites, they approved.
Joshua 22:31	The priest Phinehas son of Eleazar said to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the Manassites, “Now we know that YHWH is in our midst, since you have not committed such treachery against YHWH. You have indeed saved the Israelites from punishment by YHWH.”
Joshua 22:32	Then the priest Phinehas son of Eleazar and the chieftains returned from the Reubenites and the Gadites in the land of Gilead to the Israelites in the land of Canaan, and gave them their report.
Joshua 22:33	The Israelites were pleased, and the Israelites praised God; and they spoke no more of going to war against them, to ravage the land in which the Reubenites and Gadites dwelt.
Joshua 22:34	The Reubenites and the Gadites named the altar [“Witness”], meaning, “It is a witness between us and them that YHWH is [our] God.”
