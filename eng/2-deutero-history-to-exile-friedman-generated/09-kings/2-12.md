------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Jehoash was seven years old when he became king.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Jehoash began his reign in the seventh year of Jehu, and he reigned in
Jerusalem forty years. His mother’s name was Zibiah of Beer-sheba.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ All his days Jehoash did what was pleasing to YHWH, as the priest
Jehoiada instructed him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ The shrines, however, were not removed; the people continued to
sacrifice and offer at the shrines.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Jehoash said to the priests, “All the money, current money, brought
into the House of YHWH as sacred donations—any money a man may pay as
the money equivalent of persons, or any other money that a man may be
minded to bring to the House of YHWH—

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ let the priests receive it, each from his benefactor; they, in turn,
shall make repairs on the House, wherever damage may be found.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ But in the twenty-third year of King Jehoash, [it was found that] the
priests had not made the repairs on the House.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ So King Jehoash summoned the priest Jehoiada and the other priests and
said to them, “Why have you not kept the House in repair? Now do not
accept money from your benefactors any more, but have it donated for the
repair of the House.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ The priests agreed that they would neither accept money from the
people nor make repairs on the House.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And the priest Jehoiada took a chest and bored a hole in its lid. He
placed it at the right side of the altar as one entered the House of
YHWH, and the priestly guards of the threshold deposited there all the
money that was brought into the House of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Whenever they saw that there was much money in the chest, the royal
scribe and the high priest would come up and put the money accumulated
in the House of YHWH into bags, and they would count it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Then they would deliver the money that was weighed out to the
overseers of the work, who were in charge of the House of YHWH. These,
in turn, used to pay the carpenters and the laborers who worked on the
House of YHWH,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ and the masons and the stonecutters. They also paid for wood and for
quarried stone with which to make the repairs on the House of YHWH, and
for every other expenditure that had to be made in repairing the House.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ However, no silver bowls and no snuffers, basins, or trumpets—no
vessels of gold or silver—were made at the House of YHWH from the money
brought into the House of YHWH;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ this was given only to the overseers of the work for the repair of
the House of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ No check was kept on the men to whom the money was delivered to pay
the workers; for they dealt honestly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ Money brought as a guilt offering or as a sin offering was not
deposited in the House of YHWH; it went to the priests.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ At that time, King Hazael of Aram came up and attacked Gath and
captured it; and Hazael proceeded to march on Jerusalem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Thereupon King Joash of Judah took all the objects that had been
consecrated by his fathers, Kings Jehoshaphat, Jehoram, and Ahaziah of
Judah, and by himself, and all the gold that there was in the treasuries
of the Temple of YHWH and in the royal palace, and he sent them to King
Hazael of Aram, who then turned back from his march on Jerusalem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ The other events of Joash’s reign, and all his actions, are recorded
in the Annals of the Kings of Judah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ His courtiers formed a conspiracy against Joash and assassinated him
at Beth-millo that leads down to Silla.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 12:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² The courtiers who assassinated him were Jozacar son of Shimeath and
Jehozabad son of Shomer. He died and was buried with his fathers in the
City of David; and his son Amaziah succeeded him as king.
