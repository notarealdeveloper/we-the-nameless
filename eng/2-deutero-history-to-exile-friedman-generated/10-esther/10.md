------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Esther 10:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ King Ahasuerus imposed tribute on the mainland and the islands.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Esther 10:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² All his mighty and powerful acts, and a full account of the greatness
to which the king advanced Mordecai, are recorded in the Annals of the
Kings of Media and Persia.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Esther 10:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ For Mordecai the Jew ranked next to King Ahasuerus and was highly
regarded by the Jews and popular with the multitude of his brethren; he
sought the good of his people and interceded for the welfare of all his
kindred.
