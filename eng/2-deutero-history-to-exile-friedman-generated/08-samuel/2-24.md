II Samuel 24:1	The anger of YHWH again flared up against Israel; and He incited David against them, saying, “Go and number Israel and Judah.”
II Samuel 24:2	The king said to Joab, his army commander, “Make the rounds of all the tribes of Israel, from Dan to Beer-sheba, and take a census of the people, so that I may know the size of the population.”
II Samuel 24:3	Joab answered the king, “May YHWH your God increase the number of the people a hundredfold, while your own eyes see it! But why should my lord king want this?”
II Samuel 24:4	However, the king’s command to Joab and to the officers of the army remained firm; and Joab and the officers of the army set out, at the instance of the king, to take a census of the people of Israel.
II Samuel 24:5	They crossed the Jordan and encamped at Aroer, on the right side of the town, which is in the middle of the wadi of Gad, and [went on] to Jazer.
II Samuel 24:6	They continued to Gilead and to the region of Tahtim-hodshi, and they came to Dan-jaan and around to Sidon.
II Samuel 24:7	They went onto the fortress of Tyre and all the towns of the Hivites and Canaanites, and finished at Beer-sheba in southern Judah.
II Samuel 24:8	They traversed the whole country, and then they came back to Jerusalem at the end of nine months and twenty days.
II Samuel 24:9	Joab reported to the king the number of the people that had been recorded: in Israel there were 800,000 soldiers ready to draw the sword, and the men of Judah numbered 500,000.
II Samuel 24:10	But afterward David reproached himself for having numbered the people. And David said to YHWH, “I have sinned grievously in what I have done. Please, O LORD, remit the guilt of Your servant, for I have acted foolishly.”
II Samuel 24:11	When David rose in the morning, the word of YHWH had come to the prophet Gad, David’s seer:
II Samuel 24:12	“Go and tell David, ‘Thus said YHWH: I hold three things over you; choose one of them, and I will bring it upon you.’”
II Samuel 24:13	Gad came to David and told him; he asked, “Shall a seven-year famine come upon you in the land, or shall you be in flight from your adversaries for three months while they pursue you, or shall there be three days of pestilence in your land? Now consider carefully what reply I shall take back to Him who sent me.”
II Samuel 24:14	David said to Gad, “I am in great distress. Let us fall into the hands of YHWH, for His compassion is great; and let me not fall into the hands of men.”
II Samuel 24:15	YHWH sent a pestilence upon Israel from morning until the set time; and 70,000 of the people died, from Dan to Beer-sheba.
II Samuel 24:16	But when the angel extended his hand against Jerusalem to destroy it, YHWH renounced further punishment and said to the angel who was destroying the people, “Enough! Stay your hand!” The angel of YHWH was then by the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.
II Samuel 24:17	When David saw the angel who was striking down the people, he said to YHWH, “I alone am guilty, I alone have done wrong; but these poor sheep, what have they done? Let Your hand fall upon me and my father’s house!”
II Samuel 24:18	Gad came to David the same day and said to him, “Go and set up an altar to YHWH on the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.”
II Samuel 24:19	David went up, following Gad’s instructions, as YHWH had commanded.
II Samuel 24:20	Araunah looked out and saw the king and his courtiers approaching him. So Araunah went out and bowed low to the king, with his face to the ground.
II Samuel 24:21	And Araunah asked, “Why has my lord the king come to his servant?” David replied, “To buy the threshing floor from you, that I may build an altar to YHWH and that the plague against the people may be checked.”
II Samuel 24:22	And Araunah said to David, “Let my lord the king take it and offer up whatever he sees fit. Here are oxen for a burnt offering, and the threshing boards and the gear of the oxen for wood.
II Samuel 24:23	All this, O king, Araunah gives to Your Majesty. And may YHWH your God,” Araunah added, “respond to you with favor!”
II Samuel 24:24	But the king replied to Araunah, “No, I will buy them from you at a price. I cannot sacrifice to YHWH my God burnt offerings that have cost me nothing.” So David bought the threshing floor and the oxen for fifty shekels of silver.
II Samuel 24:25	And David built there an altar to YHWH and sacrificed burnt offerings and offerings of well-being. YHWH responded to the plea for the land, and the plague against Israel was checked.
