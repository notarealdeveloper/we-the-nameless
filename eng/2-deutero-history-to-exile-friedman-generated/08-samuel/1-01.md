------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ There was a man from Ramathaim of the Zuphites, in the hill country of
Ephraim, whose name was Elkanah son of Jeroham son of Elihu son of Tohu
son of Zuph, an Ephraimite.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² He had two wives, one named Hannah and the other Peninnah; Peninnah
had children, but Hannah was childless.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ This man used to go up from his town every year to worship and to
offer sacrifice to YHWH of Hosts at Shiloh.—Hophni and Phinehas, the two
sons of Eli, were priests of YHWH there.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ One such day, Elkanah offered a sacrifice. He used to give portions to
his wife Peninnah and to all her sons and daughters;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ but to Hannah he would give one portion only—though Hannah was his
favorite—for YHWH had closed her womb.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Moreover, her rival, to make her miserable, would taunt her that YHWH
had closed her womb.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ This happened year after year: Every time she went up to the House of
YHWH, the other would taunt her, so that she wept and would not eat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Her husband Elkanah said to her, “Hannah, why are you crying and why
aren’t you eating? Why are you so sad? Am I not more devoted to you than
ten sons?”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ After they had eaten and drunk at Shiloh, Hannah rose.—The priest Eli
was sitting on the seat near the doorpost of the temple of YHWH.—

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ In her wretchedness, she prayed to YHWH, weeping all the while.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And she made this vow: “O LORD of Hosts, if You will look upon the
suffering of Your maidservant and will remember me and not forget Your
maidservant, and if You will grant Your maidservant a male child, I will
dedicate him to YHWH for all the days of his life; and no razor shall
ever touch his head.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² As she kept on praying before YHWH, Eli watched her mouth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Now Hannah was praying in her heart; only her lips moved, but her
voice could not be heard. So Eli thought she was drunk.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Eli said to her, “How long will you make a drunken spectacle of
yourself? Sober up!”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And Hannah replied, “Oh no, my lord! I am a very unhappy woman. I
have drunk no wine or other strong drink, but I have been pouring out my
heart to YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Do not take your maidservant for a worthless woman; I have only been
speaking all this time out of my great anguish and distress.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ “Then go in peace,” said Eli, “and may the God of Israel grant you
what you have asked of Him.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ She answered, “You are most kind to your handmaid.” So the woman
left, and she ate, and was no longer downcast.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Early next morning they bowed low before YHWH, and they went back
home to Ramah. Elkanah knew his wife Hannah and YHWH remembered her.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ Hannah conceived, and at the turn of the year bore a son. She named
him Samuel, meaning, “I asked YHWH for him.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And when the man Elkanah and all his household were going up to offer
to YHWH the annual sacrifice and his votive sacrifice,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² Hannah did not go up. She said to her husband, “When the child is
weaned, I will bring him. For when he has appeared before YHWH, he must
remain there for good.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Her husband Elkanah said to her, “Do as you think best. Stay home
until you have weaned him. May YHWH fulfill His word.” So the woman
stayed home and nursed her son until she weaned him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ When she had weaned him, she took him up with her, along with three
bulls, one ephah of flour, and a jar of wine. And though the boy was
still very young, she brought him to the House of YHWH at Shiloh.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ After slaughtering the bull, they brought the boy to Eli.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ She said, “Please, my lord! As you live, my lord, I am the woman who
stood here beside you and prayed to YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ It was this boy I prayed for; and YHWH has granted me what I asked of
Him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 1:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ I, in turn, hereby lend him to YHWH. For as long as he lives he is
lent to YHWH.” And they bowed low there before YHWH.
