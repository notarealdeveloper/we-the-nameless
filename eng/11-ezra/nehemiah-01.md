------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 1:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ The words of Nehemiah the son of Hacaliah. And it was in the month Kislev, in year twenty, and I was in Susa the fortress,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 1:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² that Hanani, one of my brothers, came, he and men from Judah. And I asked them about the Jews who had escaped, who were left from the captivity, and about Jerusalem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 1:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And they said to me, The survivors who are left from the captivity there in the province are in a great bad state and in disgrace, and Jerusalem's wall is broken down, and its gates have been burned with fire.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 1:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And it was, when I heard these words, that I sat down and wept and mourned for days, and I was fasting and praying before the God of the skies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 1:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And I said, I ask, YHWH God of the skies, the great and fearsome God, who keeps covenant and loyalty for those who love Him and observe His commandments:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 1:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Let Your ear now be attentive and Your eyes open, to listen to Your servant's prayer, that I pray before You now, day and night, for the children of Israel Your servants, and confessing the sins of the children of Israel, that we have sinned against You. And I and my father's house have sinned.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 1:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ We have dealt corruptly against You, and we have not observed the commandments and the laws and the judgments that You commanded Moses Your servant.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 1:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Remember, please, the word that You commanded Moses Your servant, saying: If you act unfaithfully, I'll scatter you among the peoples.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 1:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And if you return to Me and observe My commandments and do them, if your scattered ones are at the end of the skies, from there I'll gather them and bring them to the place where I chose to make My name live.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 1:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And they are Your servants and Your people, whom You redeemed by Your great power and by Your strong hand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 1:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Please, my Lord, let Your ear be attentive to Your servant's prayer and to the prayer of Your servants who desire to fear Your name. And give success, please, to Your servant today, and give him mercy in front of this man. And I was cupbearer to the king.
