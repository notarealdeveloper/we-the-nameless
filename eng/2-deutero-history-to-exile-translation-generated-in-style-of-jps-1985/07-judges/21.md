Judges 21:1	Now the men of Israel had taken an oath at Mizpah: “None of us will give his daughter in marriage to a Benjaminite.”
Judges 21:2	The people came to Bethel and sat there before God until evening. They wailed and wept bitterly,
Judges 21:3	and they said, “O LORD God of Israel, why has this happened in Israel, that one tribe must now be missing from Israel?”
Judges 21:4	Early the next day, the people built an altar there, and they brought burnt offerings and offerings of well-being.
Judges 21:5	The Israelites asked, “Is there anyone from all the tribes of Israel who failed to come up to the assembly before the LORD?” For a solemn oath had been taken concerning anyone who did not go up to the LORD at Mizpah: “He shall be put to death.”
Judges 21:6	The Israelites now relented toward their kinsmen the Benjaminites, and they said, “This day one tribe has been cut off from Israel!
Judges 21:7	What can we do to provide wives for those who are left, seeing that we have sworn by the LORD not to give any of our daughters to them in marriage?”
Judges 21:8	They inquired, “Is there anyone from the tribes of Israel who did not go up to the LORD at Mizpah?” Now no one from Jabesh-gilead had come to the camp, to the assembly.
Judges 21:9	For, when the roll of the troops was taken, not one of the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead was present.
Judges 21:10	So the assemblage dispatched 12,000 of the warriors, instructing them as follows: “Go and put the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead to the sword, women and children included.
Judges 21:11	This is what you are to do: Proscribe every man, and every woman who has known a man carnally.”
Judges 21:12	They found among the inhabitants of Jabesh-gilead 400 maidens who had not known a man carnally; and they brought them to the camp at Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan.
Judges 21:13	Then the whole community sent word to the Benjaminites who were at the Rock of Rimmon, and offered them terms of peace.
Judges 21:14	Thereupon the Benjaminites returned, and they gave them the girls who had been spared from the women of Jabesh-gilead. But there were not enough of them.
Judges 21:15	Now the people had relented toward Benjamin, for the LORD had made a breach in the tribes of Israel.
Judges 21:16	So the elders of the community asked, “What can we do about wives for those who are left, since the women of Benjamin have been killed off?”
Judges 21:17	For they said, “There must be a saving remnant for Benjamin, that a tribe may not be blotted out of Israel;
Judges 21:18	yet we cannot give them any of our daughters as wives,” since the Israelites had taken an oath: “Cursed be anyone who gives a wife to Benjamin!”
Judges 21:19	They said, “The annual feast of the LORD is now being held at Shiloh.” (It lies north of Bethel, east of the highway that runs from Bethel to Shechem, and south of Lebonah.)
Judges 21:20	So they instructed the Benjaminites as follows: “Go and lie in wait in the vineyards.
Judges 21:21	As soon as you see the girls of Shiloh coming out to join in the dances, come out from the vineyards; let each of you seize a wife from among the girls of Shiloh, and be off for the land of Benjamin.
Judges 21:22	And if their fathers or brothers come to us to complain, we shall say to them, ‘Be generous to them for our sake! We could not provide any of them with a wife on account of the war, and you would have incurred guilt if you yourselves had given them [wives].’”
Judges 21:23	The Benjaminites did so. They took to wife, from the dancers whom they carried off, as many as they themselves numbered. Then they went back to their own territory, and rebuilt their towns and settled in them.
Judges 21:24	Thereupon the Israelites dispersed, each to his own tribe and clan; everyone departed for his own territory.
Judges 21:25	In those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did as he pleased.
