I Kings 18:1	Much later, in the third year, the word of the LORD came to Elijah: “Go, appear before Ahab; then I will send rain upon the earth.”
I Kings 18:2	Thereupon Elijah set out to appear before Ahab. The famine was severe in Samaria.
I Kings 18:3	Ahab had summoned Obadiah, the steward of the palace. (Obadiah revered the LORD greatly.
I Kings 18:4	When Jezebel was killing off the prophets of the LORD, Obadiah had taken a hundred prophets and hidden them, fifty to a cave, and provided them with food and drink.)
I Kings 18:5	And Ahab had said to Obadiah, “Go through the land, to all the springs of water and to all the wadis. Perhaps we shall find some grass to keep horses and mules alive, so that we are not left without beasts.”
I Kings 18:6	They divided the country between them to explore it, Ahab going alone in one direction and Obadiah going alone in another direction.
I Kings 18:7	Obadiah was on the road, when Elijah suddenly confronted him. [Obadiah] recognized him and flung himself on his face, saying, “Is that you, my lord Elijah?”
I Kings 18:8	“Yes, it is I,” he answered. “Go tell your lord: Elijah is here!”
I Kings 18:9	But he said, “What wrong have I done, that you should hand your servant over to Ahab to be killed?
I Kings 18:10	As the LORD your God lives, there is no nation or kingdom to which my lord has not sent to look for you; and when they said, ‘He is not here,’ he made that kingdom or nation swear that you could not be found.
I Kings 18:11	And now you say, ‘Go tell your lord: Elijah is here!’
I Kings 18:12	When I leave you, the spirit of the LORD will carry you off I don’t know where; and when I come and tell Ahab and he does not find you, he will kill me. Yet your servant has revered the LORD from my youth.
I Kings 18:13	My lord has surely been told what I did when Jezebel was killing the prophets of the LORD, how I hid a hundred of the prophets of the LORD, fifty men to a cave, and provided them with food and drink.
I Kings 18:14	And now you say, ‘Go tell your lord: Elijah is here.’ Why, he will kill me!”
I Kings 18:15	Elijah replied, “As the LORD of Hosts lives, whom I serve, I will appear before him this very day.”
I Kings 18:16	Obadiah went to find Ahab, and informed him; and Ahab went to meet Elijah.
I Kings 18:17	When Ahab caught sight of Elijah, Ahab said to him, “Is that you, you troubler of Israel?”
I Kings 18:18	He retorted, “It is not I who have brought trouble on Israel, but you and your father’s House, by forsaking the commandments of the LORD and going after the Baalim.
I Kings 18:19	Now summon all Israel to join me at Mount Carmel, together with the four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal and the four hundred prophets of Asherah, who eat at Jezebel’s table.”
I Kings 18:20	Ahab sent orders to all the Israelites and gathered the prophets at Mount Carmel.
I Kings 18:21	Elijah approached all the people and said, “How long will you keep hopping between two opinions? If the LORD is God, follow Him; and if Baal, follow him!” But the people answered him not a word.
I Kings 18:22	Then Elijah said to the people, “I am the only prophet of the LORD left, while the prophets of Baal are four hundred and fifty men.
I Kings 18:23	Let two young bulls be given to us. Let them choose one bull, cut it up, and lay it on the wood, but let them not apply fire; I will prepare the other bull, and lay it on the wood, and will not apply fire.
I Kings 18:24	You will then invoke your god by name, and I will invoke the LORD by name; and let us agree: the god who responds with fire, that one is God.” And all the people answered, “Very good!”
I Kings 18:25	Elijah said to the prophets of Baal, “Choose one bull and prepare it first, for you are the majority; invoke your god by name, but apply no fire.”
I Kings 18:26	They took the bull that was given them; they prepared it, and invoked Baal by name from morning until noon, shouting, “O Baal, answer us!” But there was no sound, and none who responded; so they performed a hopping dance about the altar that had been set up.
I Kings 18:27	When noon came, Elijah mocked them, saying, “Shout louder! After all, he is a god. But he may be in conversation, he may be detained, or he may be on a journey, or perhaps he is asleep and will wake up.”
I Kings 18:28	So they shouted louder, and gashed themselves with knives and spears, according to their practice, until the blood streamed over them.
I Kings 18:29	When noon passed, they kept raving until the hour of presenting the meal offering. Still there was no sound, and none who responded or heeded.
I Kings 18:30	Then Elijah said to all the people, “Come closer to me”; and all the people came closer to him. He repaired the damaged altar of the LORD.
I Kings 18:31	Then Elijah took twelve stones, corresponding to the number of the tribes of the sons of Jacob—to whom the word of the LORD had come: “Israel shall be your name”—
I Kings 18:32	and with the stones he built an altar in the name of the LORD. Around the altar he made a trench large enough for two seahs of seed.
I Kings 18:33	He laid out the wood, and he cut up the bull and laid it on the wood.
I Kings 18:34	And he said, “Fill four jars with water and pour it over the burnt offering and the wood.” Then he said, “Do it a second time”; and they did it a second time. “Do it a third time,” he said; and they did it a third time.
I Kings 18:35	The water ran down around the altar, and even the trench was filled with water.
I Kings 18:36	When it was time to present the meal offering, the prophet Elijah came forward and said, “O LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel! Let it be known today that You are God in Israel and that I am Your servant, and that I have done all these things at Your bidding.
I Kings 18:37	Answer me, O LORD, answer me, that this people may know that You, O LORD, are God; for You have turned their hearts backward.”
I Kings 18:38	Then fire from the LORD descended and consumed the burnt offering, the wood, the stones, and the earth; and it licked up the water that was in the trench.
I Kings 18:39	When they saw this, all the people flung themselves on their faces and cried out: “The LORD alone is God, The LORD alone is God!”
I Kings 18:40	Then Elijah said to them, “Seize the prophets of Baal, let not a single one of them get away.” They seized them, and Elijah took them down to the Wadi Kishon and slaughtered them there.
I Kings 18:41	Elijah said to Ahab, “Go up, eat and drink, for there is a rumbling of [approaching] rain,”
I Kings 18:42	and Ahab went up to eat and drink. Elijah meanwhile climbed to the top of Mount Carmel, crouched on the ground, and put his face between his knees.
I Kings 18:43	And he said to his servant, “Go up and look toward the Sea.” He went up and looked and reported, “There is nothing.” Seven times [Elijah] said, “Go back,”
I Kings 18:44	and the seventh time, [the servant] reported, “A cloud as small as a man’s hand is rising in the west.” Then [Elijah] said, “Go say to Ahab, ‘Hitch up [your chariot] and go down before the rain stops you.’”
I Kings 18:45	Meanwhile the sky grew black with clouds; there was wind, and a heavy downpour fell; Ahab mounted his chariot and drove off to Jezreel.
I Kings 18:46	The hand of the LORD had come upon Elijah. He tied up his skirts and ran in front of Ahab all the way to Jezreel.
