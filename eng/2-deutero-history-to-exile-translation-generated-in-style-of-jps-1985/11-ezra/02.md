Ezra 2:68	Some of the chiefs of the clans, on arriving at the House of the LORD in Jerusalem, gave a freewill offering to erect the House of God on its site.
Ezra 2:69	In accord with their means, they donated to the treasury of the work: gold—61,000 drachmas, silver—5,000 minas, and priestly robes—100.
Ezra 2:70	The priests, the Levites and some of the people, and the singers, gatekeepers, and the temple servants took up residence in their towns and all Israel in their towns.
