------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Now in the twenty-fourth day of this month the children of Israel were assembled with fasting, and with sackcloth, and earth upon them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And the seed of Israel separated themselves from all foreigners, and stood and confessed their sins, and the guilt of their fathers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And they stood up in their place, and read in the book of the law of YHWH their God one fourth part of the day; and another fourth part they confessed, and worshipped YHWH their God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And stood up upon the stairs, of the Levites, Jeshua, and Bani, Kadmiel, Shebaniah, Bunni, Sherebiah, Bani, and Chenani, and cried with a loud voice to YHWH their God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Then the Levites, Jeshua, and Kadmiel, Bani, Hashabniah, Sherebiah, Hodijah, Shebaniah, and Pethahiah, said, Stand up and bless YHWH your God forever and ever: and blessed be your glorious name, that is exalted above all blessing and praise.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ You, even you, are YHWH alone; you have made heaven, the skies, the skies of skies, with all their host, the earth, and all things that are in it, the seas, and all that is in it, and you preserve them all; and the host of the skies worships you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ You are YHWH the God, who did choose Abram, and brought him forth out of Ur of the Chaldeans, and gave him the name of Abraham;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And found his heart faithful before you, and made a covenant with him to give the land of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Jebusites, and the Girgashites, to give it, I say, to his seed, and have performed your words; for you are righteous:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And did see the bad state of our fathers in Egypt, and heard their cry by the Sea of Reeds;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And showed signs and wonders upon Pharaoh, and on all his servants, and on all the people of his land: for you knew that they dealt proudly against them. So did you make Yourself a name, as it is this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And you did divide the sea before them, so that they went through the midst of the sea on the dry land; and their persecutors you threw into the deeps, as a stone into the mighty waters.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Moreover you led them in the day by a cloudy pillar; and in the night by a pillar of fire, to give them light in the way in that they should go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ You came down also on Mount Sinai, and spoke with them from the skies, and gave them right judgments, and true laws, good laws and commandments:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And made known to them your holy sabbath, and commanded them precepts, laws, and laws, by the hand of Moses your servant:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And gave them bread from the skies for their hunger, and brought forth water for them out of the rock for their thirst, and promised them that they should go in to possess the land that you had sworn to give them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ But they and our fathers dealt proudly, and hardened their necks, and listened not to your commandments,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And refused to obey, nor were mindful of your wonders that you did among them; but hardened their necks, and in their rebellion appointed a commander to return to their slavery: but you are a God ready to forgive, gracious and compassionate, slow to anger, and of great loyalty, and did not forsake them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And when they had made them a molten calf, and said, This is your God that brought you up out of Egypt, and had done great provocations;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Yet you in your many acts of mercy did not forsake them in the wilderness: the pillar of the cloud departed not from them by day, to lead them in the way; nor the pillar of fire by night, to show them light, and the way in that they should go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ You gave also your good spirit to instruct them, and withheld not your manna from their mouth, and gave them water for their thirst.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And forty years did you sustain them in the wilderness, so that they lacked nothing; their clothes did not wear out, and their feet swelled not.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² Moreover you gave them kingdoms and nations, and did divide them into grainers: so they possessed the land of Sihon, and the land of the king of Heshbon, and the land of Og king of Bashan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Their children also multiplied you as the stars of the skies, and brought them into the land, concerning that you had promised to their fathers, that they should go in to possess it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ So the children went in and possessed the land, and you subdued before them the inhabitants of the land, the Canaanites, and gave them into their hands, with their kings, and the people of the land, that they might do with them as they would.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And they took strong cities, and a fat land, and possessed houses full of all goods, wells digged, vineyards, and oliveyards, and fruit trees in abundance: so they did eat, and were filled, and became fat, and delighted themselves in your great goodness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ Nevertheless they were disobedient, and rebelled against you, and cast your law behind their backs, and killed your prophets that testified against them to turn them to you, and they did great provocations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ Therefore you delivered them into the hand of their enemies, who pressed them: and in the time of their trouble, when they cried to you, you heard them from the skies; and according to your many acts of mercy you gave them saviors, who saved them out of the hand of their enemies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ But after they had rest, they did evil again before you: therefore You left them in the land of their enemies, so that they had the dominion over them: yet when they returned, and cried to you, you heard them from the skies; and many times did you deliver them according to your acts of mercy;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ And testified against them, that you might bring them again to your law: yet they dealt proudly, and listened not to your commandments, but sinned against your judgments, (that if a man do, he shall live in them;) and withdrew the shoulder, and hardened their neck, and would not hear.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ Yet many years did you forbear them, and testified against them by your spirit in your prophets: yet would they not give ear: therefore gave you them into the hand of the people of the lands.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ Nevertheless for Your great mercy you did not utterly consume them, nor forsake them; for you are a gracious and compassionate God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² And now, our God, the great, the mighty, and the fearsome God, who keeps covenant and loyalty, let not all the trouble seem little before you, that has come upon us, on our kings, on our chiefs, and on our priests, and on our prophets, and on our fathers, and on all your people, since the time of the kings of Assyria to this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ But you are just in all that is brought upon us; for you have done right, but we have done wickedly:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ Nor have our kings, our chiefs, our priests, nor our fathers, kept your law, nor listened to your commandments and your testimonies, with that you did testify against them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ For they have not served you in their kingdom, and in your great goodness that you gave them, and in the large and fat land that you gave before them, and they did not turn from their wicked works.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ Here, we're servants this day, and for the land that you gave to our fathers to eat the fruit of it and the good of it, here, we're servants in it:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 9:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ And it yields much increase to the kings whom you have set over us because of our sins: also they have dominion over our bodies, and over our cattle, at their pleasure, and we're in great distress.
