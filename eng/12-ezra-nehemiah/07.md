## Ezra 7:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Now after these things, in the reign of Artaxerxes king of Persia, Ezra the son of Seraiah, the son of Azariah, the son of Hilkiah,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² The son of Shallum, the son of Zadok, the son of Ahitub,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ The son of Amariah, the son of Azariah, the son of Meraioth,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ The son of Zerahiah, the son of Uzzi, the son of Bukki,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ The son of Abishua, the son of Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the chief priest:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ This Ezra went up from Babylon; and he was a skilled scribe in the law of Moses, that YHWH God of Israel had given: and the king granted him all his request, according to the hand of YHWH his God upon him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And there went up some of the children of Israel, and of the priests, and the Levites, and the singers, and the gatekeepers, and the Nethinim, to Jerusalem, in the seventh year of Artaxerxes the king.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And he came to Jerusalem in the fifth month, that was in the seventh year of the king.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ For upon the first day of the first month began he to go up from Babylon, and on the first day of the fifth month came he to Jerusalem, according to the good hand of his God upon him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ For Ezra had prepared his heart to seek the law of YHWH, and to do it, and to teach in Israel laws and judgments.

---

Footnote 7:10. Ezra sets his heart to seek, to do, and to teach. The order matters: study, practice, instruction. A scribe who skips the middle verb is just a man with ink.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Now this is the copy of the letter that the king Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe, even a scribe of the words of the commandments of YHWH, and of his laws to Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, a scribe of the law of the God of the skies, complete peace, and so forth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ I make a decree, that all they of the people of Israel, and of his priests and Levites, in my kingdom, that are moved by his own free will to go up to Jerusalem, go with you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Because you are sent of the king, and of his seven counselors, to inquire concerning Judah and Jerusalem, according to the law of your God that is in your hand;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And to carry the silver and gold, that the king and his counselors have freely offered to the God of Israel, whose dwelling is in Jerusalem,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And all the silver and gold that you can find in all the province of Babylon, with the freewill offering of the people, and of the priests, offering willingly for the house of their God that is in Jerusalem:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ That you may buy quickly with this money bulls, rams, lambs, with their food offerings and their drink offerings, and offer them upon the altar of the house of your God that is in Jerusalem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And whatever shall seem good to you, and to your brothers, to do with the rest of the silver and the gold, that do after the will of your God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ The things also that are given you for the service of the house of your God, those deliver you before the God of Jerusalem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And whatever more shall be needed for the house of your God, that you shall have occasion to bestow, bestow it out of the king's treasure house.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And I, even I Artaxerxes the king, do make a decree to all the treasurers that are across the River, that whatever Ezra the priest, the scribe of the law of the God of the skies, shall require of you, it be done quickly,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² To a hundred talents of silver, and to a hundred measures of wheat, and to a hundred baths of wine, and to a hundred baths of oil, and salt without prescribing how much.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Whatever is commanded by the God of the skies, let it be diligently done for the house of the God of the skies: for why should there be wrath against the kingdom of the king and his sons?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ Also we make known to you, that concerning any of the priests and Levites, singers, gatekeepers, Nethinim, or servants of this house of God, it shall not be allowed to impose toll, tribute, or custom, upon them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And you, Ezra, after the wisdom of your God, that is in your hand, set magistrates and judges, that may judge all the people that are across the River, all such as know the laws of your God; and teach you them that know them not.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ And whoever won't do the law of your God, and the law of the king, let judgment be executed quickly upon him, whether it be to death, or to banishment, or to rooting out, or to prison.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ Blessed be YHWH God of our fathers, that has put such a thing as this in the king's heart, to beautify the house of YHWH that is in Jerusalem:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 7:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And has extended mercy to me before the king, and his counselors, and before all the king's mighty chiefs. And I was strengthened as the hand of YHWH my God was upon me, and I gathered together out of Israel chief men to go up with me.
