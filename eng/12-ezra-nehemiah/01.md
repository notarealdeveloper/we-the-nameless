------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 1:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And in the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, to finish YHWH's word from the mouth of Jeremiah, YHWH stirred the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, and he passed a voice through all his kingdom, and also in writing, saying:

---

Footnote 1:1. Cyrus' spirit is stirred by YHWH, and the exiles' spirits are stirred by God in v. 5. Empire and return move by the same verb. The text is not subtle about who has the hand on the switch.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 1:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Thus says Cyrus king of Persia: YHWH, God of the skies, has given me all the kingdoms of the earth, and He has charged me to build Him a house in Jerusalem, that is in Judah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 1:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Who among you is from all His people? May his God be with him, and let him go up to Jerusalem, that is in Judah, and build the house of YHWH, God of Israel: He is the God who is in Jerusalem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 1:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And everyone who remains, from all the places where he lives as an alien, let the men of his place lift him up with silver and with gold and with goods and with animals, besides the freewill offering for the house of God that is in Jerusalem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 1:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And the heads of the fathers of Judah and Benjamin, and the priests and the Levites, got up: everyone whose spirit God had stirred to go up to build the house of YHWH that is in Jerusalem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 1:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And all who were around them strengthened their hands with things of silver, with gold, with goods, and with animals, and with precious things, besides all that was freely offered.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 1:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And King Cyrus brought out the things of the house of YHWH, that Nebuchadnezzar had brought out from Jerusalem and had put in the house of his god.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 1:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And Cyrus king of Persia brought them out by the hand of Mithredath the treasurer, and he counted them out to Sheshbazzar the chieftain of Judah.

---

Footnote 1:8. Sheshbazzar is called the chieftain of Judah. The title keeps the old tribal word inside the Persian administrative world. Bureaucracy has arrived, but the old names haven't left.
