------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 6:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ In the first year of Cyrus the king the that Cyrus the king made a decree concerning the house of God at Jerusalem, Let the house be built, the place where they offered sacrifices, and let the foundations of it be strongly laid; the height of it sixty cubits, and the breadth of it sixty cubits;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 6:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ With three rows of great stones, and a row of new timber: and let the expenses be given out of the king's house:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 6:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And also let the golden and silver things of the house of God, that Nebuchadnezzar took forth out of the temple that is at Jerusalem, and brought to Babylon, be restored, and brought again to the temple that is at Jerusalem, everyone to his place, and place them in the house of God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 6:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And now, Tatnai, governor across the River, Shetharboznai, and your companions the Apharsachites, that are across the River, be you far from there:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 6:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Let the work of this house of God alone; let the governor of the Jews and the elders of the Jews build this house of God in his place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 6:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Moreover I make a decree what you shall do to the elders of these Jews for the building of this house of God: that of the king's goods, even of the tribute across the River, forthwith expenses be given to these men, that they be not hindered.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 6:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And that they have need of, both young bulls, and rams, and lambs, for the burnt offerings of the God of the skies, wheat, salt, wine, and oil, according to the appointment of the priests that are at Jerusalem, let it be given them day by day without fail:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 6:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ That they may offer sacrifices of sweet savours to the God of the skies, and pray for the life of the king, and of his sons.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ezra 6:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Also I have made a decree, that whoever shall change this word, let timber be pulled down from his house, and being set up, let him be hanged on it; and let his house be made a trash heap for this.

## Ezra 6:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And the God that has caused his name to dwell there destroy all kings and people, that shall put to their hand to alter and to destroy this house of God that is at Jerusalem. I Darius have made a decree; let it be done quickly.

## Ezra 6:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Then Tatnai, governor across the river, Shetharboznai, and their companions, according to what Darius the king had sent, so they did quickly.

## Ezra 6:15
