## 1 Samuel 31:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And said Saul to his armor-bearer, Draw your sword, and thrust me through with it: or else these uncircumcised come and thrust me through, and abuse me. But his armor-bearer wouldn't: for he was very afraid. Therefore Saul took a sword, and fell on it.

---

Footnote 31:4. Saul falls on his own sword after fearing what the uncircumcised will do to him. The first royal story ends with Israel's king becoming his own executioner.
