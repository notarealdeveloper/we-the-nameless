## 1 Samuel 26:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And the Ziphites came to Saul to Gibeah, saying, Does not David hide himself in the hill of Hachilah, which is before Jeshimon?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And Saul arose, and went down to the wilderness of Ziph, having three thousand chosen men of Israel with him, to seek David in the wilderness of Ziph.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And Saul pitched in the hill of Hachilah, which is before Jeshimon, by the way. But David abode in the wilderness, and he saw that Saul came after him into the wilderness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ David therefore sent out spies, and understood that Saul had come in very deed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And David arose, and came to the place where Saul had pitched: and David beheld the place where Saul lay, and Abner the son of Ner, the captain of his army: and Saul lay in the trench, and the people pitched round about him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And answered David and said to Ahimelech the Hittite, and to Abishai the son of Zeruiah, brother to Joab, saying, Who will go down with me to Saul to the camp? And Abishai said, I'll go down with you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And David and Abishai came to the people by night: and, here: Saul lay sleeping within the trench, and his spear stuck in the ground at his bolster: but Abner and the people lay round about him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And said Abishai to David, God has delivered your enemy into your hand this day: now therefore let me strike him, please, with the spear even to the earth at once, and I'll not strike him the second time.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And David said to Abishai, Destroy him not: for who can stretch forth his hand against YHWH’s anointed, and be guiltless?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ David said furthermore, As YHWH lives, YHWH shall strike him: or his day shall come to die: or he shall descend into battle, and perish.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ YHWH forbid that I should stretch forth my hand against YHWH’s anointed: but, please, take you now the spear that is at his bolster, and the cruse of water, and let us go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And David took the spear and the cruse of water from Saul’s bolster: and they got them away, and no man saw it, nor knew it, neither awaked: for they were all asleep: because a deep sleep from YHWH was fallen on them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And David went over to the other side, and stood on the top of an hill afar off: a great space being between them:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And David cried to the people, and to Abner the son of Ner, saying, Do you answer you not, Abner? Then Abner answered and said, Who are you that cry to the king?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And David said to Abner, Are not you a valiant man? and who is like to you in Israel? why then have you not kept your lord the king? for there came one of the people in to destroy the king your lord.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ This thing is not good that you've done. As YHWH lives, you're worthy to die, because you've not kept your master, YHWH’s anointed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And Saul knew David’s voice, and said, Is this your voice, my son David? And David said, It is my voice, my lord, O king.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And he said, And so does my lord thus pursue after his servant? for what have I done? or what bad is in my hand?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And therefore, please, let my lord the king hear the words of his servant. If YHWH have stirred you up against me, let him accept an offering: but if they be the children of men, cursed be they before YHWH: for they've driven me out this day from abiding in the legacy of YHWH, saying, Go, serve other gods.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 26:20

²⁰ And therefore, let not my blood fall to the earth before the face of YHWH: for the king of Israel has come out to seek a flea, as when one does hunt a partridge in the mountains.

## 1 Samuel 26:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And said Saul, I've sinned: return, my son David: for I'll no more do you harm, because my life was precious in your eyes this day: here: I've played the fool, and have erred exceedingly.

## 1 Samuel 26:22

## 1 Samuel 26:23
