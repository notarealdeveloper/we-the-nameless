------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Job 42:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Up spoke Job to YHWH and he said:


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Job 42:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

I have known you are able to do all;

That you cannot be blocked from any scheme.

---

Footnote 42:2. Job speaks of the deity in the disdainful terms that God had spoken of the builders of the Tower of Babel (Genesis 11:6).


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Job 42:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

“Who is this hiding counsel without knowledge?”

Truly I’ve spoken without comprehending—

Wonders beyond me that I do not know.

---

Footnote 42:3. If a similar Arabic verb is evoked, there is a double entendre: “make known.”

Footnote 42:3. Job mimics the deity in 38:2.

Footnote 42:3. A mock concession.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Job 42:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

“Hear now and I will speak!

I will ask you, and you help me know!”

---

Footnote 42:4. Job mimics the deity in 38:3; 40:7.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Job 42:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

As a hearing by the ear I have heard you,

And now my eye has seen you.

---

Footnote 42:5. A mock concession; Job has already said this (13:1).


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Job 42:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

That is why I am fed up;

I take pity on “dust and ashes!”

---

Footnote 42:6. For *ma’as* used intransitively in the sense of being “fed up,” see 7:16.

Footnote 42:6. See for example 2 Samuel 13:39; Psalm 90:13.

Footnote 42:6. “Dust and ashes” is a figure for wretched humanity; see its two other occurrences in Genesis 18:27 and Job 30:19.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Job 42:7–8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

It happened, after YHWH spoke these words to Job, that YHWH said to Eliphaz the Teimanite:

“I am angry at you and your two companions,

for you did not speak about me in honesty as did my servant Job.

Now then, take yourselves seven bulls and seven rams and go to my servant Job,

and offer up a burnt-offering on your own behalf;

and my servant Job will pray for you;

for I will lift up his face without doing anything unseemly to you—

for you did not speak about me in honesty as did my servant Job.”

---

Footnote 42:7–8. The latecomer Elihu is not included; see the introduction to his discourses.

Footnote 42:7–8. For the preposition *’el* in the sense of *‘al*, see for example Jeremiah 40:16.

Footnote 42:7–8. Compare *ken* “honest, truthful” in Genesis 41:32. The friends relied on traditional wisdom instead of the knowledge that is learned from experience (including revelation).


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Job 42:9–10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

So they went, Eliphaz the Teimanite and Bildad the Shuhite and Zophar the Na’amathite, and they did as YHWH had spoken to them;

And YHWH lifted up Job’s face.

For YHWH had restored Job’s restoration when he had prayed for his companions.

YHWH added double to what Job had had.

---

Footnote 42:9–10. The conjunction is missing in the Hebrew, but it should be restored.

Footnote 42:9–10. A twofold restoration is required of thieves (Exodus 22:3). It may be inferred that the removal of Job’s property was a wrongful act. Or perhaps it is for consolation.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Job 42:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Then they came to him, all his brothers and all his sisters and all his friends from beforehand,

and they ate a meal with him in his house,

and they shook-their-heads-in-pity and they consoled him for all the evil that YHWH had brought upon him;

and they each gave him one chip-of-silver and one gold ring.

---

Footnote 42:11. An archaic unit of payment (see Genesis 33:19). It may be derived from a verb meaning “to chip off,” like *qashata* in Arabic (compare *qushata* “chip”).

Footnote 42:11. A ring for the ear or nose.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Job 42:12–15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

So YHWH blessed Job’s latter-life more than his former-life.

He came to have fourteen thousand small cattle and six thousand camels and a thousand yoke of large cattle and a thousand she-asses.

And he came to have seven sons and three daughters.

He called the name of the first Yemima/Turtle-Dove and the name of the second Ketsiah/Cassia and the name of the third Keren Ha-Pukh/Horn of Kohl.

One could not find women as fair as Job’s daughters in all the land;

and he, their father, gave them an inheritance in the midst of their brothers.

---

Footnote 42:12–15. Mascara; cosmetics were kept in a horn.

Footnote 42:12–15. Like that of their brothers (Machinist); see Numbers 27:7.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Job 42:16–17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

After this Job lived one hundred and forty years.

He saw his sons and his sons’ sons—four generations.

Job died old and sated with days.

---

Footnote 42:16–17. Like a patriarch; see Genesis 50:23.

Footnote 42:16–17. Like a patriarch; see Genesis 35:29.
