------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Job 25:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Up spoke Bildad the Shuhite and he said:


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Job 25:2–3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Dominion and dread are his.

He makes peace in his heights.

Is there a number to his celestial troops?

On whom does his daylight not rise?

---

Footnote 25:2–3. A figure for the stars; see Judges 5:20; Isaiah 40:26.

Footnote 25:2–3. An ironic contrast to the “daylight,” which is night, to which the murderer “rises” in Job 24:14; compare 38:15.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Job 25:4–6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

“How can a mortal be righteous before El?

How can a man born of woman be innocent?

If the moon cannot shine brightly enough,

And the stars are not pure in his eyes,

Then all the less a mortal, a maggot;

(All the less) a son of a human, a worm!”

---

Footnote 25:4–6. Compare Job 4:17; 15:14.

Footnote 25:4–6. Literally, “be pure, clean.” Compare Job 15:14.

Footnote 25:4–6. The *aleph* only indicates a vowel; the form is a *Hiph‘il* of *h-l-l*.

Footnote 25:4–6. Compare Job 15:15.

Footnote 25:4–6. Compare Job 4:19, where the human is merely one born in dust, not a worm.
