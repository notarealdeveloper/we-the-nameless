------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ In the seventh year of Jehu Jehoash began to reign: and forty years reigned he in Jerusalem. And his mother's name was Zibiah of Beersheba.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And Jehoash did that that was right in the eyes of YHWH all his days wherein Jehoiada the priest instructed him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ But the high places were not taken away: the people still sacrificed and burned incense in the high places.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And Jehoash said to the priests, All the money of the dedicated things that is brought into the house of YHWH, even the money of every one that passes the account, the money that every man is set at, and all the money that comes into any man's heart to bring into the house of YHWH,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Let the priests take it to them, every man of his acquaintance: and let them repair the breaches of the house, wheresoever any breach shall be found.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ But it was so, that in the three and twentieth year of king Jehoash the priests had not repaired the breaches of the house.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And king Jehoash called for Jehoiada the priest, and the other priests, and said to them, Why repair you not the breaches of the house? now therefore receive no more money of your acquaintance, but deliver it for the breaches of the house.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And the priests consented to receive no more money of the people, neither to repair the breaches of the house.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ But Jehoiada the priest took a chest, and bored a hole in the lid of it, and set it beside the altar, on the right side as one comes into the house of YHWH: and the priests that kept the door put in it all the money that was brought into the house of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And it was so, when they saw that there was much money in the chest, that the king's scribe and the high priest came up, and they put up in bags, and told the money that was found in the house of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And they gave the money, being told, into the hands of them that did the work, that had the oversight of the house of YHWH: and they laid it out to the carpenters and builders, who worked on the house of YHWH,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And to masons, and hewers of stone, and to buy timber and hewed stone to repair the breaches of the house of YHWH, and for all that was laid out for the house to repair it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Only there were not made for the house of YHWH bowls of silver, snuffers, basons, trumpets, any vessels of gold, or vessels of silver, of the money that was brought into the house of YHWH:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ But they gave that to the workmen, and repaired with it the house of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And they reckoned not with the men, into whose hand they delivered the money to be bestowed on workmen: for they dealt faithfully.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ The trespass money and sin money was not brought into the house of YHWH: it was the priests'.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And Hazael king of Aram went up, and fought against Gath, and took it: and Hazael set his face to go up to Jerusalem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And Jehoash king of Judah took all the hallowed things that Jehoshaphat, and Jehoram, and Ahaziah, his fathers, kings of Judah, had dedicated, and his own hallowed things, and all the gold that was found in the treasures of the house of YHWH, and in the king's house, and sent it to Hazael king of Aram: and he went away from Jerusalem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And the rest of the acts of Joash, and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And his servants arose, and made a conspiracy, and killed Joash in the house of Millo, that goes down to Silla.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 12:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ For Jozachar the son of Shimeath, and Jehozabad the son of Shomer, his servants, struck him, and he died: and they buried him with his fathers in the city of David: and Amaziah his son reigned in his place.
