## 2 Kings 2:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And it was, when they were gone over, that Elijah said to Elisha, Ask what I'll do for you, before I am taken away from you. And Elisha said, please, let a double portion of your spirit be on me.

---

Footnote 2:9. A double mouthful is inheritance language. Elisha is not asking for twice Elijah’s power so much as the firstborn share of his spirit.

## 2 Kings 2:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ And he went up from there to Bethel: and as he was going up by the way, there came out small boys out of the city, and mocked him, and said to him, Go up, you baldhead: go up, you baldhead.

---

Footnote 2:23. “Go up, baldhead” is not playground teasing in a vacuum. The verb “go up” has just carried Elijah out of the story; the boys are mocking prophetic departure and Elisha’s succession. Still: bears. The text is not housebroken.
