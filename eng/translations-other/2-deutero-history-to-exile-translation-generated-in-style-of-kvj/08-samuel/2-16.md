## 2 Samuel 16:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And when David was a little past the top of the hill, here: Ziba the servant of Mephibosheth met him, with a couple of asses saddled, and on them two hundred loaves of bread, and a hundred bunches of raisins, and a hundred summer fruits, and a bottle of wine.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 16:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And the king said to Ziba, What are these to you? And Ziba said, The asses be for the king’s household to ride on; and the bread and summer fruit for the young men to eat; and the wine, that such as be faint in the wilderness may drink.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 16:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And the king said, And where is your master’s son? And Ziba said to the king, Here: he stays at Jerusalem: for he said, Today shall the house of Israel restore me the kingdom of my father.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 16:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And the king said to Ziba, Here: all that belonged to Mephibosheth is yours. And Ziba said, Please let me find favor in your eyes, my lord, O king.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 16:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And when king David came to Bahurim, here: from there came out a man of the family of the house of Saul, whose name was Shimei, the son of Gera: he came forth, and cursed still as he came.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 16:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And he cast stones at David, and at all the servants of king David: and all the people and all the heroes were on his right hand and on his left.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 16:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And thus said Shimei when he cursed, Come out, come out, you bloody man, and you man of Belial:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 16:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ YHWH has returned on you all the blood of the house of Saul, in whose stead you have reigned; and YHWH has delivered the kingdom into the hand of Absalom your son: and, here: you are taken in your mischief, because you are a bloody man.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 16:9
