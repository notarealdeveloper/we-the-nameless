## 2 Samuel 1:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And it was after the death of Saul, when David was returned from the slaughter of the Amalekites, and David had stayed two days in Ziklag;

## 2 Samuel 1:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And it was on the third day: here: a man came from the camp from Saul with his clothes torn, and earth on his head: and so it was, when he came to David, that he fell to the earth, and bowed down.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 1:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And David said to him, From where do you come? And he said to him, I escaped from the camp of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 1:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And David said to him, What was the thing? please, tell me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 1:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And David said to the young man that told him, How do you know that Saul and Jonathan his son are dead?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 1:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And the young man that told him said, As I happened by chance on mount Gilboa, here: Saul leaned on his spear; and, here: the chariots and horsemen followed hard after him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 1:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And when he looked behind him, he saw me, and called to me. And I answered, Here am I.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 1:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And he said to me, Who are you? And I answered him, I'm an Amalekite.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 1:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ He said to me again, Stand, please, on me, and kill me: for anguish has seized me, because my life is still in me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 1:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ So I stood on him, and killed him, because I was secure that he couldn't live after he had fallen: and I took the crown that was on his head, and the bracelet that was on his arm, and I have brought them here to my lord.

## 2 Samuel 1:11

¹¹ And David held his clothes and tore them; and likewise all the men that were with him:

## 2 Samuel 1:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And they mourned, and wept, and fasted until evening, for Saul, and for Jonathan his son, and for the people of YHWH, and for the house of Israel; because they were fallen by the sword.

## 2 Samuel 1:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And David said to the young man that told him, From where are you? And he answered, I'm the son of a stranger, an Amalekite.

## 2 Samuel 1:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And David said to him, How were you not afraid to send out your hand to destroy YHWH's anointed?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 1:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And David called one of the young men, and said, Go near, and fall on him. And he struck him that he died.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Samuel 1:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And David said to him, Your blood be on your head; for your mouth has testified against you, saying, I've killed YHWH's anointed.

---

Footnote 1:16. David takes the Amalekite's story at its mouth: his own mouth testifies against him. The word for blood stays concrete: blood on your head.
