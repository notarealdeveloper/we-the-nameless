## 1 Samuel 28:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And the king said to her, Be not afraid: for what saw you? And the woman said to Saul, I saw gods ascending out of the earth.

---

Footnote 28:13. The woman says she sees gods coming up from the earth. Whatever one thinks happened at Endor, the text lets the dead rise in plural grammar before Samuel speaks in singular doom.
