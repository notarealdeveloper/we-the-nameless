------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And Joshua called the Reubenites, and the Gadites, and the half tribe of Manasseh,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And said to them, You have kept all that Moses the servant of YHWH commanded you, and have obeyed my voice in all that I commanded you:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ You have not left your brothers these many days to this day, but have kept the charge of the commandment of YHWH your God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And now YHWH your God has given rest to your brothers, as he promised them: therefore now return you, and get you to your tents, and to the land of your possession, that Moses the servant of YHWH gave you across the Jordan.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ But take diligent heed to do the commandment and the law, that Moses the servant of YHWH charged you, to love YHWH your God, and to walk in all his ways, and to keep his commandments, and to cling to him, and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And Joshua blessed them, and sent them away: and they went to their tents.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Now to the one half of the tribe of Manasseh Moses had given possession in Bashan: but to the other half of it gave Joshua among their brothers across the Jordan westward. And when Joshua sent them away also to their tents, then he blessed them,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And he spoke to them, saying, Return with much riches to your tents, and with very much cattle, with silver, and with gold, and with brass, and with iron, and with very much clothing: divide the spoil of your enemies with your brothers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the half tribe of Manasseh returned, and departed from the children of Israel out of Shiloh, that is in the land of Canaan, to go to the country of Gilead, to the land of their possession, of that they were possessed, according to the word of YHWH by the hand of Moses.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And when they came to the borders of Jordan, that are in the land of Canaan, the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the half tribe of Manasseh built there an altar by Jordan, a great altar to see to.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And the children of Israel heard say, Here, the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the half tribe of Manasseh have built an altar over against the land of Canaan, in the borders of Jordan, at the passage of the children of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And when the children of Israel heard of it, the whole congregation of the children of Israel gathered themselves together at Shiloh, to go up to war against them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And the children of Israel sent to the children of Reuben, and to the children of Gad, and to the half tribe of Manasseh, into the land of Gilead, Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And with him ten chieftains, of each chief house a chieftain throughout all the tribes of Israel; and each one was a head of the house of their fathers among the thousands of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And they came to the children of Reuben, and to the children of Gad, and to the half tribe of Manasseh, to the land of Gilead, and they spoke with them, saying,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Thus says the whole congregation of YHWH, What trespass is this that you have committed against the God of Israel, to turn away this day from following YHWH, in that you have built you an altar, that you might rebel this day against YHWH?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ Is the guilt of Peor too little for us, from which we are not cleansed until this day, although there was a plague in the congregation of YHWH,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ But that you must turn away this day from following YHWH? and it will be, seeing you rebel to day against YHWH, that to next day he will be angry with the whole congregation of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Notwithstanding, if the land of your possession be unclean, then pass you over to the land of the possession of YHWH, in that YHWH’s tabernacle lives, and take possession among us: but rebel not against YHWH, nor rebel against us, in building you an altar beside the altar of YHWH our God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ Did not Achan the son of Zerah commit a trespass in the banned thing, and wrath fell on all the congregation of Israel? and that man perished not alone in his guilt.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the half tribe of Manasseh answered, and said to the heads of the thousands of Israel,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² YHWH, God of gods, YHWH, God of gods, He knows, and Israel he shall know; if it be in rebellion, or if in transgression against YHWH, (don’t save us this day,)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ That we have built us an altar to turn from following YHWH, or if to offer on it burnt offering or meat offering, or if to offer peace offerings on it, let YHWH himself require it;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And if we have not rather done it for fear of this thing, saying, In time to come your children might speak to our children, saying, What have you to do with YHWH God of Israel?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ For YHWH has made Jordan a border between us and you, you children of Reuben and children of Gad; you have no part in YHWH: so shall your children make our children cease from fearing YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ Therefore we said, Let us now prepare to build us an altar, not for burnt offering, nor for sacrifice:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ But that it may be a witness between us, and you, and our generations after us, that we might do the service of YHWH before him with our burnt offerings, and with our sacrifices, and with our peace offerings; that your children may not say to our children in time to come, You have no part in YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ Therefore said we, that it shall be, when they should so say to us or to our generations in time to come, that we may say again, Here the pattern of the altar of YHWH, that our fathers made, not for burnt offerings, nor for sacrifices; but it is a witness between us and you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ Far be it that we should rebel against YHWH, and turn this day from following YHWH, to build an altar for burnt offerings, for meat offerings, or for sacrifices, beside the altar of YHWH our God that is before his tabernacle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ And when Phinehas the priest, and the chieftains of the congregation and heads of the thousands of Israel that were with him, heard the words that the children of Reuben and the children of Gad and the children of Manasseh spoke, it pleased them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ And Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest said to the children of Reuben, and to the children of Gad, and to the children of Manasseh, This day we perceive that YHWH is among us, because you have not committed this trespass against YHWH: now you have delivered the children of Israel out of the hand of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² And Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest, and the chieftains, returned from the children of Reuben, and from the children of Gad, out of the land of Gilead, to the land of Canaan, to the children of Israel, and brought them word again.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ And the thing pleased the children of Israel; and the children of Israel blessed God, and did not intend to go up against them in battle, to destroy the land in that the children of Reuben and Gad lived.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 22:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ And the children of Reuben and the children of Gad called the altar Ed: for it shall be a witness between us that YHWH is God.
