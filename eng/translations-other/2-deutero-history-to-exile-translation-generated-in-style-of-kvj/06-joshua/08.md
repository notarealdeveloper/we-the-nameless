------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And YHWH said to Joshua, Don’t fear, and don’t be shattered: take all the people of war with you, and arise, go up to Ai: see, I've given into your hand the king of Ai, and his people, and his city, and his land:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And you shall do to Ai and her king as you did to Jericho and her king: only the spoil of it, and the cattle of it, shall you take for a prey to yourselves: lay you an ambush for the city behind it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And Joshua arose, and all the people of war, to go up against Ai: and Joshua chose out thirty thousand strong men of force, and sent them away by night.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And he commanded them, saying, Here, you shall lie in wait against the city, even behind the city: don’t go very far from the city, but be you all ready:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And I, and all the people that are with me, will approach to the city: and it shall come to pass, when they come out against us, as at the first, that we'll flee before them,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ (For they will come out after us) until we have drawn them from the city; for they will say, They flee before us, as at the first: therefore we'll flee before them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And you shall rise up from the ambush, and seize on the city: for YHWH your God will deliver it into your hand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And it shall be, when you have taken the city, that you shall set the city on fire: according to the commandment of YHWH shall you do. See, I have commanded you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Joshua therefore sent them forth: and they went to lie in ambush, and stayed between Bethel and Ai, on the west side of Ai: but Joshua lodged that night among the people.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And Joshua rose up early in the morning, and numbered the people, and went up, he and the elders of Israel, before the people to Ai.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And all the people, even the people of war that were with him, went up, and came near, and came before the city, and camped on the north side of Ai: now there was a valley between them and Ai.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And he took about five thousand men, and set them to lie in ambush between Bethel and Ai, on the west side of the city.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And when they had set the people, even all the army that was on the north of the city, and their liers in wait on the west of the city, Joshua went that night into the midst of the valley.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And it was, when the king of Ai saw it, that they hasted and rose up early, and the men of the city went out against Israel to battle, he and all his people, at a time appointed, before the plain; but he wist not that there were liers in ambush against him behind the city.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And Joshua and all Israel made as if they were beaten before them, and fled by the way of the wilderness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And all the people that were in Ai were called together to pursue after them: and they pursued after Joshua, and were drawn away from the city.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And there was not a man left in Ai or Bethel, that went not out after Israel: and they left the city open, and pursued after Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And YHWH said to Joshua, Stretch out the spear that is in your hand toward Ai; for I'll give it into your hand. And Joshua stretched out the spear that he had in his hand toward the city.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And the ambush arose quickly out of their place, and they ran as soon as he had stretched out his hand: and they entered into the city, and took it, and hasted and set the city on fire.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And when the men of Ai looked behind them, they saw, and, here, the smoke of the city ascended up to heaven, and they had no power to flee this way or that way: and the people that fled to the wilderness turned back on the pursuers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And when Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambush had taken the city, and that the smoke of the city ascended, then they turned again, and struck the men of Ai.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And the other issued out of the city against them; so they were in the midst of Israel, some on this side, and some on that side: and they struck them, so that they let none of them stay or escape.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ And the king of Ai they took alive, and brought him to Joshua.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And it was, when Israel had made an end of slaying all the inhabitants of Ai in the field, in the wilderness in that they chased them, and when they were all fallen on the mouth of the sword, until they were consumed, that all the Israelites returned to Ai, and struck it by the mouth of the sword.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And so it was, that all that fell that day, both of men and women, were twelve thousand, even all the men of Ai.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ For Joshua came not his hand back, with that he stretched out the spear, until he had put all under the ban the inhabitants of Ai.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ Only the cattle and the spoil of that city Israel took for a prey to themselves, according to the word of YHWH that he commanded Joshua.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And Joshua burnt Ai, and made it an heap for ever, even a desolation to this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ And the king of Ai he hanged on a tree until evening: and as soon as the sun was down, Joshua commanded that they should take his body down from the tree, and cast it at the entering of the gate of the city, and raise on it a great heap of stones, that remains to this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ And Joshua built an altar to YHWH God of Israel in mount Ebal,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ As Moses the servant of YHWH commanded the children of Israel, as it is written in the book of the law of Moses, an altar of whole stones, over that no man has lift up any iron: and they offered on it burnt offerings to YHWH, and sacrificed peace offerings.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² And he wrote there on the stones a copy of the law of Moses, that he wrote in the presence of the children of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ And all Israel, and their elders, and officers, and their judges, stood on this side the ark and on that side before the priests the Levites, that bare the ark of the covenant of YHWH, as well the stranger, as he that was born among them; half of them over against mount Gerizim, and half of them over against mount Ebal; as Moses the servant of YHWH had commanded before, that they should bless the people of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ And afterward he read all the words of the law, the blessings and cursings, according to all that is written in the book of the law.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 8:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ There was not a word of all that Moses commanded, that Joshua read not before all the congregation of Israel, with the women, and the little ones, and the strangers that were conversant among them.
