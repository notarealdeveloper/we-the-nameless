------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And it was a long time after that YHWH had given rest to Israel from all their enemies round about, that Joshua grew old and stricken in age.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And Joshua called for all Israel, and for their elders, and for their heads, and for their judges, and for their officers, and said to them, I am old and stricken in age:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And you have seen all that YHWH your God has done to all these nations because of you; for YHWH your God is he that has fought for you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Here, I have divided to you by lot these nations that stay, to be an inheritance for your tribes, from Jordan, with all the nations that I have cut off, even to the great sea westward.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And YHWH your God, He shall expel them from before you, and drive them from out of your sight; and you shall possess their land, as YHWH your God has promised to you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Be you therefore very courageous to keep and to do all that is written in the book of the law of Moses, that you turn not aside from it to the right hand or to the left;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ That you come not among these nations, these that stay among you; neither make mention of the name of their gods, nor cause to swear by them, neither serve them, nor bow yourselves to them:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ But cling to YHWH your God, as you have done to this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ For YHWH has driven out from before you great nations and strong: but as for you, no man has been able to stand before you to this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ One man of you shall chase a thousand: for YHWH your God, He is the one that fights for you, as He has promised you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Take good heed therefore to yourselves, that you love YHWH your God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Else if you do just go back, and cling to the remnant of these nations, even these that stay among you, and shall make marriages with them, and go in to them, and they to you:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Know for a certainty that YHWH your God will no more drive out any of these nations from before you; but they shall be snares and traps to you, and scourges in your sides, and thorns in your eyes, until you perish from off this good land that YHWH your God has given you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And, here, this day I am going the way of all the earth: and you know in all your hearts and in all your souls, that not one thing has failed of all the good things that YHWH your God spoke concerning you; all are come to pass to you, and not one thing has failed of it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Therefore it shall come to pass, that as all good things are come on you, that YHWH your God promised you; so shall YHWH bring on you all evil things, until he have destroyed you from off this good land that YHWH your God has given you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Joshua 23:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ When you have transgressed the covenant of YHWH your God, that he commanded you, and have gone and served other gods, and bowed yourselves to them; then shall the anger of YHWH be kindled against you, and you shall perish quickly from off the good land that he has given to you.
