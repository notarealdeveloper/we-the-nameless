------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And it was after Joshua’s death that the children of Israel inquired of YHWH, saying, Who shall go up for us first against the Canaanite, to fight against him?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And YHWH said, Judah shall go up. Here: I’ve given the land in his hand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And Judah said to Simeon his brother, Come up with me into my lot, that we may fight against the Canaanite: and I likewise will go with you into your lot. So Simeon went with him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And Judah went up: and YHWH gave the Canaanite and the Perizzite into their hand: and they killed of them in Bezek ten thousand companions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And they found Adonibezek in Bezek: and they fought against him, and they killed the Canaanite and the Perizzite.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ But Adonibezek fled: and they pursued him, and caught him, and cut off his thumbs and his big toes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And Adoni-bezek said, Seventy kings, their thumbs and their big toes cut off, used to gather scraps under my table. As I did, so God has paid me back. And they brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.

---

Footnote 1:7. Adoni-bezek counts seventy kings with thumbs and big toes cut off. The Hebrew puts the body parts first, as if the missing thumbs and toes are what he sees before he sees his own theology.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And the children of Judah fought against Jerusalem, and captured it, and struck it by the mouth of the sword, and set the city on fire.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And afterward the children of Judah went down to fight against the Canaanite, who lived in the mountain, and in the south, and in the valley.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And Judah went against the Canaanite who lived in Hebron: (now the name of Hebron before was Kiriath-arba:) and they killed Sheshai, and Ahiman, and Talmai.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And from there he went against the inhabitants of Debir: and the name of Debir before was Kiriath-sepher:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And Caleb said, He that strikes Kiriath-sepher, and takes it, to him will I give Achsah my daughter as a wife.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And Othniel the son of Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother, took it: and he gave him Achsah his daughter as a wife.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And it was when she came to him, that she moved him to ask of her father a field: and she got down from her ass: and Caleb said to her, What will you?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And she said to him, Give me a blessing: for you have given me a Negeb land; give me also springs of water. And Caleb gave her the upper pools and the lower pools.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And the children of the Kenite, Moses’ father-in-law, went up out of the city of palm trees with the children of Judah into the wilderness of Judah, that lies in the south of Arad: and they went and lived among the people.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And Judah went with Simeon his brother, and they killed the Canaanite who lived in Zephath, and devoted it. And the name of the city was called Hormah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Also Judah took Gaza with the border of it, and Ashkelon with the border of it, and Ekron with the border of it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And YHWH was with Judah, and he took possession of the mountain, but not to drive out the inhabitants of the valley, because they had iron chariots.

---

Footnote 1:19. The sentence lets the failure stand in plain sight: YHWH was with Judah, but iron chariots were still iron chariots. Judges is already getting its theology dirty.

²⁵ And when he showed them the entrance into the city, they struck the city by the mouth of the sword; but they let go the man and all his family.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 1:30
