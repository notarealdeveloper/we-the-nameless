------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ But it was after days, in the time of wheat harvest, that Samson visited his wife with a kid: and he said, I’ll go in to my wife to the room. But her father would not allow him to go in.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And her father said, I verily thought that you had completely hated her; therefore I gave her to your companion: isn’t her younger sister fairer than she? take her, please, instead of her.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And Samson said concerning them, And shall I be more blameless than the Philistines, though I do them a displeasure.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And Samson went and caught three hundred foxes, and took firebrands, and turned tail to tail, and put a firebrand inside between two tails.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And when he had set the brands on fire, he let them go into the standing corn of the Philistines, and burned up both the shocks, and also the standing corn, with the vineyards and olives.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Then the Philistines said, Who has done this? And they answered, Samson, the son-in-law of the Timnite, because he captured his wife, and given her to his companion. And the Philistines came up, and burned her and her father with fire.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And Samson said to them, Yough you have done this, yet will I be avenged of you, and after that I’ll cease.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And he struck them hip and thigh with a great slaughter: and he went down and lived in the top of the rock Etam.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Then the Philistines went up, and pitched in Judah, and spread themselves in Lehi.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And the men of Judah said, Why are you come up against us? And they answered, To bind Samson are we come up, to do to him as he has done to us.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Then three thousand men of Judah went to the top of the rock Etam, and said to Samson, Don’t you know that the Philistines are rulers over us? what is this that you have done to us? And he said to them, As they did to me, so have I done to them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And they said to him, We are come down to bind you, that we may rescue you in the hand of the Philistines. And Samson said to them, Swear to me, that you’ll not fall upon me yourselves.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And they spoke to him, saying, No: but we’ll bind you fast, and rescue you into their hand: but surely we’ll not kill you. And they bound him with two new cords, and brought him up from the rock.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And when he came to Lehi, the Philistines shouted against him: and spirit of YHWH came mightily upon him, and the cords that were upon his arms became as flax that was burned with fire, and his bands loosed from off his hands.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And he found a new jawbone of an ass, and put out his hand, and took it, and killed a thousand men with it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And Samson said, With the jawbone of an ass, heaps upon heaps, with the jaw of an ass have I killed a thousand companions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And it was when he had made an end of speaking, that he cast away the jawbone out of his hand, and called that place Ramathlehi.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And he was sore athirst, and called on YHWH, and said, You have given this great deliverance in the hand of your servant: and now shall I die for thirst, and fall in the hand of the uncircumcised?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 15:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ But God split an hollow place that was in the jaw, and there came water from it: and when he had drunk, his spirit came again, and he revived: therefore he called the name of it Enhakkore, that is in Lehi to this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
