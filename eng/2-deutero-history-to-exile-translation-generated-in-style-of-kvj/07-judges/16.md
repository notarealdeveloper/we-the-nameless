------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And Samson went to Gaza, and saw there a whore, and went in to her.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And it was told the Gazites, saying, Samson has come here. And they surrounded him, and lay in wait for him all night in the gate of the city, and kept quiet all the night, saying, In the morning, when it is day, we shall kill him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And Samson lay till midnight, and arose at midnight, and took the doors of the gate of the city, and the two posts, and went away with them, with the bar, and put them upon his shoulders, and carried them up to the top of a hill that is before Hebron.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And it was afterward, that he loved a woman in the valley of Sorek, whose name was Delilah.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And the lords of the Philistines came up to her, and said to her, Entice him, and see in that his great strength lies, and by what means we may prevail against him, that we may bind him to afflict him: and we’ll give you each one of us eleven hundred pieces of silver.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And Delilah said to Samson, Tell me, please, where your great strength is, and by what you might be bound to afflict you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And Samson said to her, If they bind me with seven fresh bowstrings that have not been dried, then shall I be weak, and be as one of the men.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Then the lords of the Philistines brought up to her seven fresh bowstrings that had not been dried, and she bound him with them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And there were men lying in wait, abiding with her in the chamber.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And Delilah said to Samson, Here, you have mocked me, and told me lies: now tell me, please, with what you might be bound.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And he said to her, If they bind me fast with new ropes that had not been used, then shall I be weak, and be as one of the men.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Delilah therefore took new ropes, and bound him with it, and said to him, The Philistines are on you, Samson. And there were those lying in wait abiding in the chamber. And he broke them from off his arms like a thread.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And Delilah said to Samson, Hitherto you have mocked me, and told me lies: tell me with what you might be bound. And he said to her, If you weave the seven locks of my head with the web.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And she fastened it with the pin, and said to him, The Philistines are on you, Samson. And he woke out of his sleep, and went away with the pin of the beam, and with the web.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And she said to him, How can you say, I love you, when your heart isn’t with me? you have mocked me these three times, and have not told me where your great strength is.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And it was when she pressed him daily with her words, and urged him, so that his soul was vexed to death;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ That he told her all his heart, and said to her, There has not come a razor upon my head: for I’ve been a Nazirite to God from my mother’s womb: if I am shaved, then my strength will go from me, and I’ll become weak, and be like any other man.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And when Delilah saw that he had told her all his heart, she sent and called for the lords of the Philistines, saying, Come up this once: for he has shown me all his heart. Then the lords of the Philistines came up to her, and brought money in their hand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And she made him sleep upon her knees: and she called for a man, and she caused him to shave off the seven locks of his head: and she began to afflict him, and his strength went from him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And she said, Philistines are on you, Samson. And he woke from his sleep and said, I’ll go out as at other times, and I’ll shake myself free. And he did not know that YHWH had turned away from him.

---

Footnote 16:20. He did not know that YHWH had turned away from him. The sentence is quieter than the haircut, and worse.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ But the Philistines took him, and put out his eyes, and brought him down to Gaza, and bound him with fetters of bronze: and he ground in the prison house.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² But the hair of his head began to grow again after it was shaved.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Then the lords of the Philistines gathered them together to offer a great sacrifice to Dagon their god, and to rejoice: for they said, Our god has given Samson our enemy into our hand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And when the people saw him, they praised their god: for they said, Our god has given into our hands our enemy, and the destroyer of our country, that killed many of us.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And it was when their heart was merry, that they said, Call for Samson, that he may play for us. And they called for Samson out of the prison house: and he played before them: and they set him between the pillars.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ And Samson said to the lad that held him by the hand, Allow me that I may feel the pillars on which the house stands, that I may lean upon them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ And the house was full of men and women: and all the lords of the Philistines were there: and there were on the roof about three thousand men and women, that watched while Samson played.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And Samson called to YHWH, and said, Lord God, remember me, please, and strengthen me, please, only this once, God, that I may be at once avenged of the Philistines for my two eyes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ And Samson grasped of the two middle pillars on which the house stood, and on which it was borne up, of the one with his right hand, and of the other with his left.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ And Samson said, Let me die with the Philistines. And he bowed himself with all his might: and the house fell by the lords, and upon all the people that were in it. So the dead that he killed at his death were more than they that he killed in his life.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 16:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ Then his brothers and all the house of his father came down, and took him, and brought him up, and buried him between Zorah and Eshtaol in the burying place of Manoah his father. And he judged Israel twenty years.
