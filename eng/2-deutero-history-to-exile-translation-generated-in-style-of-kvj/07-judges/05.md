## Judges 5:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Then sang Deborah and Barak the son of Abinoam on that day, saying,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 5:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Bless YHWH for the avenging of Israel, when the people willingly offered themselves.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 5:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Listen, kings; give ear, you princes; I, even I, will sing to YHWH; I’ll sing praise to YHWH God of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 5:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ YHWH, when you went out of Seir, when you marched of the field of Edom, the earth trembled, and the skies dropped, the clouds also dropped water.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 5:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ The mountains melted from in front of YHWH, even that Sinai from in front of YHWH God of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 5:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ In the days of Shamgar the son of Anath, in the days of Jael, the highways were unoccupied, and the travelers walked through byways.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Judges 5:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ The villages ceased in Israel; they ceased, until I, Deborah, arose, until I arose, a mother in Israel.

---

Footnote 5:7. The song says the villages ceased until Deborah arose. The poetry makes the rescue begin in grammar before it begins in battle.
