------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah began Hoshea the son of Elah to reign in Samaria over Israel nine years.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And he did that that was bad in the eyes of YHWH, but not as the kings of Israel that were in front of him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Against him came up Shalmaneser king of Assyria: and Hoshea became his servant, and gave him presents.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And the king of Assyria found conspiracy in Hoshea: for he had sent messengers to So king of Egypt, and brought no present to the king of Assyria, as he had done year by year: therefore the king of Assyria shut him up, and bound him in prison.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And the king of Assyria came up throughout all the land, and went up to Samaria, and laid siege to it three years.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ In the ninth year of Hoshea the king of Assyria took Samaria, and carried Israel away into Assyria, and placed them in Halah and in Habor by the river of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ For so it was, that the children of Israel had sinned against YHWH their God, that had brought them up out of the land of Egypt, from under the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and had feared other gods,

---

Footnote 17:7. The fall of Israel is explained as exodus reversal. The God who brought them up from Egypt now lets Assyria carry them away.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And walked in the statutes of the nations, whom YHWH cast out from before the children of Israel, and of the kings of Israel, that they had made.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And the children of Israel did secretly those things that were not right against YHWH their God, and they built them high places in all their cities, from the tower of the watchmen to the fortified city.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And they set them up images and groves in every high hill, and under every green tree:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And there they burned incense in all the high places, as did the nations whom YHWH carried away in front of them: and did bad things to provoke YHWH to anger:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² For they served idols, of which YHWH had said to them, You won't do this thing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Yet YHWH testified against Israel, and against Judah, by all the prophets, and by all the seers, saying, Turn you from your bad ways, and keep my commandments and my statutes, according to all the law that I commanded your fathers, and that I sent to you by my servants the prophets.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Still they would not hear, but hardened their necks, like to the neck of their fathers, that didn't believe in YHWH their God.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And they rejected his statutes, and his covenant that he made with their fathers, and his testimonies that he testified against them: and they followed vanity, and became vain, and went after the nations that were round about them, concerning whom YHWH had charged them, that they should not do like them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And they left all the commandments of YHWH their God, and made them cast images, even two calves, and made a grove, and worshipped all the army of skies, and served Baal.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And they caused their sons and their daughters to pass through the fire, and used divination and enchantments, and sold themselves to do bad in the eyes of YHWH, to provoke him to anger.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Therefore YHWH was very angry with Israel, and removed them out of his sight: there was none left but the tribe of Judah only.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Also Judah kept not the commandments of YHWH their God, but walked in the statutes of Israel that they made.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And YHWH rejected all the seed of Israel, and afflicted them, and delivered them into the hand of spoilers, until he had cast them out of his sight.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ For he tore Israel from the house of David: and they made Jeroboam the son of Nebat king: and Jeroboam drove Israel from following YHWH, and made them sin a great sin.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² For the children of Israel walked in all the sins of Jeroboam that he did: they didn't depart from them:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Until YHWH removed Israel out of his sight, as he had said by all his servants the prophets. So was Israel carried away out of their own land to Assyria to this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And the king of Assyria brought men from Babylon, and from Cuthah, and from Ava, and from Hamath, and from Sepharvaim, and placed them in the cities of Samaria instead of the children of Israel: and they possessed Samaria, and lived in the cities of it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And so it was at the beginning of their dwelling there, that they feared not YHWH: therefore YHWH sent lions among them, that killed some of them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ Why they spoke to the king of Assyria, saying, The nations that you have removed, and placed in the cities of Samaria, know not the manner of the God of the land: therefore he has sent lions among them, and, here: they kill them, because they know not the manner of the God of the land.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ And the king of Assyria commanded, saying, Carry there one of the priests whom you brought from there: and let them go and live there, and let him teach them the manner of the God of the land.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And one of the priests whom they had carried away from Samaria came and lived in Bethel, and taught them how they should fear YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ Only every nation made gods of their own, and put them in the houses of the high places that the Samaritans had made, every nation in their cities wherein they lived.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ And the men of Babylon made Succothbenoth, and the men of Cuth made Nergal, and the men of Hamath made Ashima,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ And the Avites made Nibhaz and Tartak, and the Sepharvites burned their children in fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² So they feared YHWH, and made to themselves of the lowest of them priests of the high places, that sacrificed for them in the houses of the high places.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ They feared YHWH, and served their own gods, after the manner of the nations whom they carried away from there.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ To this day they do after the former manners: they don't fear YHWH, neither do they after their statutes, or after their ordinances, or after the law and commandment that YHWH commanded the children of Jacob, whom he named Israel:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ With whom YHWH had made a covenant, and charged them, saying, You won't fear other gods, nor bow yourselves to them, nor serve them, nor sacrifice to them:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ But YHWH, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt with great power and a stretched out arm, him shall you fear, and him shall you worship, and to him shall you do sacrifice.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ And the statutes, and the ordinances, and the law, and the commandment, that he wrote for you, you shall observe to do forevermore: and you won't fear other gods.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:38

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁸ And the covenant that I have made with you you won't forget: neither shall you fear other gods.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:39

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁹ But YHWH your God you shall fear: and he shall deliver you from the hand of all your enemies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:40

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁰ Only they didn't hearken, but they did after their former manner.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 17:41

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴¹ So these nations feared YHWH, and served their carved images, both their children, and their children's children: as did their fathers, so do they to this day.
