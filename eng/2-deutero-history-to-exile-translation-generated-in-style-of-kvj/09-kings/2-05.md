## 2 Kings 5:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And Naaman, commander of the army of the king of Aram, was a great man with his master, and honored, because by him YHWH had given deliverance to Aram: he was also a mighty man in strength, but he was one with skin disease.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 5:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And the Arameans had gone out by companies, and had brought away captive out of the land of Israel a little maid: and she waited on Naaman's wife.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 5:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And she said to her mistress, If only my lord were with the prophet that is in Samaria! for he would recover him of his skin disease.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 5:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And one went in, and told his lord, saying, Thus and thus said the maid that is of the land of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 5:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And the king of Aram said, Go, go, and I'll send a letter to the king of Israel. And he departed, and took with him ten talents of silver, and six thousand pieces of gold, and ten changes of clothes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 5:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And he brought the letter to the king of Israel, saying, And when this letter comes to you, here: I have with it sent Naaman my servant to you, that you may recover him of his skin disease.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 5:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And it was, when the king of Israel had read the letter, that he tore his clothes, and said, Am I God, to kill and to make alive, that this man does send to me to recover a man of his skin disease? why consider, please, and see how he seeks a quarrel against me.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 5:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And it was so, when Elisha the man of God had heard that the king of Israel had torn his clothes, that he sent to the king, saying, Why have you torn your clothes? let him come now to me, and he shall know that there is a prophet in Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 5:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ So Naaman came with his horses and with his chariot, and stood at the door of the house of Elisha.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 5:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And Elisha sent a messenger to him, saying, Go and wash in Jordan seven times, and your flesh shall come again to you, and you shall be clean.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 5:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ But Naaman was angry, and went away, and said, Here: I thought, He will surely come out to me, and stand, and call on the name of YHWH his God, and strike his hand over the place, and recover the one with skin disease.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 5:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Are not Abana and Pharpar, rivers of Damascus, better than all the waters of Israel? may I not wash in them, and be clean? So he turned and went away in a rage.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 5:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And his servants came near, and spoke to him, and said, My father, if the prophet had told you do some great thing, would you not have done it? how much rather then, when he says to you, Wash, and be clean?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 5:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And went he down, and dipped himself seven times in Jordan, according to the word of the man of God: and his flesh came again like to the flesh of a little child, and he was clean.

---

Footnote 5:14. Naaman goes down seven times. The great man has to become small enough to fit through a river.
