## 2 Kings 6:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And the sons of the prophets said to Elisha, Here now, the place where we live with you is too strait for us.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Let us go, we please, to Jordan, and take from there every man a beam, and let us make us a place there, where we may live. And he answered, Go you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And one said, Be content, please, and go with your servants. And he answered, I'll go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ So he went with them. And when they came to Jordan, they cut down forest.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ But as one was felling a beam, the axe head fell into the water: and he cried, and said, Alas, master! for it was borrowed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And the man of God said, Where fell it? And he showed him the place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Therefore said he, Take it up to you. And he put out his hand, and took it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And the king of Aram warred against Israel, and took counsel with his servants, saying, In such and such a place shall be my camp.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And the man of God sent to the king of Israel, saying, Beware that you pass not such a place: for there the Arameans are come down.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And the king of Israel sent to the place that the man of God told him and warned him of, and saved himself there, not once nor twice.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Therefore the heart of the king of Aram was very troubled for this thing: and he called his servants, and said to them, Will you not show me that of us is for the king of Israel?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And one of his servants said, None, my lord, O king: but Elisha, the prophet that is in Israel, telleth the king of Israel the words that you speakest in your bedchamber.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And he said, Go and spy where he is, that I may send and fetch him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Therefore sent he there horses, and chariots, and a great army: and they came by night, and surrounded the city about.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And when the servant of the man of God was risen early, and gone forth, here: an army surrounded the city both with horses and chariots. And his servant said to him, Alas, my master! how shall we do?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And he answered, Don't fear: for they that be with us are more than they that be with them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 6:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And Elisha prayed, and said, YHWH, please, open his eyes, that he may see. And YHWH opened the eyes of the young man: and he saw: and, here: the mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire round about Elisha.

---

Footnote 6:17. Elisha does not ask for more horses and chariots. He asks that the servant’s eyes be opened to what is already there.

## 2 Kings 6:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------
