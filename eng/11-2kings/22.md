------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Josiah was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned thirty-one years in Jerusalem. And his mother's name was Jedidah, the daughter of Adaiah of Boscath.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And he did that that was right in the eyes of YHWH, and walked in all the way of David his father, and didn't turn aside to the right hand or to the left.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And it was in the eighteenth year of king Josiah, that the king sent Shaphan the son of Azaliah, the son of Meshullam, the scribe, to the house of YHWH, saying,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Go up to Hilkiah the high priest, that he may sum the silver that is brought into the house of YHWH, that the keepers of the door have gathered of the people:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And let them deliver it into the hand of the doers of the work, that have the oversight of the house of YHWH: and let them give it to the doers of the work that is in the house of YHWH, to repair the breaches of the house,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ To carpenters, and builders, and masons, and to buy timber and hewn stone to repair the house.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Only there was no reckoning made with them of the money that was delivered into their hand, because they dealt faithfully.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And Hilkiah the high priest said to Shaphan the scribe, I have found the book of the law in the house of YHWH. And Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan, and he read it.

---

Footnote 22:8. Finding the scroll is either reform, rediscovery, or a controlled explosion in the archive. The text lets the book do what kings could not.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And Shaphan the scribe came to the king, and brought the king word again, and said, Your servants have gathered the money that was found in the house, and have delivered it into the hand of them that do the work, that have the oversight of the house of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And Shaphan the scribe showed the king, saying, Hilkiah the priest has delivered me a book. And Shaphan read it before the king.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And it was, when the king had heard the words of the book of the law, that he tore his clothes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And the king commanded Hilkiah the priest, and Ahikam the son of Shaphan, and Achbor the son of Michaiah, and Shaphan the scribe, and Asahiah a servant of the king's, saying,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Go you, inquire of YHWH for me, and for the people, and for all Judah, concerning the words of this book that is found: for great is the wrath of YHWH that is kindled against us, because our fathers have not hearkened to the words of this book, to do according to all that that is written concerning us.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ So Hilkiah the priest, and Ahikam, and Achbor, and Shaphan, and Asahiah, went to Huldah the prophetess, the wife of Shallum the son of Tikvah, the son of Harhas, keeper of the wardrobe: (now she lived in Jerusalem in the college:) and they communed with her.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And she said to them, Thus says YHWH God of Israel, Tell the man that sent you to me,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Thus says YHWH, Here: I'll bring bad on this place, and on the inhabitants of it, even all the words of the book that the king of Judah has read:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ Because they have forsaken me, and have burned incense to other gods, that they might provoke me to anger with all the works of their hands: therefore my wrath shall be kindled against this place, and won't be quenched.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ But to the king of Judah that sent you to inquire of YHWH, thus shall you say to him, Thus says YHWH God of Israel, As touching the words that you have heard:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Because your heart was tender, and you have humbled yourself in front of YHWH, when you heard what I spoke against this place, and against the inhabitants of it, that they should become a desolation and a curse, and have torn your clothes, and wept in front of me: I also have heard you, says YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 22:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ Here therefore, I'll gather you to your fathers, and you shall be gathered into your grave in peace: and your eyes won't see all the bad that I'll bring on this place. And they brought the king word again.
