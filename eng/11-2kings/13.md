## 2 Kings 13:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ In the three and twentieth year of Joash the son of Ahaziah king of Judah Jehoahaz the son of Jehu began to reign over Israel in Samaria, and reigned seventeen years.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 13:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And he did that that was bad in the eyes of YHWH, and followed the sins of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, that made Israel to sin: he didn't depart therefrom.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 13:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And the anger of YHWH was kindled against Israel, and he delivered them into the hand of Hazael king of Aram, and into the hand of Benhadad the son of Hazael, all their days.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 13:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And Jehoahaz pleaded with YHWH, and YHWH hearkened to him: for he saw the oppression of Israel, because the king of Aram oppressed them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 13:5

⁵ (And YHWH gave Israel a saviour, so that they went out from under the hand of the Arameans: and the children of Israel lived in their tents, as beforetime.
