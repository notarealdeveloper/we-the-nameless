## 2 Kings 10:3

## 2 Kings 10:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 10:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 10:6

And the king's sons, being seventy persons, were with the great men of the city, that brought them up.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 10:7

## 2 Kings 10:8

## 2 Kings 10:9

⁹ And it was in the morning, that he went out, and stood, and said to all the people, You be righteous: here: I conspired against my master, and killed him: but who killed all these?
