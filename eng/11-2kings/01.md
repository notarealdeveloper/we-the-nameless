## 2 Kings 1:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 1:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And he said to them, What kind of man was he that came up to meet you, and told you these words?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2 Kings 1:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And they answered him, He was a hairy man, and belted with a belt of leather about his loins. And he said, It is Elijah the Tishbite.

---

Footnote 1:8. A hairy man can also be read as “a possessor of hair.” The story recognizes Elijah by costume, not by résumé.
