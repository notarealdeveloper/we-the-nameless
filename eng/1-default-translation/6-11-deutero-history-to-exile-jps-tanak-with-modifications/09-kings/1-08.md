------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Then Solomon convoked the elders of Israel—all the heads of the tribes
and the ancestral chieftains of the Israelites—before King Solomon in
Jerusalem, to bring up the Ark of the Covenant of YHWH from the City of
David, that is, Zion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² All the men of Israel gathered before King Solomon at the Feast, in
the month of Ethanim—that is, the seventh month.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ When all the elders of Israel had come, the priests lifted the Ark

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ and carried up the Ark of YHWH. Then the priests and the Levites
brought the Tent of Meeting and all the holy vessels that were in the
Tent.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Meanwhile, King Solomon and the whole community of Israel, who were
assembled with him before the Ark, were sacrificing sheep and oxen in
such abundance that they could not be numbered or counted.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ The priests brought the Ark of YHWH’s Covenant to its place underneath
the wings of the cherubim, in the Shrine of the House, in the Holy of
Holies;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ for the cherubim had their wings spread out over the place of the Ark,
so that the cherubim shielded the Ark and its poles from above.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ The poles projected so that the ends of the poles were visible in the
sanctuary in front of the Shrine, but they could not be seen outside;
and there they remain to this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ There was nothing inside the Ark but the two tablets of stone which
Moses placed there at Horeb, when YHWH made [a covenant] with the
Israelites after their departure from the land of Egypt.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ When the priests came out of the sanctuary—for the cloud had filled
the House of YHWH

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ and the priests were not able to remain and perform the service
because of the cloud, for the Presence of YHWH filled the House of YHWH—

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² then Solomon declared: “YHWH has chosen To abide in a thick cloud:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ I have now built for You A stately House, A place where You May dwell
forever.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Then, with the whole congregation of Israel standing, the king faced
about and blessed the whole congregation of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ He said: “Praised be YHWH, the God of Israel, who has fulfilled with
deeds the promise He made to my father David. For He said,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ ‘Ever since I brought My people Israel out of Egypt, I have not
chosen a city among all the tribes of Israel for building a House where
My name might abide; but I have chosen David to rule My people Israel.’

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ “Now my father David had intended to build a House for the name of
YHWH, the God of Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ But YHWH said to my father David, ‘As regards your intention to build
a House for My name, you did right to have that intention.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ However, you shall not build the House yourself; instead, your son,
the issue of your loins, shall build the House for My name.’

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ “And YHWH has fulfilled the promise that He made: I have succeeded my
father David and have ascended the throne of Israel, as YHWH promised. I
have built the House for the name of YHWH, the God of Israel;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ and I have set a place there for the Ark, containing the covenant
which YHWH made with our fathers when He brought them out from the land
of Egypt.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² Then Solomon stood before the altar of YHWH in the presence of the
whole community of Israel; he spread the palms of his hands toward
heaven

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ and said, “O LORD God of Israel, in the heavens above and on the
earth below there is no god like You, who keep Your gracious covenant
with Your servants when they walk before You in wholehearted devotion;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ You who have kept the promises You made to Your servant, my father
David, fulfilling with deeds the promise You made—as is now the case.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And now, O LORD God of Israel, keep the further promise that You made
to Your servant, my father David: ‘Your line on the throne of Israel
shall never end, if only your descendants will look to their way and
walk before Me as you have walked before Me.’

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ Now, therefore, O God of Israel, let the promise that You made to
Your servant my father David be fulfilled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ “But will God really dwell on earth? Even the heavens to their
uttermost reaches cannot contain You, how much less this House that I
have built!

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ Yet turn, O LORD my God, to the prayer and supplication of Your
servant, and hear the cry and prayer which Your servant offers before
You this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ May Your eyes be open day and night toward this House, toward the
place of which You have said, ‘My name shall abide there’; may You heed
the prayers which Your servant will offer toward this place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ And when You hear the supplications which Your servant and Your
people Israel offer toward this place, give heed in Your heavenly
abode—give heed and pardon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ “Whenever one man commits an offense against another, and the latter
utters an imprecation to bring a curse upon him, and comes with his
imprecation before Your altar in this House,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² oh, hear in heaven and take action to judge Your servants, condemning
him who is in the wrong and bringing down the punishment of his conduct
on his head, vindicating him who is in the right by rewarding him
according to his righteousness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ “Should Your people Israel be routed by an enemy because they have
sinned against You, and then turn back to You and acknowledge Your name,
and they offer prayer and supplication to You in this House,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ oh, hear in heaven and pardon the sin of Your people Israel, and
restore them to the land that You gave to their fathers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ “Should the heavens be shut up and there be no rain, because they
have sinned against You, and then they pray toward this place and
acknowledge Your name and repent of their sins, when You answer them,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ oh, hear in heaven and pardon the sin of Your servants, Your people
Israel, after You have shown them the proper way in which they are to
walk; and send down rain upon the land which You gave to Your people as
their heritage.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ So, too, if there is a famine in the land, if there is pestilence,
blight, mildew, locusts or caterpillars, or if an enemy oppresses them
in any of the settlements of the land. “In any plague and in any
disease,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:38

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁸ in any prayer or supplication offered by any person among all Your
people Israel—each of whom knows his own affliction—when he spreads his
palms toward this House,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:39

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁹ oh, hear in Your heavenly abode, and pardon and take action! Render
to each man according to his ways as You know his heart to be—for You
alone know the hearts of all men—

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:40

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁰ so that they may revere You all the days that they live on the land
that You gave to our fathers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:41

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴¹ “Or if a foreigner who is not of Your people Israel comes from a
distant land for the sake of Your name—

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:42

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴² for they shall hear about Your great name and Your mighty hand and
Your outstretched arm—when he comes to pray toward this House,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:43

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴³ oh, hear in Your heavenly abode and grant all that the foreigner asks
You for. Thus all the peoples of the earth will know Your name and
revere You, as does Your people Israel; and they will recognize that
Your name is attached to this House that I have built.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:44

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁴ “When Your people take the field against their enemy by whatever way
You send them, and they pray to YHWH in the direction of the city which
You have chosen, and of the House which I have built to Your name,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:45

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁵ oh, hear in heaven their prayer and supplication and uphold their
cause.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:46

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁶ “When they sin against You—for there is no man who does not sin—and
You are angry with them and deliver them to the enemy, and their captors
carry them off to an enemy land, near or far;

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:47

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁷ and then they take it to heart in the land to which they have been
carried off, and they repent and make supplication to You in the land of
their captors, saying: ‘We have sinned, we have acted perversely, we
have acted wickedly,’

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:48

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁸ and they turn back to You with all their heart and soul, in the land
of the enemies who have carried them off, and they pray to You in the
direction of their land which You gave to their fathers, of the city
which You have chosen, and of the House which I have built to Your name—

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:49

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁹ oh, give heed in Your heavenly abode to their prayer and
supplication, uphold their cause,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:50

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵⁰ and pardon Your people who have sinned against You for all the
transgressions that they have committed against You. Grant them mercy in
the sight of their captors that they may be merciful to them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:51

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵¹ For they are Your very own people that You freed from Egypt, from the
midst of the iron furnace.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:52

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵² May Your eyes be open to the supplication of Your servant and the
supplication of Your people Israel, and may You heed them whenever they
call upon You.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:53

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵³ For You, O Lord GOD, have set them apart for Yourself from all the
peoples of the earth as Your very own, as You promised through Moses
Your servant when You freed our fathers from Egypt.”

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:54

------------------------------------------------------------------------

supplication, he rose from where he had been kneeling, in front of the
altar of YHWH, his hands spread out toward heaven.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:55

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵⁵ He stood, and in a loud voice blessed the whole congregation of
Israel:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:56

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵⁶ “Praised be YHWH who has granted a haven to His people Israel, just
as He promised; not a single word has failed of all the gracious
promises that He made through His servant Moses.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:57

------------------------------------------------------------------------

abandon or forsake us.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:58

------------------------------------------------------------------------

and keep the commandments, the laws, and the rules, which He enjoined
upon our fathers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:59

------------------------------------------------------------------------

before YHWH, be close to YHWH our God day and night, that He may provide
for His servant and for His people Israel, according to each day’s
needs—

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:60

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶⁰ to the end that all the peoples of the earth may know that YHWH alone
is God, there is no other.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:61

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:62

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:63

------------------------------------------------------------------------

well-being to YHWH. Thus the king and all the Israelites dedicated the
House of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:64

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶⁴ That day the king consecrated the center of the court that was in
front of the House of YHWH. For it was there that he presented the burnt
offerings, the meal offerings, and the fat parts of the offerings of
well-being, because the bronze altar that was before YHWH was too small
to hold the burnt offerings, the meal offerings, and the fat parts of
the offerings of well-being.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:65

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Lebo-hamath to the Wadi of Egypt—observed the Feast at that time before
YHWH our God, seven days and again seven days, fourteen days in all.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 8:66

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶⁶ On the eighth day he let the people go. They bade the king good-bye
and went to their homes, joyful and glad of heart over all the goodness
that YHWH had shown to His servant David and His people Israel.
