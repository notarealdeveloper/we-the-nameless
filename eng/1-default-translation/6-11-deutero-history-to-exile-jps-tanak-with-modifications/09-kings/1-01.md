## I Kings 1:1

## I Kings 1:2

## I Kings 1:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

and waited upon him; but the king was not intimate with her.

provided himself with chariots and horses, and an escort of fifty
outrunners.

## I Kings 1:6

one born after Absalom and, like him, was very handsome.

and they supported Adonijah;

## I Kings 1:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Shimei and Rei, and David’s own fighting men did not side with Adonijah.

## I Kings 1:9

Zoheleth stone which is near En-rogel; he invited all his brother
