------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And Solomon made affinity with Pharaoh king of Egypt, and took Pharaoh's daughter, and brought her into the city of David, until he had made an end of building his own house, and the house of YHWH, and the wall of Jerusalem round about.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Only the people sacrificed in high places, because there was no house built to the name of YHWH, until those days.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And Solomon loved YHWH, walking in the statutes of David his father: only he sacrificed and burnt incense in high places.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And the king went to Gibeon to sacrifice there: for that was the great high place: a thousand burnt offerings did Solomon offer on that altar.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ In Gibeon YHWH appeared to Solomon in a dream by night: and God said, Ask what I'll give you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And Solomon said, You have showed to your servant David my father great mercy, according as he walked before you in truth, and in righteousness, and in uprightness of heart with you: and you have kept for him this great kindness, that you have given him a son to sit on his throne, as it is this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And now, YHWH my God, you have made your servant king instead of David my father: and I am but a little child: I know not how to go out or come in.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And your servant is in the midst of your people that you have chosen, a great people, that can't be numbered nor counted for multitude.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And give Your servant a listening heart to judge Your people, to understand between good and bad: because who is able to judge this heavy people of Yours?

---

Footnote 3:9. Solomon asks for a listening heart. In Hebrew, wisdom begins in the ear, not the head.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And God said to him, Because you have asked this thing, and have not asked for yourself long life: neither have asked riches for yourself, nor have asked the life of your enemies: but have asked for yourself understanding to discern judgment:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Here: I have done according to your words. Here: I have given you a wise and understanding heart, so that there has been none like you before you, and after you none like you shall arise.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And I have also given you that that you have not asked, both riches, and honour: so that there shall not be any among the kings like to you all your days.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And if you'll walk in my ways, to keep my statutes and my commandments, as your father David did walk, then I'll lengthen your days.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And Solomon awoke: and, here: it was a dream. And he came to Jerusalem, and stood before the ark of the covenant of YHWH, and offered up burnt offerings, and offered peace offerings, and made a feast to all his servants.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And came there two women, that were harlots, to the king, and stood before him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And the one woman said, O my lord, I and this woman dwell in one house: and I was delivered of a child with her in the house.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And it was the third day after that I was delivered, that this woman was delivered also: and we were together: there was no stranger with us in the house, save we two in the house.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And this woman's child died in the night: because she overlaid it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And she arose at midnight, and took my son from beside me, while your maidservant slept, and laid it in her bosom, and laid her dead child in my bosom.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And when I rose in the morning to give my child suck, here: it was dead: but when I had considered it in the morning, here: it was not my son, that I did bear.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And the other woman said, Nay: but the living is my son, and the dead is your son. And this said, No: but the dead is your son, and the living is my son. Thus they spoke before the king.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ And said the king, The one says, This is my son that lives, and your son is the dead: and the other says, Nay: but your son is the dead, and my son is the living.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And the king said, Bring me a sword. And they brought a sword before the king.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And the king said, Divide the living child in two, and give half to the one, and half to the other.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ And spoke the woman whose the living child was to the king, for her bowels yearned on her son, and she said, O my lord, give her the living child, and in no way kill it. But the other said, Let it be neither my nor your, but divide it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 3:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ And the king answered and said, Give her the living child, and in no way kill it: she is the mother of it.

## 1 Kings 3:28
