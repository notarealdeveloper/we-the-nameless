------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And King Solomon loved many foreign women, and Pharaoh’s daughter, Moabite, Ammonite, Edomite, Sidonian, Hittite women,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Of the nations concerning which YHWH said to the children of Israel, You shall not go in to them, neither shall they come in to you: for surely they'll turn away your heart after their gods: Solomon clung to these in love.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And he had seven hundred wives, princesses, and three hundred concubines: and his wives turned away his heart.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And it was, at the time of Solomon’s old age, his wives turned his heart after other gods; and his heart was not complete with YHWH his God, like the heart of David his father.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ For Solomon went after Ashtoreth the goddess of the Zidonians, and after Milcom the abomination of the Ammonites.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And Solomon did what was bad in YHWH's eyes, and went not fully after YHWH, as did David his father.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And did Solomon build an high place for Chemosh, the abomination of Moab, in the hill that is before Jerusalem, and for Molech, the abomination of the children of Ammon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And likewise did he for all his strange wives, which burnt incense and sacrificed to their gods.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And YHWH was angry with Solomon, because his heart was turned from YHWH God of Israel, which had appeared to him twice,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And had commanded him concerning this thing, that he should not go after other gods: but he kept not that which YHWH commanded.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Why YHWH said to Solomon, Because as this is done of you, and you have not kept my covenant and my statutes, that I have commanded you, I'll surely rend the kingdom from you, and will give it to your servant.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Notwithstanding in your days I'll not do it for David your father's sake: but I'll rend it out of the hand of your son.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ But I'll not rend away all the kingdom: but will give one tribe to your son for David my servant's sake, and for Jerusalem's sake that I have chosen.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And YHWH stirred up an adversary to Solomon, Hadad the Edomite: he was of the king's seed in Edom.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ For it was when David was in Edom, and Joab the commander of the army was gone up to bury the killed, after he had struck every male in Edom:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ (For six months did Joab remain there with all Israel, until he had cut off every male in Edom:)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ That Hadad fled, he and certain Edomites of his father's servants with him, to go into Egypt: Hadad being yet a little child.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And they arose out of Midian, and came to Paran: and they took men with them out of Paran, and they came to Egypt, to Pharaoh king of Egypt: which gave him a house, and appointed him food, and gave him land.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And Hadad found great favour in the sight of Pharaoh, so that he gave him to wife the sister of his own wife, the sister of Tahpenes the queen.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And the sister of Tahpenes bore him Genubath his son, whom Tahpenes weaned in Pharaoh's house: and Genubath was in Pharaoh's household among the sons of Pharaoh.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And when Hadad heard in Egypt that David lay down with his fathers, and that Joab the commander of the army was dead, Hadad said to Pharaoh, Let me depart, that I may go to my own country.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And Pharaoh said to him, But what have you lacked with me, that, here: you seek to go to your own country? And he answered, Nothing: but let me go anyway.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ And God stirred him up another adversary, Rezon the son of Eliadah, which fled from his lord Hadadezer king of Zobah:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ And he gathered men to him, and became commander over a band, when David slaughtered them of Zobah: and they went to Damascus, and dwelt in it, and reigned in Damascus.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And he was an adversary to Israel all the days of Solomon, beside the bad that Hadad did: and he abhorred Israel, and reigned over Syria.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ And Jeroboam the son of Nebat, an Ephrathite of Zereda, Solomon's servant, whose mother's name was Zeruah, a widow woman, even he lifted up his hand against the king.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ And this was the cause that he lifted up his hand against the king: Solomon built Millo, and repaired the breaches of the city of David his father.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And the man Jeroboam was a mighty man of valour: and Solomon seeing the young man that he was industrious, he made him ruler over all the charge of the house of Joseph.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ And it was at that time when Jeroboam went out of Jerusalem, that the prophet Ahijah the Shilonite found him in the way: and he had clad himself with a new clothes: and they two were alone in the field:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ And Ahijah caught the new clothes that was on him, and tore it in twelve pieces:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ And he said to Jeroboam, Take you ten pieces: for thus says YHWH, the God of Israel, Here: I'll rend the kingdom out of the hand of Solomon, and will give ten tribes to you:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² (But he shall have one tribe for my servant David's sake, and for Jerusalem's sake, the city that I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel:)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ Because that they have forsaken me, and have worshipped Ashtoreth the goddess of the Zidonians, Chemosh the god of the Moabites, and Milcom the god of the children of Ammon, and have not walked in my ways, to do that which is right in my eyes, and to keep my statutes and my judgments, as did David his father.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ But I'll not take the whole kingdom out of his hand: but I'll make him prince all the days of his life for David my servant's sake, whom I chose, because he kept my commandments and my statutes:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ But I'll take the kingdom out of his son's hand, and will give it to you, even ten tribes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ And to his son will I give one tribe, that David my servant may have a light alway before me in Jerusalem, the city that I have chosen me to put my name there.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ And I'll take you, and you shall reign according to all that your life desires, and shall be king over Israel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:38

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁸ And it shall be, if you'll listen to all that I command, and will walk in my ways, and do that is right in my sight, to keep my statutes and my commandments, as David my servant did: that I'll be with you, and build you a sure house, as I built for David, and will give Israel to you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:39

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁹ And I'll for this afflict the seed of David, but not for ever.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:40

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁰ Solomon sought therefore to kill Jeroboam. And Jeroboam arose, and fled into Egypt, to Shishak king of Egypt, and was in Egypt until the death of Solomon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:41

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴¹ And the rest of the acts of Solomon, and all that he did, and his wisdom, are they not written in the book of the acts of Solomon?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:42

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴² And the time that Solomon reigned in Jerusalem over all Israel was forty years.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 11:43

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴³ And Solomon lay down with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David his father: and Rehoboam his son reigned in his place.
