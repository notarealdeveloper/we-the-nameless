------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ In the four hundred and eightieth year after the Israelites left the
land of Egypt, in the month of Ziv—that is, the second month—in the
fourth year of his reign over Israel, Solomon began to build the House
of YHWH.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² The House which King Solomon built for YHWH was 60 cubits long, 20
cubits wide, and 30 cubits high.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ The portico in front of the Great Hall of the House was 20 cubits
long—along the width of the House—and 10 cubits deep to the front of the
House.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ He made windows for the House, recessed and latticed.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Against the outside wall of the House—the outside walls of the House
enclosing the Great Hall and the Shrine—he built a storied structure;
and he made side chambers all around.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ The lowest story was 5 cubits wide, the middle one 6 cubits wide, and
the third 7 cubits wide; for he had provided recesses around the outside
of the House so as not to penetrate the walls of the House.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ When the House was built, only finished stones cut at the quarry were
used, so that no hammer or ax or any iron tool was heard in the House
while it was being built.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ The entrance to the middle [story of] the side chambers was on the
right side of the House; and winding stairs led up to the middle
chambers, and from the middle chambers to the third story.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ When he finished building the House, he paneled the House with beams
and planks of cedar.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ He built the storied structure against the entire House—each story 5
cubits high, so that it encased the House with timbers of cedar.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Then the word of YHWH came to Solomon,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² “With regard to this House you are building—if you follow My laws and
observe My rules and faithfully keep My commandments, I will fulfill for
you the promise that I gave to your father David:



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ I will abide among the children of Israel, and I will never forsake
My people Israel.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ When Solomon had completed the construction of the House,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ he paneled the walls of the House on the inside with planks of cedar.
He also overlaid the walls on the inside with wood, from the floor of
the House to the ceiling. And he overlaid the floor of the House with
planks of cypress.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Twenty cubits from the rear of the House, he built [a partition] of
cedar planks from the floor to the walls; he furnished its interior to
serve as a shrine, as the Holy of Holies.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ The front part of the House, that is, the Great Hall, measured 40
cubits.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ The cedar of the interior of the House had carvings of gourds and
calyxes; it was all cedar, no stone was exposed.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ In the innermost part of the House, he fixed a Shrine in which to
place the Ark of YHWH’s Covenant.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ The interior of the Shrine was 20 cubits long, 20 cubits wide, and 20
cubits high. He overlaid it with solid gold; he similarly overlaid [its]
cedar altar.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ Solomon overlaid the interior of the House with solid gold; and he
inserted golden chains into the door of the Shrine. He overlaid [the
Shrine] with gold,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² so that the entire House was overlaid with gold; he even overlaid
with gold the entire altar of the Shrine. And so the entire House was
completed.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ In the Shrine he made two cherubim of olive wood, each 10 cubits
high.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ [One] had a wing measuring 5 cubits and another wing measuring 5
cubits, so that the spread from wingtip to wingtip was 10 cubits;



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ and the wingspread of the other cherub was also 10 cubits. The two
cherubim had the same measurements and proportions:



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ the height of the one cherub was 10 cubits, and so was that of the
other cherub.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ He placed the cherubim inside the inner chamber. Since the wings of
the cherubim were extended, a wing of the one touched one wall and a
wing of the other touched the other wall, while their wings in the
center of the chamber touched each other.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ He overlaid the cherubim with gold.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ All over the walls of the House, of both the inner area and the outer
area, he carved reliefs of cherubim, palms, and calyxes,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ and he overlaid the floor of the House with gold, both the inner and
the outer areas.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ For the entrance of the Shrine he made doors of olive wood, the
pilasters and the doorposts having five sides.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² The double doors were of olive wood, and on them he carved reliefs of
cherubim, palms, and calyxes. He overlaid them with gold, hammering the
gold onto the cherubim and the palms.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ For the entrance of the Great Hall, too, he made doorposts of
oleaster wood, having four sides,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ and the double doors of cypress wood, each door consisting of two
rounded planks.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ On them he carved cherubim, palms, and calyxes, overlaying them with
gold applied evenly over the carvings.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ He built the inner enclosure of three courses of hewn stones and one
course of cedar beams.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ In the fourth year, in the month of Ziv, the foundations of the House
of YHWH were laid;



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 6:38

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁸ and in the eleventh year, in the month of Bul—that is, the eighth
month—the House was completed according to all its details and all its
specifications. It took him seven years to build it.
