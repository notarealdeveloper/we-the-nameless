------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ In the second year of King Joash son of Joahaz of Israel, Amaziah son of King Joash of Judah became king.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem; his mother’s name was Jehoaddan of Jerusalem.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ He did what was pleasing to YHWH, but not like his ancestor David; he did just as his father Joash had done.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ However, the shrines were not removed; the people continued to sacrifice and make offerings at the shrines.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Once he had the kingdom firmly in his grasp, he put to death the courtiers who had assassinated his father the king.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ But he did not put to death the children of the assassins, in accordance with what is written in the Book of the Teaching of Moses, where YHWH commanded, “Parents shall not be put to death for children, nor children be put to death for parents; a person shall be put to death only for his own crime.”
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ He defeated ten thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt, and he captured Sela in battle and renamed it Joktheel, as is still the case.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Then Amaziah sent envoys to King Jehoash son of Jehoahaz son of Jehu of Israel, with this message: “Come, let us confront each other.”
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ King Jehoash of Israel sent back this message to King Amaziah of Judah: “The thistle in Lebanon sent this message to the cedar in Lebanon, ‘Give your daughter to my son in marriage.’ But a wild beast in Lebanon went by and trampled down the thistle.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Because you have defeated Edom, you have become arrogant. Stay home and enjoy your glory, rather than provoke disaster and fall, dragging Judah down with you.”
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ But Amaziah paid no heed; so King Jehoash of Israel advanced, and he and King Amaziah of Judah confronted each other at Beth-shemesh in Judah.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² The Judites were routed by Israel, and they all fled to their homes.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ King Jehoash of Israel captured King Amaziah son of Jehoash son of Ahaziah of Judah at Beth-shemesh. He marched on Jerusalem, and he made a breach of four hundred cubits in the wall of Jerusalem, from the Ephraim Gate to the Corner Gate.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ He carried off all the gold and silver and all the vessels that there were in the House of YHWH and in the treasuries of the royal palace, as well as hostages; and he returned to Samaria.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ The other events of Jehoash’s reign, and all his actions and exploits, and his war with King Amaziah of Judah, are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Israel.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Jehoash slept with his fathers, and was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel; his son Jeroboam succeeded him as king.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ King Amaziah son of Joash of Judah lived fifteen years after the death of King Jehoash son of Jehoahaz of Israel.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ The other events of Amaziah’s reign are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Judah.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ A conspiracy was formed against him in Jerusalem and he fled to Lachish; but they sent men after him to Lachish, and they killed him there.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ They brought back his body on horses, and he was buried with his fathers in Jerusalem, in the City of David.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ Then all the people of Judah took Azariah, who was sixteen years old, and proclaimed him king to succeed his father Amaziah.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² It was he who rebuilt Elath and restored it to Judah, after King [Amaziah] slept with his fathers.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ In the fifteenth year of King Amaziah son of Joash of Judah, King Jeroboam son of Joash of Israel became king in Samaria—for forty-one years.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ He did what was displeasing to YHWH; he did not depart from all the sins that Jeroboam son of Nebat had caused Israel to commit.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ It was he who restored the territory of Israel from Lebo-hamath to the sea of the Arabah, in accordance with the promise that YHWH, the God of Israel, had made through His servant, the prophet Jonah son of Amittai from Gath-hepher.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ For YHWH saw the very bitter plight of Israel, with neither bond nor free left, and with none to help Israel.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ And YHWH resolved not to blot out the name of Israel from under heaven; and he delivered them through Jeroboam son of Joash.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ The other events of Jeroboam’s reign, and all his actions and exploits, how he fought and recovered Damascus and Hamath for Judah in Israel, are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Israel.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 14:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ Jeroboam slept with his fathers, the kings of Israel, and his son Zechariah succeeded him as king.
