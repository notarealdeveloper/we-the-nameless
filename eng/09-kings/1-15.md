------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ In the eighteenth year of King Jeroboam son of Nebat, Abijam became king over Judah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² He reigned three years in Jerusalem; his mother’s name was Maacah daughter of Abishalom.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ He continued in all the sins that his father before him had committed; he was not wholehearted with YHWH his God, like his father David.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Yet, for the sake of David, YHWH his God gave him a lamp in Jerusalem, by raising up his descendant after him and by preserving Jerusalem.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ For David had done what was pleasing to YHWH and never turned throughout his life from all that He had commanded him, except in the matter of Uriah the Hittite.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ There was war between Abijam and Jeroboam all the days of his life.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ The other events of Abijam’s reign and all his actions are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Judah; there was war between Abijam and Jeroboam.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Abijam slept with his fathers; he was buried in the City of David, and his son Asa succeeded him as king.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ In the twentieth year of King Jeroboam of Israel, Asa became king over Judah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ He reigned forty-one years in Jerusalem; his mother’s name was Maacah daughter of Abishalom.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Asa did what was pleasing to YHWH, as his father David had done.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² He expelled the male prostitutes from the land, and he removed all the idols that his ancestors had made.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ He also deposed his mother Maacah from the rank of queen mother, because she had made an abominable thing for [the goddess] Asherah. Asa cut down her abominable thing and burnt it in the Wadi Kidron.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ The shrines, indeed, were not abolished; however, Asa was wholehearted with YHWH his God all his life.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ He brought into the House of YHWH all the consecrated things of his father and his own consecrated things —silver, gold, and utensils.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ There was war between Asa and King Baasha of Israel all their days.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ King Baasha of Israel advanced against Judah, and he fortified Ramah to prevent anyone belonging to King Asa from going out or coming in.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ So Asa took all the silver and gold that remained in the treasuries of the House of YHWH as well as the treasuries of the royal palace, and he entrusted them to his officials. King Asa sent them to King Ben-hadad son of Tabrimmon son of Hezion of Aram, who resided in Damascus, with this message:



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ “There is a pact between you and me, and between your father and my father. I herewith send you a gift of silver and gold: Go and break your pact with King Baasha of Israel, so that he may withdraw from me.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ Ben-hadad responded to King Asa’s request; he sent his army commanders against the towns of Israel and captured Ijon, Dan, Abel-beth-maacah, and all Chinneroth, as well as all the land of Naphtali.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ When Baasha heard about it, he stopped fortifying Ramah and remained in Tirzah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² Then King Asa mustered all Judah, with no exemptions; and they carried away the stones and timber with which Baasha had fortified Ramah. With these King Asa fortified Geba of Benjamin, and Mizpah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ All the other events of Asa’s reign, and all his exploits, and all his actions, and the towns that he fortified, are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Judah. However, in his old age he suffered from a foot ailment.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ Asa slept with his fathers and was buried with his fathers in the city of his father David. His son Jehoshaphat succeeded him as king.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ Nadab son of Jeroboam had become king over Israel in the second year of King Asa of Judah, and he reigned over Israel for two years.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ He did what was displeasing to YHWH; he continued in the ways of his father, in the sins which he caused Israel to commit.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ Then Baasha son of Ahijah, of the House of Issachar, conspired against him; and Baasha struck him down at Gibbethon of the Philistines, while Nadab and all Israel were laying siege to Gibbethon.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ Baasha killed him in the third year of King Asa of Judah and became king in his stead.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ As soon as he became king, he struck down all the House of Jeroboam; he did not spare a single soul belonging to Jeroboam until he destroyed it—in accordance with the word that YHWH had spoken through His servant Ahijah the Shilonite—



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ because of the sins which Jeroboam committed and which he caused Israel to commit, thereby vexing YHWH, the God of Israel.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ The other events of Nadab’s reign and all his actions are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Israel.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² There was war between Asa and King Baasha of Israel all their days.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ In the third year of King Asa of Judah, Baasha son of Ahijah became king in Tirzah over all Israel—for twenty-four years.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Kings 15:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ He did what was displeasing to YHWH; he followed the ways of Jeroboam and the sins which he caused Israel to commit.
