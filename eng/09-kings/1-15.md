------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And in the eighteenth year of king Jeroboam the son of Nebat reigned Abijam over Judah.

## 1 Kings 15:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 15:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And he walked in all the sins of his father, that he had done before him: and his heart was not perfect with YHWH his God, as the heart of David his father.

## 1 Kings 15:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Nevertheless for David's sake did YHWH his God give him a lamp in Jerusalem, to set up his son after him, and to establish Jerusalem:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 15:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Because David did that that was straight in YHWH's eyes, and turned not aside from any thing that he commanded him all the days of his life, save only in the matter of Uriah the Hittite.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 15:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And there was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam all the days of his life.

## 1 Kings 15:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 15:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 15:13

## 1 Kings 15:17

## 1 Kings 15:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Kings 15:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ There is a league between me and you, and between my father and your father: here:, I have sent to you a present of silver and gold: come and break your league with Baasha king of Israel, that he may depart from me.
