------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Manasseh was twelve years old when he became king, and he reigned fifty-five years in Jerusalem; his mother’s name was Hephzibah.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² He did what was displeasing to YHWH, following the abhorrent practices of the nations that YHWH had dispossessed before the Israelites.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ He rebuilt the shrines that his father Hezekiah had destroyed; he erected altars for Baal and made a sacred post, as King Ahab of Israel had done. He bowed down to all the host of heaven and worshiped them,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ and he built altars for them in the House of YHWH, of which YHWH had said, “I will establish My name in Jerusalem.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ He built altars for all the hosts of heaven in the two courts of the House of YHWH.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ He consigned his son to the fire; he practiced soothsaying and divination, and consulted ghosts and familiar spirits; he did much that was displeasing to YHWH, to vex Him.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ The sculptured image of Asherah that he made he placed in the House concerning which YHWH had said to David and to his son Solomon, “In this House and in Jerusalem, which I chose out of all the tribes of Israel, I will establish My name forever.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And I will not again cause the feet of Israel to wander from the land that I gave to their fathers, if they will but faithfully observe all that I have commanded them—all the Teachings with which My servant Moses charged them.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ But they did not obey, and Manasseh led them astray to do greater evil than the nations that YHWH had destroyed before the Israelites.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Therefore YHWH spoke through His servants the prophets:


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ “Because King Manasseh of Judah has done these abhorrent things—he has outdone in wickedness all that the Amorites did before his time—and because he led Judah to sin with his fetishes,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² assuredly, thus said YHWH, the God of Israel: I am going to bring such a disaster on Jerusalem and Judah that both ears of everyone who hears about it will tingle.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ I will apply to Jerusalem the measuring line of Samaria and the weights of the House of Ahab; I will wipe Jerusalem clean as one wipes a dish and turns it upside down.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And I will cast off the remnant of My own people and deliver them into the hands of their enemies. They shall be plunder and prey to all their enemies


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ because they have done what is displeasing to Me and have been vexing Me from the day that their fathers came out of Egypt to this day.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Moreover, Manasseh put so many innocent persons to death that he filled Jerusalem [with blood] from end to end—besides the sin he committed in causing Judah to do what was displeasing to YHWH.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ The other events of Manasseh’s reign, and all his actions, and the sins he committed, are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Judah.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Manasseh slept with his fathers and was buried in the garden of his palace, in the garden of Uzza; and his son Amon succeeded him as king.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Amon was twenty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem; his mother’s name was Meshullemeth daughter of Haruz of Jotbah.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ He did what was displeasing to YHWH, as his father Manasseh had done.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ He walked in all the ways of his father, worshiping the fetishes which his father had worshiped and bowing down to them.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² He forsook YHWH, the God of his fathers, and did not follow the way of YHWH.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Amon’s courtiers conspired against him; and they killed the king in his palace.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ But the people of the land put to death all who had conspired against King Amon, and the people of the land made his son Josiah king in his stead.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ The other events of Amon’s reign [and] his actions are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Judah.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 21:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ He was buried in his tomb in the garden of Uzza; and his son Josiah succeeded him as king.
