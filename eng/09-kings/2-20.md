------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ In those days Hezekiah fell dangerously ill. The prophet Isaiah son of
Amoz came and said to him, “Thus said YHWH: Set your affairs in order,
for you are going to die; you will not get well.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Thereupon Hezekiah turned his face to the wall and prayed to YHWH. He
said,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ “Please, O LORD, remember how I have walked before You sincerely and
wholeheartedly, and have done what is pleasing to You.” And Hezekiah
wept profusely.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Before Isaiah had gone out of the middle court, the word of YHWH came
to him:



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ “Go back and say to Hezekiah, the ruler of My people: Thus said YHWH,
the God of your father David: I have heard your prayer, I have seen your
tears. I am going to heal you; on the third day you shall go up to the
House of YHWH.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And I will add fifteen years to your life. I will also rescue you and
this city from the hands of the king of Assyria. I will protect this
city for My sake and for the sake of My servant David.”—



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Then Isaiah said, “Get a cake of figs.” And they got one, and they
applied it to the rash, and he recovered.—



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Hezekiah asked Isaiah, “What is the sign that YHWH will heal me and
that I shall go up to the House of YHWH on the third day?”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Isaiah replied, “This is the sign for you from YHWH that YHWH will do
the thing that He has promised: Shall the shadow advance ten steps or
recede ten steps?”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Hezekiah said, “It is easy for the shadow to lengthen ten steps, but
not for the shadow to recede ten steps.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ So the prophet Isaiah called to YHWH, and He made the shadow which
had descended on the dial of Ahaz recede ten steps.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² At that time, King Berodach-baladan son of Baladan of Babylon sent
[envoys with] a letter and a gift to Hezekiah, for he had heard about
Hezekiah’s illness.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Hezekiah heard about them and he showed them all his
treasure-house—the silver, the gold, the spices, and the fragrant
oil—and his armory, and everything that was to be found in his
storehouses. There was nothing in his palace or in all his realm that
Hezekiah did not show them.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Then the prophet Isaiah came to King Hezekiah. “What,” he demanded of
him, “did those men say to you? Where have they come to you from?” “They
have come,” Hezekiah replied, “from a far country, from Babylon.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Next he asked, “What have they seen in your palace?” And Hezekiah
replied, “They have seen everything that is in my palace. There was
nothing in my storehouses that I did not show them.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Then Isaiah said to Hezekiah, “Hear the word of YHWH:



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ A time is coming when everything in your palace which your ancestors
have stored up to this day will be carried off to Babylon; nothing will
remain behind, said YHWH.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And some of your sons, your own issue, whom you will have fathered,
will be taken to serve as eunuchs in the palace of the king of Babylon.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Hezekiah declared to Isaiah, “The word of YHWH that you have spoken
is good.” For he thought, “It means that safety is assured for my time.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ The other events of Hezekiah’s reign, and all his exploits, and how
he made the pool and the conduit and brought the water into the city,
are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Judah.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 20:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ Hezekiah slept with his fathers, and his son Manasseh succeeded him
as king.
