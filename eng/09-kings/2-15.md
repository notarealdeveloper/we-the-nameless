------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ In the twenty-seventh year of King Jeroboam of Israel, Azariah son of King Amaziah of Judah became king.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² He was sixteen years old when he became king, and he reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem; his mother’s name was Jecoliah of Jerusalem.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ He did what was pleasing to YHWH, just as his father Amaziah had done.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ However, the shrines were not removed; the people continued to sacrifice and make offerings at the shrines.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ YHWH struck the king with a plague, and he was a leper until the day of his death; he lived in isolated quarters, while Jotham, the king’s son, was in charge of the palace and governed the people of the land.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ The other events of Azariah’s reign, and all his actions, are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Judah.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Azariah slept with his fathers, and he was buried with his fathers in the City of David; his son Jotham succeeded him as king.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ In the thirty-eighth year of King Azariah of Judah, Zechariah son of Jeroboam became king over Israel in Samaria—for six months.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ He did what was displeasing to YHWH, as his fathers had done; he did not depart from the sins which Jeroboam son of Nebat had caused Israel to commit.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Shallum son of Jabesh conspired against him and struck him down before the people and killed him, and succeeded him as king.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ The other events of Zechariah’s reign are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Israel.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² This was in accord with the word that YHWH had spoken to Jehu: “Four generations of your descendants shall occupy the throne of Israel.” And so it came about.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Shallum son of Jabesh became king in the thirty-ninth year of King Uzziah of Judah, and he reigned in Samaria one month.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Then Menahem son of Gadi set out from Tirzah and came to Samaria; he attacked Shallum son of Jabesh in Samaria and killed him, and he succeeded him as king.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ The other events of Shallum’s reign, and the conspiracy that he formed, are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Israel.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ At that time, [marching] from Tirzah, Menahem subdued Tiphsah and all who were in it, and its territory; and because it did not surrender, he massacred [its people] and ripped open all its pregnant women.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ In the thirty-ninth year of King Azariah of Judah, Menahem son of Gadi became king over Israel in Samaria—for ten years.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ He did what was displeasing to YHWH; throughout his days he did not depart from the sins which Jeroboam son of Nebat had caused Israel to commit.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ King Pul of Assyria invaded the land, and Menahem gave Pul a thousand talents of silver that he might support him and strengthen his hold on the kingdom.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ Menahem exacted the money from Israel: every man of means had to pay fifty shekels of silver for the king of Assyria. The king of Assyria withdrew and did not remain in the land.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ The other events of Menahem’s reign, and all his actions, are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Israel.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² Menahem slept with his fathers, and his son Pekahiah succeeded him as king.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ In the fiftieth year of King Azariah of Judah, Pekahiah son of Menahem became king over Israel in Samaria—for two years.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ He did what was displeasing to YHWH; he did not depart from the sins which Jeroboam son of Nebat had caused Israel to commit.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ His aide, Pekah son of Remaliah, conspired against him and struck him down in the royal palace in Samaria; with him were fifty Gileadites, with men from Argob and Arieh; and he killed him and succeeded him as king.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ The other events of Pekahiah’s reign, and all his actions, are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Israel.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ In the fifty-second year of King Azariah of Judah, Pekah son of Remaliah became king over Israel and Samaria—for twenty years.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ He did what was displeasing to YHWH; he did not depart from the sins which Jeroboam son of Nebat had caused Israel to commit.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ In the days of King Pekah of Israel, King Tiglath-pileser of Assyria came and captured Ijon, Abel- beth-maacah, Janoah, Kedesh, Hazor—Gilead, Galilee, the entire region of Naphtali; and he deported the inhabitants to Assyria.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ Hoshea son of Elah conspired against Pekah son of Remaliah, attacked him, and killed him. He succeeded him as king in the twentieth year of Jotham son of Uzziah.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ The other events of Pekah’s reign, and all his actions, are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Israel.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² In the second year of King Pekah son of Remaliah of Israel, Jotham son of King Uzziah of Judah became king.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ He was twenty-five years old when he became king, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem; his mother’s name was Jerusha daughter of Zadok.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ He did what was pleasing to YHWH, just as his father Uzziah had done.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ However, the shrines were not removed; the people continued to sacrifice and make offerings at the shrines. It was he who built the Upper Gate of the House of YHWH.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ The other events of Jotham’s reign, and all his actions, are recorded in the Annals of the Kings of Judah.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ In those days, YHWH began to incite King Rezin of Aram and Pekah son of Remaliah against Judah.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 15:38

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁸ Jotham slept with his fathers, and he was buried with his fathers in the city of his ancestor David; his son Ahaz succeeded him as king.
