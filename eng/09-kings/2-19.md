------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ When King Hezekiah heard this, he rent his clothes, and covered himself with sackcloth, and went into the House of YHWH.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² He also sent Eliakim, who was in charge of the palace, Shebna the scribe, and the senior priests, covered with sackcloth, to the prophet Isaiah son of Amoz.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ They said to him, “Thus said Hezekiah: This day is a day of distress, of chastisement, and of disgrace. The babes have reached the birthstool, but the strength to give birth is lacking.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Perhaps YHWH your God will take note of all the words of the Rabshakeh, whom his master the king of Assyria has sent to blaspheme the living God, and will mete out judgment for the words that YHWH your God has heard—if you will offer up prayer for the surviving remnant.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ When King Hezekiah’s ministers came to Isaiah,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Isaiah said to them, “Tell your master as follows: Thus said YHWH: Do not be frightened by the words of blasphemy against Me that you have heard from the minions of the king of Assyria.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ I will delude him; he will hear a rumor and return to his land, and I will make him fall by the sword in his land.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ The Rabshakeh, meanwhile, heard that [the king] had left Lachish; he turned back and found the king of Assyria attacking Libnah.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ But [the king of Assyria] learned that King Tirhakah of Nubia had come out to fight him; so he again sent messengers to Hezekiah, saying,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ “Tell this to King Hezekiah of Judah: Do not let your God, on whom you are relying, mislead you into thinking that Jerusalem will not be delivered into the hands of the king of Assyria.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ You yourself have heard what the kings of Assyria have done to all the lands, how they have annihilated them; and can you escape?


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Were the nations that my predecessors destroyed—Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, and the Beth-edenites in Telassar—saved by their gods?


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Where is the king of Hamath? And the king of Arpad? And the kings of Lair, Sepharvaim, Hena, and Ivvah?”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ Hezekiah took the letter from the messengers and read it. Hezekiah then went up to the House of YHWH and spread it out before YHWH.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And Hezekiah prayed to YHWH and said, “O LORD of Hosts, Enthroned on the Cherubim! You alone are God of all the kingdoms of the earth. You made the heavens and the earth.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ O LORD, incline Your ear and hear; open Your eyes and see. Hear the words that Sennacherib has sent to blaspheme the living God!


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ True, O LORD, the kings of Assyria have annihilated the nations and their lands,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ and have committed their gods to the flames and have destroyed them; for they are not gods, but man’s handiwork of wood and stone.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ But now, O LORD our God, deliver us from his hands, and let all the kingdoms of the earth know that You alone, O LORD, are God.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ Then Isaiah son of Amoz sent this message to Hezekiah: “Thus said YHWH, the God of Israel: I have heard the prayer you have offered to Me concerning King Sennacherib of Assyria.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ This is the word that YHWH has spoken concerning him: “Fair Maiden Zion despises you, She mocks at you; Fair Jerusalem shakes Her head at you.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² Whom have you blasphemed and reviled? Against whom made loud your voice And haughtily raised your eyes? Against the Holy One of Israel!


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Through your envoys you have blasphemed my Lord. Because you thought, ‘Thanks to my vast chariotry, It is I who have climbed the highest mountains, To the remotest parts of the Lebanon, And have cut down its loftiest cedars, Its choicest cypresses, And have reached its remotest lodge, Its densest forest.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ It is I who have drawn and drunk the waters of strangers; I have dried up with the soles of my feet All the streams of Egypt.’


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ Have you not heard? Of old I planned that very thing, I designed it long ago, And now have fulfilled it. And it has come to pass, Laying waste fortified towns In desolate heaps.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ Their inhabitants are helpless, Dismayed and shamed. They were but grass of the field And green herbage, Grass of the roofs that is blasted Before the standing grain.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ I know your stayings And your goings and comings, And how you have raged against Me.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ Because you have raged against Me, And your tumult has reached My ears, I will place My hook in your nose And My bit between your jaws; And I will make you go back by the road By which you came.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ “And this is the sign for you: This year you eat what grows of itself, and the next year what springs from that; and in the third year, sow and reap, and plant vineyards and eat their fruit.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ And the survivors of the House of Judah that have escaped shall regenerate its stock below and produce boughs above.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ For a remnant shall come forth from Jerusalem, Survivors from Mount Zion. The zeal of YHWH of Hosts Shall bring this to pass.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² Assuredly, thus said YHWH concerning the king of Assyria: He shall not enter this city: He shall not shoot an arrow at it, Or advance upon it with a shield, Or pile up a siege mound against it.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ He shall go back By the way he came; He shall not enter this city —declares YHWH.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ I will protect and save this city for My sake, And for the sake of My servant David.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ That night an angel of YHWH went out and struck down one hundred and eighty-five thousand in the Assyrian camp, and the following morning they were all dead corpses.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ So King Sennacherib of Assyria broke camp and retreated, and stayed in Nineveh.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Kings 19:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ While he was worshiping in the temple of his god Nisroch, his sons Adrammelech and Sarezer struck him down with the sword. They fled to the land of Ararat, and his son Esarhaddon succeeded him as king.
