------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ and YHWH sent Nathan to David. He came to him and said, “There were
two men in the same city, one rich and one poor.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² The rich man had very large flocks and herds,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ but the poor man had only one little ewe lamb that he had bought. He
tended it and it grew up together with him and his children: it used to
share his morsel of bread, drink from his cup, and nestle in his bosom;
it was like a daughter to him.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ One day, a traveler came to the rich man, but he was loath to take
anything from his own flocks or herds to prepare a meal for the guest
who had come to him; so he took the poor man’s lamb and prepared it for
the man who had come to him.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ David flew into a rage against the man, and said to Nathan, “As YHWH
lives, the man who did this deserves to die!



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ He shall pay for the lamb four times over, because he did such a thing
and showed no pity.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And Nathan said to David, “That man is you! Thus said YHWH, the God of
Israel: ‘It was I who anointed you king over Israel and it was I who
rescued you from the hand of Saul.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ I gave you your master’s house and possession of your master’s wives;
and I gave you the House of Israel and Judah; and if that were not
enough, I would give you twice as much more.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Why then have you flouted the command of YHWH and done what displeases
Him? You have put Uriah the Hittite to the sword; you took his wife and
made her your wife and had him killed by the sword of the Ammonites.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Therefore the sword shall never depart from your House—because you
spurned Me by taking the wife of Uriah the Hittite and making her your
wife.’



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Thus said YHWH: ‘I will make a calamity rise against you from within
your own house; I will take your wives and give them to another man
before your very eyes and he shall sleep with your wives under this very
sun.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² You acted in secret, but I will make this happen in the sight of all
Israel and in broad daylight.’”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ David said to Nathan, “I stand guilty before YHWH!” And Nathan
replied to David, “YHWH has remitted your sin; you shall not die.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ However, since you have spurned the enemies of YHWH by this deed,
even the child about to be born to you shall die.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Nathan went home, and YHWH afflicted the child that Uriah’s wife had
borne to David, and it became critically ill.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ David entreated God for the boy; David fasted, and he went in and
spent the night lying on the ground.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ The senior servants of his household tried to induce him to get up
from the ground; but he refused, nor would he partake of food with them.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ On the seventh day the child died. David’s servants were afraid to
tell David that the child was dead; for they said, “We spoke to him when
the child was alive and he wouldn’t listen to us; how can we tell him
that the child is dead? He might do something terrible.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ When David saw his servants talking in whispers, David understood
that the child was dead; David asked his servants, “Is the child dead?”
“Yes,” they replied.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ Thereupon David rose from the ground; he bathed and anointed himself,
and he changed his clothes. He went into the House of YHWH and
prostrated himself. Then he went home and asked for food, which they set
before him, and he ate.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ His courtiers asked him, “Why have you acted in this manner? While
the child was alive, you fasted and wept; but now that the child is
dead, you rise and take food!”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² He replied, “While the child was still alive, I fasted and wept
because I thought: ‘Who knows? YHWH may have pity on me, and the child
may live.’



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ But now that he is dead, why should I fast? Can I bring him back
again? I shall go to him, but he will never come back to me.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ David consoled his wife Bathsheba; he went to her and lay with her.
She bore a son and she named him Solomon. YHWH favored him,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ and He sent a message through the prophet Nathan; and he was named
Jedidiah at the instance of YHWH.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ Joab attacked Rabbah of Ammon and captured the royal city.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ Joab sent messengers to David and said, “I have attacked Rabbah and I
have already captured the water city.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ Now muster the rest of the troops and besiege the city and capture
it; otherwise I will capture the city myself, and my name will be
connected with it.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ David mustered all the troops and marched on Rabbah, and he attacked
it and captured it.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ The crown was taken from the head of their king and it was placed on
David’s head—it weighed a talent of gold, and [on it] were precious
stones. He also carried off a vast amount of booty from the city.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 12:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ He led out the people who lived there and set them to work with saws,
iron threshing boards, and iron axes, or assigned them to brickmaking;
David did this to all the towns of Ammon. Then David and all the troops
returned to Jerusalem.
