## I Samuel 23:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ David was told: “The Philistines are raiding Keilah and plundering the threshing floors.”

## I Samuel 23:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² David consulted YHWH, “Shall I go and attack those Philistines?” And YHWH said to David, “Go; attack the Philistines and you will save Keilah.”

## I Samuel 23:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ But David’s men said to him, “Look, we are afraid here in Judah, how much more if we go to Keilah against the forces of the Philistines!”

## I Samuel 23:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ So David consulted YHWH again, and YHWH answered him, “March down at once to Keilah, for I am going to deliver the Philistines into your hands.”
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 23:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ David and his men went to Keilah and fought against the Philistines; he drove off their cattle and inflicted a severe defeat on them. Thus David saved the inhabitants of Keilah.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 23:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ When Abiathar son of Ahimelech fled to David at Keilah, he brought down an ephod with him.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 23:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Saul was told that David had come to Keilah, and Saul thought, “God has delivered him into my hands, for he has shut himself in by entering a town with gates and bars.”
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 23:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Saul summoned all the troops for war, to go down to Keilah and besiege David and his men.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 23:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ When David learned that Saul was planning to harm him, he told the priest Abiathar to bring the ephod forward.

## I Samuel 23:11

## I Samuel 23:12

¹² David continued, “Will the citizens of Keilah deliver me and my men into Saul’s hands?” And YHWH answered, “They will.”
