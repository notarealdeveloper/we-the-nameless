------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Then Samuel said to all Israel, “I have yielded to you in all you have asked of me and have set a king over you.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² Henceforth the king will be your leader. “As for me, I have grown old and gray—but my sons are still with you—and I have been your leader from my youth to this day.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Here I am! Testify against me, in the presence of YHWH and in the presence of His anointed one: Whose ox have I taken, or whose ass have I taken? Whom have I defrauded or whom have I robbed? From whom have I taken a bribe to look the other way? I will return it to you.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ They responded, “You have not defrauded us, and you have not robbed us, and you have taken nothing from anyone.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ He said to them, “YHWH then is witness, and His anointed is witness, to your admission this day that you have found nothing in my possession.” They responded, “He is!”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Samuel said to the people, “YHWH [is witness], He who appointed Moses and Aaron and who brought your fathers out of the land of Egypt.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Come, stand before YHWH while I cite against you all the kindnesses that YHWH has done to you and your fathers.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ “When Jacob came to Egypt,…your fathers cried out to YHWH, and YHWH sent Moses and Aaron, who brought your fathers out of Egypt and settled them in this place.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ But they forgot YHWH their God; so He delivered them into the hands of Sisera the military commander of Hazor, into the hands of the Philistines, and into the hands of the king of Moab; and these made war upon them.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ They cried to YHWH, ‘We are guilty, for we have forsaken YHWH and worshiped the Baalim and Ashtaroth. Oh, deliver us from our enemies and we will serve You.’


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And YHWH sent Jerubbaal and Bedan and Jephthah and Samuel, and delivered you from the enemies around you; and you dwelt in security.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² But when you saw that Nahash king of the Ammonites was advancing against you, you said to me, ‘No, we must have a king reigning over us’—though YHWH your God is your King.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ “Well, YHWH has set a king over you! Here is the king that you have chosen, that you have asked for.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ “If you will revere YHWH, worship Him, and obey Him, and will not flout YHWH’s command, if both you and the king who reigns over you will follow YHWH your God, [well and good].


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ But if you do not obey YHWH and you flout YHWH’s command, the hand of YHWH will strike you as it did your fathers.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ “Now stand by and see the marvelous thing that YHWH will do before your eyes.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ It is the season of the wheat harvest. I will pray to YHWH and He will send thunder and rain; then you will take thought and realize what a wicked thing you did in the sight of YHWH when you asked for a king.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Samuel prayed to YHWH, and YHWH sent thunder and rain that day, and the people stood in awe of YHWH and of Samuel.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ The people all said to Samuel, “Intercede for your servants with YHWH your God that we may not die, for we have added to all our sins the wickedness of asking for a king.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ But Samuel said to the people, “Have no fear. You have, indeed, done all those wicked things. Do not, however, turn away from YHWH, but serve YHWH with all your heart.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ Do not turn away to follow worthless things, which can neither profit nor save but are worthless.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² For the sake of His great name, YHWH will never abandon His people, seeing that YHWH undertook to make you His people.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ “As for me, far be it from me to sin against YHWH and refrain from praying for you; and I will continue to instruct you in the practice of what is good and right.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ Above all, you must revere YHWH and serve Him faithfully with all your heart; and consider how grandly He has dealt with you.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 12:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ For if you persist in your wrongdoing, both you and your king shall be swept away.”
