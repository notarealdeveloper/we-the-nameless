------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ Samuel said to Saul, “I am the one YHWH sent to anoint you king over His people Israel. Therefore, listen to YHWH’s command!


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² “Thus said YHWH of Hosts: I am exacting the penalty for what Amalek did to Israel, for the assault he made upon them on the road, on their way up from Egypt.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Now go, attack Amalek, and proscribe all that belongs to him. Spare no one, but kill alike men and women, infants and sucklings, oxen and sheep, camels and asses!”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Saul mustered the troops and enrolled them at Telaim: 200,000 men on foot, and 10,000 men of Judah.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Then Saul advanced as far as the city of Amalek and lay in wait in the wadi.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ Saul said to the Kenites, “Come, withdraw at once from among the Amalekites, that I may not destroy you along with them; for you showed kindness to all the Israelites when they left Egypt.” So the Kenites withdrew from among the Amalekites.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Saul destroyed Amalek from Havilah all the way to Shur, which is close to Egypt,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ and he captured King Agag of Amalek alive. He proscribed all the people, putting them to the sword;


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ but Saul and the troops spared Agag and the best of the sheep, the oxen, the second-born, the lambs, and all else that was of value. They would not proscribe them; they proscribed only what was cheap and worthless.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ The word of YHWH then came to Samuel:


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ “I regret that I made Saul king, for he has turned away from Me and has not carried out My commands.” Samuel was distressed and he entreated YHWH all night long.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Early in the morning Samuel went to meet Saul. Samuel was told, “Saul went to Carmel, where he erected a monument for himself; then he left and went on down to Gilgal.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ When Samuel came to Saul, Saul said to him, “Blessed are you of YHWH! I have fulfilled YHWH’s command.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ “Then what,” demanded Samuel, “is this bleating of sheep in my ears, and the lowing of oxen that I hear?”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Saul answered, “They were brought from the Amalekites, for the troops spared the choicest of the sheep and oxen for sacrificing to YHWH your God. And we proscribed the rest.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Samuel said to Saul, “Stop! Let me tell you what YHWH said to me last night!” “Speak,” he replied.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And Samuel said, “You may look small to yourself, but you are the head of the tribes of Israel. YHWH anointed you king over Israel,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ and YHWH sent you on a mission, saying, ‘Go and proscribe the sinful Amalekites; make war on them until you have exterminated them.’


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Why did you disobey YHWH and swoop down on the spoil in defiance of YHWH’s will?”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ Saul said to Samuel, “But I did obey YHWH! I performed the mission on which YHWH sent me: I captured King Agag of Amalek, and I proscribed Amalek,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ and the troops took from the spoil some sheep and oxen—the best of what had been proscribed—to sacrifice to YHWH your God at Gilgal.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² But Samuel said: “Does YHWH delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices As much as in obedience to YHWH’s command? Surely, obedience is better than sacrifice, Compliance than the fat of rams.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ For rebellion is like the sin of divination, Defiance, like the iniquity of teraphim. Because you rejected YHWH’s command, He has rejected you as king.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ Saul said to Samuel, “I did wrong to transgress YHWH’s command and your instructions; but I was afraid of the troops and I yielded to them.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ Please, forgive my offense and come back with me, and I will bow low to YHWH.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ But Samuel said to Saul, “I will not go back with you; for you have rejected YHWH’s command, and YHWH has rejected you as king over Israel.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ As Samuel turned to leave, Saul seized the corner of his robe, and it tore.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ And Samuel said to him, “YHWH has this day torn the kingship over Israel away from you and has given it to another who is worthier than you.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ Moreover, the Glory of Israel does not deceive or change His mind, for He is not human that He should change His mind.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ But [Saul] pleaded, “I did wrong. Please, honor me in the presence of the elders of my people and in the presence of Israel, and come back with me until I have bowed low to YHWH your God.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ So Samuel followed Saul back, and Saul bowed low to YHWH.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² Samuel said, “Bring forward to me King Agag of Amalek.” Agag approached him with faltering steps; and Agag said, “Ah, bitter death is at hand!”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ Samuel said: “As your sword has bereaved women, So shall your mother be bereaved among women.” And Samuel cut Agag down before YHWH at Gilgal.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ Samuel then departed for Ramah, and Saul went up to his home at Gibeah of Saul.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 15:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ Samuel never saw Saul again to the day of his death. But Samuel grieved over Saul, because YHWH regretted that He had made Saul king over Israel.
