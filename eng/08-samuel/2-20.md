## II Samuel 20:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ A scoundrel named Sheba son of Bichri, a Benjaminite, happened to be
there. He sounded the horn and proclaimed: “We have no portion in David,
No share in Jesse’s son! Every man to his tent, O Israel!”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² All the men of Israel left David and followed Sheba son of Bichri; but
the men of Judah accompanied their king from the Jordan to Jerusalem.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ David went to his palace in Jerusalem, and the king took the ten
concubines he had left to mind the palace and put them in a guarded
place; he provided for them, but he did not cohabit with them. They
remained in seclusion until the day they died, in living widowhood.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ The king said to Amasa, “Call up the men of Judah to my standard, and
report here three days from now.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Amasa went to call up Judah, but he took longer than the time set for
him.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And David said to Abishai, “Now Sheba son of Bichri will cause us more
trouble than Absalom. So take your lord’s servants and pursue him,
before he finds fortified towns and eludes us.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Joab’s men, the Cherethites and Pelethites, and all the warriors,
marched out behind him. They left Jerusalem in pursuit of Sheba son of
Bichri.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ They were near the great stone in Gibeon when Amasa appeared before
them. Joab was wearing his military dress, with his sword girded over it
and fastened around his waist in its sheath; and, as he stepped forward,
it fell out.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Joab said to Amasa, “How are you, brother?” and with his right hand
Joab took hold of Amasa’s beard as if to kiss him.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ Amasa was not on his guard against the sword in Joab’s [left] hand,
and [Joab] drove it into his belly so that his entrails poured out on
the ground and he died; he did not need to strike him a second time.
Joab and his brother Abishai then set off in pursuit of Sheba son of
Bichri,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ while one of Joab’s henchmen stood by the corpse and called out,
“Whoever favors Joab, and whoever is on David’s side, follow Joab!”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Amasa lay in the middle of the road, drenched in his blood; and the
man saw that everyone stopped. And when he saw that all the people were
stopping, he dragged Amasa from the road into the field and covered him
with a garment.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Once he was removed from the road, everybody continued to follow Joab
in pursuit of Sheba son of Bichri.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:14

Beth-maacah; and all the Beerites assembled and followed him inside.

the troops with Joab were engaged in battering the wall,

to come over here so I can talk to him.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

answered; and she said to him, “Listen to what your handmaid has to
say.” “I’m listening,” he replied.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:18

inquire of Abel,’ and that was the end of the matter.

## II Samuel 20:19

you seek to bring death upon a mother city in Israel! Why should you
destroy YHWH’s possession?”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 20:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ Joab replied, “Far be it, far be it from me to destroy or to ruin!
