## I Samuel 24:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ David went from there and stayed in the wildernesses of En-gedi.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² When Saul returned from pursuing the Philistines, he was told that David was in the wilderness of En-gedi.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ So Saul took three thousand picked men from all Israel and went in search of David and his men in the direction of the rocks of the wild goats;


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ and he came to the sheepfolds along the way. There was a cave there, and Saul went in to relieve himself. Now David and his men were sitting in the back of the cave.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ David’s men said to him, “This is the day of which YHWH said to you, ‘I will deliver your enemy into your hands; you can do with him as you please.’” David went and stealthily cut off the corner of Saul’s cloak.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ But afterward David reproached himself for cutting off the corner of Saul’s cloak.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ He said to his men, “YHWH forbid that I should do such a thing to my lord—YHWH’s anointed—that I should raise my hand against him; for he is YHWH’s anointed.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ David rebuked his men and did not permit them to attack Saul. Saul left the cave and started on his way.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Then David also went out of the cave and called after Saul, “My lord king!” Saul looked around and David bowed low in homage, with his face to the ground.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And David said to Saul, “Why do you listen to the people who say, ‘David is out to do you harm?’


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ You can see for yourself now that YHWH delivered you into my hands in the cave today. And though I was urged to kill you, I showed you pity; for I said, ‘I will not raise a hand against my lord, since he is YHWH’s anointed.’


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² Please, sir, take a close look at the corner of your cloak in my hand; for when I cut off the corner of your cloak, I did not kill you. You must see plainly that I have done nothing evil or rebellious, and I have never wronged you. Yet you are bent on taking my life.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ May YHWH judge between you and me! And may He take vengeance upon you for me, but my hand will never touch you.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ As the ancient proverb has it: ‘Wicked deeds come from wicked men!’ My hand will never touch you.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ Against whom has the king of Israel come out? Whom are you pursuing? A dead dog? A single flea?


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ May YHWH be arbiter and may He judge between you and me! May He take note and uphold my cause, and vindicate me against you.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ When David finished saying these things to Saul, Saul said, “Is that your voice, my son David?” And Saul broke down and wept.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ He said to David, “You are right, not I; for you have treated me generously, but I have treated you badly.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ Yes, you have just revealed how generously you treated me, for YHWH delivered me into your hands and you did not kill me.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ If a man meets his enemy, does he let him go his way unharmed? Surely, YHWH will reward you generously for what you have done for me this day.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ I know now that you will become king, and that the kingship over Israel will remain in your hands.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## I Samuel 24:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² So swear to me by YHWH that you will not destroy my descendants or wipe out my name from my father’s house.”
