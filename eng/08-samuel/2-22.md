------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ David addressed the words of this song to YHWH, after YHWH had saved him from the hands of all his enemies and from the hands of Saul.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² He said: O LORD, my crag, my fastness, my deliverer!


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ O God, the rock wherein I take shelter: My shield, my mighty champion, my fortress and refuge! My savior, You who rescue me from violence!


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ All praise! I called on YHWH, And I was delivered from my enemies.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ For the breakers of Death encompassed me, The torrents of Belial terrified me;


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ The snares of Sheol encircled me, The coils of Death engulfed me.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ In my anguish I called on YHWH, Cried out to my God; In His Abode He heard my voice, My cry entered His ears.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ Then the earth rocked and quaked, The foundations of heaven shook— Rocked by His indignation.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Smoke went up from His nostrils, From His mouth came devouring fire; Live coals blazed forth from Him.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ He bent the sky and came down, Thick cloud beneath His feet.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ He mounted a cherub and flew; He was seen on the wings of the wind.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² He made pavilions of darkness about Him, Dripping clouds, huge thunderheads;


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ In the brilliance before Him Blazed fiery coals.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ YHWH thundered forth from heaven, The Most High sent forth His voice;


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ He let loose bolts, and scattered them; Lightning, and put them to rout.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ The bed of the sea was exposed, The foundations of the world were laid bare By the mighty roaring of YHWH, At the blast of the breath of His nostrils.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ He reached down from on high, He took me, Drew me out of the mighty waters;


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ He rescued me from my enemy so strong, From foes too mighty for me.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ They attacked me on my day of calamity, But YHWH was my stay.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ He brought me out to freedom, He rescued me because He was pleased with me.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ YHWH rewarded me according to my merit, He requited the cleanness of my hands.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² For I have kept the ways of YHWH And have not been guilty before my God;


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ I am mindful of all His rules And have not departed from His laws.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ I have been blameless before Him, And have guarded myself against sinning—


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And YHWH has requited my merit, According to my purity in His sight.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:26

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁶ With the loyal You deal loyally; With the blameless hero, blamelessly.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:27

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁷ With the pure You act in purity, And with the perverse You are wily.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:28

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁸ To humble folk You give victory, And You look with scorn on the haughty.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:29

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁹ You, O LORD, are my lamp; YHWH lights up my darkness.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:30

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁰ With You, I can rush a barrier, With my God, I can scale a wall.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:31

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³¹ The way of God is perfect, The word of YHWH is pure. He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:32

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³² Yea, who is a god except YHWH, Who is a rock except God?—


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:33

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³³ The God, my mighty stronghold, Who kept my path secure;


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:34

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁴ Who made my legs like a deer’s, And set me firm on the heights;


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:35

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁵ Who trained my hands for battle, So that my arms can bend a bow of bronze!


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:36

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁶ You have granted me the shield of Your protection And Your providence has made me great.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:37

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁷ You have let me stride on freely, And my feet have not slipped.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:38

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁸ I pursued my enemies and wiped them out, I did not turn back till I destroyed them.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:39

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³⁹ I destroyed them, I struck them down; They rose no more, they lay at my feet.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:40

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁰ You have girt me with strength for battle, Brought low my foes before me,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:41

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴¹ Made my enemies turn tail before me, My foes—and I wiped them out.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:42

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴² They looked, but there was none to deliver; To YHWH, but He answered them not.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:43

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴³ I pounded them like dust of the earth, Stamped, crushed them like dirt of the streets.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:44

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁴ You have rescued me from the strife of peoples, Kept me to be a ruler of nations; Peoples I knew not must serve me.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:45

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁵ Aliens have cringed before me, Paid me homage at the mere report of me.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:46

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁶ Aliens have lost courage And come trembling out of their fastnesses.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:47

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁷ YHWH lives! Blessed is my rock! Exalted be God, the rock Who gives me victory;


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:48

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁸ The God who has vindicated me And made peoples subject to me,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:49

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴⁹ Rescued me from my enemies, Raised me clear of my foes, Saved me from lawless men!


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:50

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵⁰ For this I sing Your praise among the nations And hymn Your name:


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 22:51

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵¹ Tower of victory to His king, Who deals graciously with His anointed, With David and his offspring evermore.
