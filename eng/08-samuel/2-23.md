------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ These are the last words of David: The utterance of David son of Jesse, The utterance of the man set on high, The anointed of the God of Jacob, The favorite of the songs of Israel:


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² The spirit of YHWH has spoken through me, His message is on my tongue;


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ The God of Israel has spoken, The Rock of Israel said concerning me: “He who rules men justly, He who rules in awe of God


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ Is like the light of morning at sunrise, A morning without clouds— Through sunshine and rain [Bringing] vegetation out of the earth.”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ Is not my House established before God? For He has granted me an eternal pact, Drawn up in full and secured. Will He not cause all my success And [my] every desire to blossom?


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ But the wicked shall all Be raked aside like thorns; For no one will take them in his hand.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Whoever touches them Must arm himself with iron And the shaft of a spear; And they must be burned up on the spot.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ These are the names of David’s warriors: Josheb-basshebeth, a Tahchemonite, the chief officer—he is Adino the Eznite; [he wielded his spear] against eight hundred and slew them on one occasion.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ Next to him was Eleazar son of Dodo son of Ahohi. He was one of the three warriors with David when they defied the Philistines gathered there for battle. The Israelite soldiers retreated,


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ but he held his ground. He struck down Philistines until his arm grew tired and his hand stuck to his sword; and YHWH wrought a great victory that day. Then the troops came back to him—but only to strip [the slain].


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Next to him was Shammah son of Age the Ararite. The Philistines had gathered in force where there was a plot of ground full of lentils; and the troops fled from the Philistines.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² But [Shammah] took his stand in the middle of the plot and defended it, and he routed the Philistines. Thus YHWH wrought a great victory.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ Once, during the harvest, three of the thirty chiefs went down to David at the cave of Adullam, while a force of Philistines was encamped in the Valley of Rephaim.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ David was then in the stronghold, and a Philistine garrison was then at Bethlehem.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ David felt a craving and said, “If only I could get a drink of water from the cistern which is by the gate of Bethlehem!”


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ So the three warriors got through the Philistine camp and drew water from the cistern which is by the gate of Bethlehem, and they carried it back. But when they brought it to David he would not drink it, and he poured it out as a libation to YHWH.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ For he said, “YHWH forbid that I should do this! Can [I drink] the blood of the men who went at the risk of their lives?” So he would not drink it. Such were the exploits of the three warriors.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ Abishai, the brother of Joab son of Zeruiah, was head of another three. He once wielded his spear against three hundred and slew them.


------------------------------------------------------------------------

## II Samuel 23:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ He won a name among the three; since he was the most highly regarded among the three, he became their leader. However, he did not attain to the three.
