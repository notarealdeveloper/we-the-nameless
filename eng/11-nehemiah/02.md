------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ In the month of Nisan, in the twentieth year of King Artaxerxes, wine
was set before him; I took the wine and gave it to the king—I had never
been out of sorts in his presence.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² The king said to me, “How is it that you look bad, though you are not
ill? It must be bad thoughts.” I was very frightened,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ but I answered the king, “May the king live forever! How should I not
look bad when the city of the graveyard of my ancestors lies in ruins,
and its gates have been consumed by fire?”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ The king said to me, “What is your request?” With a prayer to the God
of Heaven,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ I answered the king, “If it please the king, and if your servant has
found favor with you, send me to Judah, to the city of my ancestors’
graves, to rebuild it.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ With the consort seated at his side, the king said to me, “How long
will you be gone and when will you return?” So it was agreeable to the
king to send me, and I gave him a date.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ Then I said to the king, “If it please the king, let me have letters
to the governors of the province of Beyond the River, directing them to
grant me passage until I reach Judah;



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ likewise, a letter to Asaph, the keeper of the King’s Park, directing
him to give me timber for roofing the gatehouses of the temple fortress
and the city walls and for the house I shall occupy.” The king gave me
these, thanks to my God’s benevolent care for me.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ When I came to the governors of the province of Beyond the River I
gave them the king’s letters. The king also sent army officers and
cavalry with me.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ When Sanballat the Horonite and Tobiah the Ammonite servant heard, it
displeased them greatly that someone had come, intent on improving the
condition of the Israelites.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ I arrived in Jerusalem. After I was there three days



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² I got up at night, I and a few men with me, and telling no one what
my God had put into my mind to do for Jerusalem, and taking no other
beast than the one on which I was riding,



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ I went out by the Valley Gate, at night, toward the Jackals’ Spring
and the Dung Gate; and I surveyed the walls of Jerusalem that were
breached, and its gates, consumed by fire.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ I proceeded to the Fountain Gate and to the King’s Pool, where there
was no room for the beast under me to continue.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ So I went up the wadi by night, surveying the wall, and, entering
again by the Valley Gate, I returned.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ The prefects knew nothing of where I had gone or what I had done,
since I had not yet divulged it to the Jews—the priests, the nobles, the
prefects, or the rest of the officials.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ Then I said to them, “You see the bad state we are in—Jerusalem lying
in ruins and its gates destroyed by fire. Come, let us rebuild the wall
of Jerusalem and suffer no more disgrace.”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ I told them of my God’s benevolent care for me, also of the things
that the king had said to me, and they said, “Let us start building!”
They were encouraged by [His] benevolence.



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ When Sanballat the Horonite and Tobiah the Ammonite servant and
Geshem the Arab heard, they mocked us and held us in contempt and said,
“What is this that you are doing? Are you rebelling against the king?”



------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Nehemiah 2:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ I said to them in reply, “The God of Heaven will grant us success,
and we, His servants, will start building. But you have no share or
claim or stake in Jerusalem!”
