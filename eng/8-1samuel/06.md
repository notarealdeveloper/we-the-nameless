------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And the ark of YHWH was in the country of the Philistines seven months.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And the Philistines called for the priests and the diviners, saying, What shall we do to the ark of YHWH? tell us wherewith we shall send it to his place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And they said, If you send away the ark of the God of Israel, send it not empty: but surely return him a trespass offering: then you shall be healed, and it shall be known to you why his hand is not removed from you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And said they, What shall be the trespass offering which we shall return to him? They answered, Five golden tumors, and five golden mice, according to the number of the lords of the Philistines: for one plague was on you all, and on your lords.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And so you shall make images of your tumors, and images of your mice that mar the land: and you shall give glory to the God of Israel: perhaps he'll lighten his hand from you, and from your gods, and from your land.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And so then do you harden your hearts, as the Egyptians and Pharaoh hardened their hearts? when he had wrought wonderfully among them, did they not let the people go, and they went away?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And therefore make a new cart, and take two milch cows, on which there has come no yoke, and tie the cows to the cart, and bring their calves home from them:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And take the ark of YHWH, and lay it on the cart: and put the gold objects, which you return him for a trespass offering, in a box by the side of it: and send it away, that it may go.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And see, if it goes up by the way of his own border to Beth-shemesh, then he has done us this great bad: but if not, then we shall know that it is not his hand that struck us: it was a chance that happened to us.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And the men did so: and took two milch cows, and tied them to the cart, and closed their calves at home:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And they laid the ark of YHWH on the cart, and the box with the mice of gold and the images of their tumors.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And the cows took the straight way to the way of Beth-shemesh, and went along the highway, lowing as they went, and turned not aside to the right hand or to the left: and the lords of the Philistines went after them to the border of Beth-shemesh.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And they of Beth-shemesh were reaping their wheat harvest in the valley: and they lifted up their eyes, and saw the ark, and rejoiced to see it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And the cart came into the field of Joshua, a Bethshemite, and stood there, where there was a great stone: and they clung the wood of the cart, and offered the cows a burnt offering to YHWH.

## 1 Samuel 6:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And the Levites took down the ark of YHWH, and the box that was with it, in which the gold objects were, and put them on the great stone: and the men of Beth-shemesh offered burnt offerings and sacrificed sacrifices the same day to YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 6:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And when the five lords of the Philistines had seen it, they returned to Ekron the same day.

## 1 Samuel 6:17

## 1 Samuel 6:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And the golden mice, according to the number of all the cities of the Philistines belonging to the five lords, both of fenced cities, and of country villages, even to the great stone of Abel, where they set down the ark of YHWH: which stone remains to this day in the field of Joshua, the Bethshemite.
