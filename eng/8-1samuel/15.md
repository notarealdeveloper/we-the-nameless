## 1 Samuel 15:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² And Samuel said, Has YHWH as great delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices, as in obeying the voice of YHWH? Here: to obey is better than sacrifice, and to listen than the fat of rams.

---

Footnote 15:22. to listen is better than sacrifice. Samuel does not abolish sacrifice. He puts obedience in front of ritual, which is exactly where Saul has not put it.
