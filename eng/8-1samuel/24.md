------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And David went up from from there, and lived in strong holds at Engedi.

## 1 Samuel 24:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And it was when Saul was returned from following the Philistines, that it was told him, saying, Here: David is in the wilderness of Engedi.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 24:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And Saul took three thousand chosen men out of all Israel, and went to seek David and his men on the rocks of the wild goats.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 24:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And he came to the sheepcotes by the way, where was a cave: and Saul went in to cover his feet: and David and his men remained in the sides of the cave.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 24:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And the men of David said to him, Here: the day of which YHWH said to you, Here: I'll deliver your enemy into your hand, that you may do to him as it shall seem good to you. Then David arose, and cut off the skirt of Saul’s robe quietly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 24:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And it came to pass afterward, that David’s heart struck him, because he had cut off Saul’s skirt.

---

Footnote 24:6. YHWH's anointed. David has Saul in his hand and refuses the hand. The phrase protects a king who has become dangerous, which is why the story is more interesting than revenge.

## 1 Samuel 24:9

## 1 Samuel 24:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ Here: this day your eyes have seen how that YHWH had delivered you today into my hand in the cave: and some bade me kill you: but my eye spared you: and I said, I'll not put forth my hand against my lord: for he is YHWH’s anointed.
