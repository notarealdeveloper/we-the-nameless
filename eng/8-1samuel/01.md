------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And there was one man of Ramathaimzophim, of mount Ephraim, and his name was Elkanah, the son of Jeroham, the son of Elihu, the son of Tohu, the son of Zuph, an Ephrathite:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And he had two wives: the name of the one was Hannah, and the name of the other Peninnah: and Peninnah had children, but Hannah had no children.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And this man went up out of his city yearly to bow down and to sacrifice to YHWH of Armies in Shiloh. And the two sons of Eli, Hophni and Phinehas, the priests of YHWH, were there.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And when the time was that Elkanah offered, he gave to Peninnah his wife, and to all her sons and her danythingers, portions:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And to Hannah he gave a worthy portion: for he loved Hannah: but YHWH had closed her womb.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And her adversary also provoked her bitterly, for to make her fret, because YHWH had closed her womb.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And as he did so year by year, when she went up to the house of YHWH, so she provoked her: therefore she wept, and didn't eat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ And said Elkanah her husband to her, Hannah, why do you weep? and why don't you eat? and why is your heart grieved? am not I better to you than ten sons?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And Hannah rose up after they had eaten in Shiloh, and after they had drunk. Now Eli the priest sat on a seat by a post of the palace of YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And she was in bitterness of life, and prayed to YHWH, and wept bitterly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And she vowed a vow, and said, O YHWH of Armies, if you'll indeed look on the affliction of your maidservant, and remember me, and not forget your maidservant, but will give to your maidservant a man child, then I'll give him to YHWH all the days of his life, and there shall no razor come on his head.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And it was as she continued praying before YHWH, that Eli marked her mouth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And Hannah, she spoke in her heart: only her lips moved, but her voice was not heard: therefore Eli thought she had been drunk.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ And Eli said to her, How long will you be drunk? put away your wine from you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And Hannah answered and said, No, my lord, I'm a woman of a sorrowful spirit: I've drunk neither wine nor strong drink, but have poured out my life before YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ Count not your maidservant for a danythinger of Belial: for out of the abundance of my complaint and grief have I spoken hitherto.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ And Eli answered and said, Go in peace: and the God of Israel grant you your petition that you've asked of him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And she said, Let your maidservant find favor in your eyes. So the woman went her way, and did eat, and her face was no more sad.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And they rose up in the morning early, and bowed down before YHWH, and returned, and came to their house to Ramah: and Elkanah knew Hannah his wife: and YHWH remembered her.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 1:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And so it came to pass, when the time had come about after Hannah had conceived, that she bore a son, and called his name Samuel, saying, Because I've asked him of YHWH.

---

Footnote 1:20. The name Samuel is made to sound like asking from YHWH. It is not an etymology for a lexicon. It is Hannah's theology in the shape of a name.
