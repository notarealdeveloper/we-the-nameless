## 1 Samuel 16:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And YHWH said to Samuel, How long will you mourn for Saul, seeing I've rejected him from reigning over Israel? fill your horn with oil, and go, I'll send you to Jesse the Bethlehemite: for I've provided me a king among his sons.

## 1 Samuel 16:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And Samuel said, How can I go? if Saul hear it, he'll kill me. And YHWH said, Take an heifer with you, and say, I'm come to sacrifice to YHWH.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 16:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ And call Jesse to the sacrifice, and I'll show you what you shall do: and you shall anoint to me him whom I name to you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 16:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And Samuel did that which YHWH spoke, and came to Bethlehem. And the elders of the town trembled at his coming, and said, Do you come in peace?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 16:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And he said, Peaceably: I'm come to sacrifice to YHWH: sanctify yourselves, and come with me to the sacrifice. And he sanctified Jesse and his sons, and called them to the sacrifice.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 16:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And it was when they had come, that he looked on Eliab, and said, Surely YHWH’s anointed is before him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 16:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And YHWH said to Samuel, Look not on his face, or on the height of his stature: because I've refused him: for YHWH sees not as man sees: for man looks on the outward appearance, but YHWH looks on the heart.

---

Footnote 16:7. The human sees by the eyes, and YHWH sees by the heart. The verse makes election a problem of vision before it makes it a problem of kingship.
