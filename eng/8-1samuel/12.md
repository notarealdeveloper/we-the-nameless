------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:1

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹ And Samuel said to all Israel, Here: I've listened to your voice in all that you said to me, and have made a king over you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:2

------------------------------------------------------------------------

² And now, here: the king walks before you: and I'm old and gray-headed: and, here: my sons are with you: and I've walked before you from my childhood to this day.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

³ Here: here I'm: witness against me before YHWH, and before his anointed: whose ox have I taken? or whose ass have I taken? or whom have I defrauded? whom have I oppressed? or of whose hand have I received any bribe to blind my eyes with it? and I'll restore it you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:4

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁴ And they said, You have not defrauded us, nor oppressed us, neither have you taken anything of any man’s hand.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:5

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁵ And he said to them, YHWH is witness against you, and his anointed is witness this day, that you've not found anything in my hand. And they answered, He is witness.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:6

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁶ And Samuel said to the people, It is YHWH that advanced Moses and Aaron, and that brought your fathers up out of the land of Egypt.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:7

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁷ And therefore stand still, that I may reason with you before YHWH of all the righteous acts of YHWH, which he did to you and to your fathers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:8

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁸ When Jacob had come into Egypt, and your fathers cried to YHWH, then YHWH sent Moses and Aaron, which brought forth your fathers out of Egypt, and made them live in this place.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:9

------------------------------------------------------------------------

⁹ And when they forgat YHWH their God, he sold them into the hand of Sisera, captain of the army of Hazor, and into the hand of the Philistines, and into the hand of the king of Moab, and they fought against them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:10

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁰ And they cried to YHWH, and said, We have sinned, because we've forsaken YHWH, and have served Baalim and Ashtaroth: but now deliver us from the hand of our enemies, and we'll serve you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:11

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹¹ And YHWH sent Jerubbaal, and Bedan, and Jephthah, and Samuel, and delivered you from the hand of your enemies on every side, and you dwelled safe.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:12

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹² And when you saw that Nahash the king of the children of Ammon came against you, you said to me, No: but a king shall reign over us: when YHWH your God was your king.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:13

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹³ And therefore here: the king whom you've chosen, and whom you've desired! and, here: YHWH has set a king over you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:14

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁴ If you'll fear YHWH, and serve him, and obey his voice, and not rebel against the commandment of YHWH, then shall both you and also the king that reigns over you continue following YHWH your God:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:15

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁵ And if you'll not obey the voice of YHWH, but rebel against the commandment of YHWH, then shall the hand of YHWH be against you, as it was against your fathers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:16

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁶ And therefore stand and see this great thing, which YHWH will do before your eyes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:17

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁷ Is it not wheat harvest today? I'll call to YHWH, and he shall send thunder and rain: that you may perceive and see that your wickedness is great, which you've done in the sight of YHWH, in asking you a king.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:18

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁸ And Samuel called to YHWH: and YHWH sent thunder and rain that day: and all the people greatly feared YHWH and Samuel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:19

------------------------------------------------------------------------

¹⁹ And all the people said to Samuel, Pray for your servants to YHWH your God, that we die not: for we've added to all our sins this bad, to ask us a king.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:20

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁰ And Samuel said to the people, Fear not: you've done all this wickedness: yet turn not aside from following YHWH, but serve YHWH with all your heart:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:21

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²¹ And turn you not aside: for then should you go after vain things, which cannot profit nor deliver: for they're vain.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:22

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²² For YHWH won't forsake his people for his great name’s sake: because it has pleased YHWH to make you his people.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:23

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²³ Moreover as for me, God forbid that I should sin against YHWH in ceasing to pray for you: but I'll teach you the good and the right way:

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:24

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁴ Only fear YHWH, and serve him in truth with all your heart: for consider how great things he has done for you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1 Samuel 12:25

------------------------------------------------------------------------

²⁵ And if you shall still do wickedly, you shall be consumed, both you and your king.
