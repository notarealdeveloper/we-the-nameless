\Verse{1}

		whom you'll serve; whether the gods that your fathers served
		that were on across the River, or the gods of the Amorites, in
		whose land you live: but as for me and my house, we'll serve
		YHWH.
	}
}
{
%	\footnote{
%		Footnote 24:15. as for me and my house. This is the most quoted line in Joshua,
%		usually made smoother than it is. In context it is not a pleasant motto for a
%		wall. It is a challenge after a history lesson and before a warning that the
%		people may not be able to do what they are promising.
%	}
