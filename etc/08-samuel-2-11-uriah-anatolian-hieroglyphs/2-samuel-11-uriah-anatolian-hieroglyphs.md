## Uriah Anatolian

The Hittites around this time used Anatolian hieroglyphs.

So we want to translate this:

“The Ark and Israel and Judah are located at Succoth, and my master Joab and Your Majesty’s men are camped in the open; how can I go home and eat and drink and sleep with my wife? As you live, by your very life, I will not do this!”

Into a series of pictographic jokes using the following orthography.
