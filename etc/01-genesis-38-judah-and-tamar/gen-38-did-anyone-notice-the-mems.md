
## Q

Can you find me any citations where someone has noted that Onan / Amnon and Solomon / Shelah are basically the same names (especially in Paleo Hebrew) with the M deleted, in one case a slight permutation (barely visible in this 𐤀𐤁𐤂𐤃𐤄𐤅𐤆𐤇𐤈𐤉𐤊𐤋𐤌𐤍𐤎𐤏𐤐𐤑𐤒𐤓𐤔𐤕 alphabet) and in the other case an exact match?

## A

Yes but not the Paleo comparison.

1. The exact citation: Rendsburg, 1986, p. 444, footnote 16

Gary A. Rendsburg, “David and His Circle in Genesis XXXVIII,” Vetus Testamentum 36, no. 4 (1986): 438–446, especially 443–444 and 444 n. 16. The freely accessible Rutgers PDF puts the crucial footnote on its seventh page.

2. An explicit nineteenth-century predecessor: Bernstein, quoted by Conway

Moncure Daniel Conway, Solomon and Solomonic Literature (Chicago: The Open Court Publishing Company, 1899), chapter VI, pp. 47–49, especially p. 49. Conway quotes a discussion by Aaron Bernstein that identifies both Onan with Amnon and Shelah with Solomon.
