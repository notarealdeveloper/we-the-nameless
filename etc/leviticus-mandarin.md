The really good hits

The Cartesian product produces a few combinations that work far better than chance ought to allow:

Mandarin	Literal meanings	Leviticus

鸦煨 yā-wēi	crow/bird + roast	bird sacrifice
芽味 yá-wèi	sprout + flavor/food	grain offerings / firstfruits
丫尾 yā-wěi	fork/branch + tail	fat tail
哑伪 yǎ-wěi	silent + false	false testimony
雅位 yǎ-wèi	august/proper + rank	priesthood/high priest
押违 yā-wéi	escort/detain + transgression	scapegoat
押委 yā-wěi	transfer/entrust + commission	putting guilt onto scapegoat
涯未 yá-wèi	edge + remainder	gleanings left at field edges
讶违 yà-wéi	shocking + violation/disobedience	prohibited acts / covenant violations
讶胃 yà-wèi	horrifying + stomach	cannibalism, Lev 26:29
呀畏 yā-wèi	AH! + fear	covenant terror


For what you're apparently building, 押違/押违 yāwéi for the scapegoat is probably the jackpot: 押 can mean detain, escort, take under guard, pledge/mortgage, while 違 is violate, transgress, disobey. So yā-wéi can be made to read almost like “escort + transgression.”
