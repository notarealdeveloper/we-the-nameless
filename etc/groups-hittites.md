## Hittites


| Hittites                                           | Where             | What they actually do / what is said about them                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hittites — promised land**                       | **Gen 15:20**     | Listed among the peoples whose land YHWH promises to Abram's descendants. No action or dialogue.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Hittites — inhabitants of promised land**        | **Exod 3:8**      | YHWH tells Moses that he will bring Israel into the land inhabited by the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Hittites — inhabitants of promised land**        | **Exod 3:17**     | Essentially repeats 3:8: YHWH promises to bring Israel from Egyptian oppression into the land of the Hittites and the other peoples.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Hittites — inhabitants of promised land**        | **Exod 13:5**     | Moses describes the land YHWH swore to the ancestors as the land of the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Hivites, and Jebusites.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Hittites — to be destroyed**                     | **Exod 23:23**    | YHWH's angel will lead Israel to the Hittites and other peoples, and YHWH says he will wipe them out.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Hittites — to be driven out**                    | **Exod 23:28**    | YHWH says he will send the hornet ahead of Israel to drive out the Hivites, Canaanites, and Hittites.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Hittites — to be driven out**                    | **Exod 33:2**     | After the golden calf, YHWH says he will send an angel ahead of Israel and drive out the Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Hittites — to be driven out**                    | **Exod 34:11**    | YHWH commands Israel to observe his commands and says he is driving out the Amorites, Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Hittites — one of the seven nations**            | **Deut 7:1–2**    | Hittites are one of seven nations YHWH will clear away before Israel. Israel is commanded to defeat them, place them under the ban, make no covenant with them, and show them no mercy.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Hittites — under the ban**                       | **Deut 20:16–18** | Israel is commanded not to leave alive anything that breathes in the nations' cities; the Hittites are explicitly included among the peoples to be completely destroyed, lest they teach Israel their religious practices.                                                                                                                               |
| **“Land of the Hittites”**                         | **Josh 1:4**      | The promised territory is described as extending from the wilderness and Lebanon as far as the great river Euphrates, including “all the land of the Hittites,” and west to the Mediterranean. This usage may mean a much larger northern/Syrian territory than the little Canaanite groups in the conquest lists.                                       |
| **Hittites — to be driven out**                    | **Josh 3:10**     | Joshua says Israel will know that the living God is among them because he will drive out the Canaanites, Hittites, Hivites, Perizzites, Girgashites, Amorites, and Jebusites.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Hittite kings hear about Israel**                | **Josh 9:1–2**    | The kings west of the Jordan—including the Hittites—hear what has happened and gather together to fight Joshua and Israel. This is one of the rare collective references where the Hittites actually *do* something.                                                                                                                                     |
| **Hittites — conquest population**                 | **Josh 11:3**     | Hittites are named among the northern peoples summoned into the coalition against Joshua. **Text-critical warning:** the Hittite/Hivite wording here is disputed in the textual tradition and scholarship.                                                                                                                                               |
| **Hittites — conquered population**                | **Josh 12:8**     | Listed among the peoples whose territories were taken by Joshua: Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Hittites — defeated by Israel**                  | **Josh 24:11**    | In Joshua's retrospective speech, the Hittites are among the peoples who fought Israel after the Jordan crossing and whom YHWH gave into Israel's hand.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Man from Bethel goes to “land of the Hittites”** | **Judg 1:26**     | A man from Luz/Bethel, spared after showing Joseph's troops the entrance to his city, goes to the “land of the Hittites,” builds another city, and names it Luz. Particularly interesting because “land of the Hittites” here sounds like an actual external geographical region rather than merely another Canaanite-list formula.                      |
| **Hittites live among Israel**                     | **Judg 3:5–6**    | Israelites live among the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites; they marry their daughters, give their own daughters to their sons, and worship their gods. So the Hittites were *not*, according to Judges itself, actually exterminated.                                                                                 |
| **Possible “land of the Hittites”**                | **2 Sam 24:6**    | **Text-critical case.** MT has an obscure place-name/text usually represented something like “Tahtim-hodshi,” but an old and influential emendation reads something like “the land of the Hittites toward Kadesh.” Don't count this as an unambiguous MT Hittite occurrence, but it is potentially very interesting.                                     |
| **Hittite remnant becomes forced labor**           | **1 Kgs 9:20–21** | Solomon conscripts the surviving non-Israelite population—including Hittites—whose descendants Israel had been unable to destroy completely, imposing forced labor on them.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Kings of the Hittites**                          | **1 Kgs 10:29**   | Solomon's merchants export Egyptian chariots and horses to the “kings of the Hittites” and kings of Aram. This sounds much more like the actual northern Syrian/Neo-Hittite political world than a generic Canaanite conquest list.                                                                                                                      |
| **Hittite women**                                  | **1 Kgs 11:1–2**  | Solomon loves many foreign women, including Hittite women. They are among the foreign peoples concerning whom YHWH had forbidden intermarriage because they might turn Israel toward their gods.                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Kings of the Hittites terrify Aram**             | **2 Kgs 7:6–7**   | During the siege of Samaria, YHWH makes the Arameans hear the sound of a huge army. They conclude that the king of Israel has hired the **kings of the Hittites and the kings of Egypt** against them, panic, abandon the entire camp, and run for their lives. This is probably the most historically suggestive collective Hittite reference in Kings. |
