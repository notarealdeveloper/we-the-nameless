
| Group                                               | Concrete thing you can give them                                                                                                                              | Commentary possibilities                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hittites / Neo-Hittites**                         | **Anatolian/Luwian hieroglyphs** alongside the older cuneiform tradition                                                                                      | “Those guys write with little dudes.” Have Israelites visually imitate 𔐀-style signs when talking about them.                                                                                |
| **Egyptians**                                       | Hieroglyphic, hieratic, later demotic; Egyptian names/titles; wildly distinctive theology and bureaucracy                                                     | Egyptian speech can occasionally become 𓀀𓂀𓃀𓅓; bureaucratic passages can become increasingly Egyptian-looking.                                                                             |
| **Philistines**                                     | Aegean-derived material culture; distinctive pottery, pork consumption, architecture; probable Aegean connections; later adoption of local alphabetic writing | Early Israelites can basically regard them as **weird overseas people**: different pots, food, weapons, architecture, names. “Those fucking sea people.”                                      |
| **Moabites**                                        | **Moabite language is extremely close to Hebrew.** Mesha Stele; essentially the same alphabetic culture                                                       | Best joke: Israelites expect foreigners and then discover that Moabites basically talk like them with a funny accent. **“Oh shit, I can understand him.”**                                    |
| **Ammonites**                                       | Ammonite is likewise a close Northwest Semitic relative of Hebrew; distinctive Ammonite names and monumental statuary                                         | Another “foreigners who suspiciously sound like us,” but with their own royal vocabulary/names.                                                                                               |
| **Edomites**                                        | Edomite language closely related to Hebrew; distinctive script; Qos as major national god                                                                     | Israelites meet supposedly alien Edomites and understand almost everything—then encounter names invoking **Qos** instead of YHWH.                                                             |
| **Phoenicians / Sidonians / Tyrians**               | **The alphabet guys.** Phoenician script, maritime trade, cedar, purple dye, monumental building, Mediterranean colonies                                      | Perfect recurring characterization: insanely connected merchant/sailor people whose writing looks irritatingly familiar. “They've got our letters but everything is sideways.”                |
| **Arameans**                                        | **Aramaic**, eventually the international language of enormous parts of the Near East; alphabetic script ancestral to later Jewish scripts                    | This one gives you an actual biblical joke: **2 Kgs 18:26** explicitly contrasts Aramaic with Judean/Hebrew because officials understand Aramaic but ordinary Jerusalemites supposedly don't. |
| **Assyrians**                                       | Akkadian in **cuneiform**; enormous palace reliefs; colossal winged bulls; brutally propagandistic royal inscriptions                                         | Assyrians should feel like encountering a civilization with **government paperwork made out of wedges and nightmare monsters guarding the doors.**                                            |
| **Babylonians**                                     | Akkadian cuneiform; astronomical/omen scholarship; enormous scribal tradition; sexagesimal mathematics                                                        | “These guys can predict an eclipse but need 47 wedges to write their name.” Excellent once Kings reaches Babylon.                                                                             |
| **Cushites**                                        | Egyptian cultural interaction; later Napatan/Meroitic cultures; Meroitic writing is later than most Primary History                                           | Be careful chronologically. But Cush can evoke the **Upper Nile/Nubian world**, Egyptian interaction, archery, very different geography/material culture.                                     |
| **Midianites**                                      | NW Arabian world; early alphabetic inscriptions in the wider region; mobile pastoralism and long-distance routes                                              | Great opportunity to make “Midian” mean **desert caravan/network people**, not merely “another -ite.”                                                                                         |
| **Amalekites**                                      | Unfortunately almost no securely identifiable Amalekite material culture or writing                                                                           | This absence itself matters. **Don't manufacture an Amalekite font.** Let them remain archaeologically elusive.                                                                               |
| **Canaanites**                                      | Cuneiform *and* early alphabetic writing; Ugaritic gives an extraordinary window onto Canaanite-ish language/religion                                         | One of the richest: alphabet experiments, Baal/El/Asherah cultural vocabulary, familiar Northwest Semitic language.                                                                           |
| **Ugaritians**                                      | **Alphabetic cuneiform!** An alphabet written using wedge-shaped signs: 𐎀𐎁𐎂…                                                                               | Perhaps second only to Anatolian hieroglyphs for comedy value: **“They invented an alphabet and then inexplicably kept the fucking wedges.”**                                                 |
| **Gibeonites / Hivites / Perizzites / Girgashites** | Often frustratingly little securely distinctive archaeological/linguistic information                                                                         | Don't force it. Their very opacity can become part of the commentary: “Nobody knows who the hell a Perizzite is either.”                                                                      |
