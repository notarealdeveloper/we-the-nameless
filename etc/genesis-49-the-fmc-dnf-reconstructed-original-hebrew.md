### So FMC/DNF think 49:3 originally looked roughly like

ראובן בכר  
את כח  
ראשית אן  
יתר עז

Which they understand as:

> Reuben, my firstborn  
> You are my strength  
> The prime of my manhood  
> The best of my might

The crucial thing is that **בכר / כח / אן / עז aren't shorter words**. FMC/DNF are deliberately backdating the spelling. They think the poem had four rhyming words pronounced approximately:

בכרי / כחי / אוני / עזי  
*-î / -î / -î / -î*

but that a very early written copy could spell those final vowels defectively.

### This is the BIG distinction

MT actually says:

פחז כמים אל תותר

roughly:

> Turbulent/reckless as water, you shall not excel.

FMC/DNF print:

`_____`  
`_____`

They therefore mean:

> **"Those MT words exist. We can see them. But we think they're the mangled remains of TWO ORIGINAL POETIC LINES, and we can't recover those lines."**

They are **NOT** saying:

> "Our Masoretic manuscript has a physical blank here."

### So MT has

פחז כמים אל תותר  
כי עלית משכבי אביך  
אז חללת יצועי עלה

Approximately:

> Reckless as water, you shall not excel,
> because you went up to your father's beds;
> then you profaned my couch—he went up.

And that last **עלה** is famously WTF.

### FMC/DNF instead reconstruct

כ עלת <על> משכבי [ ]

Meaning approximately:

> [lost/corrupt line]

And suddenly the second half is a nice balanced **3:3** couplet.
