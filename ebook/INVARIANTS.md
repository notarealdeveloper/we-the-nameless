# Ebook invariants

- Genesis 2:25 deliberately appears immediately after the Genesis 3 heading and before Genesis 3:1. Its displayed reference and verse anchor must remain Genesis 2:25 (`genesis-2-25`), regardless of its source file or presentation chapter.
- The `hlA`, `hlB`, and `hlC` highlights use the lite TeX screens (12% black, red, and blue on white) and preserve the surrounding foreground ink.
- The ביום in Genesis 2:4 commentary is unpointed; the source verse retains its Hebrew pointing.
