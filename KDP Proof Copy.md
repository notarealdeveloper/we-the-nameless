
Test Printing!

KDP calls it a “proof copy.”

You upload the complete paperback/hardcover, leave it unpublished, approve it in KDP's Print Previewer, and then order a physical copy of the actual printed book. 

For We / Genesis, the workflow is basically:

1. Finish the KDP setup enough to upload the interior PDF and cover.

2. Launch Print Previewer and approve it.

3. Do not click Publish.

4. Back on the Bookshelf, hit … → Request Printed Proofs.

5. Order 1–5 copies.

6. Amazon prints and ships them to you.

7. Inspect the physical thing—colors, margins, gutter, tiny Paleo-Hebrew, Egyptian glyphs, image quality, etc.

8. Make whatever changes you want, upload new files, and repeat if necessary.

9. Only then hit Publish. 

The proof is essentially the real manufactured book, except KDP puts a “Not for Resale” watermark on it and uses a special barcode rather than the retail ISBN barcode. 

You pay the printing cost + shipping, rather than your eventual retail price. Proofs don't qualify for Prime/free shipping. 

And importantly, you absolutely do not have to publish while waiting for the proof. Amazon explicitly says you can leave the book in Draft, receive and inspect the proof, revise it, order another proof, etc. 

For this particular book, I would 100% get one before publishing. There are enough unusual typography/color/layout things in it that the physical KDP output is worth seeing rather than trusting the PDF/Print Previewer alone.
