\Chapter{30}

\Verse{1}
{
	\hP{וַיֹּאמֶר מֹשֶׁה אֶל בְּנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל כְּכֹל אֲשֶׁר צִוָּה יְהֹוָה אֶת מֹשֶׁה׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		And Moses said it to the children of Israel, according
		to everything that YHWH commanded Moses.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{2}
{
	\hP{וַיְדַבֵּר מֹשֶׁה אֶל רָאשֵׁי הַמַּטּוֹת לִבְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל לֵאמֹר זֶה הַדָּבָר אֲשֶׁר צִוָּה יְהֹוָה׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		And Moses spoke to the heads of the tribes of the
		children of Israel, saying, “This is the thing that YHWH
		commanded:
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{3}
{
	\hP{אִישׁ כִּי יִדֹּר נֶדֶר לַיהֹוָה אוֹ הִשָּׁבַע שְׁבֻעָה לֶאְסֹר אִסָּר עַל נַפְשׁוֹ לֹא יַחֵל דְּבָרוֹ כְּכל הַיֹּצֵא מִפִּיו יַעֲשֶׂה׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		A man who will make a vow to YHWH or has sworn an oath to
		make a restriction on himself shall not desecrate his
		word. He shall do it according to everything that comes
		out of his mouth.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{4}
{
	\hP{וְאִשָּׁה כִּי תִדֹּר נֶדֶר לַיהֹוָה וְאָסְרָה אִסָּר בְּבֵית אָבִיהָ בִּנְעֻרֶיהָ׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		“And a woman who will make a vow to YHWH and made a
		restriction in her father’s house in her youth,
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{5}
{
	\hP{וְשָׁמַע אָבִיהָ אֶת נִדְרָהּ וֶאֱסָרָהּ אֲשֶׁר אָסְרָה עַל נַפְשָׁהּ וְהֶחֱרִישׁ לָהּ אָבִיהָ וְקָמוּ כּל נְדָרֶיהָ וְכל אִסָּר אֲשֶׁר אָסְרָה עַל נַפְשָׁהּ יָקוּם׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		and her father hears her vow and her restriction that she
		made on herself, and her father keeps quiet to her, then
		all her vows shall stand, and every restriction that she
		made on herself shall stand.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{6}
{
	\hP{וְאִם הֵנִיא אָבִיהָ אֹתָהּ בְּיוֹם שׁמְעוֹ כּל נְדָרֶיהָ וֶאֱסָרֶיהָ אֲשֶׁר אָסְרָה עַל נַפְשָׁהּ לֹא יָקוּם וַיהֹוָה יִסְלַח לָהּ כִּי הֵנִיא אָבִיהָ אֹתָהּ׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		But if her father held her back on the day he heard all
		her vows and her restrictions that she made on herself, it
		will not stand. And YHWH will forgive her because her
		father held her back.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{7}
{
	\hP{וְאִם הָיוֹ תִהְיֶה לְאִישׁ וּנְדָרֶיהָ עָלֶיהָ אוֹ מִבְטָא שְׂפָתֶיהָ אֲשֶׁר אָסְרָה עַל נַפְשָׁהּ׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		“And if she will have a husband while her vows or the
		thing she let out of her lips with which she restricted
		herself are on her,
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{8}
{
	\hP{וְשָׁמַע אִישָׁהּ בְּיוֹם שׁמְעוֹ וְהֶחֱרִישׁ לָהּ וְקָמוּ נְדָרֶיהָ וֶאֱסָרֶהָ אֲשֶׁר אָסְרָה עַל נַפְשָׁהּ יָקֻמוּ׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		and her husband hears and keeps quiet on the day he heard
		it, then her vows will stand, and her restrictions that
		she made on herself will stand.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{9}
{
	\hP{וְאִם בְּיוֹם שְׁמֹעַ אִישָׁהּ יָנִיא אוֹתָהּ וְהֵפֵר אֶת נִדְרָהּ אֲשֶׁר עָלֶיהָ וְאֵת מִבְטָא שְׂפָתֶיהָ אֲשֶׁר אָסְרָה עַל נַפְשָׁהּ וַיהֹוָה יִסְלַח לָהּ׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		But if, on the day her husband hears it, he will hold her
		back and will break her vow that is on her and what was
		let out of her lips by which she restricted herself, then
		YHWH will forgive her.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{10}
{
	\hP{וְנֵדֶר אַלְמָנָה וּגְרוּשָׁה כֹּל אֲשֶׁר אָסְרָה עַל נַפְשָׁהּ יָקוּם עָלֶיהָ׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		“And a widow’s or divorced woman’s vow, everything by
		which she has restricted herself, shall stand regarding
		her.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{11}
{
	\hP{וְאִם בֵּית אִישָׁהּ נָדָרָה אוֹ אָסְרָה אִסָּר עַל נַפְשָׁהּ בִּשְׁבֻעָה׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		“And if she vowed or made a restriction on herself by an
		oath at her husband’s house,
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{12}
{
	\hP{וְשָׁמַע אִישָׁהּ וְהֶחֱרִשׁ לָהּ לֹא הֵנִיא אֹתָהּ וְקָמוּ כּל נְדָרֶיהָ וְכל אִסָּר אֲשֶׁר אָסְרָה עַל נַפְשָׁהּ יָקוּם׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		and her husband heard it and kept quiet to her—he did not
		hold her back—then all her vows shall stand, and every
		restriction that she made on herself shall stand.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{13}
{
	\hP{וְאִם הָפֵר יָפֵר אֹתָם אִישָׁהּ בְּיוֹם שׁמְעוֹ כּל מוֹצָא שְׂפָתֶיהָ לִנְדָרֶיהָ וּלְאִסַּר נַפְשָׁהּ לֹא יָקוּם אִישָׁהּ הֲפֵרָם וַיהֹוָה יִסְלַח לָהּ׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		But if her husband broke them on the day he heard all that
		came out of her lips for her vows and for restricting
		herself, it shall not stand. Her husband had broken them,
		and YHWH will forgive her.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{14}
{
	\hP{כּל נֵדֶר וְכל שְׁבֻעַת אִסָּר לְעַנֹּת נָפֶשׁ אִישָׁהּ יְקִימֶנּוּ וְאִישָׁהּ יְפֵרֶנּוּ׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		Every vow and every oath of restriction to degrade
		oneself: her husband shall make it stand, and her husband
		shall break it.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{15}
{
	\hP{וְאִם הַחֲרֵשׁ יַחֲרִישׁ לָהּ אִישָׁהּ מִיּוֹם אֶל יוֹם וְהֵקִים אֶת כּל נְדָרֶיהָ אוֹ אֶת כּל אֱסָרֶיהָ אֲשֶׁר עָלֶיהָ הֵקִים אֹתָם כִּי הֶחֱרִשׁ לָהּ בְּיוֹם שׁמְעוֹ׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		And if her husband will keep quiet in regard to her from
		that day to the next, then he has made all her vows and
		all her restrictions that are on her stand. He has made
		them stand because he kept quiet to her on the day he
		heard it.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{16}
{
	\hP{וְאִם הָפֵר יָפֵר אֹתָם אַחֲרֵי שׁמְעוֹ וְנָשָׂא אֶת עֲוֺנָהּ׃}
}
{
	\eP{
		And if he will break them after he has heard them, then he
		shall bear her crime.”
	}
}
{
%	\footnote{
%		he shall bear her crime. Having power over another person also means
%		taking responsibility for that person’s actions. If a man causes his
%		wife to violate a vow she made, then it is he who must live with the
%		consequences.
%	}
}

\Verse{17}
{
	\hOther{אֵלֶּה הַחֻקִּים אֲשֶׁר צִוָּה יְהֹוָה אֶת מֹשֶׁה בֵּין אִישׁ לְאִשְׁתּוֹ בֵּין אָב לְבִתּוֹ בִּנְעֻרֶיהָ בֵּית אָבִיהָ׃}
}
{
	\eOther{
		These are the laws that YHWH commanded Moses between a man
		and his wife, between a father and his daughter in her
		youth at her father’s house.
	}
}
{
%	\footnote{
%		between a man and his wife, between a father and his daughter. This law,
%		like several others in the Torah, stands at a juncture in the
%		development of the balance between women and men. On one hand, it
%		accepts the fact that men are in a position of such power over women
%		that they can prevent their daughters and wives from keeping vows that
%		they have made to their God—and it gives this power the force of law.
%		But, on the other hand, it places a tremendous burden of responsibility
%		on the men. If they dare to prevent their daughters and wives from
%		keeping these vows, they bear the guilt themselves for whatever comes of
%		it.
%	}
}
