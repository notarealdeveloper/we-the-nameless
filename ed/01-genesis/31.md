\Verse{53}
{
	\hE{אֱלֹהֵי אַבְרָהָם וֵאלֹהֵי נָחוֹר יִשְׁפְּטוּ בֵינֵינוּ אֱלֹהֵי אֲבִיהֶם וַיִּשָּׁבַע יַעֲקֹב בְּפַחַד אָבִיו יִצְחָק׃}

	\eE{
		Let Abraham’s God and Nahor’s gods, their father’s gods,
		judge between us.” And Jacob swore by the Awe of his
		father, Isaac.
	}
}
{
	\aC{
		Laban says ``Let Abraham's God and Nahor's gods judge between us.''

		Jacob doesn't swear by any of those.

		He swears by his dad Laughter's nightmare.%
		\fB{
			\heb{פחד} (\emph{paḥad}): fear, dread, terror, panic, alarm—the
			kind of fear that suddenly seizes you. It can mean either the
			terror itself or the terrifying thing that causes it. Here it
			appears in place of the term ``God,'' to refer to YHWH's
			relationship to Isaac, a relationship that was, at times,
			at least moderately startling, to put it mildly.
		}
		\fC{
			REF: ``God'' and ``gods'' are both \heb{אלהים}, so exactly
			how many gods Laban means here is unclear.
		}
	}
