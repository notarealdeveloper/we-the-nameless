{
	\eDtrH{
		In the thirty-seventh year of the exile of King Jehoiachin of
		Judah, on the twenty-seventh day of the twelfth month, King
		Evil-merodach of Babylon, in the year he became king, took note of
		King Jehoiachin of Judah and released him from prison.
	}
}

\Verse{28}
{
	\hDtrH{וַיְדַבֵּר אִתּוֹ טֹבוֹת וַיִּתֵּן אֶת־כִּסְאוֹ מֵעַל כִּסֵּא הַמְּלָכִים אֲשֶׁר אִתּוֹ בְּבָבֶל׃}
}
{
	\eDtrH{
		He spoke kindly to him, and gave him a throne above those of other
		kings who were with him in Babylon.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{29}
{
	\hDtrH{וְשִׁנָּא אֵת בִּגְדֵי כִלְאוֹ וְאָכַל לֶחֶם תָּמִיד לְפָנָיו כּל־יְמֵי חַיָּיו׃}
}
{
	\eDtrH{
		His prison garments were removed, and [Jehoiachin] received
		regular rations by his favor for the rest of his life.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{30}
{
	\hDtrH{וַאֲרֻחָתוֹ אֲרֻחַת תָּמִיד נִתְּנָה־לּוֹ מֵאֵת הַמֶּלֶךְ דְּבַר־יוֹם בְּיוֹמוֹ כֹּל יְמֵי חַיָּו׃}
}
{
	\eDtrH{
		A regular allotment of food was given him at the instance of the
		king—an allotment for each day—all the days of his life.
	}
}
