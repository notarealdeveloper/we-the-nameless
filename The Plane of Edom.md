
The son of J went to Edom again.

And the Son of J was on a plane in Edom in the south.

Then a woman came to him, she was a streward who hosted many people on the planes.

And the woman said to him,

There is a place further up, in the middle between two people.

And the Son of J said to the woman, "Why do you tell this to me and not the others on this plane?," for the Son of J knew not why the woman was treating him as different from the others.

Then a man seated next to him on the plane said, "Because you're the best person here." The man's name was J.C. Amalick.\fC{Perhaps an Amalekite name, a group in the OT who died out long ago.}

And the Son of J spoke to the man, saying, "If you believe I am the best of all the people here, then I am dead. If I am alive, is this not like the story of the Showing of the True Man.
