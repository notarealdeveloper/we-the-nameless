
| Group                                 | Israelite stereotype / running joke                                                                                                              | Basis                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Confidence                                       |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Hittites / Neo-Hittites**           | **Those northern guys with kings everywhere and little-picture writing.** “Why is that man a letter?”                                            | Biblical texts know both ordinary “Hittites” and powerful **kings of the Hittites** (1 Kgs 10:29; 2 Kgs 7:6). Anatolian hieroglyphs are archaeologically real, though the Bible never comments on them.                                                                                                   | **Mixed:** kings textual; writing reconstructed  |
| **Egyptians**                         | **Rich, clean-shaven, bread-hating bureaucrats who think shepherds are disgusting.** Also: horses. So many fucking horses.                       | Genesis explicitly calls shepherds abhorrent to Egyptians (Gen 46:34) and stresses Egyptian/non-Egyptian dining separation (43:32). Egypt repeatedly supplies horses/chariots (Deut 17:16; 1 Kgs 10:28–29). Bureaucratic stereotype fits Egyptian textual culture extremely well.                         | **Strong**                                       |
| **Philistines**                       | **Uncircumcised coastal assholes with better weapons.**                                                                                          | “Uncircumcised Philistine(s)” is an actual biblical insult (Judg 14:3; 15:18; 1 Sam 14:6; 17:26, 36; 31:4). 1 Sam 13:19–22 famously associates Philistine dominance with control of smithing/iron weapons.                                                                                                | **Very strong**                                  |
| **Moabites**                          | **Incest cousins who worship that asshole Chemosh.**                                                                                             | Gen 19:30–38 derives Moab from Lot impregnating his daughter; Num 21:29 calls Moab the people of Chemosh. Mesha independently calls himself a devotee of Chemosh.                                                                                                                                         | **Extremely strong—and viciously polemical**     |
| **Ammonites**                         | **The other incest cousins. Also: Molech/Milcom people.**                                                                                        | Same Lot-and-daughters origin story (Gen 19:30–38). 1 Kgs 11 associates Ammon with Milcom/Molech. Deut 23:4–5 complains that Ammon and Moab wouldn't provide Israel bread and water.                                                                                                                      | **Strong**                                       |
| **Edomites**                          | **Our hairy red asshole brother who lives in the rocks.**                                                                                        | Practically handed to us by Genesis: Esau is hairy (Gen 25:25), associated through wordplay with red/Edom (25:30), and explicitly made Israel's brother. Edom's mountainous territory makes the rock/highland element geographically apt.                                                                 | **Very strong**                                  |
| **Phoenicians / Tyrians / Sidonians** | **Rich coastal merchants who can build absolutely anything and will sell absolutely anything.**                                                  | Hiram supplies cedar, craftsmen and maritime expertise to David/Solomon (2 Sam 5:11; 1 Kgs 5; 7; 9–10). Later prophetic texts make Tyre the archetypal fantastically wealthy trading city (esp. Ezek 27).                                                                                                 | **Very strong**                                  |
| **Sidonians specifically**            | **Dangerously fancy foreigners with extremely effective gods/women.**                                                                            | Jezebel is explicitly Sidonian royalty (1 Kgs 16:31); Solomon follows Ashtoreth “the goddess of the Sidonians” (11:5, 33). Judges also remembers Israel serving Sidonian gods.                                                                                                                            | **Strong, but heavily polemical**                |
| **Arameans / Syrians**                | **Our northern cousins who keep invading us and somehow everybody important can speak their language.**                                          | Constant Israel-Aram warfare dominates Kings. 2 Kgs 18:26 explicitly shows educated Judean officials understanding Aramaic while ordinary Jerusalemites supposedly understand Judean instead.                                                                                                             | **Strong**                                       |
| **Assyrians**                         | **Terrifying imperial assholes who brag for a living.**                                                                                          | Kings and Isaiah actually give the Assyrian spokesman an extended arrogant imperial speech (2 Kgs 18–19). Assyrian royal inscriptions independently confirm that spectacular boasting, intimidation and violence were central to imperial rhetoric.                                                       | **Extremely strong**                             |
| **Babylonians**                       | **Rich eastern nerds who look at everything, write everything down, and eventually steal everything that isn't nailed down.**                    | In 2 Kgs 20, Babylonian envoys visit Hezekiah and are shown *everything* in his treasury; Isaiah immediately predicts Babylon will eventually carry it all away. Scribal/astronomical “nerd” component comes from Mesopotamian evidence rather than biblical stereotype.                                  | **Mixed, but excellent**                         |
| **Cushites**                          | **Very dark-skinned southerners from impossibly far away; famous runners/messengers and formidable warriors.**                                   | Jer 13:23 explicitly invokes the Cushite's skin color. A Cushite runner carries news to David in 2 Sam 18:19–32. Cush/Ethiopia also evokes distant southern power in prophetic literature.                                                                                                                | **Mostly textual**                               |
| **Midianites**                        | **Desert people with camels, caravans, gold jewelry, and confusing family relationships with us.**                                               | Gen 37 has Midianite/Ishmaelite traders carrying goods toward Egypt. Judg 6–8 gives Midian enormous camel forces and explicitly comments on their gold ornaments (8:24–26). Moses also marries into a Midianite priestly family.                                                                          | **Very strong**                                  |
| **Amalekites**                        | **Those fucking raiders who attack you when you're exhausted.**                                                                                  | Almost literally Deut 25:17–18: Amalek attacks Israel on the road and strikes the weak/stragglers when Israel is weary. 1 Sam 30 depicts Amalekites raiding settlements and carrying people away.                                                                                                         | **Extremely strong**                             |
| **Canaanites**                        | **The people who were here before us, with their cities, gods, sex, and irritating tendency not to disappear when God says we killed them all.** | Biblical conquest ideology stereotypically associates Canaanites with forbidden religious practices; Judges simultaneously depicts Israelites continuing to live among them. The “sex” stereotype is particularly prominent in later biblical polemic but should not be mistaken for neutral ethnography. | **Strong as biblical polemic**                   |
| **Amorites**                          | **Huge old-time mountain people whom our ancestors supposedly smashed.**                                                                         | Amorite kings Sihon and Og become legendary defeated enemies (Num 21; Deut 2–3). Amos 2:9 describes the Amorite hyperbolically as **tall as cedars and strong as oaks**.                                                                                                                                  | **Very strong**                                  |
| **Bashan / Og's people**              | **Fucking giants.**                                                                                                                              | Og is the last of the Rephaim in Deut 3:11, complete with the wonderfully unnecessary dimensions of his enormous bed/couch. Bashan subsequently carries associations with strength, cattle and imposing terrain.                                                                                          | **Basically supplied by the Bible**              |
| **Gibeonites**                        | **Sneaky bastards who will put on old shoes and moldy bread to get you to sign something.**                                                      | That's essentially the plot of Josh 9: they disguise themselves as travelers from far away and trick Israel into making a treaty.                                                                                                                                                                         | **Extremely strong**                             |
| **Hivites**                           | **...which ones are the Hivites again?**                                                                                                         | They occur mainly in conventional population lists, although individual narratives identify Shechem and the Gibeonites as Hivites. We know frustratingly little that would justify a distinctive cultural stereotype.                                                                                     | **Deliberately unknown**                         |
| **Perizzites**                        | **Country people, maybe? Nobody knows.**                                                                                                         | Biblical usage often pairs them with Canaanites; the traditional connection with unwalled/rural settlements is plausible from the name but uncertain. There is no securely identifiable “Perizzite civilization” archaeologically.                                                                        | **Very uncertain**                               |
| **Girgashites**                       | **Who?**                                                                                                                                         | Almost nothing narrative is said about them. They're overwhelmingly a name in conventional lists of pre-Israelite peoples.                                                                                                                                                                                | **Excellent running joke; terrible ethnography** |
| **Jebusites**                         | **Those assholes in Jerusalem who think their fortress is literally impossible to capture.**                                                     | 2 Sam 5:6 has the Jebusites taunt David that even “the blind and the lame” could keep him out. David takes the city anyway.                                                                                                                                                                               | **Extremely strong**                             |
| **Ishmaelites**                       | **Camel caravan cousins carrying expensive smelly shit to Egypt.**                                                                               | Gen 37:25 depicts an Ishmaelite caravan from Gilead with camels carrying gum, balm and myrrh to Egypt. Judges associates Ishmaelites with gold ornaments.                                                                                                                                                 | **Strong**                                       |
| **Kenites**                           | **Desert in-laws who are weirdly good friends to have.**                                                                                         | Moses' Midianite/Kenite relations complicate the genealogy, but Judges depicts Kenites as connected to Moses' father-in-law (Judg 1:16; 4:11). Jael the Kenite famously kills Sisera while Israel benefits.                                                                                               | **Fairly strong**                                |
| **Rephaim / Anakim**                  | **GIANTS. Nope. Nope nope nope.**                                                                                                                | Num 13:28–33 makes the Anakim terrifying giants in the spies' report; Deut connects various enormous ancient peoples with Rephaim.                                                                                                                                                                        | **Extremely strong**                             |
