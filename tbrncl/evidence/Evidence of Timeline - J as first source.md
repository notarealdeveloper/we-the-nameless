| Sparse Angelology | J generally | J uses fewer developed angels/demons/cosmic intermediaries. | Fits earlier stage before later theological elaboration. |
| No Resurrection Theology | J generally | J lacks developed afterlife/resurrection speculation. | Consistent with earlier Israelite religion. |
| Oral-Formulaic Features | J narratives | Repetition, type-scenes, dialogue patterns resemble oral epic style. | Suggests roots in older oral tradition. |
| Friedman’s Dating | Friedman and source critics | Friedman places J in early monarchy, likely 10th–9th century BCE Judah. | J best fits earliest major prose source in Torah. |
