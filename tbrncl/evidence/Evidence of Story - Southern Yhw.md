## 2. External Sources

| Approx. Date | Source | Quotation / Text | Southern Region Mentioned | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14th c. BC | Soleb inscription | Egyptian: "t3 š3sw yhw3" ("land/group of the Shasu of Yhw") | Southern Transjordan / Edom region | Earliest plausible extra-biblical reference to Yahweh |
| 13th c. BC | Amarah-West inscriptions | Another Egyptian reference to "Shasu of Yhw" | Southern Levant | Reinforces existence of Yhw-associated southern nomadic groups |
| 13th c. BC | Papyrus Anastasi VI | Refers to permitting "the Shasu tribes of Edom" to enter Egyptian territory during famine | Edom | Demonstrates real southern nomadic populations in exactly the region tied to early Yahweh traditions |
| 13th–12th c. BC | Ugaritic texts | Yahweh absent from known Ugaritic pantheon lists dominated by El, Baal, Athirat, Anat, etc. | Canaan proper lacks Yahweh | Suggests Yahweh was not originally a standard northern Canaanite deity |
