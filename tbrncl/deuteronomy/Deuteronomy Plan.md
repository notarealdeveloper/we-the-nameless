
When we get to Dudetheyreontome, we do the same thing that the Deuteronomistic Historian did.

That is, we cut up the old bibles and intersperse them with our new text.

That will let us _include_
- Deuteronomy 
- Joshua
- Judges
- Samuel 
- Kings

in Dudetheyreontome as needed, while also allowing us to give the reader a break because Deuteronomy is pretty goddamn boring at times.
