
## There is more humor in the Old Testament than in the New Testament

> _Now there is humor in the Old Testament, and that’s striking. People have noted that there is more humor in the Old Testament than in the New Testament. There are better jokes about the New Testament than there are about the Old Testament—I know much better Jesus jokes than Moses jokes, I mean that’s just a fact. But I suspect that those are related facts._
> 
> _The New Testament comes across as a much more serious book, in many ways, than the Old—we could argue that—but there are fewer scenes that are played for comedy, let us say, in the New Testament than in the Old. And it is precisely things that are taken seriously that we are more likely to joke about._
> 
> -Richard Elliot Friedman, The Hebrew Bible, Lecture 16, The Kingdom of Israel
