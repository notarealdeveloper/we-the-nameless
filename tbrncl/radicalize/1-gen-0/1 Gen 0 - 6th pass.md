# J Gen 1:26 — Rivers flow from the eyes of Rahab

| BCV | Content |
|------|------|
| J Gen 1:26 (Heb) | ומעיני רהב יצאו נהרות בארץ |
| J Gen 1:26 (Eng) | And from the eyes of Rahab rivers came forth in the earth. |
