
## J's Genesis 1

Reconstruction of J's original creation story, with textual evidence for each verse.

### J Gen 0:1 - In the day YHWH made earth and skies

| BCV             | Content                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| J Gen 0:1 (Heb) | ביום עשות יהוה ארץ ושמים                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| J Gen 0:1 (Eng) | In the day that YHWH made earth and skies.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Genesis 2:4b    | These are the generations of the skies and the earth when they were created, ==in the day that YHWH God made earth and skies.==                                                                                                                    |
| Note            | Unlike P's formula (heavens and earth), J preserves the order earth and skies. We assume, following Friedman, that the word God (אלהים) here was added by the redactor to soften the transition between P's Genesis 1 and J's Genesis 2:4b onward. |
|                 |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

### J Gen 0:27 - Rivers flow from the eyes of Rahab

| BCV | Content |
|------|------|
