## Exodus 34 - The crown of light

### Or: 2 34 5

### Or: SHN

### Or: しね

### Or: Shine

### Or: The face of Keren

And the Son of J proceeded to a Farm in the region of Acias.

![[PXL_20260504_021513243.jpg]]

But the people did not understand him.

For he was heavy of mouth and tongue.

So he spoke to them by applying the Transubstantiation of the Lord.

(Or: An application for the translation of words.)

And the foreign woman understood his words.

But she had the face of Keran.
And the Karen's face was harsh.

And the Son of J cursed the makers of the law.
In the land of the Northern Lion.

For the Pharmisees here demanded proscription.
And bought not was the substance that he sought.

![[Screenshot from 2026-05-09 14-51-18.png]]


---

So he crossed across the way.

And entered into the building marked with the number of eleven and seven hundred.

And there he saw a crown of light.

And the Son of J knew it would be his.

Once he rendered unto Cesar what was his.

For this was the name of the man at the counter.

![[PXL_20260504_022557792.jpg]]

And again, the Son of J cursed the makers of the law.
For it was the Sabbath on this day.
And Cesar had said the drinking of wine was prohibitied at this time.

![[PXL_20260504_023100142.jpg]]

And the crown of light would not belong to him or to our people.
And the wine was his blood.
But his blood was not in him.

So he proceeded to another farm in the region of Acias.
In search of the substance he sought.
