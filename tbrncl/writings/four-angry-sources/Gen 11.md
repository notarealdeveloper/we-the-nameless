
---

(No one speaks. All eyes on J.)

---

(J doesn’t look at them. He just begins.)

---

J: And the whole world was of one language and one speech.

---

J:

And the united people moved east.

And they found a plain in Sumer.

And they settled there.

---

J:

And they said to each other—

> Come.
> 
> Let’s build a city.
>
> And a tower.
>
> That will reach to the heavens.
>
> And let’s make a name for ourselves.
> 
> So that We do not forget.
> 
> So that We can hear and know each other.
> 
> So that We do not forget We are one.
> 
> And become scattered across the earth.
> 
> Unable to hear one another's words.

---

(short pause)

---

J:

And Yahweh came down.

To see what the humans had built.

Now that they were One People.

And Yahweh said—

---

(slight shift — now quoting)

---

J:

Look

One people.

One language.

---

(small pause)

---

J:

And if this is what they do.

Nothing they plan will be beyond them.

For if they work together

They will no longer need us

---

(J continues)

---

J:

Come.

Let us go down.

And confuse their language.

So they won’t understand each other.

---

(J looks up)

---

So Yahweh scattered them

From there

Across the earth

They stopped building the city

That is why it is called Babel.

"Gate of God."

Because there he confused the language of all the people of the world.

And the people forgot how to hear each other.

---

(long silence)

---

P (quietly): What sort of god is this?

---

J (without looking up): There is only one god, P.

---

P (firm): Answer my question.

---

(J finally looks at him)

---

J:

The creator of the universe.

He's the inspiration for all my writing.

I don't think there's any disagreement between us.

My Yahweh is the first author.

The one I think about all day.

Everything I do here is because of him.

I can say the same for you.

I'm often not sure of the quality of my work.

But I'm sure today's chapter is a faithful tribute to him.

We all know his name.

But we would never dare to say it aloud.

My god is the author of Genesis 1.

So I'm not quite sure what your question is.

I have no doubt that He's your god too.

---

(silence)
