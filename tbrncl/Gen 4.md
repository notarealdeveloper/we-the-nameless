

R: The next section.

---

(J begins, flat.)

---

J: The woman bears a son.

I have acquired a man with Yahweh.

His name is Cain.


---

(beat)

---

J: She bears another.

Abel.

---

(pause)

---

J: Once there were two brothers.

One worked the ground.

One kept flocks.

---

J: They brought offerings.

Yahweh regarded Abel.

Not Cain.

---

(no pause)

---

J: Cain rose.

He killed his brother.

---

(silence)

---

J: Yahweh said:

“Where is your brother?”

---

J:
“I do not know.
Am I my brother’s keeper?”

---

(beat)

---

J:

Your brother’s blood cries out from the ground.

You are cursed.

You will wander.”

---

(pause)

---

J: Cain said:

“They will kill me.”

---

J: Yahweh said:

“No.

Whoever kills Cain—

sevenfold.”

---

(quiet)

---

J: He marked him.

So no one would strike him.

---

(silence)
