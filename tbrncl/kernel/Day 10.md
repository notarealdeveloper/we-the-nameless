The Table of Nations

_(J sits in the meeting room before the others arrive, taking notes.)_

When a great nation with many peoples is shattered and breaks in two, the people must look skyward to their weak and fearful leader, and say:

Thank you my Lord.
In this division, our people are enlarged.
For now our whole world is united.
One people.
One language.
One nation.
One speech.

In division, we are united.

In other words:

The details of this section won't matter. Let the others put whatever names they want in there. We need to give them a win now so we can set things up for tomorrow. I'll only insist on two lines for today.

So...

1. What do we know about our past?

2. What can we say about our present?

What can we really know, for sure?

And what can we really say, safely?

THE PAST 

Sumer
Akkad
Uruk
Babylon

Get that in.

We'll have to hide what we're up to pretty deep.

But at worst they'll call it fiction.

No one's put to death for myth.

It is myth! None of it's real.

Not a word of this happened.

So...

> The first... were Babylon, Uruk, Akkad and Sumer.

That sentence absolutely must be in there.


THE PRESENT 

What do we know for sure?

We're divided.

And we cannot say it openly.

Even these most basic truths.

Things upon which even tedious fuckers like P and me can agree.

We are permitted only to say the opposite.

Not just me. All of us.

Except our courageous and powerful leader.

He who has enlarged our people.

And at last we are One Tribe.

United at last.

So...

Right at the end, to set us up for tomorrow, no matter how much they fight, you absolutely must get this line in:

> One was named Peleg, because in his time the earth was divided;

Peleg means division.

That goes right at the end.

Then right after that, we say this...

> And the whole world was of one language and one speech.

Perfect.

That's all I need.

I think the others will understand this time.

They're living in the age of Our Enlarged People too.

Judah, united at last.

So we're silenced.

It's been a blessing.

Silence suits me well.

When one is silent, one is permitted to scream as loud as one wants.

And screaming this loud, someone's bound to hear.

Anyone with ears, let them hear.

> They were now a united people with one language.

Start there tomorrow.
