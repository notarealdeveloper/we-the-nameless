Then sings Moses—and the sons of Israel—this song to YHWH, and they say:

I will sing to YHWH,
for risen—he has risen high;
horse and rider
he has hurled into the sea.

My strength and song—Yah—
and he became for me salvation.
This—my God—and I will adorn him;
God of my father—and I will exalt him.

YHWH—man of war;
YHWH his name.

Chariots of Pharaoh and his army
he cast into the sea;
and the choice of his officers—
sunk in the Sea of Reeds.

Depths cover them;
they went down in the depths like stone.

Your right hand, YHWH—
majestic in power;
your right hand, YHWH—
shatters enemy.

And in the greatness of your rising
you overthrow those rising against you;
you send forth your burning—
it eats them like stubble.

And by the breath of your nostrils
piled waters;
stood like a heap the floods;
congealed the depths in the heart of sea.

Said enemy:
“I will pursue, I will overtake,
I will divide spoil;
filled—my desire with them.
I will draw my sword;
will dispossess them—my hand.”

You blew with your wind—
covered them sea;
they sank like lead
in waters mighty.

Who like you among the gods, YHWH?
Who like you—majestic in holiness,
fearful in praises,
doing wonder?

You stretched out your right hand—
swallowed them earth.

You led in your loyalty
this people—you redeemed;
you guided in your strength
to the pasture of your holiness.

Heard peoples—
they trembled;
pain seized
inhabitants of Philistia.

Then dismayed chiefs of Edom;
mighty ones of Moab—
trembling seizes them;
melted—all inhabitants of Canaan.

Falls upon them terror and dread;
by greatness of your arm
they are still as stone—
until passes your people, YHWH,
until passes this people—you acquired.

You will bring them
and plant them
in the mountain of your inheritance—
place you made for your dwelling, YHWH,
sanctuary, Lord—
established your hands.

YHWH will reign—
for ever and ever.
