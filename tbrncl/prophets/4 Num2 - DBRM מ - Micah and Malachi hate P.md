## M

### Micah

Anti-elite rural prophet.
Hates rich landowners, corrupt judges, priests-for-hire.
Deeply anti-sacrifice when detached from justice.
"Do justice, love mercy, walk humbly."
Feels like anti-centralized-Jerusalem covenant morality.
Very Amos/Hosea adjacent.

### Malachi

Late temple critic FROM INSIDE temple system.
Priests are corrupt and offerings defective.
Still pro-sacrifice in principle.
Wants purified priesthood, proper tithes, covenant fidelity.
Almost proto-rabbinic transition energy.
