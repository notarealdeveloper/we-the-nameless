If:

𐤌𐤔 𐤕𐤁 𐤄 𐤏𐤅𐤕 𐤐𐤔 𐤀𐤋

MŠ TB H ʿWT PŠ ʾL

then:

| Segment | Possible Meaning |
|---|---|
| MŠ | consecration, anointing, offering, drawing out |
| TB | good, pleasing, pure, favorable |
| H | the, this |
| ʿWT | sign, testimony, witness, omen |
| PŠ | spread, proclaim, engrave, display |
| ʾL | El, god, deity |

Possible readings:

- “The good sign/testimony of El is proclaimed.”
- “The pleasing witness of El is displayed.”
- “The pure omen of El is engraved.”
- “The consecrated good sign of El.”
- “The favorable testimony of El is set forth.”
- “The good witness of El is proclaimed.”

This actually works extremely well semantically because:

TB
𐤈𐤁

is one of the oldest and most stable Semitic adjective roots:
- good
- pleasing
- favorable
- auspicious

So:

𐤈𐤁 𐤄 𐤏𐤅𐤕

TB HʿWT

very naturally reads like:

- “good sign”
- “favorable omen”
- “auspicious testimony”

which is exactly the sort of phrase one could imagine in a Bronze Age cultic inscription.

Or...

Assumed reading:

𐤌𐤔 𐤈𐤁 𐤄 𐤏𐤅𐤕 𐤐𐤔 𐤀𐤋

MŠ ṬB H ʿWT PŠ ʾL

Reparsed under:
- MŠ = MŠH (Moses) with omitted terminal He
- ṬB = good, pleasing, holy, favorable
- H = the, this
- ʿWT = sign, testimony, witness, covenant-token
- PŠ = proclaim, spread forth, inscribe, declare
- ʾL = El, god

Possible meanings:

| Segment | Meaning |
|---|---|
| MŠ | Moses, the drawer-out, Egyptian-Semitic name element |
| ṬB | good, holy, favorable, blessed |
| HʿWT | the testimony, the sign, the witness, the covenant-token |
| PŠ | proclaimed, spread forth, declared, inscribed |
| ʾL | El, god |

Possible readings:

- “Moses proclaimed the good testimony of El.”
- “Moses spread forth the holy sign of El.”
- “The good covenant-sign of El was proclaimed by Moses.”
- “Moses declared the witness of El.”
- “The blessed testimony of El was set forth by Moses.”
- “Moses inscribed the holy sign of El.”

Song-of-the-Sea-style conceptual resonance:

- holy mountain imagery
- covenant/testimony language
- El/Yahweh overlap period
- proclamation after deliverance
- mountain people / wilderness cult associations
- public declaration of divine victory
- archaic poetic compression

Strongest conceptual reading:

“Moses proclaimed the holy testimony/sign of El.”
