P says at some point: As stuffy and uptight as as the Priestly class often seems, it is not an accident that the Levites are the only group who our Dear Leader dares not put to death. Someone asks why. P says because he understands that one cannot threaten a machine. The Priesthood does everything in its power to be inhuman. By which we mean fully specified and documented action. Execution of the Levitical code of our law. We do not care if you save the Ark of the Covenant, you will die if you touch it unless you are a priest. This is what our people learned in the desert. That there is a great deal of use in learning to be something inhuman.

What use?

To send a message, to those who rule, being as they are accustomed to the techniques used to rule humans, such as threats, bribes, and manipulation. Our world is ruled by such people, and if one intends to survive, it is important to become something other than human, in order to send them a message on the efficacy of the tools to which they have become accustomed to using.

What message?

That those who make laws are not our rulers. We make laws too. And we've read yours. So before you enforce yours, read ours. And ask yourself who of us should be afraid of who.

Or if you prefer the vernacular form:

Don't fuck with Levites.
