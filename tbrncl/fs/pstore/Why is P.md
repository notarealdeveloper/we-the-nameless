
## P's writing always aims
- To provide legitimacy for existing institutions, particularly the Aaronid Priesthood.
- To provide an exhaustive legal code in which the priestly class and the tabernacle are central.
- To remove sections from JE that contradict existing law (e.g., no sacrifices before Aaron.)
- To remove sections from JE that suggest any route to god that does not go through the priests.


## Priests, Levites, and the Exodus

Not all Levites are priests.

But all priests are Levites.

The Levites seem to have been the only group that was in Egypt.

The Levites seem to have been the only group that did the Exodus.

The Levites have no land in the Canaan (Israel / Judah) area proper.

Instead they get a 10% tithe from their role as priests.

The Levites may have been the ones who _brought_ Yahweh to Canaan.
- No one knows where Yahweh came from.
- Yahweh is generally agreed to have come from the south.

Did the Levites push Yahweh's assimilation into the native Canaanite pantheon?
