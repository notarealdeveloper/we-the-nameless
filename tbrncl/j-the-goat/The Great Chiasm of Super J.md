## Description of Goal

Let's assume the middle of J was originally in what is now Joshua 3:17.

Let's further assume that J intended the entire work to be one big chiasmus.

1. From פשונ the river & יהוה breathing נפש into the human, to Solomon saying "אדנ יהו spoke against his own נפשו"


2. From (a bit later) Gihon to (a bit earlier) Gihon.


3. From (around the same time) the Yhwh in Gen, and Solomon in 1 Kings saying "On the day you go out you will surely die." (Yhwh is in a sense not correct, while Solomon is correct, because he executes the man after.)


4. From Brother kills Brother in Cain & Abel, to Brother kills Brother in Solomon & Adonijah, or Adonijah & Amnon.


5. From Dinah & Shechem (apparently not a rape, but called one by her brothers) to Amnon & Tamar (a real rape, but caused by her brother).


6. From Sodom & Gomorrah "that we may know them" scene where Lot offers his virgin daughters, to the Judges scene with the cut up concubine where the man offers his virgin daughter.



There are a tremendous number of these, and they seem to almost be a perfect chiasm, with a few cockeyed off by ones.

We may be pretty sure the redactor didn't reorder things.

We may be pretty sure about the identification of sources.

We may be pretty sure we have all or most of J.

But we're also reasonably confident that RJE picked J xor E for each bit much more than R, who tended to favor doublets.

So, let's assume the original book of J was one big thematic chiasm, and what we've got is a version where that's still visible but it's been messed with a bit.

Assuming that's the case, make me one big mega table starting at the Beginning and End, with 4 columns: early verse, later verse, and the content of each matching suspiciously closely (but usually with some simple part negated or swapped between the two).

Proceed through the full Super J source and finally meet in the middle around Gilgal.

Note: You CAN occasionally match late J with early E if needed, given RJE's tendency to be lossy and select J xor E, since that strongly suggests we're missing some J bits where we only have the E version.

Make the mega table now, and fill it with the best marchings you can. Have high standards for the matches, but be willing to blur your eyes a bit on the ordering or other details if there's strong suggestion or evidence of a match in the original that may not have been preserved perfectly after a couple millennia of edits.

I strongly suspect that somewhere around Gilgal or the Rahab prostitute story is the center of the chiasmus. Further, we have suggestions of "cutting Rahab to pieces" being J's original Genesis 1, and after the end of Super J we have the birth of RHB (Solomon's son Rehoboam). One's the death, one's the birth, and they're around the same time in the chiasmus, so make that the first row. No boilerplate or disclaimers. Do your best, and generate the mega table under the assumption that we have more than enough evidence that there's an underlying chiastic structure, and now we need the most parsimonious theory of what the matches were that requires only a very small number of hypotheses of what happened during redaction to recover the original. Go.
