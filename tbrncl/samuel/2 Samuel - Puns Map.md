## 2 Samuel 7 — The House Chapter

| Reference | Type | Name / Word | Hebrew | Meaning / Pun Tie |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 Sam 7:1–16 | House | בית | house | Central pun of the chapter |
| 2 Sam 7 | Temple | בית | house | David wants to build God's house |
| 2 Sam 7 | Dynasty | בית | house | God promises David a house instead |
| 2 Sam 7 | Household | בית | house | Triple meaning carried throughout speech |

This is probably the single most important pun in Samuel.
