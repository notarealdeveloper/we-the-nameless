## sheshakh

- occurs: Jeremiah 25:26; 51:41
- spelling: ששך
- decoding:
    - shin (ש) → bet (ב)
    - shin (ש) → bet (ב)
    - kaf (ך) → lamed (ל)
- result: Babylon (בבל)

## lev kamai

- occurs: Jeremiah 51:1
- spelling: לב קמי
- decoding:
    - lamed (ל) → kaf (כ)
    - bet (ב) → shin (ש)
    - qof (ק) → dalet (ד)
    - mem (מ) → yod (י)
    - yod (י) → mem (מ)
- result: Chaldeans (כשדים)
