
> The tradition of consecrating churches is thought to date back to the first century, and the antiquity of the specific ceremony we are treating today is already attested to already by the time of St. Gregory the Great.
>
> One of the ceremonies that always draws a great deal of interest in this regard is the act of the prelate inscribing the letters of the Latin and Greek alphabet onto the pavement (i.e. floor) of the church with his crozier.
