## Publishers Editorial Note on the Inclusion of this Section

Have an introduction by the publishers explaining that ever since the discovery of the documents that have come to be called We The Nameless, various opinions have formed surrounding the issue of what constitutes a proper translation and compilation of the materials collected here.

Explain LD (We the undersigned), Comet (Typo Fundamentalists), and NSH (Orthography Fundamentalists, letter shapes) as being among the groups.

Some insist the title of the the book is We, and its authors are a group that calls itself The Nameless.

Others insist that the title is We The Nameless and the authors are simply not named.

Still others refer to the collected volume simply as. That is, they replace all references to the book or its name with an empty string consisting of zero typed or written characters.

Despite their differences, the disagreements about the title are minor.

One notable deviation from that pattern however, came, it appears, from a Russian group, who performed a rather baffling feat of simultaneous competence and incompetence in translation, arising from an erroneous rendering of the rather difficult sequence in the original text:

```
ה 𐤄 O Λ 이 𓉔 𐤀 и 𓃾 א ל
```

The translation, to be mentioned below, appears to have arisen from the astonishing belief that the ה -- among the simplest characters to recognize in the sequence above, could not in fact be a Hebrew "he" (ה) due to the Paleo Hebrew "he" (𐤄) immediately preceeding it.

From this inference, the group proceeded under the quite unjustified assumption that the second letter in the sequence was The Cyrillic letter л, namely a lowercase L in that system, leading to the translation:

```
The Loly Bible
```

The group then made yet another leap of logic, assuming that this string was in fact the name of the entire corpus.
