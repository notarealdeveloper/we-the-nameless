}

	}

\Verse{3}

	\hX{

		כּל־אֲשֶׁר תַּעֲשֶׂה וְאֵת כּל־אֲשֶׁר תִּפְנֶה שָׁם׃
	}
}
{
	\eX{
		And observe YHWH your God’s charge, to walk in His ways, to
		observe His laws, His commandments, His judgments, and His
		testimonies, as written in Moses’ law, so that you’ll act wisely
		in all that you do and everywhere you turn there.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{4}
{
	\hX{

		אִישׁ מֵעַל כִּסֵּא יִשְׂרָאֵל׃
	}
}
{
	\eX{
		That YHWH may continue his word that he spoke concerning me,
		saying, If your children take heed to their way, to walk before
		me in truth with all their heart and with all their life, there
		shall not fail you (said he) a man on the throne of Israel.
	}
}
{
}

	}
}
{
	\eX{

		and what he did to the two commanders of the armies of Israel,
		to Abner the son of Ner, and to Amasa the son of Jether, whom he
		slaughtered, and shed the blood of war in peace, and put the
		blood of war on his belt that was about his waist, and in his
		shoes that were on his feet.

}
{

		and I swore to him by YHWH, saying, I'll not put you to death
		with the sword.

	\hX{וְהַשְּׁמֻעָה בָּאָה עַד־יוֹאָב כִּי יוֹאָב נָטָה אַחֲרֵי אֲדֹנִיָּה וְאַחֲרֵי אַבְשָׁלוֹם לֹא נָטָה וַיָּנס יוֹאָב אֶל־אֹהֶל יְהֹוָה וַיַּחֲזֵק בְּקַרְנוֹת הַמִּזְבֵּחַ׃}
}
{
	\eX{
		And tidings came to Joab: for Joab had turned after Adonijah,
		though he turned not after Absalom. And Joab fled to the
		tabernacle of YHWH, and caught hold on the horns of the altar.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{29}
{
	\hX{וַיֻּגַּד לַמֶּלֶךְ שְׁלֹמֹה כִּי נָס יוֹאָב אֶל־אֹהֶל יְהֹוָה וְהִנֵּה אֵצֶל הַמִּזְבֵּחַ וַיִּשְׁלַח שְׁלֹמֹה אֶת־בְּנָיָהוּ בֶן־יְהוֹיָדָע לֵאמֹר לֵךְ פְּגַע־בּוֹ׃}
}
{
	\eX{
		And it was told king Solomon that Joab was fled to the
		tabernacle of YHWH: and, here: he is by the altar. And Solomon
		sent Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, saying, Go, fall on him.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{30}
{
	\hX{
		וַיָּבֹא בְנָיָהוּ אֶל־אֹהֶל יְהֹוָה וַיֹּאמֶר אֵלָיו כֹּה־אָמַר הַמֶּלֶךְ צֵא וַיֹּאמֶר לֹא כִּי פֹה אָמוּת וַיָּשֶׁב בְּנָיָהוּ אֶת־הַמֶּלֶךְ דָּבָר לֵאמֹר כֹּה־דִבֶּר יוֹאָב
		וְכֹה עָנָנִי׃
	}
}
{
	\eX{
		And Benaiah came to the tabernacle of YHWH, and said to him,
		Thus says the king, Come forth. And he said, Nay: but I'll die
		here. And Benaiah brought the king word again, saying, Thus said
		Joab, and thus he answered me.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{31}
{
	\hX{וַיֹּאמֶר לוֹ הַמֶּלֶךְ עֲשֵׂה כַּאֲשֶׁר דִּבֶּר וּפְגַע־בּוֹ וּקְבַרְתּוֹ וַהֲסִירֹתָ דְּמֵי חִנָּם אֲשֶׁר שָׁפַךְ יוֹאָב מֵעָלַי וּמֵעַל בֵּית אָבִי׃}
}
{
	\eX{
		And the king said to him, Do as he has said, and fall on him,
		and bury him: that you may take away the innocent blood, which
		Joab shed, from me, and from the house of my father.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{32}
{
	\hX{
		וְהֵשִׁיב יְהֹוָה אֶת־דָּמוֹ עַל־רֹאשׁוֹ אֲשֶׁר פָּגַע בִּשְׁנֵי־אֲנָשִׁים צַדִּקִים וְטֹבִים מִמֶּנּוּ וַיַּהַרְגֵם בַּחֶרֶב וְאָבִי דָוִד לֹא יָדָע אֶת־אַבְנֵר בֶּן־נֵר
		שַׂר־צְבָא יִשְׂרָאֵל וְאֶת־עֲמָשָׂא בֶן־יֶתֶר שַׂר־צְבָא יְהוּדָה׃
	}
}
{
	\eX{
		And YHWH shall return his blood on his own head, who fell on two
		men more righteous and better than he, and slaughtered them with
		the sword, my father David not knowing of it, that is, Abner the
		son of Ner, commander of the army of Israel, and Amasa the son
		of Jether, commander of the army of Judah.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{33}
{
	\hX{וְשָׁבוּ דְמֵיהֶם בְּרֹאשׁ יוֹאָב וּבְרֹאשׁ זַרְעוֹ לְעֹלָם וּלְדָוִד וּלְזַרְעוֹ וּלְבֵיתוֹ וּלְכִסְאוֹ יִהְיֶה שָׁלוֹם עַד־עוֹלָם מֵעִם יְהֹוָה׃}
}
{
	\eX{
		Their blood shall therefore return on the head of Joab, and on
		the head of his seed for ever: but on David, and on his seed,
		and on his house, and on his throne, shall there be peace for
		ever from YHWH.
	}
}
{
}

}

	\eX{

\Verse{37}

}

		תָּמוּת וַתֹּאמֶר אֵלַי טוֹב הַדָּבָר שָׁמָעְתִּי׃

		word that I have heard is good.
