\Verse{1}

	\hX{וְהַמֶּלֶךְ דָּוִד זָקֵן בָּא בַּיָּמִים וַיְכַסֻּהוּ בַּבְּגָדִים וְלֹא יִחַם לוֹ׃}
}
{
	\eX{
		And King David was old, coming in days; and they covered him
		with clothes, but he was not warm.

%		Footnote 1:1. Coming in days is the Hebrew old-age idiom. The body of David, so
%		hot in Samuel, is now a cold political fact.
%	}
}
