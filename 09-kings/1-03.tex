\Verse{1}

{
	\eX{
		And Solomon said, You have showed to your servant David my

		him a son to sit on his throne, as it is this day.

	\eX{

\Verse{9}
{
	\hX{וְנָתַתָּ לְעַבְדְּךָ לֵב שֹׁמֵעַ לִשְׁפֹּט אֶת־עַמְּךָ לְהָבִין בֵּין־טוֹב לְרָע כִּי מִי יוּכַל לִשְׁפֹּט אֶת־עַמְּךָ הַכָּבֵד הַזֶּה׃}
}
{
	\eX{
		And give Your servant a listening heart to judge Your people, to
		understand between good and bad: because who is able to judge
		this heavy people of Yours?
	}
}
{
%	\footnote{
%		Footnote 3:9. Solomon asks for a listening heart. In Hebrew, wisdom begins in the
%		ear, not the head.
%	}
