}
{
	\eX{

}
{
	\eX{

\Verse{10}

	\hX{

		תְּדַבֵּר אֲלֵיהֶם קטנִּי עָבָה מִמּתְנֵי אָבִי׃

}
{
	\eX{
		And the young men that were grown up with him spoke to him,
		saying, Thus shall you speak to this people that spoke to you,
		saying, Your father made our yoke heavy, but make you it lighter
		to us: thus shall you say to them, My little finger shall be
		thicker than my father's waist.

}
{
}

\Verse{28}
{
	\hX{וַיִּוָּעַץ הַמֶּלֶךְ וַיַּעַשׂ שְׁנֵי עֶגְלֵי זָהָב וַיֹּאמֶר אֲלֵהֶם רַב־לָכֶם מֵעֲלוֹת יְרוּשָׁלַ͏ִם הִנֵּה אֱלֹהֶיךָ יִשְׂרָאֵל אֲשֶׁר הֶעֱלוּךָ מֵאֶרֶץ מִצְרָיִם׃}
}
{
	\eX{
		Whereon the king took counsel, and made two calves of gold, and
		said to them, It is too much for you to go up to Jerusalem:
		here: your gods, O Israel, which brought you up out of the land
		of Egypt.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{31}
{
	\hX{וַיַּעַשׂ אֶת־בֵּית בָּמוֹת וַיַּעַשׂ כֹּהֲנִים מִקְצוֹת הָעָם אֲשֶׁר לֹא־הָיוּ מִבְּנֵי לֵוִי׃}
}
{
	\eX{
		And he made a house of high places, and made priests of the
		lowest of the people, that were not of the sons of Levi.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{32}
{
	\hX{
		וַיַּעַשׂ יָרבְעָם חָג בַּחֹדֶשׁ הַשְּׁמִינִי בַחֲמִשָּׁה־עָשָׂר יוֹם לַחֹדֶשׁ כֶּחָג אֲשֶׁר בִּיהוּדָה וַיַּעַל עַל־הַמִּזְבֵּחַ כֵּן עָשָׂה בְּבֵית־אֵל לְזַבֵּחַ לָעֲגָלִים
		אֲשֶׁר־עָשָׂה וְהֶעֱמִיד בְּבֵית אֵל אֶת־כֹּהֲנֵי הַבָּמוֹת אֲשֶׁר עָשָׂה׃
	}
}
{
	\eX{
		And Jeroboam ordained a feast in the eighth month, on the
		fifteenth day of the month, like to the feast that is in Judah,
		and he offered on the altar. And did he in Bethel, sacrificing
		to the calves that he had made: and he placed in Bethel the
		priests of the high places that he had made.
	}
}
{
}

\Verse{33}
{
	\hX{
		וַיַּעַל עַל־הַמִּזְבֵּחַ אֲשֶׁר־עָשָׂה בְּבֵית־אֵל בַּחֲמִשָּׁה עָשָׂר יוֹם בַּחֹדֶשׁ הַשְּׁמִינִי בַּחֹדֶשׁ אֲשֶׁר־בָּדָא (מלבד) [מִלִּבּוֹ] וַיַּעַשׂ חָג לִבְנֵי יִשְׂרָאֵל
		וַיַּעַל עַל־הַמִּזְבֵּחַ לְהַקְטִיר׃
	}
}
{
	\eX{
		And he offered on the altar that he had made in Bethel the
		fifteenth day of the eighth month, even in the month that he had
		devised of his own heart: and ordained a feast to the children
		of Israel: and he offered on the altar, and burnt incense.
	}
}
{
}
