## The Jews

Also known as

1. The Jews Version Zero
2. The Humans
3. Babel _(disputed; possibly a distinct sect)_

This sect of The Nameless, confusingly, simply calls themselves "The Jews."

Not to be confused with the Jewish people as conventionally understood.

They share little in common with members of reform, conservative, orthodox, or hassidic Judaism proper, aside from, of course, the central importance placed by the sect on the text of _We,_ which itself is a commentary on the Tanak.

### Apocrypha

In addition to the main text, this group's version of the We The Nameless includes the following apocryphal and pseudepigraphic texts.

1. How To Control Everything. (Non-textual source.)

2. Let's Start The Jews. (LSTJ). A text, asserted to be among the earliest composed by the authors of _We,_ though this claim is disputed by virtually all scholars.)
