
## The Jewv

Also known as:

1. Je𓈖 (Spelled with a letter the group refers to as "tripleyou.")
2. Jwvɴиᴧ? (A common attempt at parsing the above)
3. The Juveniles (Colloquial / vernacular name)
4. We The 𐤊ikings (?) (Kikings, Vikings, or Kings, we're not sure.)

Often asserted not to be a sect at all, but rather simply a typo of "The Jews," others have made a convincing case that the sect does in fact exist, though likely in a decentralized form without leadership or management.

The primary function of this group appears to be distributing what they claim to be copies of the manuscript sanctioned by other more respectable groups, with no modifications to any serious issues of theology or translation, preferring instead to insert minor nearly unnoticeable rewordings of a word here and there such that the result spells something like "tits" when read vertically.

### Apocrypha

The group appears to have no sacred text, and to consist primarily of part-time employees of print shops and publishing companies associated with We The Nameless and related texts.
