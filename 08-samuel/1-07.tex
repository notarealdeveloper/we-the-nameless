\Verse{1}

}
{
	\eJ{
		The men of Kiriath-jearim came and took up the Ark of YHWH and
		brought it into the house of Abinadab on the hill; and they
		consecrated his son Eleazar to have charge of the Ark of YHWH.
	}
}
{
	So the north east folks come get the box and put it in a guy's house.
	
	His name is Abinadab, which is like ``father of generosity.''

	In solemn religious language, they declare that his son is in charge of the box.

	The son's name is Eleazar, which sort of means ``God is helpful.''
