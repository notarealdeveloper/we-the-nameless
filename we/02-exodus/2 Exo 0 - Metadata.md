## Plan

1. Open your kindle notes on Commentary on the Torah on your phone.
2. Starting from Exodus 1, generate the chapters one by one.
3. Go through your notes and have black explain to red all the things in your notes, briefly.

You don't need to say a lot.
You just need to point at the text,
highlight the same words as in your notes,
and let the reader do the rest.

Once we hit the 10 plagues and things really get going, black should remove the source information and write every verse in black.

Then he'll show red how you can identify the distinct voices by knowing that:
1. P is an Aaronid Priest (pretentious, unforgiving)
2. E is a Mushite Priest (religious, forgiving)
3. J is not religious at all (satire, puns, etc.)
