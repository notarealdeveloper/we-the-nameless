> The Levites who went far from me, going astray from me after their idols when Israel went astray, shall bear their punishment.
> -Ezekiel 44:10

> They shall not come near to me, to serve me as priest, nor come near any of my holy things…
> -Ezekiel 44:13

> Yet they shall be ministers in my sanctuary, having oversight at the gates of the temple and serving in the temple…
> -Ezekiel 44:11

> But the Levitical priests, the sons of Zadok, who kept the charge of my sanctuary when the people of Israel went astray from me, shall come near to me…
> -Ezekiel 44:15

The punishment is hereditary/institutional.

The whole class is stigmatized.

> They shall bear their shame and the abominations they committed.
> -Ezekiel 44:13

> You shall give to the Levitical priests of the family of Zadok…
> -Ezekiel 43:19
