### Pishon

Pishon: [פישון](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%9F)
- One of the four rivers in Eden.
- Only used once in the Bible, in the passage quoted above.
- Possibly from the root פ־ו־שׁ (p-w-sh) or פ־י־שׁ (p-i-sh), meaning "to spring about".
- Ugaritic analogue: [To open, to spread out](https://chatgpt.com/share/69d6b51d-cb54-83e8-9d94-61380946ca3c)

Pāšāh (פָּשָׁה)
- to spread out
- to extend
- sometimes: to invade / break out / raid

Suppose we conflate the two S and H like phonemes. Then we get:

PSH: [פסח](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D7%A4%D7%A1%D7%97))


### Havilah

Google Translate translates הַֽחֲוִילָ֔ה as literally: "The Beginning"

Root: חול (ḥ-w-l) — to whirl / writhe / twist

Meanings include:
- to writhe (like a serpent)
- to twist / dance / rotate
- sometimes: labor pains (intense bodily motion)

That's already very "serpent / winding / circular" flavored.

Root: חוּל / חִיל (ḥ-w-l / ḥ-y-l) — what this root actually does

You’re looking at a very old Semitic motion-root that shows up in a few overlapping shapes:

Root: חוּל / חוֹל (ḥûl / ḥōl)
Root: חִיל (ḥîl)

[Isaiah 13:8](https://biblehub.com/text/isaiah/13-8.htm) uses the root verb to describe a woman's labor pains.
[Jeremiah 4:31](https://biblehub.com/text/jeremiah/4-31.htm) uses the root verb to describe a woman's labor pains too.

### Gold

Hebrew: זהב
- The Hebrew [זהב](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D7%96%D7%94%D7%91) is cognate with Arabic [ذهب](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D8%B0%D9%87%D8%A8#Arabic) meaning:
1. go, leave
2. to go away, to leave, to depart
3. to disappear, to vanish
4. to perish, to die
- Also related to Arabic [ذهاب](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D8%B0%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%A8#Arabic) meaning:
2. going
3. outbound
4. going away, disappearing

### Kush

Looking for possible puns on: כוש

Tl;dr:
- Across semitic languages:
- k-š ≈ break / strip / expose / fail: see [here](https://chatgpt.com/share/69d6c323-e57c-83e8-9d96-665c7b9d4723)
- kos ≈ cup / vessel / concavity: see [here](https://chatgpt.com/share/69d6c37b-b590-83e8-aa2b-f899a387bf49)

Gihon is said to circle Kush, כוש.
- Swapping the S, we get כוס which means "pussy, vagina."
- In which case the pun would be "pussy at center of belly."
- In Arabic: كُسّ (_kuss_) means pussy (Vulgar term, not classical literary Arabic.)
- Here's a saying literally meaning [Your mother's pussy](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A1_%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%9B).
- Another source for the semitic "pussy" root [here](https://doitinhebrew.com/Translate/default.aspx?kb=IL%20Hebrew%20Phonetic&l1=iw&l2=en&txt=%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A1&s=1).
- Also כוס means "cup".
- Strong says כוס is from an unused root meaning to hold together.

At best, something like:
- A root **k-s / k-w-s** with meanings around:
    - bending
    - concavity
    - enclosure

And then later:
- Arabic dialects → sexual meaning
- Hebrew → borrowing from Arabic
