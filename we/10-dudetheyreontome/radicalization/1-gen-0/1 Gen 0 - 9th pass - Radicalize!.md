

## J's Genesis 1

### J Gen 0:1 - In the day YHWH made earth and skies

| BCV | Content | Radical |
|------|------|------|
| J Gen 0:1 (Heb) | ביום עשות יהוה ארץ ושמים | 神界天工 |
| J Gen 0:1 (Eng) | In the day that YHWH made earth and skies. | 神界天工 |
| Genesis 2:4b | These are the generations of the skies and the earth when they were created, in the day that YHWH God made earth and skies. | 神界天工 |
| Enuma Elish I:1 | When on high no word was used for heaven. | 天 |
| Enuma Elish I:2 | Nor below was firm ground called by name. | 界 |
| Note | Unlike P's formula (heavens and earth), J preserves the order earth and skies. We assume, following Friedman, that the word God (אלהים) here was added by the redactor to soften the transition between P's Genesis 1 and J's Genesis 2:4b onward. | 界天神 |
| Observation | Enuma Elish begins with unnamed heaven and earth and ends with named gods and ordered destinies. Genesis likewise moves from primordial disorder toward an ordered and differentiated world. | 天界多神工 |
